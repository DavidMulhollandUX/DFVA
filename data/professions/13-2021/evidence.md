## L1 — regulatory
- Australian Property Institute / state valuation authorities, "Valuers Registration & Licensing" —
  https://australianproperty.network/apn-codex/series-30000/series-36000/series-36200/series-36220 (2024)
  > "valuer registration and licensing in Australia is administered separately by each state and territory,
  > operating alongside the Australian Property Institute's own non-statutory certification scheme, the Certified
  > Practising Valuer designation."
  Dual structure: QLD/WA retain statutory registration; NSW repealed the Valuers Act 2003 (2016) and uses
  "suitably qualified valuer" (API CPV/AVI or RICS). NCCP Act 2009 (ASIC) engages where valuations inform
  consumer credit. AI/automated valuation models are an emerging edge not yet separately licensed.
