Degree Future-Viability Assessment (DFVA) — scoring university degree programs against AI-driven labour-market disruption. This map identifies who cares about the problem, who has influence, and where Evidura's value proposition lands.

Program: MEC Discover · 7 weeks Venture: Evidura Date: Sep 2026
Problem Statement

AI is reshaping the labour market faster than universities can adapt curriculum. Degree programs that produced employable graduates five years ago may no longer align with emerging job profiles. Universities lack a systematic, evidence-based method to assess which programs remain viable, which need redesign, and which should be sunset. Evidura provides a data-driven assessment tool that scores degree programs against AI labour-market disruption risk — giving faculty leadership the evidence to act.
