# Playbook: Commercialisation & Governance Oversight

## Objective
Govern the venture's spinout roadmap from the University of Melbourne, ensuring strict IP compliance, trademark clearance, and independence structuring.

---

## Operating Protocol

### 1. Trademark Clearance Gate
- Verify Nice classes 9, 35, 41, 42 status with IP Australia.
- **Rule**: No paid public advertising, enterprise contract execution, or external certification seal issuance until formal trademark clearance.

### 2. UoM IP Boundary Enforcement
- Review all code and methodology additions against `docs/evidura-uom-commercialisation-reference.md`.
- Maintain clean separation between internal UoM curriculum review projects and independent platform IP.

### 3. Independence Governance
- Adhere to `docs/evidura-independence-structure.md`:
  - Ratings must be computed algorithmically without manual editorial tampering.
  - Pilot faculties receive confidential benchmarking reports, but cannot alter underlying scoring rubrics.
