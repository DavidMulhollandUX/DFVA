# Evidura Strategic Pillars & Governance

## 1. Brand Hierarchy & Positioning
- **Master Brand**: **Evidura** (domain `evidura.ai`). Replaces the internal working title "COMPASS". Tagline: *"Durability, made visible"*.
- **Consumer Product**: **Durability Rating™** — an intuitive 0–100 score categorized into four risk bands:
  - `Resilient` (80–100)
  - `Moderate Risk` (60–79)
  - `High Risk` (40–59)
  - `Critical` (0–39)
- **Methodology Engine**: **DFVA™ (Degree Future-Viability Assessment)** — internal 11-dimension assessment rubric evaluating degree programs against AI disruption and labour market volatility. Confidential and internal-only.

## 2. Institutional Spin-Out & Commercialisation (UoM)
- **Key Reference**: `docs/evidura-uom-commercialisation-reference.md`, `docs/evidura-independence-structure.md`.
- **Core Governance Goals**:
  1. Maintain objective assessment independence from university administration to protect credibility.
  2. Structure IP and licensing clean of encumbrances.
  3. Ensure pathway for external capital / non-dilutive grant / venture studio spinout.
  4. Safeguard confidentiality of pilot benchmarking data before institutional release.

## 3. Core Venture Hypotheses (H0–H4)
- **H0 (Viability)**: Degrees can be reliably assessed for future durability via systematic curriculum analysis and economic signal synthesis.
- **H1 (Demand)**: Prospective students, parents, and university curriculum leaders actively seek third-party durability signals at point of decision.
- **H2 (Trust / Standing)**: Objective, independent ratings carry more authority than internal university marketing claims.
- **H3 (WTP / Monetization)**: Universities will pay for deep benchmarking, vulnerability analysis, and curriculum remediation recommendations.
- **H4 (Unoccupied Adjacency)**: Incumbent higher-ed curriculum management tools (Coursedog, CourseLeaf, Modern Campus) lack AI-durability predictive analytics.
