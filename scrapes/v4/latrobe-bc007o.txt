## Graduate Certificate in Business Analytics - On-Line
##### BC007O
##### 60 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
If you’re keen to give your business career a boost, La Trobe’s Graduate Certificate in Business Analytics is the perfect place to learn how to build smarter, more resilient business solutions with data. Designed in consultation with industry leaders, this course equips you with practical, future-ready skills to thrive in the rapidly evolving digital economy.

Explore how artificial intelligence is transforming business decisionmaking. Understand the fundamentals of cybersecurity and how to safeguard digital systems. Get hands-on with prototyping and low-code development through innovative coding tools. Develop your analytical mindset and learn how to utilise business data to create a real impact. 

You’ll learn through applied, team-based projects and gain experience with technologies and practices used across sectors. Plus, through guest lectures and industry connections, you’ll hear directly from experts from leading organisations.

After successfully completing the Graduate Certificate in Business Analytics, you may be eligible for up to one semester of advanced standing toward La Trobe’s Master of Business Analytics, allowing you to fast-track your studies.

You will learn:

  * AI for Business  * Explore the fundamentals of AI and how it's transforming industries. Learn how to apply AI tools ethically to create smarter, data-driven business solutions.

  * Principles of Business Analytics   * Build foundational skills in data interpretation, visualisation, and decision-making. Learn how to turn business data into insights that drive strategy and performance.

  * Cybersecurity for Business   * Understand the key principles, risks, and frameworks that protect digital business systems. Learn how to identify threats, ensure compliance, and build cyber-resilient organisations.

  * Vibe Coding for Startups 
  * Develop rapid prototypes and innovative solutions using low-code tools. Learn how to bring digital ideas to life in business-focused, agile team environments.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Design and develop technology AI startup prototypes using vibe coding to address complex business challenges.
**2.**
Apply principles of cybersecurity, ethical practice, and data privacy to ensure responsible handling of information in analytics and AI contexts.
**3.**
Analyse and interpret business data using descriptive analytics methods and visualisation tools to support data-informed decision-making in organisational contexts.
**4.**
Apply AI prompting and strategies to develop chatbots that address complex business needs.
**5.**
Communicate analytics strategies, actionable insights, and AI-supported recommendations effectively to diverse organisational stakeholders.

Credit Points: 15
Overview: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the nature and function of business. However, business leaders are challenged by the effective and competitive adoption of AI. This subject provides you with an industry-ready skillset to empower productivity, innovation and decision-making across the business ecosystem. Subject: CYBERSECURITY FOR BUSINESS (BUS4006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Cybersecurity for Business provides a comprehensive overview of cybersecurity principles, practices, and frameworks for managing cybersecurity risks in organisations. Cybersecurity plays a critical role in an organisation's operations by safeguarding its assets, ensuring regulatory compliance, and enhancing overall business resilience. The subject covers key topics, including cybersecurity threats, attacks, governance frameworks, cybersecurity risk management, cybersecurity policies and procedures, and compliance. You will explore cybersecurity for businesses through a combination of tailor-made case studies and hands-on workshops, offering a comprehensive understanding of theoretical principles alongside practical application in real-world scenarios. It develops the skills necessary to craft effective cybersecurity strategies that align with organisational goals and regulatory requirements. The course will also examine the ethical, legal, and regulatory dimensions of cybersecurity.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse different types of cyber threats, attack methods, and AI-driven threats, to assess their potential impact on business operations, risk management, and compliance obligations.
- Design and justify ethical and governance-aligned cybersecurity strategies using appropriate frameworks, AI-enabled tools, and security models to strengthen threat detection, incident response, resilience, and regulatory compliance.
- Identify and apply fundamental cybersecurity principles to recognise and evaluate common organisational security challenges, including those arising from emerging technologies and AI.
- Communicate cybersecurity governance strategies and AI-informed risk mitigation plans clearly and appropriately to diverse technical and non-technical stakeholders in professional business contexts.

Subject: PRINCIPLES OF BUSINESS ANALYTICS (BUS4011)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject introduces you to business and data analytics with a strong focus on practical outcomes that are directly applicable to business contexts. It provides a comprehensive understanding of current theories, frameworks, applications, and technologies that support data-driven decision-making in organisations. You will gain hands-on experience with industry-standard analytics platforms to conduct descriptive analytics and build interactive dashboards aligned with business needs. The subject explores the strategic role of analytics in digital transformation, performance management, and continuous improvement. Key topics include descriptive analytics, visualisation tools, data ethics, and cybersecurity. Industry-based guest lectures provide insights into how organisations are applying analytics to enhance business performance and address emerging challenges. Upon completion, you will be able to explain the role of analytics in business environments, apply analytical tools, evaluate ethical considerations, and align analytics strategies with organisational objectives.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply advanced analytics techniques and tools aligned with industry best practice to generate data-driven insights, design effective dashboards, and support evidence-based decision-making.
- Evaluate cybersecurity, data protection, and ethical challenges in organisational analytics and AI, and apply frameworks to ensure responsible and compliant data practices.
- Critically examine the purpose and strategic value of analytics and AI in organisations to enhance decision-making, operational efficiency, and innovation.

Subject: VIBE CODING FOR STARTUPS (BUS4012)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Vibe coding and AI-assisted coding are recent AI capabilities that significantly reduce the cost, time and expertise required for technology adoption and development. These unlock innovation opportunities for SMEs and startups operating with limited budgets. This subject will provide you with technical, creative and problem-solving skills for the design, development and evaluation of prototypes for startups and related business environments. The risks and limitations of vibe coding will be learned alongside conventional coding methods, ethics and cybersecurity guidelines. The ecosystem, opportunities and challenges of AI startups will be a further topic of this subject.
Learning Outcomes:
- Develop and evaluate technology prototypes for diverse business and industry settings using vibe coding and AI-assisted coding.
- Develop and evaluate AI startup strategies to address complex industrial challenges and advance the UN Sustainable Development Goals, demonstrating effective collaboration and teamwork in professional settings.
- Analyse risks and limitations of vibe coding and apply conventional coding methods, ethical principles, and cybersecurity guidelines to address these challenges.
