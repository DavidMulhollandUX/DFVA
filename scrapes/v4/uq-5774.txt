Subject: MINE3110 - Mining Geomechanics
### MINE3110 - Mining Geomechanics
Curriculum unit MINE3110: Mining Geomechanics covering fundamental principles, professional methodologies, and critical evaluation at The University of Queensland.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate comprehensive understanding of key principles in mining geomechanics.
- Apply analytical frameworks and professional methods in mining geomechanics.
- Communicate findings and technical arguments effectively in professional contexts.
Assessment Tasks:
- Coursework Project: 50%
- Examination: 50%
Workload: 144 hours

Subject: MINE4126 - Mining Systems and Automation
### MINE4126 - Mining Systems and Automation
Learning Outcomes:
- Select the mining method most appropriate for a deposit in the presence of a limited set of constraints.
- Appraise mining methods with respect to metrics such as productivity, safety, sustainability and risk
- Describe and illustrate major mining methods using geotechnical and mechanical components of a range of common mining methods, along with their supporting infrastructure.
- Identify the key performance drivers and constraints of a range of common mining methods.
- Apply the systems approach to mining by Identifying the aspects of mining methods and equipment that best represent inputs, outputs, key drivers and constraints.
- Evaluate the application of automation for mining operations.
- Enhance collaborative competencies and master advanced oral and written communication techniques to effectively contribute to team-based projects.
Assessment Tasks:
- Group Assignment - Surface Mining Team or group-based: 35%%
- Group Assignment - Underground Mining Team or group-based: 35%%
- Individual Assignment with pass/fail presentation (oral examination) - Mining Technology Hurdle Identity Verified: 30%%
Workload: Applied Class 2 Hours/ Week

Subject: MINE6111 - Principles of Fleet Management
### MINE6111 - Principles of Fleet Management
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify operational safety and health issues for truck/shovel fleet operations on the basis of recent industry case studies.
- Assess contemporary mining operations in the context of established truck/shovel operating principles.
- Calculate key performance metrics for truck/shovel fleet operations.
- Apply the theory of constraints to truck/shovel operations.
- Develop continuous improvement initiatives for truck/shovel fleet management.
- Apply life cycle costing principles to shovel/truck operations.
- Evaluate truck/shovel operations in the context of sustainability principles.
Assessment Tasks:
- Presentation of Project Brief Identity Verified Online: 5%%
- Project Brief Online: 20%%
- Presentation of Report Findings and Q&A Identity Verified Online: 25%%
- Final Report Hurdle: 50%%
Workload: Contact 4 Hours/ Week

Subject: MINE6120 - Short Term Mine Planning and Scheduling
### MINE6120 - Short Term Mine Planning and Scheduling
Learning Outcomes:
- Assess the context in which they are to undertake an execution plan with consideration and compliance to technical, environmental and social variables.
- Hypothesise, test, and validate a critical path to improve mining production.
- Communicate the decision-making process in relevant mining contexts to empower operations and other stakeholders.
- Develop and justify contingency plans to provide alternative paths to achieve the main objective of the mine execution plan.
- Identify and use appropriate project management tools and strategies to develop and evaluate an execution plan.
- Visualise the critical path of key production milestones to various stakeholders using appropriate software tools.
- Apply appropriate mathematical techniques in the decision-making process to inform the critical path.
- Reflect on mining project plans and industry practitioners to derive future consideration for professional practice.
Assessment Tasks:
- Proposal Pitch Presentation Identity Verified: 10%%
- Project Brief Document: 15%%
- Final Presentation Hurdle Identity Verified: 30%%
- Final Report: 45%%
Workload: Applied Class 4 Hours/ Week Workshop 8 Hours/ One Off
