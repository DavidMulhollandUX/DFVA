##### HCSP
##### 60 credit points

### Overview
This course gives graduate physiotherapists an opportunity to develop advanced theoretical and applied skills in sports physiotherapy, critical evaluation of relevant literature and research design. The flexible design of this course allows students to continue clinical work while studying.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Work effectively and accountably within areas of professional responsibility, independently and in the environments of sporting teams
**2.**
Apply advanced knowledge and skills in the practice of sports physiotherapy
**3.**
Critically review, analyse and synthesize clinical and research evidence to find solutions for common sports-related injuries and problems
**4.**
Communicate in written and oral formats advanced technical knowledge and theoretical concepts in sports physiotherapy to lay and expert audiences
**5.**
Apply sports physiotherapy knowledge and skills to make high level independent judgments, and execute and evaluate non-specialist management plans

### Career outcomes
Physiotherapists are employed in a wide variety of settings, including private practice, general hospitals, rehabilitation centres, community health centres, industrial clinics and specialist centres. Physiotherapists assist both male and female patients with a wide variety of disorders and across the life span. Opportunities also exist in physiotherapy education and in research.

Subject: RESEARCH TO INFORM ADVANCED PHYSIOTHERAPY (PTY5024)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this fully online subject, students will develop their knowledge and skills required for evidence-informed practice in musculoskeletal or sports and exercise physiotherapy. The subject includes the completion of a Critically Appraised Topic (CAT) based on a sports or musculoskeletal physiotherapy problem that students have encountered in clinical practice. In completing the CAT, students will develop skills in formulating an answerable clinical question, searching for and critically appraising the available evidence to inform clinical reasoning and practice. Students will also refine their theoretical knowledge on the research process and human ethics to incorporate research into the clinical setting to inform advanced physiotherapy practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically appraise and interpret the evidence base of musculoskeletal/sports and exercise physiotherapy across a variety of qualitative and quantitative research methodologies.
- Generate an answerable clinical research question based on reflection and the critical evaluation of your clinical practice
- Identify and systematically address ethical considerations associated with research in the musculoskeletal/sports and exercise physiotherapy setting.
- Analyse, interpret and synthesise critically appraised research findings from an answerable clinical question to inform the assessment and management of patients in clinical practice

Subject: ADVANCED PHYSIOTHERAPY FOR THE UPPER BODY (PTY5025)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this blended subject, students will gain advanced knowledge and clinical skills in musculoskeletal and sports and exercise physiotherapy, with a focus on the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine. The subject begins with an online clinical reasoning module designed to enhance decision-making and problem-solving of complex cases. This is followed by an online module to enhance diagnostic imaging interpretation for upper limb and cervical presentations. Practical components include applied clinical anatomy wet labs and advanced physiotherapy assessment and management of upper limb, cervical and thoracic conditions. This subject prepares qualified physiotherapists to integrate advanced assessment and management techniques into clinical practice. This subject includes professional learning, practical experiences or portfolio activities which contribute to the completion of the Silver Career Ready AdvantageTM award .
Learning Outcomes:
- Integrate contemporary, evidence-informed practices into clinical scenarios involving the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine, demonstrating the ability to adapt and apply advanced physiotherapy techniques in a variety of clinical settings.
- Apply advanced clinical reasoning skills to develop and implement comprehensive assessment and management strategies for complex upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine conditions in musculoskeletal and sports physiotherapy
- Apply advanced theoretical knowledge and practical skills to critically analyse and address complex neuromusculoskeletal presentations of the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine, with a focus on optimising patient outcomes
- Proficiently interpret diagnostic imaging and integrate applied clinical anatomy to inform evidence-based decision-making in patient care for conditions of the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine
- Critically apply self-reflection to identify strengths and areas for improvement in the assessment and management of upper limb, cervical, and thoracic conditions, fostering ongoing professional growth as an effective lifelong learner.

Subject: PATHOPHYSIOLOGY AND PAIN SCIENCE FOR ADVANCED PHYSIOTHERAPY (PTY5026)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this fully online subject, students will develop their knowledge in applying comprehensive concepts of pathophysiology, pain science, and pharmacology to differentiate between normal and abnormal neuromusculoskeletal function within the context of advanced physiotherapy. Students will critically evaluate pathology and pathogenesis related to neuromusculoskeletal dysfunction and interpret the neuromusculoskeletal system’s responses to load, including impacts on function, injury, and tissue healing. This subject provides qualified physiotherapists with comprehensive knowledge of clinical sciences and their influence on assessment and treatments in advanced physiotherapy practice. This subject includes professional learning, practical experiences or portfolio activities which contribute to the completion of the Silver Career Ready AdvantageTM award.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically evaluate the pathology, and pathogenesis underlying neuromusculoskeletal dysfunctions in advanced physiotherapy practice
- Integrate a theoretical understanding of the effects of pharmacology on pain and the neuromusculoskeletal system to inform effective advanced physiotherapy assessment and management.
- Interpret how the neuromusculoskeletal system’s responds to load, including its effects on function, injury response, and tissue healing, and evaluate how these factors influence assessment and treatments in advanced physiotherapy practice
- Integrate and apply comprehensive knowledge of anatomy, physiology, and pain science to distinguish between normal and abnormal function in the neuromusculoskeletal system
