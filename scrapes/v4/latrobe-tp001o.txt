## Professional Certificate in Cybersecurity - On-Line
##### TP001O
##### 30 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Start your journey towards a career in cybersecurity.  From an exploration of unauthorised access of data, to delving into the mind of a hacker, our leading academics and industry practitioners will equip you with the essential skills so that you can be a part of Australia’s human firewall in the fight against cyber criminals.

You'll learn:

  * Cyber security principles  * Explore how to prevent unauthorised access of information and data. Learn how to respond to a data breach or cyberattack and understand how to mitigate their negative impacts.

  * Psychology of cyber criminals  * Get inside the minds of hackers and investigate their motivations and techniques. Better understand how to identify digital weaknesses and emerging online threats and learn how to develop more effective defences.

Addressing the growing demand for skilled cybersecurity professionals, this course offers learners with the opportunity for advanced standing in the Graduate Certificate and Master of Cybersecurity programs.

The Professional Certificate in Cybersecurity is a postgraduate award of the University.* 

*A non-AQF award that stacks into the Graduate Certificate, Diploma or Master of Cybersecurity and is eligible for postgraduate FEE-HELP.

### Course features
### Career outcomes
This course is ideal for professionals seeking to transition into cybersecurity or expand their technical expertise, offering practical insights into threat analysis, risk management, and ethical hacking. Delivered fully online for flexibility, it focuses on real-world applications to help learners:

  * Explore cybersecurity principles, including preventing unauthorised access, responding to data breaches, and implementing security measures like firewalls and intrusion detection systems.
  * Develop incident response strategies and risk management practices to safeguard digital assets and mitigate cyber threats.
  * Understand the psychology of cybercriminals by analysing hacker motivations, tactics, and emerging threats to build more effective defences.

Subject: CYBER SECURITY PRINCIPLES (CSE4004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In the Internet era, industries and organisations need to be aware of, and be prepared to defend against threats and attacks. Stakeholders should be familiar with the basic principles and best practices of cyber security to better protect their businesses. In this subject, the principles, the state of the art, and strategies for the future of cyber security is explored thoroughly. The topics will focus on information security, ethical and legal practices, mitigating cyber vulnerabilities, and the process of incident response and analysis. The outline of the subject is targeted at ensuring the privacy, reliability, confidentiality and integrity of information systems. Cyber security is a very broad discipline, and therefore, this subject is only intended to cover the basics of the recent state of the art and leading cyber security topics.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse approaches to security networks, firewalls, intrusion detection systems and intrusion prevention systems
- Appraise key terms and concepts in cyber security, cyber law, intellectual property and cyber crime
- Formulate approaches for incident analysis, risk management in cyber space and incident response
- Analyse various software design practices and principles of web security

Subject: INSIDE THE MIND OF A HACKER (CSE5010)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject explores the motivations, mindset and techniques used by hackers. Although their activities are illicit and illegal, hackers have a finely attuned understanding of computer networks and systems and how users/customers behave in online environments. If nothing else, they have developed new - albeit illegal and unethical business models that exploit vulnerabilities in computer networks and systems. By looking at systems and practices through the eyes of a hacker, you can better identify weaknesses, emerging threats and develop more effective defences.
Learning Outcomes:
- Recommend processes and practices to reduce the likelihood of data breaches
- Deconstruct and critically analyze the profit models, value chain, and standard business security practices from the viewpoint of a hacker.
- Critically analyse the various tactics that hackers use in breaching communication networks.
- Critically analyse typologies of hackers, their mindsets and the motivations behind hacking.
