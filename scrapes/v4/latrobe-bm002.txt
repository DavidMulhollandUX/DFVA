## Master of Logistics and Supply Chain Management - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BM002
##### 240 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Tackle real-world logistics and supply chain challenges while using emerging analytics technologies to transform business practice with La Trobe's Master of Logistics and Supply Chain Management.

Designed and delivered in consultation with industry professionals, you'll gain direct exposure to the logistics and supply chain sector through field trips to ports and distribution centres, guest lectures, hands-on assessments, and optional industry placements.

Explore topics across AI, blockchain, Internet of Things, cybersecurity governance and applied data analytics, covering the technical skills while exploring the ethical, environmentally responsible supply chain innovation through integrated circular economy and carbon accountability practices — preparing you for future-ready roles at the forefront of Industry 5.0 innovations.

You'll benefit from tailored pathways depending on your professional experience and prior learning with options to complete your Master's degree in 1 or 1.5 years or exit early with a Graduate Certificate or Graduate Diploma. You'll also study through a combination of online and on campus classes with full-or part-time study options – giving you greater flexibility to balance work and life commitments.

By studying La Trobe's Master of Logistics and Supply Chain Management, you will:

  * master sustainable and responsible business practice and the circular economy in a national and global context
  * build strong foundational expertise across all key areas of supply chain strategy, including logistics, sourcing, marketing, ICT, and customer service
  * develop broad managerial skills in human resource management, project management, and quality control to prepare for leadership roles in complex, global supply networks
  * gain hands-on data analytics skills using real-world tools and techniques, such as data visualisation, predictive analytics, and business analytics applications specific to logistics and supply chain contexts
  * understand the impact of digitalisation and technologies like artificial intelligence (AI), digital twins and cloud computing to unlock valuable insights
  * engage in industry-led experiential and work-integrated learning, including coaching and networking opportunities and access to graduate programs with our partners.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Critically analyse complex supply chain data to develop evidence-based, data-driven solutions and support innovation across end-to-end supply chains.
**2.**
Critically evaluate logistics and supply chain strategies, industry practices, and digital initiatives to enhance business efficiency, effectiveness, and adaptability.
**3.**
Formulate logistics and supply chain strategies that integrate circular economy principles and advance sustainability across environmental, social, and economic dimensions, in alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
**4.**
Apply advanced communication, business, and digital literacy skills to engage diverse stakeholders and deliver effective outcomes in logistics and supply chain management.
**5.**
Develop digitalisation solutions that leverage emerging technologies to transform logistics operations and supply chain management.

### Course features
### Work based learning (placement) requirements
During this course, you will have the opportunity to participate in a Work Based Learning (WBL) placement experience, designed to allow you to extend your formal academic learning beyond the classroom. The WBL experience will provide the opportunity to bring your learning from the university into a work environment and test out your knowledge in a professional real-world environment. Placements are expected to be self-sourced by students and be related to their studies. Subject to approval from the University, placements can be undertaken within an organisation, remotely, or as a virtual experience.
### Work integrated learning opportunities
Industry presentations, Industry informed curriculum, Industry-informed projects, Interprofessional learning, Field trips
### Career outcomes
The demand for skilled professionals in logistics and supply chain management is rapidly growing across various industries. You could find employment in a variety of industries, including logistics and transportation, government, production and manufacturing, and non-for-profit organisations.

Possible roles could include:

  * procurement, supply chain, and operations manager
  * logistics and supply chain consultant
  * e-commerce and omnichannel specialist
  * sustainability and circular supply chain specialist
  * supply chain innovation and technology manager.

Subject: INTERNATIONAL TRANSPORT AND LOGISTICS (MGT5004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: When cargo flows and supply chains extend beyond national borders, the complexity of international transport and logistics activities increases. This subject focuses on the global transport of goods, which heavily relies on ships, aircraft, and trains. As a result, more stakeholders become involved, additional documentation is generated and transferred, financial payment risks intensify, and disruptions and uncertainties can impact supply chains. In this subject, you will acquire essential knowledge and develop job-ready skills necessary for roles such as international transport and logistics coordinators and freight forwarders. This includes gaining familiarity with trade and customs policies, understanding documentation requirements for efficient cargo flows and physical distribution, developing the ability to design international transport and logistics plans for various business scenarios, creating recovery plans to address changes and disruptions in international transport and logistics, and effectively communicating information and potential changes to stakeholders. Additionally, the subject explores sustainability concerns, data analytics, and emerging technologies related to digitalisation.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design international transport and logistics plans for efficient, profitable, sustainable and resilient supply chains.
- Exercise professional judgement and evaluate international logistics management practices for a collaborative supply chain.
- Critically analyse the impact of international transport and logistics on a company's performance and collaborations within global supply chains.
- Critically analyse the implications of trade policies, international conventions, sustainability commitments, and digitalisation on global transport, logistics, and supply chains.

Subject: CIRCULAR ECONOMY IN PRACTICE (MGT5006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: The circular economy is an economic system designed to minimise waste and continually use resources. The circular economy aims to generate economic and social value while ensuring environmental sustainability by creating closed-loop systems that minimise resource input and waste output. As businesses transition towards this model, job requirements are evolving to prioritise sustainability and circular economy practices. This subject explores the interplay between the circular economy and value chain management. In consultation with industry partners, it is designed to provide an engaging learning journey to understand, strategize, and apply circular economy principles, including sustainable resource utilization, waste minimization, and the integration of repair, reuse, and recycling through the lens of value chain management. Students will have the opportunity to work on selected industry projects to enhance their practical skills in identifying and addressing circular economy challenges, utilizing various tools and data-driven methods. Emphasizing the significance and urgency of circular value chain strategies, this subject aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals, preparing students to make informed and responsible decisions in various business settings, both individually and collectively. The applied nature of the subject delivery enables the development of critical business skills essential for roles in the circular economy and sustainability domains.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate teamwork and leadership skills to collectively strategise, communicate, and execute practical circular economy practices in various business settings.
- Exercise professional judgment in addressing complex circular economy challenges.
- Critically analyse circular economy principles within value chains to enhance competitive advantage of organisations in various settings.
