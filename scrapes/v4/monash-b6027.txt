### Structure
The Master of Business Innovation is structured in four parts: Part A. Advanced preparatory studies, Part B. Discipline studies, Part C. Application studies and Part D. Elective studies

Part A. Advanced preparatory studies

Through multidisciplinary studies you will decipher the essence of innovation and explore the role of management, marketing, regulation and resource allocation in shaping our socio-economic fortunes. Drawing on innovation methodologies and tools you will explore innovation in the Indonesian context and further your ability to do business in complex environments.

Part B. Discipline studies

Building on the foundation capabilities you acquired in Part A you will extend your knowledge of innovation with models and theories from finance, leadership, institutional theory and operations management. You will explore how digitisation is altering the 'rules of the game' for businesses and government and seek out opportunities to apply your knowledge to current problems and implement solutions that create and capture value for Indonesia.

Part C. Application studies

The focus of these studies is professional or scholarly work that can contribute to a portfolio of professional development. We will explore innovative approaches to human capital development and seek out ways to increase the productivity of Indonesia's informal sector labour force.

Part D. Elective studies

This will enable you to deepen your knowledge in innovation, or in business and economics more broadly. Alternatively, you may select units from across the University, subject to meeting the relevant enrolment requirements.

Master's entry points

Depending on prior qualifications you may receive entry level credit (a form of block credit) which determines your point of entry to the course:

  * If you are admitted at entry level 1 you complete 72 credit points, comprising Part A, Part B, Part C and Part D.
  * If you are admitted at entry level 2 you complete 48 credit points, comprising Part B, Part C and Part D. 
  * If you are admitted at entry level 3 you complete 24 credit points, comprising Part C and 6 credit points from Part D.

Note: If you are eligible for credit for prior studies you may elect not to receive the credit and complete one of the higher credit-point options.

### Requirements
The course comprises 72 credit points structured into four parts: Part A. Advanced preparatory studies (24 credit  points), Part B. Discipline studies (18 credit points), Part C. Application studies (18 credit points) and Part D. Elective studies (12 credit points).

Units are 6 credit points unless otherwise stated.

Part A. Advanced preparatory studies (24 credit points)

You must complete the following four units (24 credit points):

  * ACI5903 Resource allocation and business model performance

  * ECI5953 Regulation, prices and markets

  * MGI5976 Strategic management for innovation

  * MKI5955 The theory and practice of innovative marketing

Part B. Discipline studies (18 credit points)

You must complete the following three units (18 credit points):

  * BFI5954 Financial approaches to calculating value and risk

  * MGI5928 Strategic leadership

  * MGI5771 Process and operations management for innovation

Part C. Application studies (18 credit points)

You must complete the following three units (18 credit points):

  * ADI5111 Design thinking for business

  * BEI5411 Creativity for change and value creation

  * MKI5123 Entrepreneurial Innovation

Part D. Elective studies (12 credit points)

You must complete 12 credit points of electives at either Level 4 or 5 from the Faculty of Business and Economics or across the University as long as you have met the prerequisites and there are no restrictions on enrolling in the units.

You may choose to complete one or both of the following units to count toward your 12 credit points.

  * BTI5111 Technology: Risk and regulation in the digital age

  * ECI5590 Poverty, inequality and opportunity

Note: To complete units offered at an alternative campus please consult with your Course Director.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
demonstrate advanced knowledge of innovation to a master's level for a given business of domain:

  * apply management and specialist knowledge and skills to business problems
**2.**
demonstrate the ability to drive business model innovation namely by driving willingness-to-pay and reducing opportunity costs::

  * identify, explore and harness the drivers of business innovation.
**3.**
demonstrate mastery of innovative practices and their implementation in the Indonesian context:

  * formulate and deliver concise well supported solutions

  * establish conditions for success and execute against them
**4.**
be responsible and effective global citizens who:

  * engage in an internationalised world

  * exhibit cross-cultural competence

  * demonstrate ethical values
**5.**
be critical and creative scholars who:

  * produce innovative solutions to problems

  * apply research skills to business challenges

  * communicate effectively and perceptively

### AHEGS
The Master of Business Innovation is a postgraduate award. The course is taught in English. The course takes one and half years of full time study or part time equivalent to complete. Graduates have advanced knowledge of innovative practices and their implementation and have the ability to drive business model innovation. The Master of Business Innovation is a Level 9 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.

Subject: Technology: Risk and regulation in the digital age (BTI5111)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: As digital technologies evolve, executives are increasingly pressured to consider how adopting new technologies can improve business efficiency and reduce business and transaction costs. Some of these efficiency gains require digitising functions common to all businesses, such as financial and human resource management, stakeholder and customer engagement, risk management and regulatory compliance. Other efficiency gains arise from using technology to improve how a business produces the goods and services they bring to market. In both the operations and production context, the use of technology involves considering a complex range of ethical and legal issues. Executives who understand and address these issues must exhibit knowledge of the technology and the regulatory regimes and compliance mechanisms that govern its use. You will gain an understanding of the conceptual principles underpinning different types of technologies, their associated benefits and limitations and their application in various business contexts. The unit also examines the existing and proposed regulatory regimes and instruments (within and outside of Indonesia) governing the deployment of these technologies and the use, collection and storage of personal data. A range of case studies from different industries will illustrate how new technologies can be applied to address a range of business needs and provide context for considering the practical, legal, ethical and regulatory issues that must be managed before, during and after technology deployment.
Learning Outcomes:
- apply knowledge to evaluate how certain technologies might best address particular business needs, distinguishing between competing options by assessing the legal, ethical and regulatory issues that each alternative presents.
- communicate understanding of a variety of different technologies used in the business context, extrapolating key features and identifying how these technologies inter-relate
- describe the various international regulatory frameworks governing the use of technology in business operations and specify their purpose
- critically discuss the value of new technologies within different business contexts, and identify the relevant practical, legal, ethical and regulatory issues that must be considered before, during and after technology deployment
Assessment Tasks:
- 1 - Written: 40% (Hurdle: None)
- 3 - Artefact: 10% (Hurdle: None)
- 2 - Written: 50% (Hurdle: None)
