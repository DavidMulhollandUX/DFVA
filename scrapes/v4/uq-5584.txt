The Master of Commerce is designed for people aspiring to management roles, for current managers seeking formal qualifications in commerce and for those changing career direction. This program will help you to further develop your commerce skills, build specialist knowledge within your chosen area and accelerate your career. It's possible to complete the Master of Commerce in 2 years or 1.5 years depending on your previous studies. The 1.5 year duration is designed for graduates with a Bachelor's degree in a relevant discipline. The 2 year duration includes the fundamentals of general commerce for graduates with a Bachelor's degree from any discipline. Both duration options provide an opportunity to specialise in two fields of study or take additional research electives (which may provide a pathway to research higher degree programs). Each specialised field of study will equip you with the relevant knowledge to jumpstart or advance your chosen profession. You can choose to specialise in the areas of applied finance, information systems or professional accounting.

Subject: Business Industry Placement (RBUS7999)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This course aims to provide final year students with the opportunity to apply conceptual knowledge to the real world via a professional placement. As such, this course strongly links discipline learnings, personal employability skills and attributes developed by students across the duration of their studies with the challenges and expectations of working in industry. In doing so, students have the opportunity to gain valuable work experience in their chosen profession and to connect this experience to the development of a workplace ready skill-set. Successful completion of the course will provide students with the employability skills required for transition to the world of work. Further information about how to apply for RBUS7999 can be found on the BEL Faculty's work-integrated learning website .
Learning Outcomes:
- Reflect on and evaluate your employability skillset and attributes.
- Communicate your employability skillset and attributes.
- Create a professional development plan to further build upon your employability skills.
- Critically reflect on and articulate key business practices, processes and challenges within an organisation.
- Demonstrate the ability to think strategically from the perspective of a manager.
- Explain your industry employment requirements and trends.
- Clarify your career goals and pathways.
Assessment Tasks:
- A1: Internship Hurdle: Pass/Fail% (Hurdle: True)
- A2. Workplace reflection Identity Verified Online: 50%% (Hurdle: False)
- A3. Professional Development Plan: 50%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Seminar 3 Hours/ Week
