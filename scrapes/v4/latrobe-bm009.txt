## Master of Marketing - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BM009
##### 240 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Gain future-focused knowledge, skills, and industry experience and become a professional marketer with our innovative, applied and world-class degree that delivers real impact. Master responsible marketing practice, strategies and tactics aligned with industry demands and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. Build future-focused marketing skills supported by critical thinking and a problem-solving mindset. Gain real-world, applied experience by solving industry-based problems and engaging with industry experts.
 
By completing La Trobe's Master of Marketing, you will:  * critically analyse the business environment and solve marketing problems ethically and responsibly
  * explore topics such as sales, negotiation, customer experience and service design, digital marketing, content marketing, marketing research and analytics, entrepreneurship and innovation, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence, and marketing strategy
  * benefit from our practical and applied approach to learning, with the use of active workshops and tasks sourced from the real world, a broad student cohort, industry guest speakers and more
  * tap into industry expertise and potential job opportunities through our partnerships with major corporations and industry leaders in marketing.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Apply advanced marketing research and/or analytics techniques to generate robust, evidence-based insights that enhance strategic solution development.
**2.**
Critically analyse and synthesise advanced marketing theories to design innovative, actionable, and strategic solutions to complex marketing problems.
**3.**
Communicate complex marketing analyses, insights, and strategic recommendations effectively and professionally to diverse audiences, individually and collaboratively.
**4.**
Exercise expert judgment by integrating social, ethical, and cultural  considerations in professional decision-making across diverse marketing environments.

### Course features
### Work based learning (placement) requirements
During this course, you will have the opportunity to participate in a Work Based Learning (WBL) placement experience, designed to allow you to extend your formal academic learning beyond the classroom. The WBL experience will provide the opportunity to bring your learning from the university into a work environment and test out your knowledge in a professional real-world environment. Some courses include compulsory WBL subjects, however, we also offer a range of elective WBL subjects, both cross-discipline 
and subject-specific. La Trobe University will source placements for some subjects, while others will require you to source your own placement. If you are required to source your own placement, dedicated staff will guide and support you through the process. Elective WBL subjects generally involve an application process and Subject Coordinator approval. The location and number of hours undertaken can vary considerably depending on the activity and the discipline area. Please refer to the individual subject entries for more information.
### Work integrated learning opportunities
Industry presentations, Industry informed curriculum, Industry-informed projects, Interprofessional learning, Career development learning, Professional simulations
### Career outcomes
Graduates are in high demand, especially in Victoria where there are projected shortages for advertising and marketing specialists. Possible roles include:

  * content marketing manager
  * marketing manager
  * customer experience (CX) manager
  * digital marketing manager
  * service designer
  * sales manager.

Subject: CYBERSECURITY FOR BUSINESS (BUS4006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Cybersecurity for Business provides a comprehensive overview of cybersecurity principles, practices, and frameworks for managing cybersecurity risks in organisations. Cybersecurity plays a critical role in an organisation's operations by safeguarding its assets, ensuring regulatory compliance, and enhancing overall business resilience. The subject covers key topics, including cybersecurity threats, attacks, governance frameworks, cybersecurity risk management, cybersecurity policies and procedures, and compliance. You will explore cybersecurity for businesses through a combination of tailor-made case studies and hands-on workshops, offering a comprehensive understanding of theoretical principles alongside practical application in real-world scenarios. It develops the skills necessary to craft effective cybersecurity strategies that align with organisational goals and regulatory requirements. The course will also examine the ethical, legal, and regulatory dimensions of cybersecurity.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse different types of cyber threats, attack methods, and AI-driven threats, to assess their potential impact on business operations, risk management, and compliance obligations.
- Design and justify ethical and governance-aligned cybersecurity strategies using appropriate frameworks, AI-enabled tools, and security models to strengthen threat detection, incident response, resilience, and regulatory compliance.
- Identify and apply fundamental cybersecurity principles to recognise and evaluate common organisational security challenges, including those arising from emerging technologies and AI.
- Communicate cybersecurity governance strategies and AI-informed risk mitigation plans clearly and appropriately to diverse technical and non-technical stakeholders in professional business contexts.

Subject: HEURISTICS AND BIASES IN MARKETING DECISIONS (MKT5005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Heuristics and Biases in Marketing Decisions offers overall knowledge of heuristics in marketing. Heuristics are mental shortcuts that people use to make decisions quickly and efficiently, but nonetheless, they normally lead to biases and, hence, irrational decisions. Relatedly, managing heuristics and biases can shape the quality of marketing decisions. You will learn to identify and analyse heuristics utilised in marketing decisions along with the biases they promote. You will learn about mechanisms that help suppress biases in marketing decisions. Further, you will learn to utilise your knowledge of heuristics and biases in marketing to critique business performance.
Learning Outcomes:
- Develop and evaluate ethically responsible, theoretically grounded strategies to suppress biases in marketing decision-making.
- Critically analyse and synthesise how heuristics and cognitive biases shape marketing decisions, drawing on evidence-based analysis, individually and/or collaboratively.
- Integrate advanced knowledge of consumer heuristics and cognitive biases to critically evaluate marketing decisions and outcomes in real-world high-profile cases.
