Work Integrated Learning (WIL) is embedded across our curriculum, with a strong focus in our major capstone courses. Specifically, RBUS3888 and RBUS3999 offer students the chance to apply theoretical and conceptual knowledge in real-world contexts. These courses enable you to either collaborate with industry partners on live projects, addressing genuine business challenges, or undertake a professional placement to gain practical workplace experience. This integrated approach helps you build employability skills, deepen your understanding of professional workplace dynamics, expand your network, and engage with current business issues.

Subject: Business Industry Placement (RBUS3999)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This course aims to provide final year students with the opportunity to apply conceptual knowledge to the real world via a professional placement. As such, this course strongly links discipline learnings, personal employability skills and attributes developed by students across the duration of their studies with the challenges and expectations of working in industry. In doing so, students have the opportunity to gain valuable work experience in their chosen profession and to connect this experience to the development of a workplace ready skill-set. Successful completion of the course will provide students with the employability skills required for transition to the world of work. Further information about how to apply for RBUS3999 can be found on the BEL Faculty's work-integrated learning website .
Learning Outcomes:
- Reflect on and evaluate your employability skillset and attributes.
- Communicate your employability skillset and attributes.
- Create a professional development plan to further build upon your employability skills.
- Critically reflect on and articulate key business practices, processes and challenges within an organisation.
Assessment Tasks:
- A1: Internship Hurdle: Pass/Fail% (Hurdle: True)
- A2. Workplace reflection Identity Verified Online: 50%% (Hurdle: False)
- A3. Professional Development Plan: 50%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Seminar 3 Hours/ Week
