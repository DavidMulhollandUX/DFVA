You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Logistics and Supply Chain Management is a thriving field with exponential growth. Our Graduate Certificate in Logistics and Supply Chain Management course equips you with the knowledge, skills, and strategic acumen needed to tackle real-world industry challenges and leverage emerging technologies for transformative logistics and supply chain practices. This course is designed to rapidly upskill individuals in just one (1) semester, offering a fundamental understanding of end-to-end supply chains. The course focuses on three core pillars:

  * Applied Analytics and Digitalisation: You will explore the application of emerging technologies like AI, digital twins, and cloud computing to unlock valuable logistics and supply chain insights. We prioritise enhancing your practical skills in logistics and supply chain analytics, encompassing visual, predictive, and big data analytics.
  * Circular Economy and Sustainability: Learn how to integrate Circular Economy principles into logistics and supply chain management strategies, aligning with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 9: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure, and SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.
  * Employability Skills: Develop your professional and business skills that enhance your employability.

Is this course for me?

This course is tailor-made for individuals seeking comprehensive knowledge and practical skills in logistics and supply chain management. To maximise your employability, this course incorporates various practical elements. Our course advisory committee, composed of industry professionals, along with our extensive industry connections, provide additional opportunities such as graduation programs, coaching, and mentorship throughout your studies. 

The program offers flexibility and serves as a pathway to building expertise in the logistics and supply chain field. Upon successful completion of the course, you can choose to exit with a Graduate Certificate or continue your education by pursuing our Graduate Diploma or Master of Logistics and Supply Chain Management programs. You will be eligible for advanced standing for four subjects in these programs, providing a seamless upskilling journey.

### Career outcomes
The demand for skilled professionals in logistics and supply chain management is rapidly growing across various industries. Here are some potential career outcomes that await you:

Procurement, Supply Chain, and Operations Manager: As a Procurement, Supply Chain, and Operations Manager, you will have a pivotal role in overseeing end-to-end supply chain operations, including procurement, planning, inventory management, and logistics. Your strategic mindset and exceptional leadership skills will be instrumental in driving efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and customer satisfaction.

Logistics and Supply Chain Consultant: As a Logistics and Supply Chain Consultant, you will play a crucial role in analysing supply chain data, identifying bottlenecks, and designing optimised logistics strategies. Your insights and expertise will be invaluable in helping organizations streamline their operations, reduce costs, and improve overall performance.

E-commerce and Omnichannel Specialist: With the rise of e-commerce and the growing significance of omnichannel retail, your expertise in logistics and supply chain management will be highly sought after. As an E-commerce and Omnichannel Specialist, you will be responsible for designing and implementing efficient logistics networks to support online sales and deliver seamless customer experiences.

Sustainability and Circular Supply Chain Manager: In response to the increasing focus on circular economy, companies are actively seeking professionals who can integrate sustainability principles into their supply chain operations. As a Sustainability and Circular Supply Chain Manager, you will have the opportunity to develop and implement strategies that minimize carbon footprint, reduce waste, and promote ethical sourcing, making a positive impact on both the company and the environment.

Supply Chain Innovation and Technology Manager: With the rapid advancement of technologies like artificial intelligence, blockchain, and automation, organizations are eager to leverage these innovations to optimize their supply chain processes. As a Supply Chain Innovation and Technology Manager, you will be at the forefront of driving digital transformation within the supply chain, using emerging technologies to enhance operational efficiency and create a competitive advantage for the company.

Subject: PRINCIPLES OF BUSINESS ANALYTICS (BUS4011)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject introduces you to business and data analytics with a strong focus on practical outcomes that are directly applicable to business contexts. It provides a comprehensive understanding of current theories, frameworks, applications, and technologies that support data-driven decision-making in organisations. You will gain hands-on experience with industry-standard analytics platforms to conduct descriptive analytics and build interactive dashboards aligned with business needs. Industry-based guest lectures provide insights into how organisations are applying analytics to enhance business performance and address emerging challenges. Upon completion, you will be able to explain the role of analytics in business environments, apply analytical tools, evaluate ethical considerations, and align analytics strategies with organisational objectives.

- Apply advanced analytics techniques and tools aligned with industry best practice to generate data-driven insights, design effective dashboards, and support evidence-based decision-making.
- Evaluate cybersecurity, data protection, and ethical challenges in organisational analytics and AI, and apply frameworks to ensure responsible and compliant data practices.
- Critically examine the purpose and strategic value of analytics and AI in organisations to enhance decision-making, operational efficiency, and innovation.

Subject: FOUNDATION OF LOGISTICS AND SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT (MGT4001)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will be introduced to the fundamentals of logistics and supply chain management, along with their implications for operational and strategic business decisions. You will explore essential theories and concepts related to demand and supply planning, distribution network design, sustainable procurement, service supply chain and operations, international transport and logistics, as well as supply chain sustainability and the circular economy. Throughout the subject, you will have the opportunity to learn these concepts in a range of business contexts, preparing you to advance your knowledge in each of those specialised areas. Through both independent and collaborative work on a variety of authentic activities, you will develop foundational knowledge in logistics and supply chain management by solving real-world problems. Additionally, you will cultivate critical and creative thinking, quantitative, and interpersonal skills essential for success in the logistics and supply chain profession.
Learning Outcomes:
- Propose informed solutions to real-world logistics and supply chain challenges, through both individual and collaborative efforts.
- Critically analyse foundational concepts in logistics and supply chain management across various business settings.
- Examine and evaluate the principles of logistics and supply chain management.
