Subject: COSC7500 - High Performance Computing
### COSC7500 - High Performance Computing
Curriculum unit COSC7500: High Performance Computing covering fundamental principles, professional methodologies, and critical evaluation at The University of Queensland.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate comprehensive understanding of key principles in high performance computing.
- Apply analytical frameworks and professional methods in high performance computing.
- Communicate findings and technical arguments effectively in professional contexts.
Assessment Tasks:
- Coursework Project: 50%
- Examination: 50%
Workload: 144 hours

Subject: MATH4090 - Computation in Financial Mathematics
### MATH4090 - Computation in Financial Mathematics
Learning Outcomes:
- Use binomial trees (lattice methods) to price financial derivatives with focus on European and American-style, and barrier (path-dependent) options.
- Develop efficient Monte Carlo simulation and effective variance reduction techniques for pricing options, with focus on European, American style (early exercise), and Asian (path-dependent) options.
- Derive partial differential equations arising in option pricing, and solve them efficiently numerically, with focus on European, American-style (early exercise) and Asian (path-dependent) options.
- Understand the concepts of convergence and stability of numerical partial differential equation methods, and be able to carry out relevant analysis.
- Understand and be able to develop numerical methods for pricing derivatives under more realistic models, such as local or stochastic volatility, or stochastic interest rates.
Assessment Tasks:
- Assignment 1: 15%%
- Assignment 2: 15%%
- Assignment 3: 15%%
- End of Semester Examination Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 55%%
Workload: Lecture 2 Hours/ Week Applied Class 1 Hour/ Week 2L1A

Subject: MATH4105 - General Relativity
### MATH4105 - General Relativity
Learning Outcomes:
- Manipulate multicomponent tensors, with a fluent grasp of the summation convention.
- Understand covariant differentiation of tensors, and the related concepts of connections and parallel transport on pseudo-Riemannian manifolds.
- Understand the structure of equations governing geodesics (and null geodesics).
- Appreciate the notion of curvature of a pseudo-Riemannian manifold and how to characterise it using the curvature tensor.
- Know the structure of special tensors of importance to General Relativity (Ricci, Weyl, Einstein tensors). Understand the derivation of the Bianchi identities.
- Express the physics of special relativity and electromagnetism in terms of the mathematical language of vectors and tensors described in the first half of the course.
- Recount the physical principles that led Einstein to the equations of general relativity and their implications in a range of situations including planetary motion, the gravitational deflection of light beams and cosmology.
- Apply the equations of general relativity to solve simple problems in astronomy and cosmology, including gravitational redshifts and time dilation.
- Explain in detail how general relativity reduces to Newton's theory of gravitation in many circumstances and describe some experimental predictions of the new theory that require us to adopt it as a more accurate description.
- Explain the predictions of simple cosmological models based on general relativity and discuss these in the context of recent astronomical observations of the expansion of the universe.
Assessment Tasks:
- 4 Assignments: 40% Assignments are equally weighted%
- End of Semester examination Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 60%%
Workload: Lecture 3 Hours/ Week Applied Class 1 Hour/ Week 3L1A

Subject: MATH7232 - Operations Research & Mathematical Planning
### MATH7232 - Operations Research & Mathematical Planning
Learning Outcomes:
- Formulate practical contexts as mathematical optimisation problems.
- Understand a range of optimisation methods and be able to select a suitable method to solve a particular problem.
- Confidently use software to implement an optimisation model and obtain solutions.
- Communicate a solution to an appropriate audience in terms of the original problem description.
Assessment Tasks:
- Assignments: 55%%
- End of Semester Examination Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 45%%
Workload: Lecture 2 Hours/ Week Applied Class 2 Hours/ 2L 2A
