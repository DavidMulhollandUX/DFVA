# Graduate Diploma in Law, Policy and Government (21340)

## Program Overview
- **Institution**: The University of Western Australia (UWA)
- **Course Code**: 21340
- **Academic Year**: 2026
- **Study Level**: Postgraduate
- **Handbook URL**: https://www.handbooks.uwa.edu.au/coursedetails?code=21340

## Course Overview
This course aims to provide students with an understanding of socio-legal theory, policy development and the skills required to work in or with governments. The course combines theory with more practically oriented units. Study includes foundational courses in law, policy and socio-legal theory. Students can choose to articulate to a master's degree and tailor their study through elective units to focus on policy areas of interest such as crime and justice, medicine and law, international law or governance.

All units have a value of six points unless otherwise stated.

Students with a recognised Law degree, take
POLS5501
Public Policy (6 points) and
LAWS5260
Intersections of Law, Policy and Government (6 points) from Group A, and option units to the value of 12 points from Group B.
Students without a recognised Law degree, take all units (18 points) from Group A, and option units to the value of 6 points from Group B.

Subject: Intersections of Law, Policy and Governance (LAWS5260)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Description This unit examines the intersections between law, policy and governance from both theoretical and practical perspectives. Students explore: (1) the meaning of governance, ‘good governance' and environmental, and the concept of environmental social and governance (ESG); (2) how law and legal tools are used as a policy lever; (3) the practical application of law, policy and governance; and (4) law and policy gap analyses, providing an opportunity to assess effectiveness in contemporary case study areas. Credit 6 points Offering (see Timetable ) Availability Location Mode Non-standard teaching period UWA (Perth) On-campus Outcomes Students are able to (1) distinguish between law, policy and governance, and how they intersect; (2) analyse contemporary issues and debates at the intersection of law, policy and governance; (3) critically assess the effectiveness of law and legal tools in the context of government public policy and governance goals; (4) design, develop and deliver research projects at the intersection of law, policy and governance; and; and (5) create persuasive arguments through written and oral work. Assessment Indicative assessments in this unit are as follows: (1) research essay; (2) assignment; and (3) participation. Further information is available in the unit outline. Student may be offered supplementary assessment in this unit if they meet the eligibility criteria. Subject: The Politics of Public Policy (POLS5501)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Description The capacity to effectively develop, manage and evaluate policy is an essential skill in any organisation—public, private, and not-for-profit—but how do governments and corporations make good policy? This unit seeks to develop an understanding of the key concepts, tools, and theories in the processes of public policy development and evaluation. It applies theory to practice, as a grasp of both is essential to learning public policy development and administration. Credit 6 points Offering (see Timetable ) Availability Location Mode Non-standard teaching period UWA (Perth) On-campus Outcomes Students are able to (1) demonstrate an advanced knowledge of key concepts and theories in public policy; (2) reflect critically on the theory and professional practice of public policy; and (3) apply knowledge and skills to execute a professionally relevant task. Assessment Indicative assessments in this unit are as follows: (1) participation; (2) essay; and (3) exam. Further information is available in the unit outline. Student may be offered supplementary assessment in this unit if they meet the eligibility criteria.
