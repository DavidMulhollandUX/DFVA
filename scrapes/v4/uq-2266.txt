### Program Structure (Courses & Requirements)

  * [ NUMW6001 Advanced Discipline Readings ](https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=NUMW6001)
  * [ NUMW6002 Research Methods & Thesis Preparation ](https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=NUMW6002)
  * [ NUMW6003 Thesis (full-time study) ](https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=NUMW6003)
  * [ NUMW6004 Thesis (part-time study) ](https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=NUMW6004)

Subject: Advanced Discipline Readings (NUMW6001)
Credit Points: 4
Overview: This course provides students with the necessary skills to perform a review of the scientific literature relevant to the research question chosen for their research thesis. Students conduct a literature review, critique the relevant literature and synthesise the information.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify a problem suitable for research and pose a research question.
- Demonstrate knowledge of how to systematically research the scientific literature in your topic area.
- Critique and synthesize the literature throughout the review.
- Provide a written literature review of a quality that is commensurate with a publication in a relevant academic journal.
Assessment Tasks:
- Assessment 1: Development of research question: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
- Assessment 2: Literature review: 60%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Tutorial 1 Hour/ Ad Hoc Occasional contact with supervisor as arranged.

===== SOURCE: https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=NUMW6002 =====
Subject: Research Methods & Thesis Preparation (NUMW6002)
Credit Points: 4
Overview: This course provides students with an overview of the variety of research methods utilised in health research and the skills to determine which research method best answers a research question. Its focus is the development of a research proposal for the purpose of conducting research as part of a thesis.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate knowledge of a variety of qualitative and quantitative research methods utilised in health research.
- Determine which research method best answers a specific research question.
- Write a high quality research proposal for the purpose of conducting research as part of your thesis.
- Present an overview of your research proposal in a professionally prepared oral presentation.
Assessment Tasks:
- Assessment 1: Written Research Proposal: 60%% (Hurdle: False)
- Assessment 2: Presentation of Research Proposal In-person: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Tutorial 1 Hour/ Ad Hoc Occasional contact with supervisor.

===== SOURCE: https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=NUMW6003 =====
Subject: Thesis (full-time study) (NUMW6003)
Credit Points: 8
Overview: This course provides students with opportunity to implement a research proposal by conducting a research investigation and presenting the completed research findings in an academic thesis. Students should also be able to prepare a research paper of a quality commensurate with publication in an academic journal. A thesis can be completed in traditional format or by publication.
Learning Outcomes:
- Conduct a research investigation guided by a well founded research proposal.
- Demonstrate knowledge of the conventions of thesis preparation.
- Analyse, interpret and synthesise research findings and present the research in an academic thesis.
Assessment Tasks:
- Honours Thesis: 100%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Contact 1 Hour/ Ad Hoc Occasional contact with the supervisor.

===== SOURCE: https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=NUMW6004 =====
Subject: Thesis (part-time study) (NUMW6004)
Credit Points: 8
Overview: Implementation of a research proposal by conducting a research investigation & presenting the completed research findings in an academic thesis. Students should also be able to prepare & submit a paper for publication in a relevant academic journal based on their research. Students undertaking the thesis on a full-time basis in one semester should enrol in NUMW6003. A thesis can be completed in traditional format or by publication.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate knowledge of the conventions of thesis preparation.
- Conduct a research investigation guided by a well founded research proposal.
- Analyse, interpret and synthesise research findings and present the research in an academic thesis.
- Prepare an academic paper of a quality commensurate with publication in an academic journal.
Assessment Tasks:
- Honours Thesis: 100%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Contact 2 Hours/ Week
