## Master of Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### HMMSPC
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
La Trobe’s Master of Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy is a program designed for practising physiotherapists who want to gain advanced clinical skills in the field of musculoskeletal physiotherapy.

Through a blend of online and set residential block learning, you’ll learn from leading industry clinical leaders, academic staff and colleagues at one of Australia’s longest established physiotherapy schools.

You will also work under the supervision of recognised sports and exercise physiotherapists to gain invaluable clinical practice experience in hospital outpatient departments and private physiotherapy clinics.

This course is delivered part time over two to five years.

You'll learn:

  * Fundamentals of musculoskeletal physiotherapy
Learn the knowledge and skills required to apply evidence-based practice in musculoskeletal physiotherapy.
  * Clinical sciences
Develop your understanding of clinical sciences such as pain sciences and human tissue sciences. This is integral to the development of scientifically designed rehabilitation programs.
  * Advanced physiotherapy and clinical skills
Optimise the effectiveness of client centred management through your mastery of physiotherapy, clinical, neuro-musculoskeletal examination and management skills.
  * Clinical reasoning through a case-based paradigm
Hone your clinical reasoning skills through the use of case scenarios. Learn applied clinical anatomy, tissue pathology, patient presentations, and assessment and treatment information.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Critically interpret research and clinical information in formulating expert judgements to guide clinical reasoning, promote patient health and ensure safe return to activity and participation.
**2.**
Deliver client-centred management, integrating advanced biomedical and clinical knowledge with specialised clinical reasoning in musculoskeletal settings.
**3.**
Employ technical research skills to critically evaluate evidence and methodologies, to inform research design and implementation that contributes to evidence-based musculoskeletal physiotherapy practice.
**4.**
Apply advanced clinical reasoning and mastery of musculoskeletal clinical skills while demonstrating ethical, accountable, and culturally responsive care.
**5.**
Apply advanced communication skills to justify clinical reasoning to promote heath and improve outcomes across client populations, communities, and health organisations.
**6.**
Demonstrate autonomy, adaptability, and specialised musculoskeletal skills to demonstrate key professional roles as a practitioner, administrator, scholar, and educator.
**7.**
Investigate, analyse and synthesise an array of information to manage complex clinical presentations in musculoskeletal physiotherapy practice.

### Work based learning (placement) requirements
Compulsory placements (Work Based Learning)
During this course, you will participate in one or more supervised Work Based Learning (WBL) placements designed to allow you to practice newly acquired skills, behaviours and knowledge while integrating theory with practice. The placements will immerse you in professional work culture, enabling you to develop and further enhance your skills. WBL provides you with the opportunity to gain valuable industry experience at one or more of our local, interstate or international industry partners or agencies. WBL placements will generally be arranged by the Office of the Provost Placements and WBL Team at La Trobe University. The number of hours undertaken can vary considerably, depending on the requirements of the course. Students may incur additional costs and it may be necessary to travel or relocate to complete a specific placement. Please refer to the individual subject entries for more information.
### Work integrated learning opportunities
Industry presentations, Industry informed curriculum, Industry-informed projects
### Career outcomes
After graduation, you could work across a range of professional settings including private practice and advanced scope clinics.

Possible roles include:

  * Musculoskeletal physiotherapist
  * Assess, diagnose, prevent, manage and treat a range of musculoskeletal injuries relating to the joints, muscles, bones and nerves.
  * Assess, diagnose, prevent, manage and treat individuals with a range of conditions through the use of exercise-based rehabilitation.

Subject: RESEARCH TO INFORM ADVANCED PHYSIOTHERAPY (PTY5024)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this fully online subject, students will develop their knowledge and skills required for evidence-informed practice in musculoskeletal or sports and exercise physiotherapy. The subject includes the completion of a Critically Appraised Topic (CAT) based on a sports or musculoskeletal physiotherapy problem that students have encountered in clinical practice. In completing the CAT, students will develop skills in formulating an answerable clinical question, searching for and critically appraising the available evidence to inform clinical reasoning and practice. Students will also refine their theoretical knowledge on the research process and human ethics to incorporate research into the clinical setting to inform advanced physiotherapy practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically appraise and interpret the evidence base of musculoskeletal/sports and exercise physiotherapy across a variety of qualitative and quantitative research methodologies.
- Generate an answerable clinical research question based on reflection and the critical evaluation of your clinical practice
- Identify and systematically address ethical considerations associated with research in the musculoskeletal/sports and exercise physiotherapy setting.
- Analyse, interpret and synthesise critically appraised research findings from an answerable clinical question to inform the assessment and management of patients in clinical practice

Subject: ADVANCED PHYSIOTHERAPY FOR THE UPPER BODY (PTY5025)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this blended subject, students will gain advanced knowledge and clinical skills in musculoskeletal and sports and exercise physiotherapy, with a focus on the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine. The subject begins with an online clinical reasoning module designed to enhance decision-making and problem-solving of complex cases. This is followed by an online module to enhance diagnostic imaging interpretation for upper limb and cervical presentations. Practical components include applied clinical anatomy wet labs and advanced physiotherapy assessment and management of upper limb, cervical and thoracic conditions. This subject prepares qualified physiotherapists to integrate advanced assessment and management techniques into clinical practice. This subject includes professional learning, practical experiences or portfolio activities which contribute to the completion of the Silver Career Ready AdvantageTM award .
Learning Outcomes:
- Integrate contemporary, evidence-informed practices into clinical scenarios involving the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine, demonstrating the ability to adapt and apply advanced physiotherapy techniques in a variety of clinical settings.
- Apply advanced clinical reasoning skills to develop and implement comprehensive assessment and management strategies for complex upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine conditions in musculoskeletal and sports physiotherapy
- Apply advanced theoretical knowledge and practical skills to critically analyse and address complex neuromusculoskeletal presentations of the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine, with a focus on optimising patient outcomes
- Proficiently interpret diagnostic imaging and integrate applied clinical anatomy to inform evidence-based decision-making in patient care for conditions of the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine
- Critically apply self-reflection to identify strengths and areas for improvement in the assessment and management of upper limb, cervical, and thoracic conditions, fostering ongoing professional growth as an effective lifelong learner.

Subject: PATHOPHYSIOLOGY AND PAIN SCIENCE FOR ADVANCED PHYSIOTHERAPY (PTY5026)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this fully online subject, students will develop their knowledge in applying comprehensive concepts of pathophysiology, pain science, and pharmacology to differentiate between normal and abnormal neuromusculoskeletal function within the context of advanced physiotherapy. Students will critically evaluate pathology and pathogenesis related to neuromusculoskeletal dysfunction and interpret the neuromusculoskeletal system’s responses to load, including impacts on function, injury, and tissue healing. This subject provides qualified physiotherapists with comprehensive knowledge of clinical sciences and their influence on assessment and treatments in advanced physiotherapy practice. This subject includes professional learning, practical experiences or portfolio activities which contribute to the completion of the Silver Career Ready AdvantageTM award.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically evaluate the pathology, and pathogenesis underlying neuromusculoskeletal dysfunctions in advanced physiotherapy practice
- Integrate a theoretical understanding of the effects of pharmacology on pain and the neuromusculoskeletal system to inform effective advanced physiotherapy assessment and management.
- Interpret how the neuromusculoskeletal system’s responds to load, including its effects on function, injury response, and tissue healing, and evaluate how these factors influence assessment and treatments in advanced physiotherapy practice
- Integrate and apply comprehensive knowledge of anatomy, physiology, and pain science to distinguish between normal and abnormal function in the neuromusculoskeletal system

Subject: ADVANCED MUSCULOSKELETAL PHYSIOTHERAPY  (PTY5029)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this blended subject, students will develop advanced skills in musculoskeletal physiotherapy, with a focus on managing complex neuromusculoskeletal dysfunctions. Through a biopsychosocial framework, students will enhance their clinical reasoning, developing proficiency in techniques to differentiate between neuromusculoskeletal and non-mechanical presentations. The subject emphasizes advanced manual therapy, exercise prescription, and integrating theoretical knowledge with examination outcomes to inform effective treatment strategies. Students will apply evidence-based practices to assess and manage complex cases, preparing them for high-level clinical practice in musculoskeletal physiotherapy. This subject will be offered from 2027
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply advanced clinical reasoning by communicating effectively to peers about treatment and outcome evaluation approaches for patients with complex neuromusculoskeletal presentations to peers
- Critically apply advanced musculoskeletal physiotherapy skills and clinical reasoning within a biopsychosocial framework to manage musculoskeletal conditions
- Apply advanced clinical reasoning skills in musculoskeletal physiotherapy by integrating and applying theoretical knowledge, examination outcomes, and contributing factors to assess and manage complex neuromusculoskeletal dysfunction.
- Critically apply comprehensive clinical examination procedures to enable differential diagnoses of neuromusculoskeletal dysfunction.

Subject: ADVANCED MUSCULOSKELETAL PHYSIOTHERAPY CLINICAL PLACEMENT (PTY5031)
Credit Points: 45
Overview: In this subject you will develop advanced physiotherapy neuromusculoskeletal examination and management skills within a clinical reasoning paradigm. You will undertake 160 hours of supervised clinical practice within the Australian Healthcare System at hospitals/health services and/or private physiotherapy clinics. Supervised clinical practice encourages and facilitates rational decision making with respect to treatment selection, progression and planning of ongoing management programs. You will also have the opportunity to integrate and refine your advanced physiotherapy manual techniques and exercise prescription skills into your clinical practice. This subject includes professional learning, practical experiences or portfolio activities which contribute to the completion of the Career Ready AdvantageTM award. This subject will be offered from 2027
Learning Outcomes:
- Deliver ethical, accountable, and culturally responsive client-centered care, while effectively communicating with clients, carers, and health professionals to optimize treatment outcomes
- Perform safe, systematic and prioritized advanced musculoskeletal initial assessments and treatments on complex neuromusculoskeletal clients integrating appropriate outcome measures to evaluate, refine, and monitor patient outcomes.
- Synthesise initial and follow-up assessment findings of complex neuromusculoskeletal clients to refine or develop treatment goals and guide the progression of interventions within a multidisciplinary healthcare team
- Critically apply evidence informed practice and expert judgment to guide clinical reasoning in assessing and managing complex neuromusculoskeletal clients, facilitating safe return to activity and participation.
