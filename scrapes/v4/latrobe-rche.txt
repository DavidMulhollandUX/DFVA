### Overview
If you’re keen to develop your teaching capabilities and understand how to better support learning, La Trobe’s Graduate Certificate in Higher Education Curriculum, Teaching and Learning will give you a deep understanding of the key theories in teaching and curriculum design within a practice-based course.

This flexible, online course is exclusively available to staff at La Trobe who are in a teaching and learning role. The course is designed for a wide range of academic and professional staff, from academic lecturers to library learning advisors. You’ll complete 4 subjects which can be taken full-time over one year or part-time over 2 years.

This practical course will give you essential tools for teaching in higher education settings and will develop your knowledge about curriculum design. Informed by research into higher education learning and teaching, the course explores current debates on the changing nature of higher education in online and blended-learning environments. You’ll consider how you can construct effective assessments, provide meaningful feedback and improve student engagement and learning.

If you’re currently teaching, you’ll have the opportunity to apply what you learn to the issues and challenges you face in your academic and professional teaching roles.

This course is fully funded by La Trobe University, at no cost to students.[1] For additional information, check out our frequently asked questions or contact edservices@latrobe.edu.au.

[1] Note that this course is only available to La Trobe University staff and third-party provider staff who are in a teaching or learning support role. See the frequently asked questions for more details.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Critically analyse adult learning theory, teaching and assessment practices and curriculum and assessment design principles within the policy and political context of Higher Education.
**2.**
Apply knowledge, comprehension and skills of reflective teaching practice to your own professional practice in higher education.
**3.**
Integrate a scholarly approach to your practice regarding the purpose of higher education, curriculum, learning and teaching, and your ongoing professional development.
**4.**
Develop a student centred-approach to your own professional practice in relation to learning, teaching, assessment, and curriculum, including the use of appropriate learning technologies.
**5.**
Apply key learning, teaching and curriculum principles and relevant institutional policies to your professional practice in higher education.

Subject: CURRICULUM, TEACHING AND LEARNING (EDU4CTL)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject introduces participants to foundational ideas, current trends, debates and discussions in higher education curriculum, teaching and learning. Normally, this subject is taken first in the Graduate Certificate of Higher Education. Participants will investigate how teaching can promote active learning through increased opportunities for a variety of forms of learner interactions. They will explore how current trends in higher education affect an institution's teaching and learning strategic plans. Participants will have opportunities to consider their own teaching.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically reflect on how current trends in learning and teaching in higher education impact your practice.
- Critically analyse the higher education literature in relation to your practice in curriculum, teaching and learning.
- Apply the principles of learner interactions and active learning in the design of a teaching and learning activity.
- Integrate a scholarly approach to your professional practice.

Subject: DESIGN AND DELIVERY ONLINE (EDU4DDO)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, participants will have an opportunity to develop a deep knowledge and understanding of the key theories and principles in online teaching and curriculum design and apply this knowledge to their practice. They will design learner-centred activities, assessment tasks and feedback processes for online delivery that makes use of inclusive design principles and accommodates diverse learner needs. Furthermore, the subject will enable participants to explore a range of digital tools that encourage engagement and effective learning .
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and evaluate online assessments to achieve learning objectives, including formal and informal feedback processes for diverse learners.
- Integrate a scholarly approach to your professional practice.
- Apply learning theories and principles to the development and evaluation of meaningful online learning interactions to promote higher-order learning.
- Critically analyse and appraise learner-centred approaches to the design and delivery of online learning.

Subject: HIGHER EDUCATION CURRICULUM DESIGN (EDU4HEC)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Good curriculum design in higher education is a critical factor in student satisfaction, success and retention. In this subject, participants explore the practical applications of contemporary higher education curriculum design, models and theories, and reflect on their own educational beliefs and values. Participants take a scholarly approach to curriculum design and gain practical expertise in critically reviewing curricula in higher education.
Learning Outcomes:
- Integrate a scholarly approach to your professional practice.
- Critically review and evaluate curriculum design in accordance with contemporary leading practice in higher education.
- Analyse and reflect on curriculum theory and how this relates to your professional practice.
- Critically evaluate constructive alignment of curriculum in your context.

Subject: TEACHING IN HIGHER EDUCATION: EFFECTIVE, REFLECTIVE PRACTICE (EDU4HET)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Recent trends in the purpose and function of higher education have resulted in a changed understanding of the importance of teaching and learning support. This subject focuses on developing a reflective approach that seeks improvement using feedback that is viewed through a range of lenses. You will engage in critical reflection of your own practice that makes use of personal experience but also the perspectives of students, colleagues, and the literature to improve your teaching and the learning experience of your students. You will also examine the role and function of feedback in student learning and develop evidence-based strategies to improve the quality of feedback that you provide.
Learning Outcomes:
- Generate a cycle of continuous teaching improvement using self, peer and student feedback lenses to reflect on changes to your practice.
- Develop quality feedback strategies that are informed by relevant literature in assessment and feedback in higher education.
- Integrate a scholarly approach to your professional practice.
- Evaluate your teaching practice using reflective practice principles from the higher education literature.

Subject: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY MODULE (LTU0AIM)
Credit Points: 0
Overview: The academic integrity module introduces academic integrity standards to inform and support students in avoiding plagiarism and academic misconduct. Students complete four parts that encompass academic misconduct and academic integrity decisions, including cheating, plagiarism, and collusion. Students learn about Turnitin (the text-matching tool) used at La Trobe University, and how to get help and support their development of referencing skills.
Learning Outcomes:
- Understand the importance of the acknowledgment and the referencing of sources
- Locate, select, and utilise information about La Trobe's approach to academic integrity to support appropriate acknowledgement of references used.
- Recognise and understand academic integrity responsibilities.
