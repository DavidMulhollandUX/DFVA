## Master of Nurse Practitioner - On-Line
##### HM021O
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
This course aims to give students the in-depth knowledge and skills required to practice at an advanced nursing level and to provide professional leadership in contemporary clinical and professional nursing specialties. It is designed to build on the knowledge base and clinical skills of specialist nurses.

The role of nurse practitioner places particular emphasis on the development of advanced clinical assessment skills relevant to the nurse's specialty, and development of a collaborative multidisciplinary approach to patient care. The program focuses on this using the Nurse and Midwifery Board of Australia's Nurse Practitioner competencies.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Mastery of research skills to evaluate the socio-economic, geographical and political factors that construct and maintain nurse practitioner service models to advocate effectively for service improvement and clinically effective outcomes.
**2.**
Critically reflect on professional practice to generate and evaluate models of care for complex cases using research-based evidence and innovative practices.
**3.**
Analyse and synthesize advanced knowledge of medications, regulatory and legislative requirements and research evidence to prescribe therapeutic interventions and appropriate referral pathways.
**4.**
Demonstrate a person-centered care approach in the communication, assessment, management and evaluation of evidence-based patient care.
**5.**
Demonstrate mastery of advanced holistic health assessment and diagnostics interpretation to generate diagnoses utilising contemporary digital tools and informatics.

Subject: ADVANCED CLINICAL EVIDENCE FOR NURSING PRACTICE (NSM5ACE)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Advanced Clinical Evidence for Nursing Practice aims to enhance your ability to source, synthesise, and apply evidence to inform clinical practice and improve patient care and outcomes. Throughout the coursework, you will develop foundational skills in searching, synthesising, critiquing, and referencing peer-reviewed literature. You will also explore various research methodologies. Key concepts, such as translating evidence into practice, addressing underrepresented groups in evidence-based literature, and upholding ethical standards in research, will be covered. Additionally, you will examine the influence of discursive Artificial Intelligence (AI) on academic research practices. You will be encouraged to reflect on the role of nurses and midwives as leaders in evidence-based practice and research. Learning and development will be supported through research-based activities tailored to your areas of specialty or generalist practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse treatment evidence and patient preferences in clinical decision-making, considering cultural, geographical, and socio-economic factors, including language, literacy, and health beliefs.
- Source, critically appraise, and integrate evidence into nursing practice to enhance patient outcomes, inform ongoing professional development, and promote lifelong learning.
- Formulate ethically sound research questions which address evidence gaps within your practice area.
- Justify the selection and use of validated research tools to ensure methodological rigor and the integration of high-quality evidence into safe and effective consumer care.
- Utilise digital technologies and data analysis tools proficiently, while critically assessing the potential benefits and risks of discursive AI in nursing and midwifery research and practice.
- Critique how evidence translation impacts health outcomes, focusing on key barriers and the impact of underrepresented vulnerable populations in research literature.

Subject: ADVANCED CLINICAL NURSING PRACTICE (NSM5ACN)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Advanced Clinical Nursing Practice equips you with advanced skills in comprehensive health assessment and diagnostic reasoning to inform clinical decision-making in diverse healthcare settings. Through a combination of online coursework and practical application, you will develop expertise in assessing the pathophysiology of human body systems across the lifespan, with a focus on addressing national health priorities such as diabetes, dementia, mental illness, and obesity. Through interactive learning modules, case studies, clinical practice, you will enhance your diagnostic capabilities and critical thinking skills, preparing you to excel as an advanced practitioner in today's complex healthcare landscape.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply clinical reasoning and knowledge of own scope of practice to inform decision making round referrals to other healthcare professionals (for either sole or collaborative treatment).
- Demonstrate proficiency in clinical reasoning by integrating the process of history-taking, comprehensive and targeted assessment, selection of diagnostic investigations, and formulation of evidence-based primary and relevant differential diagnoses.
- Demonstrate a comprehensive, systematic clinical assessment approach integrating relevant underlying physiology and pathophysiology.
- Apply critical analysis to comprehensive health assessment and use of diagnostic investigations that consider the impact to the health care consumer.
- Develop, implement, and monitor evidence-based treatment plans, including referral to other specialists, appropriate to the Nurse Practitioner role scope in the Australian healthcare context.
- Demonstrate understanding of the Nurse Practitioner’s role in the provision of collaborative, person-centred, evidence-based healthcare, addressing issues of equity, access, and national health priorities.

Subject: THERAPEUTIC MEDICATION MANAGEMENT A (NSM5TMA)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will gain an understanding of the clinical perspectives of therapeutic medication management. The principles of Quality Use of Medicines will be covered, along with how legislative and professional issues inform judicious, safe, and appropriate prescribing and medication management in the Australian healthcare setting. Your learning will be assessed through a series of case studies, a comprehensive clinical case presentation, online examinations, and work-based learning.
Learning Outcomes:
- Justify safe and effective individualised patient medication profiles to achieve therapeutic goals based on review and analysis of relevant patient information (including comprehensive patient assessment, diagnostics and thorough patient history).
- Implement critical decision-making processes to tailor appropriate medication plans to meet therapeutic goals, patient informed choices and holistic patient management.
- Exemplify safe prescribing practices within applicable legislative and regulatory frameworks and compliance with the Quality Use of Medicines framework.
- Appraise credible and appropriate sources of medication information and evidence to implement an evidence informed approach into care planning.
- Rationalise advanced clinical practice knowledge and assessment to the pharmacological management of pain in own specialty area of practice.

Subject: THERAPEUTIC MEDICATION MANAGEMENT B (NSM5TMB)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop knowledge and skills to identify the effects of medications on physiological, biochemical, and pathophysiological body systems. Systematic clinical assessment and evidence-based critical thinking skills will be applied to justify rationales for the initiation and instigation of therapeutic medications and treatment plans. Your learning will be assessed through a systems-based case study, a comprehensive clinical case presentation, online examinations, and work-based learning.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critique available evidence to inform and support clinical decision making in the management and delivery of pharmacological interventions.
- Exemplify safe prescribing practices within applicable legislative and regulatory frameworks to meet the clinical needs of healthcare consumers.
- Utilise advanced clinical knowledge to rationalise the indications for prescribing, de-prescribing and/or use of non-pharmacological therapies in the treatment of patients.
- Rationalise the differences in pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics in patients of diverse ethnicities and alterations noted to these processes across the lifespan.
- Articulate principles of pharmacology, including the differences between pharmacodynamics and pharmacokinetics, the manner in which medications are absorbed, distributed, metabolised and excreted in the body.
- Evidence understanding of medications effects on each body system and utilise this understanding in the application of pharmacological therapies in relevant area of practice.
