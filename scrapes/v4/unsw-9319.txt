## Description
The Master of Animation and Visual Effects is a two-year (full-time equivalent) program designed for graduates and professionals who aim to thrive in the fast-moving and growing field of animation and visual effects. The program combines technical, conceptual, and creative skills to enable you to work independently and collaboratively as you build your portfolio and realise your artistic vision. With access to world-class facilities and guided by educators with diverse industry experience, the program focuses on creative experimentation and skills development in the content creation pipeline. Working from concept art and pre-visualisation to production and output, you will develop animation and VFX projects to build your experience and profile in the field. As your technical skills grow, you will also develop key attributes in digital aesthetics, communication, teamwork, collaboration, and agility, preparing you for a range of roles in digital production for this quickly evolving and dynamic field of practice.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

2. Employ critical thinking, analytical skills, and research methods to explore concepts and develop specialised knowledge in animation and visual effects.
4. Integrate self-reflexive and professional practice principles to contribute ethically and responsibly to practices and discourses in animation and visual effects.
3. Creatively explore complex concepts and the construction of meaning through animation and visual effects practice for diverse audiences using a variety of methods.
5. Work independently and collaboratively to conceive, develop, document and resolve high-quality animation and visual effects processes and projects.
1. Adapt, apply, and synthesise advanced technical and creative skills to create high-quality animation and visual effects projects.

## Progression & Academic Rules
Students who wish to exit the Masters program early may nominate to transfer to and graduate from the Graduate Certificate or Graduate Diploma provided the requirements for the program have been met.

Students in the Graduate Certificate program can request to transfer into the Graduate Diploma program provided the student has achieved a Pass in all courses and a credit average overall. The 24 UOC completed in the Graduate Certificate should be carried forward as credit in the Graduate Diploma.

Students in the Graduate Diploma program can request to transfer into the Masters program provided the student has achieved a Pass in all courses and a credit average overall. The 48 UOC completed in the Graduate Diploma should be carried forward as credit in the Masters.

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
Admission pathways into the Master of Animation and Visual Effects degree can include:

- A Bachelor's degree (AQF level 7) in any field with a weighted average mark (WAM) of at least 65 or equivalent. This provides admission into the full two-year program, requiring completion of 96 UOC (2 years full-time equivalent).

- A Bachelor's degree (AQF level 7) in a related field with a weighted average mark (WAM) of at least 65 or equivalent. This allows students to receive credit for 24 UOC (4 courses) upon commencement, meaning students can complete the program in 72 UOC (1.7 years full-time equivalent). Or,

- An Honours Degree (AQF level 8) in a related field. This allows students to receive credit for the first year of the program (48 UOC or 8 courses) upon commencement, meaning students can complete the program in 48 UOC (1 year full-time equivalent).

Related disciplines include: the Creative Arts (FOE 10, such as Visual Arts and Crafts, Performing Arts, Graphic and Design Studies, Communication and Media Studies).

Applicants who apply without a Bachelors degree can be admitted to the Graduate Certificate on a case-by-case basis by the Program Director, on the basis of professional experience. Students may then articulate up to the Graduate Diploma if they hold a credit average in their courses taken under the Graduate Certificate, and then articulate up to the Masters if they hold a credit average in their courses taken under the Graduate Diploma.

Recognition of Prior Learning

Students entering with a related Bachelor's degree with a WAM of 65+ are eligible to receive 24 UOC credit, applied as RPL for ADAD9113, DART9300, DART9001, and DART9002. Students who receive 24 UOC credit and wish to enrol in DART9001 and/or DART9002 can request the relevant credit to be applied to a substitute course from the following list: DART9101, DART9102, DART9103, DART9104, DART9105, DART9106. Such requests should be made via the Nucleus Student Hub.

Students entering with an Honours degree in a related field are eligible to receive 48 UOC credit, applied as RPL for the first year of the program (ADAD9113, ADAD9114, DART9300, ADAD9110, DART9001, DART9002, and 12 UOC in prescribed electives).

**Limitations on Recognition of Prior Learning**:
ADAD9115 Internship is not available for RPL based on industry experience.
