## Master of Business Management and Master of Marketing and Digital Communications
##### B6058
##### 96 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The double degree in Master of Business Management and Master of Marketing and Digital Communications provides emerging and established professionals with advanced business knowledge combined with marketing skills and capabilities to deliver organisational success. The Master of Business Management is an integrated and practical introduction to the practice of business management in a contemporary, dynamic and digital context. In this degree, you will learn how organisations can thrive in the context of digital disruption, transformation and accelerating technological change. Combined with the Master of Marketing and Digital Communications where you will build specialist knowledge in the digital space to keep pace with contemporary trends and become an in-demand leader, you’ll be ready for the future. With its part-time and online delivery, you can also fit study around your busy work and family commitments.

In the Master of Business Management, a broad-based business degree, you will develop core business management knowledge and skills, then deep dive into a selected professional specialisation or subject area of your choice, while also gaining the foundation of business leadership knowledge aligned to contemporary employability criteria. As part of the degree, you will also have the opportunity to develop your professional presence and behavioural attributes to equip you for a managerial career and apply the latest thinking and models for implementing transformational change. Ultimately, you will apply all of the skills you have developed by completing industry projects in a sector or business of your choice.  

In the Master of Marketing and Digital Communications you’ll gain cutting edge theoretical knowledge and problem-solving skills in marketing and develop core competencies in critical areas of advocacy and persuasion, public relations and crisis communications, audience research and data analytics which can be applied to the government, corporate, not-for-profit and community sectors. 

As a Master of Business Management and Master of Marketing and Digital Communications graduate, you’ll be strongly placed to step into a managerial career with advanced business knowledge and skills, and you’ll also gain core marketing and communications knowledge along with the full suite of skills of an in-demand digital marketing professional.

### Structure
Double degree courses include all the features of the component degree courses, except that electives may be reduced or redirected.

Master of Business Management 

This course is structured in three parts Part A. Core studies, Part B. Discipline studies, and Part C. Application studies. In this double degree you complete:

Part A. Core studies

These studies will introduce you to business management practice at an advanced graduate level. They are designed to extend your ability to manage the organisation while building on existing professional work experience.

Part C. Application studies

The focus of these studies is professional and project work that can contribute to a portfolio of professional development.

Master of Marketing and Digital Communications

The course is structured in two parts: Part A. Core studies and Part B. Elective studies. In this double degree you complete:

Part A. Core studies

These studies draw on best practices within the broad realm of communications and media studies practice and research to further your understanding of communications and media systems both locally and globally. Studies focus on the challenges posed by the emergence of digital media, globalisation and increasing levels of cross-cultural exchange. 

These studies are further enhanced and expanded on with theoretical and industry based perspectives to provide an understanding of how organisations can connect with and influence their consumers to achieve specific marketing and promotional objectives. These studies will provide insight into the marketing communications industry and how it interacts with society and the changes associated with new technologies and new media. The impact of technological change on marketing and consumer behaviour will also be critically analysed.

You are expected to complete all pre-class activities prior to your scheduled class, and post-class activities should be completed after your scheduled class. Learning activities may include a combination of teacher directed, peer directed and online engagement activities.
