Subject: Nutrition, Sport and Physical Activity Management - Industry Placement (HMST3103)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: To undertake this course, students must meet all mandatory requirements as outlined here: Prepare for your Health, Medicine and Behavioural Sciences placement and provider specific requirements. This course provides practical experience and business management techniques to assist students with future nutrition, sport & physical activity industry opportunities. During a 50-hour practical fieldwork placement, you will have the opportunity to build important relationships and networks with potential employers, test possible career outcomes, and apply knowledge and skills developed within the degree to a real-life context.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify and appraise the structures and governance of Australian nutrition, sport and/or physical activity industries
- Analyse and apply management skills and theory necessary to working within the Australian nutrition, sport and/or physical activity industry
- Build professional practices and relationships, and independently engage with stakeholders
- Utilise theoretical frameworks to promote healthy living to organisations and the broader community
- Critique organisational decisions within an ethical decision-making framework
- Identify and appraise the issues facing contemporary nutrition, sport and/or physical activity organisations within an analytic framework
- Examine the end-to-end customer service journey utilising a human-centred design framework
- Reflect on experiences to engage in continuing professional development
- Work effectively within a team in order to develop communication and process management skills, and facilitate peer-assisted, collaborative learning
Assessment Tasks:
- Assessment A - Professional acumen Team or group-based In-person: 60% Part 1 (15%), Part 2 (15%), Part 3 (5% each week – total 30%) (% (Hurdle: False)
- Assessment B -STAR Portfolio: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
- Assessment C Hurdle In-person: Pass/Fail% (Hurdle: True)
Workload: 2W 50PRA
