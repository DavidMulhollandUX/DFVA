Within the course students are exposed to the core foundations of public health theory and practice and also have the opportunity to specialise in the advanced public health training streams offered through the University of Melbourne. The Master of Public Health programs at the University of Melbourne is delivered by academic staff with extensive experience and depth of knowledge, who are leaders in their chosen fields of public health.\nThe Master of Public Health has three components:\n  * The **core component** includes up to six subjects that provide students with a rigorous, multidisciplinary foundation in public health theory and practice, that is essential to all aspects of public health practice;\n  * The **specialist component** offers students the opportunity to focus on one or more key fields in public health and gain the skills and knowledge required for practice in the area. The MD-MPH entry point is only available to students enrolled in the MD-MPH Intercalated degree.\n  * The **capstone experience** offers students the opportunity to consolidate their specialist training and apply the knowledge gained in the course to real world scenarios. Students may apply to undertake a Research Project with an expert in their field; a Professional Practice placement with a recognised agency, institution or community organisation concerned with health; or capstone selective subjects.\n\n\nThis course is delivered in a mixed attendance mode (both on campus and online).\n[Next: Entry and participation
