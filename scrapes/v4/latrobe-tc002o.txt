## Graduate Certificate in Cybersecurity - On-Line
##### TC002O
##### 60 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Equip yourself with valuable skills in the fight against cyber criminals. Designed by industry leaders, La Trobe's Graduate Certificate in Cybersecurity will give you essentials skills and knowledge to advance your career. Learn how to defend against hackers in one of the most rapid-growth sectors in Australia.

Whether you have existing experience in the information technology sector or not, this course is your pathway to start or progress your career in the cybersecurity domain.

Learn from leading academics and industry practitioners via our flexible online learning platform. 

Enhance your qualifications with Microsoft certifications (via on-campus exams) that are applicable across multiple industries and workplaces, including Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Office suites.

Scholarships and bursaries are also available to support you to succeed in your studies.

You’ll graduate prepared to manage cyberattacks or transfer directly into the second semester of La Trobe’s Master of Cybersecurity, should you decide to further your studies.

You’ll learn: 

  * Programming  * Learn the fundamentals of designing and creating software solutions using the popular programming language, Python. Discover how to analyse and solve problems through rapid testing and development techniques.

  *  Cyber security principles  * Explore how to prevent unauthorised access of information and data. Learn how to respond to a data breach or cyberattack, and understand how to mitigate their negative impacts.

  * Psychology of cyber criminals  * Get inside the minds of hackers and investigate their motivations and techniques. Better understand how to identify digital weaknesses and emerging online threats, and learn how to develop more effective defences.

  * Information technology  * Delve into the fundamental aspects of computer systems including hardware, data storage, software applications, computer networks, data communications, the internet, operating systems, information systems, and security.

There's a critical need for more cybersecurity professionals and they’re highly sought-after in a wide range of sectors, including technology, communications, education, finance, government, defence, healthcare, energy, and resources.

Graduates may be suitable for roles such as:

  * Security architect
  * Plan, implement and supervise computer and network security systems.

  * Cyber intelligence analyst
  * Use your knowledge in areas like network administration or network engineering to help counter the activities of cybercriminals such as hackers and developers of malicious software.

  * Cybersecurity consultant
  * Play both the attacker and the defender to optimise computer systems, networks and software programs.

  * Cybersecurity policy adviser
  * Work independently on different aspects of the advisory model. Understand, assess and articulate complex business and cyber policies.

Subject: CYBER SECURITY PRINCIPLES (CSE4004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In the Internet era, industries and organisations need to be aware of, and be prepared to defend against threats and attacks. Stakeholders should be familiar with the basic principles and best practices of cyber security to better protect their businesses. In this subject, the principles, the state of the art, and strategies for the future of cyber security is explored thoroughly. The topics will focus on information security, ethical and legal practices, mitigating cyber vulnerabilities, and the process of incident response and analysis. The outline of the subject is targeted at ensuring the privacy, reliability, confidentiality and integrity of information systems. Cyber security is a very broad discipline, and therefore, this subject is only intended to cover the basics of the recent state of the art and leading cyber security topics.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse approaches to security networks, firewalls, intrusion detection systems and intrusion prevention systems
- Appraise key terms and concepts in cyber security, cyber law, intellectual property and cyber crime
- Formulate approaches for incident analysis, risk management in cyber space and incident response
- Analyse various software design practices and principles of web security

Subject: INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY FUNDAMENTALS (CSE4IFU)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will learn computer system organization and its associated topics. It covers the hardware components of the computer, data storage and retrieval, and introduces system software, computer networks, data communications, the Internet, operating systems, file management systems and security. You will also be introduced to information systems and application software packages.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and construct basic web pages using HTML and style sheets based on specific requirements.
- Critically examine simple problems and develop spreadsheets to model these problems.
- Differentiate between main types of computer networks and explain the purpose of major network components including any advantages and disadvantages of different data communication media.
- Identify and critically evaluate functions of the main hardware components of computers.
- Analyse and evaluate the requirements and functionality of different types of software.
- Develop and interpret simple database tables, queries and reports based on specific requirements.

Subject: INSIDE THE MIND OF A HACKER (CSE5010)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject explores the motivations, mindset and techniques used by hackers. Although their activities are illicit and illegal, hackers have a finely attuned understanding of computer networks and systems and how users/customers behave in online environments. If nothing else, they have developed new - albeit illegal and unethical business models that exploit vulnerabilities in computer networks and systems. By looking at systems and practices through the eyes of a hacker, you can better identify weaknesses, emerging threats and develop more effective defences.
Learning Outcomes:
- Recommend processes and practices to reduce the likelihood of data breaches
- Deconstruct and critically analyze the profit models, value chain, and standard business security practices from the viewpoint of a hacker.
- Critically analyse the various tactics that hackers use in breaching communication networks.
- Critically analyse typologies of hackers, their mindsets and the motivations behind hacking.
