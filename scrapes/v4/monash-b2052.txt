### Overview
The Bachelor in Actuarial Analytics is a cutting-edge degree designed to equip you with a unique skill set tailored for the data-driven job market. By blending traditional actuarial science with advanced data analytics and risk management skills, graduates emerge with a distinctive expertise, making you highly employable across diverse industries. This program directly addresses the global surge in demand for actuaries, ensuring you are well-equipped to excel in sectors ranging from insurance and finance to government and academia. With a strong focus on data quantification techniques, you will be prepared to tackle complex data challenges, while your analytical and quantitative skills open doors to a wide range of career opportunities beyond traditional actuarial roles. Highlights include mastery of data analytics and business intelligence, and strategic adoption of emerging technologies in various business functions for enhanced efficiency and competitiveness. Upon program completion, you will become a catalyst for digital transformation within organisations, propelling impactful change in the ever-evolving landscape of data-centric industries. The program will propel you towards success in the dynamic job market of today and tomorrow.

### Structure
The Bachelor in Actuarial Analytics is structured in four parts: Part A. Core studies, Part B. Discipline studies, Part C. Capstone studies and Part D. Elective studies.

Part A. Core studies

These units will provide you with a comprehensive study of economics and econometrics disciplines and the impact they have on multi-discipline decision-making in organisations. The units consider the impact on the business, professional and public policy communities.

Part B. Discipline studies

These units will develop your capacity as a critical and creative professional who is able to apply your knowledge of actuarial science to provide discipline-based solutions to commerce. Units contained within the discipline may meet the requirements for professional accreditation bodies.

Part C. Capstone studies

The capstone unit is designed to consolidate the knowledge and skills acquired throughout the study of your specialisation.

Part D. Elective studies

This will enable you to further develop your knowledge of actuarial science, or commerce more broadly, or to select any units from across the University in which you are eligible to enrol including to complete a minor from another course.

Course progression map

The course progression map provides guidance on unit enrolment for each semester of study.

### Requirements
You must complete units as specified in Parts A, B, C and D (144 credit points), including 120 points of study in Parts A, B, and C, and 24 points of electives in Part D.

In choosing your units, you must ensure that you complete no more than 10 units (60 credit points) at level 1 and at least six units (36 credit points) at level 3, including four units (24 credit points) at level 3 offered by the Faculty of Business and Economics.

Students can apply to transfer to the Bachelor in Actuarial Science after completion of 96 credit points. To be eligible for transfer, students must achieve an overall course WAM of 70 and a minimum mark of 70 for selected units.

Units are 6 credit points unless otherwise specified.

Part A. Core studies (24 credit points)

You must complete the following four units (24 credit points):

  * ACW1120 Financial accounting 1

  * BFW1001 Foundations of finance

### AHEGS
The Bachelor in Actuarial Analytics is an undergraduate degree. The course is taught in English and normally takes three years of full-time study or six years of part-time study or equivalent. The course provides graduates with a professional education in the actuarial science and analytics discipline with a strong emphasis on constructing conceptual frameworks to analyse complex issues. Graduates gain an understanding of various classical actuarial and statistical modelling techniques as well as modern data analytics methods. The Bachelor in Actuarial Analytics is a level 7 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification.
