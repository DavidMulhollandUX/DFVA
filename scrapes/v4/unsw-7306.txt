## Description
The Graduate Certificate in Design is for those who wish to extend their professional qualifications in design or digital media with a shorter period of study and also those wishing to broaden or change directions in their design and media practice.

The Graduate Certificate in Design consists of four courses, two in the Design core introducing design processes and communication, and two chosen from Design areas of practice or other studio electives.

The Graduate Certificate in Design provides for pathways for both those with backgrounds in design and those who wish to enter design from other professional backgrounds. Graduates of the Graduate Certificate in Design have enhanced skills and understanding and can either complete a Graduate Diploma in Design or a Master of Design or work as design and media professionals.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

1. Knowledge of innovative developments in the field of professional design practice and be able to apply this knowledge to conduct Design projects.
3. Ability to work independently, and in collaborative interdisciplinary design teams
2. An understanding of critical thinking and problem solving skills in Design.

## Progression & Academic Rules
Candidates whose entry to the Graduate Diploma is approved may carry 24 units of credit from the Graduate Certificate to the Graduate Diploma, whether the lower award has been conferred or not, provided the student has achieved a Pass in all foundation courses and at least a credit average overall.

Candidates whose entry to the Masters is approved may carry 24 units of credit from the Graduate Certificate to the Masters, whether the lower award has been conferred or not, provided the student has achieved a Pass in all foundation courses and at least a credit average overall.

## Admission Requirements
**Limitations on Recognition of Prior Learning**:
Graduate Certificate candidates are not eligible for Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL).

**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
A bachelor's degree in any field.

Applicants who apply without a bachelor's degree can be admitted to the Graduate Certificate on a case-by-case basis by the Program Authority, on the basis of professional experience and submission of a portfolio.

Through hands-on research, prototyping, and iterative design processes, students will not only develop practical UX skills but also deepen their understanding of the broader impact of design on public life. - Apply inclusive UX research methods to analyse social policy challenges and opportunities by conducting human-centred research, defining stakeholders, user needs and values.

Subject: Designing and Experiencing Immersion (DDES9901)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Virtual experiences and immersive environments are an integrated aspect of contemporary work, study, and entertainment. In response to this evolving landscape, this course offers a rigorous inquiry into the principles and practices that shape meaningful virtual and immersive experiences. You will explore how, when, and why immersive technologies can be incorporated into design solutions, considering neuroscientific perspectives on perception, historical uses across the creative arts and sciences, and current industry examples. Emphasis is placed on immersion as a form of embodied cognition that connects mind and body. Building on this experience, you will then develop a proposal for an original immersive concept that demonstrates your understanding of immersion’s possibilities and applications. Learning Outcomes:
- Conceptualise and propose an original immersive experience that demonstrates understanding of foundational principles of immersion and embodied cognition
- Assess and justify why, how, when, and where different immersive design techniques and technologies should be applied
- Critically test, analyse, and evaluate immersive experiences through hands-on engagement, with a focus on how humans perceive and experience immersion
