## Master of Business (Research)
##### LMBR
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
This course requires the submission of a thesis of up to 50,000 words for examination. You can undertake supervised research in accounting, data analytics, economics, entrepreneurship, finance, management and human resources, marketing, sports management, or tourism.  The course includes four coursework subjects, which provide training in research skills and may be waived for students who have completed an Honours degree or equivalent.

### Location(s)
Melbourne (Bundoora), Bendigo

### Available only as an exit award
No

### Completion requirements
To qualify for an award of the degree, you must submit a satisfactory thesis of up to 50,000 words, demonstrating specialised knowledge and research skills in any of the following disciplines: accounting, data analytics, economics, entrepreneurship, finance, management and human resources, marketing, sports management, and tourism.  Your thesis will be examined by at least two external examiners.

The degree of Master of Business (Research) shall be awarded based on examination of the thesis and, if applicable, after the satisfactory completion of any coursework requirements.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Ability to generate and assess complex ideas and concepts at an abstract level
**2.**
Ability to design, use and evaluate research and research methods
**3.**
Advanced communication and technical skills to present coherent arguments and disseminate research findings to specialist and non-specialist audiences
**4.**
Ability to analyse and synthesise complex information, problems, concepts and theories and apply to established theories from different bodies of knowledge or practice
**5.**
Mastery of theoretical knowledge and ability to reflect critically on theory and its application

### Course features
### Work based learning (placement) requirements
A range of work-based learning opportunities are available, including internships, mentoring, and industry-linked projects.

Internship: candidates who have successfully confirmed their candidature and who are making satisfactory progress may apply to participate in an internship at least one month before they submit their thesis. Refer to the Graduate Research Candidature Policy and associated information.

Candidates will apply separately to participate in an internship through a process detailed on the Graduate Research School website.

For more information visit https://www.latrobe.edu.au/researchers/grs/industry-community
### Other opportunities
The Graduate Research School's Research Education and Development (RED) team supports and assists La Trobe graduate researchers in: quality research practices; strategies for success in research publication and funding; insight into researcher career paths and industry sectors; and practising excellent research communication skills. For more information, visit https://www.latrobe.edu.au/researchers/grs/red.
### Career outcomes
This degree will help you develop skills highly valued in a range of sectors.  The acquired skills might lead you to work in academic research, higher education, government, or corporate.  Successful completion of the Master of Business (Research) can also serve as a pathway into a PhD.

Subject: FOUNDATIONS OF BUSINESS RESEARCH (BUS5004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will learn about the epistemology of research and explore the critical role that independent research, when conducted to the highest standards, contributes to our society. You will hone your ability to undertake sophisticated literature searches and manage this body of knowledge using reference management systems in accordance with academic conventions. You will enhance your ability to analyse relevant literature to identify a research gap and develop logical and detailed justifications for your arguments.
Learning Outcomes:
- Effectively manage your literature library using a reference management system
- Evaluate the ideas and debates of the philosophy of science in the context of business research
- Communicate arguments and syntheses of research in a professional and scholarly manner
- Generate appropriate research questions from a rigorous literature review of a relevant body of knowledge

Subject: QUANTITATIVE DATA ANALYSIS (BUS5005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will explore the quantitative analysis techniques that are commonly used by business researchers, analysts and managers to analyse data to provide answers to business problems and research questions. - Generate quantitative insights utilising a prescribed statistical package.

Subject: RESEARCH DESIGN (BUS5006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will learn about research design as the foundation for conducting high quality research. You will learn the hallmarks of social research methods, including observational, survey, and experimental designs. You will develop skills to evaluate the strengths, limitations, and ethical considerations of these designs for answering specific research questions.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically evaluate various research design and methodologies
- Identify ethical issues within a research setting and develop an appropriate response to these issues
- Develop and justify a written research proposal designed to address research questions within your discipline

Subject: QUALITATIVE DATA ANALYSIS (BUS5007)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject provides you with the opportunity to develop skills in qualitative research practice in the field of business. This involves developing an appreciation of the skills needed to collect qualitative data using appropriate methods, including interviews, focus groups, document analysis and observation. You will also learn how to analyse, interpret, and report qualitative data to a variety of audiences.

- Communicate the methods and results from the qualitative approaches and analysis in a professional and scholarly way

Subject: ECONOMETRICS AND DATA ANALYTICS (BUS5008)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will enhance your proficiency in quantitative data analysis while acquiring quantitative techniques commonly used in business. These skills will enable you to effectively address critical research inquiries and make informed strategic choices. The curriculum encompasses various topics like time-series modelling and panel data analysis. Moreover, you will delve into the realm of data mining and machine learning, acquiring hands-on experience with software tools. This subject will provide you with the expertise and competence to apply econometrics and data analytics techniques in academic research and business scenarios.
Learning Outcomes:
- Appraise different quantitative data analysis techniques and justify the selection of techniques for addressing research questions in business
- Communicate the methods and results of the utilised quantitative approaches in a professional manner
- Apply appropriate and various quantitative data analysis techniques using software tools

Subject: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY MODULE (LTU0AIM)
Credit Points: 0
Overview: The academic integrity module introduces academic integrity standards to inform and support students in avoiding plagiarism and academic misconduct. Students complete four parts that encompass academic misconduct and academic integrity decisions, including cheating, plagiarism, and collusion. Students learn about Turnitin (the text-matching tool) used at La Trobe University, and how to get help and support their development of referencing skills.
Learning Outcomes:
- Understand the importance of the acknowledgment and the referencing of sources
- Locate, select, and utilise information about La Trobe's approach to academic integrity to support appropriate acknowledgement of references used.
- Recognise and understand academic integrity responsibilities.
