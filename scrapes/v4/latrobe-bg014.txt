## Graduate Diploma in Business Analytics - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BG014
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Carefully designed to keep you ahead of the trends, La Trobe’s Graduate Diploma in Business Analytics will help you grow your career in this fast-moving industry. This course equips you with the technical, analytical, and business skills necessary to build data-based solutions that address the complexity of data and information in modern businesses. In this course, disciplinary knowledge in data, analytics and AI are aligned with industry leading tools and technologies to provide a hands-on learning experience of practical skills for solving real-world business problems. The teaching team draw on their own industry and research expertise in building AI and analytics solutions to deliver a future-focused learning experience. They teach in small, interactive classes and use Gen AI teaching tools to ensure the student learning journey is personalised and fully supported.

You will learn:

  * 
Data Analytics:

  * 
Professional knowledge and skills to build and use analytics dashboards, and predictive models for real-world business problems.

  * 
Business Innovation:

  * 
AI, analytics and data-driven innovations in business settings to create value, solve problems, and improve performance.

  * 
ICT Ethics and Cybersecurity:

  * 
Knowledge of local and international regulatory frameworks and guidelines for addressing enterprise ethics and cybersecurity challenges.

  * 
Professional ICT Practice:

  * 
Power skills essentials for collaboration and teamwork in modern workplaces, including communication with a wide range of stakeholders.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Analyse and interpret business datasets using data transformations, visualisation, modelling and management techniques to support organisational decision-making.
**2.**
Use AI prompting, strategies, tools, and AI-assisted coding platforms to design and develop AI models and prototypes to address complex industrial challenges.
**3.**
Develop and communicate analytics strategies, actionable insights, and AI-supported recommendations clearly to diverse organisational stakeholders.
**4.**
Apply and evaluate predictive analytics models and AI algorithms to solve complex business problems.
**5.**
Apply principles of cybersecurity, ethical practice, and data privacy to ensure responsible handling of information in analytics and AI contexts.

### Career outcomes
The need for analytics professionals is at an all-time high. Organisations are seeking to understand the massive amounts of data now at their disposal. After graduation, you could work across a wide range of sectors in roles such as:

  * Data Analyst
  * Leverage advanced analytics, AI tools, data modelling, and visualisation to extract insights from complex data and support informed decision-making. Build effective data models and create clear, impactful visuals to communicate findings and drive business value.

  * Business Analyst 
  * Analyse business contexts to identify problems and opportunities, using data-driven methods to inform decisions.

Subject: CYBERSECURITY FOR BUSINESS (BUS4006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Cybersecurity for Business provides a comprehensive overview of cybersecurity principles, practices, and frameworks for managing cybersecurity risks in organisations. Cybersecurity plays a critical role in an organisation's operations by safeguarding its assets, ensuring regulatory compliance, and enhancing overall business resilience. The subject covers key topics, including cybersecurity threats, attacks, governance frameworks, cybersecurity risk management, cybersecurity policies and procedures, and compliance. You will explore cybersecurity for businesses through a combination of tailor-made case studies and hands-on workshops, offering a comprehensive understanding of theoretical principles alongside practical application in real-world scenarios. It develops the skills necessary to craft effective cybersecurity strategies that align with organisational goals and regulatory requirements. The course will also examine the ethical, legal, and regulatory dimensions of cybersecurity.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse different types of cyber threats, attack methods, and AI-driven threats, to assess their potential impact on business operations, risk management, and compliance obligations.
- Design and justify ethical and governance-aligned cybersecurity strategies using appropriate frameworks, AI-enabled tools, and security models to strengthen threat detection, incident response, resilience, and regulatory compliance.
- Identify and apply fundamental cybersecurity principles to recognise and evaluate common organisational security challenges, including those arising from emerging technologies and AI.
- Communicate cybersecurity governance strategies and AI-informed risk mitigation plans clearly and appropriately to diverse technical and non-technical stakeholders in professional business contexts.

Subject: DATA WRANGLING (BUS5DWR)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject introduces you to the various techniques of data wrangling with a strong focus on hands-on experience in programming using Structured Query Language (SQL) and the Python programming language. It will cover the basic concepts in relational database design including Entity Relationship (ER) modelling and SQL as a tool for basic data wrangling. You will also learn various types of data sources and common data formats. The subject equips you with skills in data wrangling, including data import and export, basic data integration, assessment and transformation. Upon completion, you will be able to perform a variety of data wrangling tasks using SQL and a programming language for different kinds of data types.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and implement database models that meet business requirements using advanced data modelling and querying techniques to enable analytics initiatives.
- Analyse data by developing SQL queries and data wrangling scripts using programming tools with responsible use of generative AI to support data transformation and insight generation for data-driven business decisions.
- Evaluate and compare data sources, formats, and structures to prepare and transform data for analysis, considering ethical, legal, and cybersecurity risks in business analytics.
- Formulate data and data-wrangling requirements that address the technical challenges of analytics tasks, incorporating data governance, ethical principles, and cybersecurity constraints.
