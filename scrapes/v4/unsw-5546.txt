## Description
The Graduate Diploma in Information Technology is designed for students who wish to develop and expand their knowledge and skills in Information Technology.

The aim of this award is to provide qualified students with an opportunity to study advanced topics in an Information Technology specialisation to complement or update their existing qualifications, and for those with minimal background in Information Technology, an opportunity to obtain a qualification in this discipline.

The program articulates to the Master of Information Technology program with full credit for courses completed in the Graduate Diploma program. Students may exit from this proposed program with a Graduate Diploma award if they have completed the required course requirements.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

6. Apply ethical approaches to professional practice.
5. Communicate information and justify decisions to other professionals, clients, stakeholders, and the broader community.
1. Explain advanced concepts, theories and techniques, relevant to information technology.
4. Synthesise algorithmic knowledge and apply appropriate computing methods to contribute to effective and/or innovative solutions to complex engineering problems.
7. Collaborate and lead effectively in diverse professional contexts.
3. Identify, define, investigate, and analyse the computing aspects of complex engineering problems.
2. Apply a breadth of relevant computing knowledge, including information technology management.

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
All candidates are required to have, at a minimum, a Bachelor Degree (AQF 7 or equivalent) in a related discipline with a weighted average mark of 65% (or equivalent).

**Limitations on Recognition of Prior Learning**:
Up to 24 UOC of advanced standing may be granted to candidates with degrees from cognate programs.
All courses completed towards the Graduate Certificate of Information Technology at UNSW will be recognised for the purpose of advanced standing for the Graduate Diploma of Information Technology.

===== SPECIALISATION / AREA OF STUDY =====
### Artificial Intelligence (COMPRS)
<p>Recent technological advances are bringing robots out of their usual industrial settings and into homes and offices. Mobile robots capable of vacuum cleaning and lawn mowing are already commercially available. As miniaturisation and sophistication increase and costs continue to decrease, it is anticipated that robots will become commonplace. Applications include domestic robots to assist in everyday chores, robots for entertainment or educational purposes as well as robots for automating office and factory tasks. Applications also include assistance for the elderly, in particular, to improve their mobility and level of care.</p>
<p>As autonomous devices become more common, potential employers include companies that can make ready use of automation. This includes companies concerned with home and office automation, entertainment companies and autonomous systems research and development. Autonomous systems and the sophisticated sensors they employ are also adopted in many industries to enhance production and to improve safety (e.g. mining, manufacturing, transport).</p>
<p>The Artificial Intelligence specialisation in the Graduate Diploma and Master of Information Technology programs focuses on the development of sophisticated software for mobile robot control. Several aspects of software are considered:</p>
<ul><li>artificial intelligence</li><li>agent programming</li><li>experimental robotics</li><li>knowledge representation and reasoning</li><li>machine learning</li><li>robot architectures</li><li>human-computer interaction</li><li>vision</li></ul>
