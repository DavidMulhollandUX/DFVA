## Graduate Diploma in International Business - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BG003
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
As international markets become increasingly interconnected and complex, the demand for agile, forward-thinking professionals continues to rise. If you are seeking to rapidly expand your expertise in international business, La Trobe’s Graduate Diploma in International Business offers an ideal pathway to advance your knowledge in just one year.

The course strengthens your cross-cultural competencies and equips you to navigate the challenges of managing people and organisations in international business contexts. You will explore how organisations adapt to diverse cultural, institutional, and regulatory settings to build effective workforce and foster collaboration across borders.

A key focus lies in the management of innovation in an international setting, examining how emerging technologies, such as artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity, are shaping operational practices and strategic decision-making. By integrating core business skills with insights into digital transformation and international talent dynamics, you will gain the tools to develop inclusive and future-oriented strategies for international business success.

By the end of the course, you will possess a distinctive blend of capabilities in international business, digital fluency and intercultural engagement, preparing you for impactful roles on the world stage.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Examine strategies for leading and managing individuals and teams in culturally diverse and international organisational settings.
**2.**
Evaluate practical solutions to issues in cross-border business operations.
**3.**
Apply ethical and strategic decision-making skills in international business environments.
**4.**
Demonstrate cross-cultural competence and communicate international business ideas effectively to diverse audiences.
**5.**
Critically analyse international business theories and concepts to address challenges in international business contexts.

Subject: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR BUSINESS (BUS4005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the nature and function of business. However, business leaders are challenged by the effective and competitive adoption of AI. This subject provides you with an industry-ready skillset to empower productivity, innovation and decision-making across the business ecosystem. You will learn to apply prompting techniques, build and evaluate AI chatbots, construct and articulate AI strategies. Alongside AI capabilities, you will recognise and address the risks of AI, ethics and cybersecurity through a deep understanding of local and international guidelines, regulations and governance. You will foster an AI-first mindset for the effective and responsible practice of AI in any business or industry environment.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply and critically evaluate prompting techniques and AI tools in diverse business contexts
- Analyse and critically evaluate AI strategies and policies to support responsible adoption in varied business environments
- Design and develop in teams, AI chatbot solutions that address complex business challenges, demonstrating alignment with ethical principles, governance frameworks, and cybersecurity standards.

Subject: CYBERSECURITY FOR BUSINESS (BUS4006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Cybersecurity for Business provides a comprehensive overview of cybersecurity principles, practices, and frameworks for managing cybersecurity risks in organisations. Cybersecurity plays a critical role in an organisation's operations by safeguarding its assets, ensuring regulatory compliance, and enhancing overall business resilience. The subject covers key topics, including cybersecurity threats, attacks, governance frameworks, cybersecurity risk management, cybersecurity policies and procedures, and compliance. You will explore cybersecurity for businesses through a combination of tailor-made case studies and hands-on workshops, offering a comprehensive understanding of theoretical principles alongside practical application in real-world scenarios. It develops the skills necessary to craft effective cybersecurity strategies that align with organisational goals and regulatory requirements. The course will also examine the ethical, legal, and regulatory dimensions of cybersecurity.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse different types of cyber threats, attack methods, and AI-driven threats, to assess their potential impact on business operations, risk management, and compliance obligations.
- Design and justify ethical and governance-aligned cybersecurity strategies using appropriate frameworks, AI-enabled tools, and security models to strengthen threat detection, incident response, resilience, and regulatory compliance.
- Identify and apply fundamental cybersecurity principles to recognise and evaluate common organisational security challenges, including those arising from emerging technologies and AI.
- Communicate cybersecurity governance strategies and AI-informed risk mitigation plans clearly and appropriately to diverse technical and non-technical stakeholders in professional business contexts.

Subject: PRINCIPLES OF BUSINESS ANALYTICS (BUS4011)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject introduces you to business and data analytics with a strong focus on practical outcomes that are directly applicable to business contexts. It provides a comprehensive understanding of current theories, frameworks, applications, and technologies that support data-driven decision-making in organisations. You will gain hands-on experience with industry-standard analytics platforms to conduct descriptive analytics and build interactive dashboards aligned with business needs. The subject explores the strategic role of analytics in digital transformation, performance management, and continuous improvement. Key topics include descriptive analytics, visualisation tools, data ethics, and cybersecurity. Industry-based guest lectures provide insights into how organisations are applying analytics to enhance business performance and address emerging challenges. Upon completion, you will be able to explain the role of analytics in business environments, apply analytical tools, evaluate ethical considerations, and align analytics strategies with organisational objectives.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply advanced analytics techniques and tools aligned with industry best practice to generate data-driven insights, design effective dashboards, and support evidence-based decision-making.
- Evaluate cybersecurity, data protection, and ethical challenges in organisational analytics and AI, and apply frameworks to ensure responsible and compliant data practices.
- Critically examine the purpose and strategic value of analytics and AI in organisations to enhance decision-making, operational efficiency, and innovation.

Subject: MANAGING INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS (IBU4003)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: As the international landscape grows increasingly complex and interconnected, effective management of international business has become a vital capability for organisations. This subject provides a comprehensive introduction to managing international business, examining key strategic approaches to cross-border operations, regionalisation, and international competition. You will analyse how organisations engage with external environments, navigate political and social dynamics, and address ethical considerations and social responsibility within international contexts. Through critical study of international business functions, you will explore how organisations adapt structures and strategies to gain competitive advantage in international markets. Learning Outcomes:
- Apply contemporary theories to align talent management with the strategic and operational demands of international business.
- Examine the role of business ethics and organisational governance in international business management.
- Evaluate management challenges by analysing cultural dynamics, regulatory environments, and the complexities of cross-border operations.
- Critically analyse how regional and international developments influence managerial approaches and strategic decision-making in international business environments.

Subject: INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS STRATEGY (IBU5001)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will work on the formulation of a business strategy for firms engaged or looking to engage in international business. With the support of industry leaders, you will apply your knowledge gathered in previous studies in the course to analyse an authentic business scenario. You will then recommend business solutions and devise strategic options to help the business succeed in the global marketplace. Studying this subject will be instrumental in preparing you for a successful career in international business and help you transition effectively into the job market.
Learning Outcomes:
- Work effectively in teams to critically evaluate the merit and application of business theories and practices to recommend business solutions and devise responsible strategic options for the success of firms in international business.
- Critically analyse an authentic international business case, and the global forces affecting it, to assess its position or potential position in the global marketplace.
- Effectively communicate complex business ideas to a diverse audience, both orally an in written form, in a professional and engaging manner.

Subject: TOTAL QUALITY MANAGEMENT (MGT5007)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject provides a comprehensive introduction to Total Quality Management (TQM) and its practical applications in modern organisations. You will explore the historical evolution of quality management and its pivotal role in business contexts. The focus is on enhancing quality management practices to ensure consistent delivery of high-quality products and services. Throughout this subject, you will develop a thorough understanding of TQM principles and their integration into organisational frameworks. Emphasis is placed on equipping you with essential tools, techniques, and practices crucial for sustained organisational success. Topics covered include quality control methodologies, continuous improvement strategies, lean principles, Six Sigma methodologies, Kaizen practices, and other essential management techniques. Additionally, this subject aims to familiarize you with emerging trends in quality management, particularly in the realms of sustainability and digital transformation. By exploring these new perspectives, you will be prepared to address evolving challenges and leverage innovative approaches to drive quality improvements within contemporary organisations.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate effective teamwork and leadership in fostering efficiency and quality in operational contexts.
- Apply TQM techniques and strategies to drive continuous improvement and enhance processes.
- Critically analyse the principles of Total Quality Management (TQM) and operational excellence.
