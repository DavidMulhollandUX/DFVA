The MEngSc (Man) combines postgraduate studies in selected fields of engineering (Electrical; Software) or (Chemical; Civil; Materials; Mechanical; Mechatronic; within the Undeclared major) with studies in selected fields of business. For existing engineering professionals, undertaking this program will meet your professional and personal development needs in the future.
The program provides for development of business skills in engineering graduates without prior formal background and caters for students with relatively advanced backgrounds by allowing them to substitute engineering courses with business management courses.

Subject: EIBS7302 - Managing Innovation
### EIBS7302 - Managing Innovation
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse how organisations use innovation strategies to respond to dynamic markets, technological change, and uncertainty.
- Develop innovation strategies using appropriate innovation management frameworks, tools, and techniques.
- Adapt innovation strategies in response to changing market conditions, competitive pressures, and organisational outcomes.
- Evaluate innovation management approaches in relation to organisational contexts, stakeholder impacts, and changing business environments.
- Communicate evidence-based innovation insights and strategic recommendations using research, industry, and real-world innovation contexts.
Assessment Tasks:
- Blogging on innovation: 30%%
- In-class reflection on strategic innovation Identity Verified In-person: 30%%
- Self-written case study: 40%%
Workload: Seminar 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: MGTS7612 - The Fundamentals of Designing and Staffing Organisations
### MGTS7612 - The Fundamentals of Designing and Staffing Organisations
Learning Outcomes:
- Explain how organisational design and strategic staffing aid businesses to meet their objectives.
- Working both individually and as a team, design essential aids to the staffing process, incorporating role design, recruitment and selection tools, while also ensuring the legal requirements for these processes are addressed.
- Critically evaluate the appropriateness of different organisational design, recruitment and selection processes for different role types and circumstances, within an organisational context and considering multiple stakeholders.
- Elaborate on how organisational design and staffing practices that take ethics, diversity and employee wellbeing into account add value to organisations and society.
- Prepare evidence-based arguments and well-designed recommendations for presentation to senior stakeholders for aspects of organisational design and staffing practices.
Assessment Tasks:
- In-Class Quiz (Individual) Identity Verified In-person: 30%%
- Recruitment & Selection Strategy Portfolio (Group) Identity Verified Team or group-based In-person: 40%%
- Recruitment & Selection Case Practical Exam (Individual) Identity Verified In-person: 30%%
Workload: Seminar 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: MGTS7309 - Decision Making and Reporting for Sustainability
### MGTS7309 - Decision Making and Reporting for Sustainability
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse the interface between business and the natural environment to explain the interdisciplinary implications of organisational sustainability.
- Evaluate organisational decision-making and sustainability reporting.
- Recommend appropriate approaches for implementing, measuring, and reporting organisational sustainability performance.
- Interpret managerial perspectives in responding to sustainability challenges.
- Collaboratively , make strategic decisions for an organisation’s environmental, social and economic priorities and reporting.
- Report on the sustainability impacts of an organisation’s operations to influence decision-making.
Assessment Tasks:
- Case Studies Identity Verified Team or group-based In-person: 60%%
- Project Presentation Identity Verified Team or group-based In-person: 40%%
Workload: Seminar 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: ELEC7309 - Power System Planning and Reliability
### ELEC7309 - Power System Planning and Reliability
Learning Outcomes:
- Construct a basic modelling of power system components for reliability evaluation and planning
- Design methodologies to solve power system generation system reliability calculation and generation planning.
- Investigate calculation of reliability indices for combined generation and transmission systems.
- Formulate reliability concepts to transmission, distribution and interconnected systems, especially in planning.
- Design power systems reliability and planning in the context of the deregulated environment.
- Develop load forecasting models for short-term and long-term power system planning
- Construct transmission and generation expansion models for both regulated and deregulated power systems.
- Develop a simple peer-to-peer energy trading algorithm for distribution system planning analysis.
- Select suitable technology options for generation and transmission planning problems using cost-benefit analysis.
- Investigate challenges and tools for solving large-scale power system planning problems.
- Construct small-scale planning/reliability problems using power system packages and toolboxes.
Assessment Tasks:
- Assignments: 40% Individual%
- Final exam during exam period Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 60% Individual%
Workload: Lecture 3 Hours/ Week Applied Class 2 Hours/ Week
