## Master of Business Analytics - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BM014
##### 240 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
In the Age of Artificial Intelligence (AI), knowledge and skills in organisational data, analytics techniques and AI capabilities are foundational for driving business success, economic growth and development. Codesigned with leading industry experts, the Master of Business Analytics is a specialised postgraduate degree that equips you with the technical, analytical, and business skills necessary to build solutions that address the complexity of data and information in modern businesses.

In this course, disciplinary knowledge in data, analytics and AI are aligned with industry leading tools and technologies to provide a hands-on learning experience of practical skills for solving real-world business problems. The teaching team draw on their own industry and research expertise in building AI and analytics solutions to deliver a future-focused learning experience. They teach in small, interactive classes and use Gen AI teaching tools to ensure the student learning journey is personalised and fully supported.

Build and grow your analytics and AI capabilities through industry placements and business case studies that foster critical thinking, ethical reasoning, strategic communication and leadership. Become a well-balanced technology professional with data, analytics, and AI capabilities that are highly sought after by AI-first organisations of the future.

You will learn:

  * Data Analytics: professional knowledge and skills to build and use analytics dashboards, predictive models, cloud applications for real-world business problems
  * Artificial Intelligence: industry-ready skillset in AI to drive productivity, innovation and decision-making across the business ecosystem
  * Business Innovation: AI, analytics and data-driven innovations in business settings to create value, solve problems, and improve performance
  * ICT Ethics and Cybersecurity: knowledge of local and international regulatory frameworks and guidelines for addressing enterprise ethics and cybersecurity challenges
  * Professional ICT Practice: power skills essential for collaboration and teamwork in modern workplaces, including communication, critical thinking, emotional intelligence, and adaptability.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Analyse and interpret business datasets by applying data transformations, management, visualisation, security and privacy practices to develop responsible AI and analytics solutions.
**2.**
Critically evaluate business objectives, ethical and sustainability considerations, stakeholder roles, and project management strategies to guide analytics and AI initiatives.
**3.**
Develop and communicate analytics strategies, actionable insights and AI-driven decision-making, to diverse stakeholders across organisational settings.
**4.**
Construct and evaluate responsible AI and analytics solutions that integrate analytical, technical, and problem-solving skills with business competencies and ethical guidelines to address complex business problems.
**5.**
Apply and evaluate analytics techniques, AI models and algorithms, and cloud technologies for solving business problems, with attention to business value, return on investment, data privacy and cybersecurity.

### Work based learning (placement) requirements
During this course, you will have the opportunity to participate in a Work Based Learning (WBL) placement experience, designed to allow you to extend your formal academic learning beyond the classroom. The WBL experience will provide the opportunity to bring your learning from the university into a work environment and test out your knowledge in a professional real-world environment. Placements are expected to be self-sourced by students and be related to their studies. Subject to approval from the University, placements can be undertaken within an organisation, remotely, or as a virtual experience.

### Career outcomes
The need for analytics professionals is at an all-time high. Organisations of all sizes are seeking to understand the massive amounts of data now at their disposal. After graduation, you could work across a wide range of sectors in roles such as:

  * Data Analyst
  * Leverage advanced analytics, artificial intelligence, data modelling, and visualisation to extract insights from complex data and support informed decision-making. Build effective data models and create clear, impactful visuals to communicate findings and drive business value.

  * Business Analyst
  * Analyse business contexts to identify problems and opportunities, using data-driven methods to inform decisions and optimise processes.

  * Report Analyst
  * Use and apply data analytics tools and platforms to generate management and operational reports, actionable insights and inform executive decision-making.

  * Data Engineer
  * Design and maintain scalable, cloud-based data infrastructure, including data warehouses and pipelines, to enable secure, reliable, and efficient access to analytics-ready data across the organisation.

  * Gen AI Specialist
  * Adopt and apply prompting techniques, generative AI models, and AI-assisted coding to develop business solutions and optimise business efficiencies.

  * Analytics and AI Consultant

  * Design and evaluate AI-enabled business solutions with a focus on cybersecurity and ethics.
