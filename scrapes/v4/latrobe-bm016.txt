## Master of International Business - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BM016
##### 240 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
As the international business landscape becomes increasingly complex and dynamic, leaders with international expertise are in high demand.

La Trobe’s Master of International Business has been collaboratively designed with industry experts to advance your career and equip you with high-level, career ready capabilities demanded by today’s leading internationally orientated organisations. This course provides advanced knowledge and cutting-edge skills needed to succeed in an ever-changing international business environment.

Throughout your journey in this Master's course, you will:

  * Examine the operations of multinational organisations, appreciate the complexities of international businesses, develop a global mindset and enhance your cultural intelligence.
  * Gain in-depth insights into key areas such as cross-cultural management, international trade, international human resource management, international corporate governance, international project management, international finance, international logistics, international marketing and innovation management.
  * Navigate the challenges of managing people and organisations internationally, exploring how they adapt to diverse cultural, institutional and regulatory environments to build an effective workforce and foster cross-border collaboration.
  * Investigate how digital innovation and emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity, are shaping international business practices and strategic decision-making.
  * Acquire the competencies to formulate and implement inclusive, future-oriented international strategies that support businesses’ economic, social and sustainability goals.
  * Benefit from practical opportunities to connect with industry, interact with experts in the field, and undertake work placements within organisations engaged in international business.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Develop practical strategies to lead and manage individuals and teams across cultures and international organisational contexts.
**2.**
Apply advanced business analysis to develop innovative, evidence-based solutions to structural and strategic challenges in cross-border operations.
**3.**
Apply ethical and strategic judgment to decision-making in dynamic international business environments.
**4.**
Evaluate how international businesses  are influenced by, and contribute to, the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
**5.**
Critically analyse international business theories and concepts to address complex international business challenges.
**6.**
Demonstrate advanced cross-cultural competence and effectively communicate international business insights to diverse audiences in a professional manner.

### Course features
### Work based learning (placement) requirements
During this course, you will have the opportunity to participate in a Work Based Learning (WBL) placement experience, designed to allow you to extend your formal academic learning beyond the classroom. The WBL experience will provide the opportunity to bring your learning from the university into a work environment and test out your knowledge in a professional real-world environment. Placements are expected to be self-sourced by students and be related to their studies. Subject to approval from the University, placements can be undertaken within an organisation, remotely, or as a virtual experience.
### Work integrated learning opportunities
Industry presentations, Industry informed curriculum, Industry-informed projects, Career development learning, Field trips

Subject: INTERNATIONAL TRADE (IBU5IBE)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In an increasingly globalised economy, trade and investment decisions made by any country can have significant repercussions for businesses worldwide. Public debates frequently arise concerning this ever-evolving economic landscape, and we must be prepared to engage actively in these changes. Central to these debates is the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 8, which aims to promote decent work and economic growth. By examining the global economy through this lens, we can better understand how international trade may foster economic growth that benefits everyone. In this subject, we will analyse the complex process of globalisation and investigate the economic factors that shape the environment in which businesses operate and nations interact. We will examine the economic foundations behind the trade and investment decisions made by nations and firms. Through the analysis of international trade models and real-world cases, you will explore critical questions such as: What are the gains from free trade and foreign investment? What determines the trade patterns or investment choices of countries? Are there winners and losers in international trade? Which trade policies benefit my business or the country as a whole?
Learning Outcomes:
- Use economic reasoning to critically evaluate the consequences of international trade, investment, migration and policy interventions to countries' welfare, production specialisation and the distribution of income.
- Analyse the patterns of international trade and the flow of production factors across countries with the effective use of complex data and the creation of key indicators in international economics.
- Apply economic theories to identify and discuss the fundamental features of international trade and the movement of production factors across countries.
- Work effectively in groups to examine how countries integrate to the global economy.
