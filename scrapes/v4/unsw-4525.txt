## Description
The purpose of Honours within the Discipline of Industrial Design is to enable students who have performed well at undergraduate level in an Industrial Design or aligned program area an opportunity to undertake a substantial individual project of research and design exploration, the equivalent of an Honours thesis. Building upon the  disciplinary foundation in knowledge, literacy and skills of the Bachelors degree, the Honours year enables academic excellence, social engagement and global perspective to a level where graduates are prepared for transition to professional practice. The Honours program is also a  means of connecting undergraduate study with supervised independent research by consolidating and extending work completed in the undergraduate program and providing an academic foundation for students who may wish to a Masters by research or a PhD program.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

2. Undertake ongoing enquiry and learning, demonstrating judgment in planning methods of inquiry and applying new knowledge to complex problems in design and other fields of learning.
3. Critically engage with, synthesise and evaluate complex ideas of social and environmental importance in the exercise of professional judgment applied in product design and related areas of practice.
4. Communicate effectively within a team and with a range of audiences, making skilled use of visual, digital and written modes of communication including the transfer of precise technical information.
1. Demonstrate advanced knowledge of technical, historical and social aspects of product design at a level expected in professional practice

Subject: Capstone Research (IDES4100)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: Capstone Research focuses on design research and design research methods, in preparation for the final capstone project in the following term. You will develop research knowledge and skills, as you produce a substantive design research report based upon an agreed topic. The report, covering problem and/or opportunity identification, data gathering, synthesis and analysis of information and research results will conclude with a design brief, and proposals to guide your capstone project the following term.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse and critique professional design practice including Intellectual Property, product standards, compliance and business cases.
- Critically reflect on theoretical knowledge, ideas and design practice(s) intergrating different perspectives.
- Select and apply suitable design research methods to identify and describe design problems, target market and user needs.
- Plan, research, and write a professional Design Research report, to industry standard.

Subject: Advanced Studio (IDES4111)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: In Advanced Studio, you will engage with a real-world design brief co-developed with an external industry partner and adopt the mindset and practices of a consultant industrial designer. The course simulates the expectations, deliverables, and pace of professional practice. You will be challenged to rapidly interpret the brief, analyse the problem space, identify stakeholder needs, and communicate your reasoning with clarity and purpose. Throughout the course, you will practise active listening, critical questioning, research, and strategic reframing to define and refine design opportunities, while developing your own iterative design process that will carry forward into your Capstone Project and future professional practice. You will develop advanced industrial design capabilities across concept exploration, research, visualisation, CAD, prototyping, and narrative-based communication. Iterative feedback from academic staff and industry partners will guide the development of your work as you progressively increase the fidelity and resolution of your design response.The assessment is a single project with presentation milestones and a final consolidated project booklet communicating design outcomes.
Learning Outcomes:
- Communicate design intent, processes, and outcomes through advanced visual, verbal, and written design communication methods
- Generate and develop creative design concepts that respond effectively to identified user needs, technical constraints, and industry expectations
- Plan and execute a staged industrial design project that meets milestone requirements and progressively advances design resolution
- Evaluate design processes and outcomes using critical reflection and feedback to refine, justify, and strengthen design decisions against professional standards
- Analyse complex design briefs to identify key problem factors and define opportunities for innovation within contemporary industrial design contexts

Subject: Industrial Design Capstone Project A (IDES4113)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: Industrial Design Capstone Project A marks the beginning of the Honours capstone design project, focusing on the translation of research insights into a viable and strategically positioned design direction. Building directly on the enquiry established in Industrial Design Capstone Research, you will define the scope of your project, evaluate alternative opportunities, and develop design concepts through iterative exploration. Emphasis is placed on critical judgement, the integration of research insight, and the systematic development and evaluation of design propositions in response to contextual, technical, and user considerations. Through iterative development, modelling, and testing, you will refine your design direction and justify decisions through evidence and analysis. The course culminates in a substantiated design proposal and development plan that establishes a clear pathway for technical and material resolution in Industrial Design Capstone Project B. Upon completion, you will demonstrate the ability to translate research insight into a viable design direction and to plan and manage the execution of a substantial Honours-level design project.
Learning Outcomes:
- Evaluate design concepts through evidence-based refinement informed by research and contextual considerations.
- Translate research insights into a clearly defined industrial design opportunity and project scope appropriate to Honours-level study.
- Communicate design intent, development process, and project rationale through visual, digital, and written formats appropriate to professional practice.
- Plan the development of a substantial design project, managing its progression through strategic organisation, judgement, and readiness for final resolution.
- Generate design concepts through iterative exploration using modelling, prototyping, and appropriate development methods.

Subject: Industrial Design Capstone Project B (IDES4114)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: Industrial Design Honours Project B is the concluding phase of the Honours capstone project. Building on the design direction established in Industrial Design Honours Project A, the course focuses on advancing the project toward a resolved design outcome suitable for professional and public presentation. You will undertake detailed development and refinement of your project through modelling, prototyping, testing, fabrication, and validation activities, integrating considerations of usability, materiality, manufacturing feasibility, functionality, aesthetics, and contextual relevance. Emphasis is placed on disciplined iteration, technical resolution, and the synthesis of research insight with professional design judgement. You will be expected to demonstrate increasing independence in project management, production planning, decision-making, and delivery while generating evidence that supports and validates design decisions. The course culminates in the delivery of a resolved Honours-level design outcome supported by technical documentation, validation evidence, and exhibition-quality communication materials. Upon completion, you will have independently resolved, validated, and communicated a substantial research-informed industrial design project demonstrating advanced technical capability, professional judgement, and readiness for professional practice or higher degree research.
Learning Outcomes:
- Deliver an independent design project through effective planning, management, and production processes.
- Demonstrate advanced material, technical, and manufacturing resolution through digital modelling, prototyping, and fabrication processes.
- Advance and resolve a substantial industrial design project to an Honours-level standard.
- Communicate design processes, project outcomes, and contributions through professional and exhibition-quality outputs to diverse audiences.
- Validate design decisions through exercising contextual, ethical, and professional judgement.
