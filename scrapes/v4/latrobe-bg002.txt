## Graduate Diploma in Logistics and Supply Chain Management - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BG002
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Logistics and Supply Chain Management is a thriving field with exponential growth. Our Graduate Diploma in Logistics and Supply Chain Management course equips you with the knowledge, skills, and strategic acumen needed to tackle real-world industry challenges and leverage emerging technologies for transformative logistics and supply chain practices. This course is designed to rapidly upskill individuals in just 12 months, providing the foundations for a successful and in-demand career by offering a comprehensive understanding of end-to-end supply chains. This course focuses on three core pillars:

  * Applied Analytics and Digitalisation: You will explore the application of emerging technologies like AI, digital twins, and cloud computing to unlock valuable logistics and supply chain insights. We prioritise enhancing your practical skills in logistics and supply chain analytics, encompassing visual, predictive, and big data analytics.
  * Circular Economy and Sustainability: Learn how to integrate Circular Economy principles into logistics and supply chain management strategies, aligning with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 9: Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure, and SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.
  * Employability Skills: Develop your professional and business skills that enhance your employability.

Is this course for me?

This course is tailor-made for individuals seeking comprehensive knowledge and practical skills in logistics and supply chain management. To maximise your employability, this course incorporates various practical elements. Our course advisory committee, composed of industry professionals, along with our extensive industry connections, provide additional opportunities such as graduation programs, coaching, and mentorship throughout your studies. 

The program offers flexibility and serves as a pathway to building expertise in the logistics and supply chain field. You could exit early after your first semester with a Graduate Certificate in Logistics and Supply Chain Management. Upon successful completion of the Graduate Diploma, you will be allowed to gain advanced standing for the subjects you’ve already completed and continue your education by pursuing Master of Logistics and Supply Chain Management programs, providing a seamless upskilling journey.

### Career outcomes
The demand for skilled professionals in logistics and supply chain management is rapidly growing across various industries. Here are some potential career outcomes that await you:

Procurement, Supply Chain, and Operations Manager: As a Procurement, Supply Chain, and Operations Manager, you will have a pivotal role in overseeing end-to-end supply chain operations, including procurement, planning, inventory management, and logistics. Your strategic mindset and exceptional leadership skills will be instrumental in driving efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and customer satisfaction.

Logistics and Supply Chain Consultant: As a Logistics and Supply Chain Consultant, you will play a crucial role in analysing supply chain data, identifying bottlenecks, and designing optimised logistics strategies. Your insights and expertise will be invaluable in helping organizations streamline their operations, reduce costs, and improve overall performance.

E-commerce and Omnichannel Specialist: With the rise of e-commerce and the growing significance of omnichannel retail, your expertise in logistics and supply chain management will be highly sought after. As an E-commerce and Omnichannel Specialist, you will be responsible for designing and implementing efficient logistics networks to support online sales and deliver seamless customer experiences.

Sustainability and Circular Supply Chain Manager: In response to the increasing focus on circular economy, companies are actively seeking professionals who can integrate sustainability principles into their supply chain operations. As a Sustainability and Circular Supply Chain Manager, you will have the opportunity to develop and implement strategies that minimize carbon footprint, reduce waste, and promote ethical sourcing, making a positive impact on both the company and the environment.

Supply Chain Innovation and Technology Manager: With the rapid advancement of technologies like artificial intelligence, blockchain, and automation, organizations are eager to leverage these innovations to optimize their supply chain processes. As a Supply Chain Innovation and Technology Manager, you will be at the forefront of driving digital transformation within the supply chain, using emerging technologies to enhance operational efficiency and create a competitive advantage for the company.

Subject: INTERNATIONAL TRADE (IBU5IBE)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In an increasingly globalised economy, trade and investment decisions made by any country can have significant repercussions for businesses worldwide. Public debates frequently arise concerning this ever-evolving economic landscape, and we must be prepared to engage actively in these changes. Central to these debates is the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 8, which aims to promote decent work and economic growth. By examining the global economy through this lens, we can better understand how international trade may foster economic growth that benefits everyone. In this subject, we will analyse the complex process of globalisation and investigate the economic factors that shape the environment in which businesses operate and nations interact. We will examine the economic foundations behind the trade and investment decisions made by nations and firms. Through the analysis of international trade models and real-world cases, you will explore critical questions such as: What are the gains from free trade and foreign investment? What determines the trade patterns or investment choices of countries? Are there winners and losers in international trade? Which trade policies benefit my business or the country as a whole?
Learning Outcomes:
- Use economic reasoning to critically evaluate the consequences of international trade, investment, migration and policy interventions to countries' welfare, production specialisation and the distribution of income.
- Analyse the patterns of international trade and the flow of production factors across countries with the effective use of complex data and the creation of key indicators in international economics.
- Apply economic theories to identify and discuss the fundamental features of international trade and the movement of production factors across countries.
- Work effectively in groups to examine how countries integrate to the global economy.
