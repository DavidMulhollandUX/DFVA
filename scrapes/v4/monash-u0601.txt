## Diploma of Arts
##### U0601
##### 96 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Diploma of Arts is your first step towards completing your Monash Arts degree. The Diploma of Arts provides you with the skills you will need to progress to an arts degree, including how to work independently, think critically and communicate effectively. The flexibility of this Diploma allows you to learn about social and political issues, language and communication and how they shape and impact our world.

In Part 1, the Diploma of Arts provides the opportunity to gain broad foundational and discipline knowledge in arts and social sciences, which is extended in Part 2 with the opportunity to develop a more specialised understanding in your chosen stream that relates to your area of interest. In Part 2 you will study subjects equivalent to first-year Monash University subjects, which will enable you to progress to second year in your chosen field. The Diploma of Arts offers a Psychology stream, sociology stream or media communication stream.

In the Diploma of Arts, you can also select to undertake the Education stream, which upon completion allows you to progress to the Bachelor of Education (Honours) and Bachelor of Arts double degree. During your studies in part 2, you will complete the same number of units as a single discipline diploma. You'll complete two education studies units and 10 days of professional work placement as part of your diploma.

### Structure
The course is structured in two parts: Part 1. Foundation knowledge and Part 2. Discipline knowledge.

Part 1: Foundation Knowledge

In this part you will study eight core units related to arts and the social sciences that provide the foundational knowledge and prepare you to progress to part 2 in your chosen Diploma of Arts stream.

Part 2: Discipline Knowledge

In this part you will study equivalent first-year university units and develop your specialist knowledge in your chosen discipline stream of either Media Communication, Psychology or Sociology.  In this part you will study eight units, comprising six core units and two elective units that align with your interests and the degree you wish to progress to.

In this part you also have the opportunity to complete the Education studies stream, which allows you to develop foundational knowledge in either primary or secondary education. In the Education stream you will complete two education studies units and 10 days of professional work placement. Note: Only Sociology or Psychology disciplines in the Diploma of Arts are available if you choose this stream.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Demonstrate knowledge of technical and theoretical issues in a variety of Arts theory and technical and language skills with depth in some areas.
**2.**
Critically apply theoretical and technical skills to solve problems in relation to a range of Arts disciplines.
**3.**
Identify and communicate advice in a variety of Arts disciplines to address problems in accord with management requirements.
**4.**
Utilise technical skills to demonstrate understanding and problem solving in relation to Arts issues involving diverse stakeholders.
**5.**
Manage work priorities and coordinate the work of others in accord with parameters set by management.

### Progression to further studies
Once you have completed your Diploma with the required entry score and prerequisite requirements, you will have guaranteed entry into second year of a related undergraduate degree at Monash University.

### AHEGS
The Diploma of Arts is an undergraduate award. The course is taught in English. It requires the successful completion of studies that develop skills in arts and social sciences disciplines, including communication and problem solving skills. Students demonstrate their ability to successfully undertake study at the university level. The course takes two years of full-time study to complete. The Diploma of Arts is a Level 5 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.
