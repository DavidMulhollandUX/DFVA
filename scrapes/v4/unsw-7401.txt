## Description
The Graduate Certificate in Education degree is designed for qualified teachers and other educational professionals who wish to study education at an advanced level. Commonwealth Supported Places (previously HECS) are available for local candidates.

The program is suitable for teachers, trainers, coaches, adult educators, vocational educators, educational designers, and other professionals in educational settings.

The program offers specialisation in Educational Studies, Curriculum and Assessment, Educational Psychology, Gifted Education, Special Education and TESOL.

Note: This program does not offer professional accreditation to teach in primary or secondary schools in NSW or Australia. For pre-service teacher education programs, please see Bachelor of Education (Secondary), Master of Teaching (Primary), and Master of Teaching (Secondary).

## Progression & Academic Rules
Articulation arrangements:

The Graduate Certificate in Education forms part of a sequence of articulated programs comprising the Graduate Certificate and Masters degree of Education. A postgraduate student enrolled in an articulated program may progress from the Graduate Certificate to the Masters level with full credit for courses completed in the Graduate Certificate in Education.

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
One of the following:

- A bachelor's degree and postgraduate teaching qualification (e.g., Graduate Diploma of Education or Master of Teaching)

- A bachelor's degree or combined degree in Education, including professional experience or practicum, equivalent to the qualifications required for Graduate Teacher status in NSW or other Australian jurisdictions

- A bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification in a related field, plus one year of relevant professional experience and development

Teaching/Education Qualification: This is considered a degree or postgraduate teaching qualification in Early Childhood, Primary, Secondary, Adult or higher education and includes practicum/placement.

Accreditation: If you hold current graduate teacher accreditation with a recognised Australian professional body (e.g. NESA, BOSTES, VIC Institute of Teachers) you must provide evidence of your accreditation record. Please include accreditation details when uploading your CV.

Related fields: Arts, humanities, media, psychology, social work, business, economics, languages, linguistics, communications, policy studies, behavioural sciences, social sciences.

Relevant professional experience: Teacher, educational developer, languages teacher, lecturer, teacher of English to speakers of other languages, educational leader, educational administration. Relevant professional experience does not include periods of Practicum or Work Placement (Work Integrated Learning) undertaken when training to become a teacher.

The following additional entry requirements apply to those students who wish to study the Educational Research specialisation (i.e., EDSTCT) in the Graduate Certificate in Education:

- The achievement of at least a WAM of 75 (or equivalent) in the most recently completed degree; AND

- Successful performance in an interview where questions will be asked about the student's interest in, and commitment to, studies in research; AND

- The availability of a suitable supervisor

Subject: Academic Writing for the Humanities (ARTS5503)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course will assist you to develop your capacity to write academic essays according to the conventions of the Humanities. It provides the knowledge and vocabulary by which you can critically analyse the work of others as well as your own. The course focuses sustained attention on various modes of academic writing that are used in essay writing and shows how these may be deployed to present a strong argument. The course includes analysis of a range of exemplars as well as essays from academic journals researched by students and related to their disciplinary interests. This study will be accompanied by writing exercises, writing workshops and detailed feedback from both peers and staff. The course is offered as a Prescribed Elective in the Master of Journalism and Communication and the Master of Public Relations and Advertising. Students enrolled in other Postgraduate Coursework Programs in UNSW Arts and Social Sciences, may take the course as an additional elective, but it will not contribute credit toward those programs.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate facility with grammar, sentence construction and conceptual exposition, associated with expectations of academic writing in the humanities.
- Structure, edit, and present a piece of writing according to conventions of academic writing in the humanities, and in conjunction with inquiry based/research based learning.
- Apply the primary modes of academic writing in the humanities, including exposition, description, narrative and argumentation.

Subject: Personalised English Language Enhancement (ARTS5505)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Personalised English Language Enhancement (PELE) is a self-directed learning course designed to help you enhance your communication skills in English. In this course, you will be guided to develop a personal project that meets your needs and implement it in a warm and safe environment being supported not only by the teachers but also by peer mentors. In addition, you will learn a number of important skills needed for life-long learning, including reflective thinking and time management.
Learning Outcomes:
- Communicate critical personal learning insights and outcomes with peers
- Develop a personal project tailored to individual linguistic needs, utilising tools and resources for language enhancement supported by relevant literature
- Critically analyse and identify specific linguistic areas that need improvement within a professional and academic context
- Critically evaluate personal and professional development through reflective practice, demonstrating advanced critical thinking and self-directed learning
- Demonstrate advanced communication skills by implementing a sustainable personal project that enhances linguistic proficiency in a professional or academic context

Subject: TESOL Advanced Professional Practice (EDST5325)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: In this course you are required to work in the school system, with the goal of working towards 25 days of school-based professional practice. The coursework and assessments are linked to the teaching experiences, observation, critical self-reflection, and peer review you will be engaged in while working in schools. The course supports graduates of the program in working towards the achievement of some of the national standards for a proficient teacher. EDST5325 TESOL Advanced Professional Practice, is a practicum class and can only be done face-to-face.
Learning Outcomes:
- Plan, implement and critically evaluate challenging and original lessons and units of work using a range of different strategies to meet varied TESOL student needs and interests
- Reflect on, describe and critically evaluate one’s own professional practice and take responsibility in collaboration with others to identify and address their own learning needs
- Design and critically evaluate appropriate programs, including needs analysis, syllabus design, program organisation and processes of assessment and evaluation in a TESOL environment
