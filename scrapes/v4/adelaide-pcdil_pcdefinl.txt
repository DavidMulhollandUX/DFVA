The Professional Certificate in Defence Industry Leadership is designed to provide students with an understanding of the complexity in the defence industry and the ability to apply adaptive leadership skills in a range of complex environments
The Professional Certificate in Defence Industry Leadership is a non-AQF program. This program has a standard full-time duration of 0.25 years however can only be taken part-time for a duration of 0.5 years.

### MANAGEMT7150 - Defence Industry Leadership I: Leading Oneself
Curriculum unit MANAGEMT7150: Defence Industry Leadership I: Leading Oneself covering fundamental principles, professional methodologies, and critical evaluation at University of Adelaide.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate comprehensive understanding of key principles in defence industry leadership i: leading oneself.
- Apply analytical frameworks and professional methods in defence industry leadership i: leading oneself.
- Communicate findings and technical arguments effectively in professional contexts.
Assessment Tasks:
- Coursework Project: 50%
- Examination: 50%
