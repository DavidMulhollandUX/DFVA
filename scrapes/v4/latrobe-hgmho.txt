### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Utilise skills in problem solving, planning and evaluation to inform clinical practice
**2.**
Apply advanced knowledge and skills to make informed clinical judgements in mental health practice
**3.**
Perform independently and as an influential team member in a variety of situations within mental health settings in accordance with professional ethical standards
**4.**
Communicate effectively and sensitively about mental health issues with colleagues, people accessing mental health services and the public in professional and social contexts in accordance with professional ethical standards
**5.**
Undertake effective reflective practice, both independently and as a team member in multidisciplinary work environments
**6.**
Make accurate assessments and informed judgements that contribute to the development of reasonable and justifiable clinical formulations and plans
**7.**
Recognise ongoing learning needs and use effective learning strategies for professional practice

Subject: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY MODULE (LTU0AIM)
Credit Points: 0
Overview: The academic integrity module introduces academic integrity standards to inform and support students in avoiding plagiarism and academic misconduct. Students complete four parts that encompass academic misconduct and academic integrity decisions, including cheating, plagiarism, and collusion. Students learn about Turnitin (the text-matching tool) used at La Trobe University, and how to get help and support their development of referencing skills.
Learning Outcomes:
- Understand the importance of the acknowledgment and the referencing of sources
- Locate, select, and utilise information about La Trobe's approach to academic integrity to support appropriate acknowledgement of references used.
- Recognise and understand academic integrity responsibilities.

Subject: COMPREHENSIVE MENTAL HEALTH ASSESSMENT (MMH4CMA)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will develop fundamental knowledge and skills required in clinical mental health practice. Students will develop comprehensive assessment and management skills and recognise the interrelationship between a person's medical, social and psychological health. This subject provides a theoretical and practical foundation that prepares students for ethically oriented professional practice. This subject enables students to develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills, and specialised clinical proficiency. Using independent judgment, you will plan, implement and evaluate practice in mental health contexts.
Learning Outcomes:
- Develop and evaluate learning strategies that are responsive to reflective practice.
- Perform and evaluate clinical assessments appropriate for mental health care.
- Critically analyse information to enable informed clinical decision making based to solve problems.
- Develop and evaluate evidence-based nursing plans that support therapeutic relationships with mental health consumers and their families and carers that are characterized by co-constructive care and collaborative engagement.
Workload: 

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH4EPM =====
Subject: EXPERIENCES AND PERCEPTIONS OF MENTAL ILLNESS (MMH4EPM)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, students will explore the complexities that exercising choice and control poses for people with mental illness and develop knowledge and skills such as supported decision making and recovery orientated practice. It will also explore the challenges and strategies for ensuring the collective voices of people with mental illness are heard about systemic issues.
Learning Outcomes:
- Develop and evaluate evidence-based interventions that support therapeutic relationships with mental health consumers and their families and carers that are characterised by co-constructive care and collaborative engagement.
- Practice professional person centered care and advocacy independently as a team member in mental health and educational settings in accordance with professional ethical standards.
- Develop and evaluate learning strategies that are responsive to reflective practice.
- Critically evaluate models of care to achieve personalized care strategies that meet the life and health needs of mental health consumers.
Workload: 

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH4MHD =====
Subject: MENTAL HEALTH DIAGNOSIS (MMH4MHD)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will critically examine psychopathology, mental health diagnosis and associated co-morbidities. This subject enables students to develop critical thinking and problem solving skills to be clinically knowledgeable in assessment and diagnosis of mental illness. The Diagnostic and Statistical Manual (DSM)and International Classification of Diseases (ICD) classification systems are critically evaluated.
Learning Outcomes:
- Consolidate and synthesise knowledge of mental health diagnosis to understand symptoms and psychopathology.
- Critically evaluate and apply available evidence to inform best practice in mental health.
- Make accurate assessments and informed judgements to develop reasonable and justifiable clinical formulations and plans.
- Recognise ongoing learning needs and use effective learning strategies for professional practice.
Workload:

Subject: THERAPEUTIC SKILLS (MMH4THK)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will extend their knowledge and application of therapeutic skills to make high level, independent clinical decisions. Students will analyse and consolidate complex communication skills required in specialist mental health practice. Students will combine interpersonal skills with evidence-based therapies to attain specialist skills required for therapeutic interventions relevant to their professional practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Generate effective communication skills to effectively and sensitively work with colleagues, people accessing mental health services and the public in accordance with professional ethical standards.
- Critically analyse information to enable informed decision making aimed to solve problems.
- Practice professional person centered care and advocacy independently and with mental health team members in accordance with professional ethical standards.
- Apply and evaluate knowledge and skills to collaborate in complex clinical practice outcomes in mental health care.
Workload: 

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH5AMM =====
Subject: ADVANCED MENTAL HEALTH PRACTICE (MMH5AMM)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will build on their advanced knowledge and skills in mental health. The subject emphasises advanced and specialist practice in mental health. Students will develop cognitive and technical skills that demonstrate a broad understanding of underlying principles and theoretical concepts in mental health practice, and an advanced understanding of mental health conditions and treatment planning in the context of recovery orientated practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Perform your advanced practice in a safe and ethical manner using the required specific skills, knowledge and attitudes.
- Utilise analytical, problem solving, planning & evaluation skills to inform your advanced practice.
- Critically reflect on your current practice and determine how to re-frame this practice to meet mental health practice requirements.
- Make accurate assessments & develop reasonable and justifiable inferences in complex scenarios.
- Critically evaluate available evidence to inform your advanced practice.
Workload: 

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH5CTR =====
Subject: CONSIDERATIONS IN TREATMENT, RECOVERY AND SERVICE DELIVERY (MMH5CTR)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will analyse the nature and extent of social, political, economic and organisational influences on mental health care. Students will engage in collaborative critical reflection on mental health practice and address constraints in negotiation of options for mental health care. Students will demonstrate the role of the mental health professional in a multidisciplinary and multi-professional approach to treatment and recovery.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically evaluate models of care relevant to the care continuum, to inform co-constructed personalised care strategies which meet the consumer’s life needs as well as health needs.
- Demonstrate the role of the mental health professional in a multidisciplinary and multi-professional approach to treatment and recovery.
- Critically appraise the nature and extent of influences on mental health to address constraints in negotiation of options for mental health care.
- Engage in collaborative critical reflection on mental health practice.
Workload: 

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH5TAP =====
Subject: THERAPEUTIC PHARMACOLOGY (MMH5TAP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, students will develop knowledge and skills to identify the effects of medications on physiological, biochemical and pathophysiological body systems. Students will apply systematic clinical assessment and evidence based critical thinking skills to justify rationales for the initiation/instigation of therapeutic medications and treatment plans.
Learning Outcomes:
- Expand your knowledge and use of psycho-pharmacology, including medication management, for common mental health and illness issues.
- Apply scientific judgement and contemporary evidence from a range of sources to inform and support clinical decision making in the management and delivery pharmacological interventions.
- Demonstrate evidence of collaborative care and communicates treatment plan clearly to patients, families and other health professionals.
- Describe principles of pharmacology, including the differences between pharmacodynamics and pharmacokinetics, the manner in which medications are absorbed, distributed, metabolised and excreted in the body.
Workload: 

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH5TBT =====
Subject: TALK BASED THERAPIES (MMH5TBT)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will critically examine theoretical frameworks for conducting talk based therapy sessions using a problem-solving approach to working with people in the context of mental health care. Students will gain enhanced knowledge and skills required to undertake therapy with a specific focus on advanced communication and language.
Learning Outcomes:
- Extend the range of psycho-therapeutic interventions applied to recovery in different situations and contexts of care.
- Describe overall formulations of individuals with mental health related issues, leading to appropriate interventions.
- Extend the knowledge and use of talk-based therapies for common mental health and illness issues.
- Engage creatively in therapeutic relationships with mental health consumers, their families/carers and significant others, which are characterized by co-construction of care and collaborative engagement.
