## Career Opportunities & Employment Outcomes
Graduates obtain employment in a range of fields depending on areas of specialisation and interest, including international consulting, infrastructure development and planning, environmental management, international finance, investment and trade, natural resource and agricultural economics, public health, treasury, actuarial science and statistics.

Subject: ECON6101 - Microeconomics for Honours
### ECON6101 - Microeconomics for Honours
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply the preference model of economic behavior to various situations including consumer and producer behavior in market economies.
- Model various market situations and derive analytic results from a set of assumptions.
- Apply and derive standard results based on the competitive and general equilibrium models of economic interaction.
- Conduct welfare analyses and prescribe potential improvements when welfare is determined to be sub-optimal.
Assessment Tasks:
- Problem Set 1: 10%%
- In-Semester Exam Identity Verified In-person: 35%%
- Problem Set 2: 10%%
- Final Exam Identity Verified In-person: 45%%

Subject: ECON6102 - Macroeconomics for Honours
### ECON6102 - Macroeconomics for Honours
Learning Outcomes:
- Interpret theoretical building blocks of modern macroeconomic models.
- Apply macroeconomic frameworks to analyse real world problems.
- Evaluate the effects and desirability of macroeconomic policies.
- Apply computational methods to solve dynamic optimisation problems in macroeconomics.
Assessment Tasks:
- Problem Set 1 Identity Verified Team or group-based: 15%%
- Problem Set 2 Identity Verified Team or group-based: 15%%
- In-Semester Exam Identity Verified In-person: 30%%
- End of Semester Exam Identity Verified In-person: 40%%

### ECON6201 - Honours Research Thesis
Curriculum unit ECON6201: Honours Research Thesis covering fundamental principles, professional methodologies, and critical evaluation at The University of Queensland.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate comprehensive understanding of key principles in honours research thesis.
- Apply analytical frameworks and professional methods in honours research thesis.
- Communicate findings and technical arguments effectively in professional contexts.
