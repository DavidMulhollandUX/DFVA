The Professional Certificate in Clinical Education is designed for health professionals supervising students in clinical settings.
You'll learn how to effectively supervise student placements and develop skills that are valuable for clinicians looking to take on leadership roles, transition into formal clinical educator positions, or improve their teaching effectiveness in day-to-day practice.
Balance work and study commitments with high-quality and flexible online and part-time study, with scheduled check-ins to support your learning journey.
When you study La Trobe's Professional Certificate in Clinical Education, you'll:
  * build skills in mentoring, supervision, facilitation, and clinical teaching
  * gain valuable skills for career progression into educator or leadership roles in clinical settings
  * learn practical teaching skills and evidence-based learning methods for supervision in clinical settings.

Subject: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY MODULE (LTU0AIM)
Credit Points: 0
Overview: The academic integrity module introduces academic integrity standards to inform and support students in avoiding plagiarism and academic misconduct. Students complete four parts that encompass academic misconduct and academic integrity decisions, including cheating, plagiarism, and collusion. Students learn about Turnitin (the text-matching tool) used at La Trobe University, and how to get help and support their development of referencing skills.
Learning Outcomes:
- Understand the importance of the acknowledgment and the referencing of sources
- Locate, select, and utilise information about La Trobe's approach to academic integrity to support appropriate acknowledgement of references used.
- Recognise and understand academic integrity responsibilities.

Subject: THEORETICAL UNDERSTANDING IN CLINICAL TEACHING AND LEARNING (LTU4005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will explore educational theories underpinning human learning, and their application to clinical teaching and learning. You will consider processes by which theoretical learning is applied in practical situations along with the impact of diversity on clinical learning. In addition, you will analyse factors that can influence learning in practice-based settings. You will learn how to use established principles of cognitive science and contemporary behavioural psychology to design clinical learning experiences for maximum impact.
Learning Outcomes:
- Differentiate scientifically-aligned educational theories and practices related to clinical education
- Utilise evidence-based approaches to enhance clinical education practices
- Recognise impacts of diversity on clinical learning
- Apply educational theories to enhance clinical learning and teaching

Subject: EFFECTIVE CLINICAL TEACHING AND EDUCATION SKILLS (LTU4006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will delve deeper into the nuances of clinical learning and teaching and develop your skills as a clinical educator. You will examine theories and practices aimed at provision of effective clinical teaching and learning, including feedback and assessment, and consider strategies to support learning in practice for diverse learners.
Learning Outcomes:
- Deliver constructive and effective feedback on clinical learning
- Facilitate the delivery of high-quality clinical learning outcomes
- Develop strategies for managing complex situations in clinical education
- Apply evidence-based approaches to analysing, designing and evaluating clinical learning experiences
