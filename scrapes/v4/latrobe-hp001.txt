## Professional Certificate in Sports and Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy Practice - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### HP001
##### 30 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Professional Certificate in Sports and Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy Practice is designed for early-career and established physiotherapists aiming to enhance their clinical assessment and management of musculoskeletal and sports-related conditions.

This certificate offers a focused, advanced study of upper body, lower body and spinal presentations. Delivered in a flexible blended format complemented by two intensive on-campus weeks, this course supports the development of advanced clinical reasoning, practical skills, and evidence-informed practice.

You'll build your skills in functional assessment, differential diagnosis, manual therapy, and exercise-based rehabilitation, with direct relevance to contemporary physiotherapy practice in sports, exercise, and general musculoskeletal settings. The certificate can be taken as a standalone qualification or used as a pathway into the Master of Sports and Exercise Physiotherapy or Master of Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy courses.

You'll learn:

  * advanced biomedical and clinical knowledge with specialised clinical reasoning across musculoskeletal and sports physiotherapy settings.
  * how to investigate, analyse and synthesise complex information to address diverse clinical presentations in musculoskeletal and sports physiotherapy practice.
  * valuable skills for career progression and to meet professional development expectations.

**2.**
Deliver client-centered management, integrating advanced biomedical and clinical knowledge with specialised clinical reasoning across musculoskeletal and sports physiotherapy settings.

Apply clinical reasoning to assess and manage complex musculoskeletal and sports-related conditions.

Investigate, analyse and synthesise complex information to address diverse clinical presentations in musculoskeletal and sports physiotherapy practice.

### Course features
### Career outcomes
The course provides early-career physiotherapists with a structured, academically credible opportunity to enhance their clinical capability, develop advanced clinical skills and reasoning, and demonstrate commitment to ongoing professional development – better positioning graduates to pursue career pathways in musculoskeletal or sports and exercise physiotherapy.

The course also serves as a direct pathway to the Master of Sports and Exercise Physiotherapy or the Master of Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy, offering students a valuable headstart into further postgraduate study.

Subject: ADVANCED PHYSIOTHERAPY FOR THE UPPER BODY (PTY5025)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this blended subject, students will gain advanced knowledge and clinical skills in musculoskeletal and sports and exercise physiotherapy, with a focus on the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine. The subject begins with an online clinical reasoning module designed to enhance decision-making and problem-solving of complex cases. This is followed by an online module to enhance diagnostic imaging interpretation for upper limb and cervical presentations. Practical components include applied clinical anatomy wet labs and advanced physiotherapy assessment and management of upper limb, cervical and thoracic conditions. This subject prepares qualified physiotherapists to integrate advanced assessment and management techniques into clinical practice. This subject includes professional learning, practical experiences or portfolio activities which contribute to the completion of the Silver Career Ready AdvantageTM award .
Learning Outcomes:
- Integrate contemporary, evidence-informed practices into clinical scenarios involving the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine, demonstrating the ability to adapt and apply advanced physiotherapy techniques in a variety of clinical settings.
- Apply advanced clinical reasoning skills to develop and implement comprehensive assessment and management strategies for complex upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine conditions in musculoskeletal and sports physiotherapy
- Apply advanced theoretical knowledge and practical skills to critically analyse and address complex neuromusculoskeletal presentations of the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine, with a focus on optimising patient outcomes
- Proficiently interpret diagnostic imaging and integrate applied clinical anatomy to inform evidence-based decision-making in patient care for conditions of the upper limb, cervical and thoracic spine
- Critically apply self-reflection to identify strengths and areas for improvement in the assessment and management of upper limb, cervical, and thoracic conditions, fostering ongoing professional growth as an effective lifelong learner.
