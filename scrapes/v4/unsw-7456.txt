## Description
Business analytics enables organisations to make data driven decisions. This Graduate Certificate will provide students with foundational knowledge in business analytics and technical skills required to generate and communicate actionable insights from data. Students will be introduced to conceptual frameworks, modelling techniques, database management, and ethical debates to provide a holistic view of business analytics.

The Graduate Certificate in Business Analytics is a pathway program for professionals who wish to obtain a fundamental grounding in business analytics with the view to articulating to either the Master of Commerce or the Master of Commerce (Extension) programs.

This program consists of two core courses and two electives relevant to the Business Analytics specialisation in the Master of Commerce. On successful completion of these courses (minimum credit average) students may have the opportunity to articulate into the Master of Commerce or Master of Commerce (Extension) degree program.

## Progression & Academic Rules
Upon successful completion of the four courses in the Graduate Certificate in Business Analytics, (with a minimum achievement of 65 overall) you may articulate into the Master of Commerce or Master of Commerce (Extension).To articulate into the Master of Commerce or Master of Commerce (Extension) you are required to complete an internal transfer request form to seek approval.

Subject: Introduction to Business Analytics (INFS5700)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: In today's data-driven world, businesses rely on analytics to drive strategic decision-making and gain a competitive advantage. This course provides students with a foundational understanding of how data can be leveraged to support management decisions and enhance business performance. Students will explore key business analytics frameworks, techniques, and tools used to collect, analyse, and manage data. The course offers hands-on experience in examining datasets using visual analytics tools, including Power BI and SAS Visual Analytics, to develop dashboards and create data-driven reports that effectively communicate insights. Additionally, the course delves into the challenges and organizational impacts of building analytical capabilities, emphasizing critical success factors for implementing business analytics solutions. Students will also examine the ethical implications of big data and analytics, with a focus on responsible business practices. Through interactive discussions and practical applications, they will develop the skills to collaborate with analytic teams and effectively communicate findings to stakeholders using compelling visualizations. By the end of the course, students will be equipped to critically assess the role of data in business, apply analytical techniques to real-world problems, and make informed, data-driven decisions.
Learning Outcomes:
- Investigate the challenges, critical factors and organisational impacts associated with being business analytically capable.
- Analyse the ethical impact of big data and analytics on responsible business practices.
- Discuss and evaluate Business Analytics framework, techniques and tools used in gathering, analysing and managing data and apply them to enhance decision making.
- Effectively utilise visualisations to communicate with analytic team members and influence stakeholder.
- Examine datasets using visual analytic techniques and communicate findings using dashboards and data driven visual reports.
- Critically evaluate the role of data in supporting management decision-making and gaining competitive advantage
- Research the emerging and global trends of business analytics tools and practices in industry.

Subject: Information Technology Infrastructure for Business Analytics (INFS5710)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Performing business analytics functions on huge volumes of data generated from various day-to-day operations requires a solid understanding of how data captured at the operational level can be re-structured and used at the enterprise level to gain maximum effective results. This course will provide you with data management knowledge in both operational and analytical database systems and infrastructure aspects, including theoretical foundations as well as meaningful hands-on experience. Examples include: standard relational database issues; analytical database topics; requirements imposed by the move to big data; business analytics infrastructure and tools used with big data. SAS software tools will be used for the skills components of the course: “SAS SQL 1: Essentials” and “Introduction to SAS and Hadoop”
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and assess the value of relational and non-relational database infrastructure.
- Evaluate and propose relevant and feasible database infrastructure solutions for specific business analytic problems.
- Apply conceptual database modelling techniques (e.g., ER modelling) and normalisation techniques.
- Analyse advanced issues in (both relational and non-relational) database design and management.
- Build and utilise database solutions to support big data analytics.

Subject: Business Analytics Methods (INFS5720)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: The proliferation of digital platforms, IoT devices, and online transactions has led to an explosion of data. In today's data-driven world, organisations are increasingly leveraging advanced analytics to extract actionable insights from this ever-growing volume of information. Predictive analytics play a vital role in helping organisations anticipate outcomes, optimise decisions, and improve business performance. This course provides a structured and conceptual foundation for predictive analytics, encompassing both forecasting and prediction techniques. It begins with the principles of data-driven inquiry—how to formulate hypotheses, design analytical approaches, and evaluate evidence. Students will learn forecasting methods such as regression and time series analysis to interpret trends and project business outcomes. The course then extends to prediction methods from supervised learning, exploring how modern machine learning algorithms can enhance decision making across marketing, finance, operations, and other business domains. While technical competence is essential, the emphasis of this course is on business interpretation and strategic application—understanding what models reveal, when they are appropriate, and how their insights can guide managerial actions. The goal is not only to build accurate models, but also to translate analytical results into business value and informed decision-making. You will apply these methods using Python, a versatile programming language with extensive libraries for data wrangling, analysis, and visualisation. Through hands-on exercises and business case studies, you will develop both technical proficiency and analytical reasoning skills essential for effective predictive analytics practice in business contexts.
Learning Outcomes:
- Predict future outcomes based on historical data through the application of analytics programming language tools.
- Examine the ethical implications of data analytics and communication and their impacts on business and society.
- Apply analytic techniques to solve problems and identify opportunities for growth in the business context.
- Compose a narrative that illustrates and solves a business problem, and present it convincingly with the aid of visual analytics.
- Collaborate with team members to achieve team objectives.

Subject: Social Media Analytics in Practice  (INFS5730)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Social media and advanced Web technologies, including generative AI tools (e.g. image recognition systems, automated audio-editors, and natural language generation software) have remarkably spurred the rise of digitally empowered consumers, challenging businesses to thrive on social media. In this digital era, consumers have gained a powerful voice, taking over online conversations by generating multimodal content across social media platforms (images, videos, audio, text, and emotional icons). Businesses striving to stay ahead of the competition leverage unstructured social media data to design and implement successful social media strategies. In this course, we equip students with the necessary knowledge and competencies to collect and analyse unstructured social media data using recent technologies such as R, SAS Viya for text analytics, Social Network Analysis, Microsoft Azure AI Services and Google Vision AI. Workshops are practical and designed to engage students in extensive hands-on activities and challenging case studies to develop their critical thinking and analytical skills to address real-life business problems. Upon successful completion of the course, students are expected to demonstrate both a professional perspective as well as technological confidence in social media analytics and its implications for the business context.
Learning Outcomes:
- Consolidate analytical and critical thinking skills to solve real-life business problems helping companies thrive on social media.
- Analyse strategic issues related to social media use to solve business problems.
- Develop collaborative teamwork skills for solving AI-driven business problems.
- Discuss ethical implications aligned with SDGs (Sustainable Development Goals) principles/objectives when conducting social media analytics and articulate their impact on individuals, organisations and society at large.
- Apply analytical techniques to analyse unstructured social media data to derive actionable insights/recommendations to address business challenges.
