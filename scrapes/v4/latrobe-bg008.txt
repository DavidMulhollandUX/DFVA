## Graduate Diploma in Business Information Systems - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BG008
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
If you're ready to deepen your digital skillset and progress your business career, La Trobe’s Graduate Diploma in Business Information Systems provides the advanced tools and practical knowledge to help you thrive in this rapidly evolving field.

Go beyond the basics to develop critical skills in AI, data analytics, cybersecurity, and modern information systems. You’ll explore how organisations are transforming through technology, and learn to design and manage business processes and systems that address complex, real-world problems. Dive into data wrangling, storytelling with data, and business intelligence to convert raw data into meaningful insights that drive strategic decision-making.

Designed in collaboration with industry leaders, the course combines academic insight with practical, hands-on learning. You’ll gain experience using leading analytics and AI tools through team-based projects that reflect real business environments.

Our expert teaching team brings a blend of research excellence and industry experience in designing innovative digital and AI-driven solutions. Throughout your studies, you’ll engage with real industry projects, collaborating with professionals to solve practical challenges.

On successful completion of the Graduate Diploma, you may be eligible for up to one year of advanced standing toward La Trobe’s Master of Business Information Systems, allowing you to fast-track your studies and broaden your career opportunities.

You’ll learn:

  * 
Modern Information Systems: professional knowledge and skills in enterprise information systems for addressing real-world business problems

  * 
Analytics and AI: Analytics and AI capabilities that drive business decisions and improve organisational productivity

  * 
Business Innovation: Innovations and applications of information systems and data-driven decision-making in diverse business settings

  * 
ICT Ethics and Cybersecurity: knowledge of local and international regulatory frameworks and guidelines for addressing enterprise ethics and cybersecurity challenges

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Apply core business information systems knowledge to address organisational challenges in data, information, processes, people and governance.
**2.**
Analyse and interpret business datasets using data transformation and data management in diverse industry settings.
**3.**
Assess cybersecurity, data ethics and data privacy in the design and use of information systems.
**4.**
Apply and evaluate analytics techniques to generate actionable insights and support data-driven decision-making.
**5.**
Build and compare AI models and strategies to address business problems in diverse and complex contexts.

Subject: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR BUSINESS (BUS4005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the nature and function of business. However, business leaders are challenged by the effective and competitive adoption of AI. This subject provides you with an industry-ready skillset to empower productivity, innovation and decision-making across the business ecosystem. You will learn to apply prompting techniques, build and evaluate AI chatbots, construct and articulate AI strategies. Alongside AI capabilities, you will recognise and address the risks of AI, ethics and cybersecurity through a deep understanding of local and international guidelines, regulations and governance. You will foster an AI-first mindset for the effective and responsible practice of AI in any business or industry environment.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply and critically evaluate prompting techniques and AI tools in diverse business contexts
- Analyse and critically evaluate AI strategies and policies to support responsible adoption in varied business environments
- Design and develop in teams, AI chatbot solutions that address complex business challenges, demonstrating alignment with ethical principles, governance frameworks, and cybersecurity standards.

Subject: CYBERSECURITY FOR BUSINESS (BUS4006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Cybersecurity for Business provides a comprehensive overview of cybersecurity principles, practices, and frameworks for managing cybersecurity risks in organisations. Cybersecurity plays a critical role in an organisation's operations by safeguarding its assets, ensuring regulatory compliance, and enhancing overall business resilience. The subject covers key topics, including cybersecurity threats, attacks, governance frameworks, cybersecurity risk management, cybersecurity policies and procedures, and compliance. You will explore cybersecurity for businesses through a combination of tailor-made case studies and hands-on workshops, offering a comprehensive understanding of theoretical principles alongside practical application in real-world scenarios. It develops the skills necessary to craft effective cybersecurity strategies that align with organisational goals and regulatory requirements. The course will also examine the ethical, legal, and regulatory dimensions of cybersecurity.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse different types of cyber threats, attack methods, and AI-driven threats, to assess their potential impact on business operations, risk management, and compliance obligations.
- Design and justify ethical and governance-aligned cybersecurity strategies using appropriate frameworks, AI-enabled tools, and security models to strengthen threat detection, incident response, resilience, and regulatory compliance.
- Identify and apply fundamental cybersecurity principles to recognise and evaluate common organisational security challenges, including those arising from emerging technologies and AI.
- Communicate cybersecurity governance strategies and AI-informed risk mitigation plans clearly and appropriately to diverse technical and non-technical stakeholders in professional business contexts.

Subject: DATA WRANGLING (BUS5DWR)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject introduces you to the various techniques of data wrangling with a strong focus on hands-on experience in programming using Structured Query Language (SQL) and the Python programming language. It will cover the basic concepts in relational database design including Entity Relationship (ER) modelling and SQL as a tool for basic data wrangling. You will also learn various types of data sources and common data formats. The subject equips you with skills in data wrangling, including data import and export, basic data integration, assessment and transformation. Upon completion, you will be able to perform a variety of data wrangling tasks using SQL and a programming language for different kinds of data types.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and implement database models that meet business requirements using advanced data modelling and querying techniques to enable analytics initiatives.
- Analyse data by developing SQL queries and data wrangling scripts using programming tools with responsible use of generative AI to support data transformation and insight generation for data-driven business decisions.
- Evaluate and compare data sources, formats, and structures to prepare and transform data for analysis, considering ethical, legal, and cybersecurity risks in business analytics.
- Formulate data and data-wrangling requirements that address the technical challenges of analytics tasks, incorporating data governance, ethical principles, and cybersecurity constraints.
