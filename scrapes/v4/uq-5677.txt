## Prerequisites
A postgraduate qualification in a relevant clinical field.

The Master of Nurse Practitioner is specifically designed to prepare experienced registered nurses to function autonomously and collaboratively in advanced and expanded clinical roles. As a nurse practitioner you will provide innovative and flexible healthcare delivery that complements other healthcare providers. You will receive extensive clinical and theoretical knowledge, which will equip you with the specialist skills required for the expanded role as a nurse practitioner. You will develop skills in assessment and management of clients that may include but is not limited to the direct referral of patients to other healthcare professionals, prescribing medications and other treatment modalities including ordering diagnostic investigations. The nurse practitioner role is grounded in the nursing profession's values, knowledge, theories and practice and provides innovative and flexible healthcare delivery that complements those provided by other healthcare providers. The scope of practice of the nurse practitioner is determined by the context in which the nurse practitioner is authorised to practice.

Subject: Advancing Research Inquiry (HLTH7315)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This course assists students to develop expert search strategies and advanced analytical and critical thinking skills to engage with scholarly literature. The course aims to enable students to source literature appropriate to their research field and to identify key themes, concepts, debates, gaps, and limitations within the existing body of knowledge. Through this process, students will be able to generate meaningful and clinically relevant research questions that contribute to the advancement of their field of study.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate advanced analytical and critical thinking skills to identify key themes, concepts, debates, gaps and limitations in the literature to generate a meaningful and clinically relevant research question.
- Demonstrate the ability to determine how to identify and source appropriate literature suitable to informing a research question.
- Demonstrate advanced communication skills to articulate complex ideas clearly, structure arguments logically, and use appropriate academic conventions and referencing styles.
- Analyse and critically reflect on literature to demonstrate a deep understanding of the selected research topic by critically reviewing and analysing relevant literature from multiple sources, including scholarly articles, books, and other academic resources.
Assessment Tasks:
- Library Search Assessment: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
- Research Question Assessment Identity Verified Online: 60% This is a sequenced assessment. Part A carries a 30% weighting and Part B carries a 30% weighting to total the 60%.% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Lecture 1 Hour/ Week Problem Based Learning 1 Hour/ Week

Subject: Applied Physiology and Pathophysiology (NURS7242)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This course expands the knowledge of advanced physiology and pathophysiology for the student. Development of an enhanced foundation enables the depth of critique and differentiation of findings required as a Nurse Practitioner to inform clinical decision making. In addition, this course introduces and builds on the development of independent practice, through the use of a structured and systemic approach to clinical assessment, and the formation of holistic and validated management plans. Learning outcomes emphasis decision making based on clinical assessment, evidenced based practice, and patient and culturally specific outcomes.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate an advanced knowledge of physiology and pathophysiology which enables the Nurse Practitioner to critical appraise the findings of clinical assessment.
- Critically apply an advanced physiology and pathophysiological knowledge to order and interpret appropriate investigations as required and inform critical decision-making processes.
- Apply at an advanced level, an autonomous clinical decision-making process using evidence informed and person-centred options to ensure appropriate differential diagnosis and safe patient outcomes.
- Utilise the NP domains of research, leadership and education to apply informed clinical decision-making processes that are culturally safe, inclusive and reflective of the Nurse Practitioner standards of practice.
Assessment Tasks:
- Case study group work Hurdle Team or group-based: 10%% (Hurdle: True)
- Case study: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
- Full episode of care Hurdle: 50%% (Hurdle: True)
- Nurse Practitioner e-portfolio Hurdle: Pass/Fail% (Hurdle: True)
Workload: Workshop 2 Hours/ One Off Lecture 2 Hours/ Week 6 modules delivered over a 13 weeks, including a 2-day intensive residential.

Subject: Physical Assessment and Clinical Decision Making for Independent Practice (NURS7243)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This course offered concurrently with NURS7242, develops advanced competence in the practice of physical assessment across the lifespan toward the scope of the Nurse Practitioner. The development of expanded clinical assessment capability provides an enhanced appreciation of the management of acute, complex and chronic illnesses. This course also develops capacity in the interpretation, validation and decision-making frameworks surrounding advanced investigations for the Nurse Practitioner. Immersion within the clinical setting supervised and mentored by the CST provides clinical context and consolidation of learning needs.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate advanced knowledge and communication skills suitable to support a structured and systematic approach to holistic clinical assessment which incorporates the patient history with the ability to collect and appraise objective and subjective data.
- Demonstrate and communicate an informed and confident approach to the use of therapeutic and investigative interventions and options that are judicious, evidenced informed, client-focused, and informed and directed by clinical findings.
- Analyse and interpret assessment findings to inform differential diagnoses and patient focused management which is evidence informed and person centred.
- Critically analyse social determinants of health and the subsequent impact of healthcare diagnosis and associated interventions on the patient, ensuring culturally safe, inclusive, and comprehensive solutions to patient management.

Subject: Pharmacology and prescribing for Independent Practice (NURS7244)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This course within the Master of Nurse Practitioner program aims to assist students to develop a comprehensive knowledge of pharmacology (pharmacodynamics & pharmacokinetics) and therapeutic interventions to be applied within clinical practice. This knowledge will underpin critical analysis of the safety and accountability in the use and evaluation of pharmacological and non-pharmacological management for consumers in line with the National Prescribing Competencies Framework. This course will further enable Nurse Practitioner students working in advanced health care roles to develop their knowledge of the human sciences underpinning pharmacological and non-pharmacological interventions.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate a comprehensive knowledge of applied pharmacology, pharmacodynamics and pharmacokinetics sufficient to support the unique needs of the patient and optimise their clinical care.
- Critically discuss the process of definitive clinical decision making, utilising evidence informed, culturally safe and inclusive strategies to ensure assessment, requested diagnostic tests and planned episodes of care align with client treatment and optimal outcomes.
- Using an integrated understanding, critically reflect on how continuous patient assessment, tailored treatment and constant evaluation assists in managing optimal clinical progress and best patient outcomes.
- Demonstrate an advanced understanding of how effective collaboration and communication with other health care professionals optimises safe, clinically focused outcomes which respect the culture, rights and choice of the patient to information and their input into determining their own care.

Subject: Quality Use of Medicines, Drug Pharmacokinetics and Drug Pharmacodynamics (PHRM7001)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This online course aims to assist Master of Nurse Practitioner students to develop comprehensive knowledge and understanding of the quality use of medicines and drug pharmacology in relation to pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics. The course will cover aspects of the Australian healthcare system, focusing particularly on the National Medicines Policy and Quality Use of Medicines. Equitable, effective, safe, judicious and appropriate medicines usage will be discussed. The course will also cover pharmacological principles around drug pharmacokinetics (absorption, distribution, metabolism and elimination), pharmacodynamics, adverse drug reactions and drug interactions. Examples will be given from different classes of drugs and students will be encouraged to consider how relevant information can be considered within their own context and scope of practice. Students will develop critical skills around safe and effective drug use, prescribing and administration which they can then apply when making client care decisions.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate and apply knowledge in legislation, regulation and policies relevant to provide better health outcomes for all Australians through the access to and wise use of medicines.
- Demonstrate an ability to consider and apply principles around equitable, effective, safe, judicious and appropriate medicines usage when making client care decisions.
- Demonstrate and apply knowledge around how drug absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion in the human body inform appropriate therapeutic use of medicines.
- Understand the relationship between drug dose and drug concentration in the body (pharmacokinetics) and drug concentration in the body and drug effect (pharmacodynamics) and to be able to make predictions based on these relationships.
- Demonstrate and apply knowledge around factors associated with the development of adverse drug reactions and drug interactions.
- Use published research data to relate and apply knowledge to issues and principles around the quality use of medicines, drug pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics.
Assessment Tasks:
- Evidence Based Medicine Critical Appraisal: 15%% (Hurdle: False)
- Quality Use of Medicines Case: 15%% (Hurdle: False)
- Clinical Pharmacology Case Study Identity Verified: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
