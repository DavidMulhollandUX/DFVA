## Description
The Master of Landscape Architecture program inspires you to produce research informed designs that celebrate equitable, adaptive, resilient and sustainable urban and regional environments. The program engages with the Sydney region as its landscape laboratory. You will be encouraged to engage with global issues and your international experiences at the local scale. The core of the program is the design studio sequence, with each studio supported by a skill and/or knowledge-based course. Your design process will be informed by research and critical analysis of landscape sites and systems, and your design outcomes will be shaped by modelling, testing and dialogue. Elective courses provide opportunities for you to pursue interdisciplinary, specialised interests across the faculty, particularly in the areas Urban Design and City Analytics. The final-year capstone studio sequence provides you with the opportunity to undertake an independent research-based design project.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

5. Identify, analyse and research complex issues in landscape architecture design projects, and to generate appropriate, innovative and soundly justified design propositions.
2. Identify and assess ethical, environmental, cultural and/or sustainability considerations in their design practice.
3. Collaborate responsibly, to reflect on their own contributions to teamwork, and on the team's processes and ability to achieve outcomes.
1. Synthesize and communicate complex landscape architectural information, concepts and propositions clearly, concisely and effectively.
6. Demonstrate personal accountability, cultural awareness, a respect for diversity, and environmental and social responsibility in their design practice.
7. Prepare and present written documents and design proposals effectively and in keeping with the professional standards of the discipline.
4. Locate, select, and apply knowledge of current disciplinary theory and professional practice to complex landscape architectural design and research challenges in local, regional and global contexts.

## Career Opportunities & Employment Areas
Career pathways include: landscape architect; urban designer; strategic planner; project manager; sustainability specialist in government agencies; artist; academic; technical and/or policy officer; heritage consultant; public domain manager; environmental manager.

## Professional Recognition & Accreditation
The program is accredited by the Australian Institute of Landscape Architects. More information on accreditation and education program in Australia is available at the AILA website: www.aila.org.au

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
3 year (144 UOC) program (Specialisation A)

- A bachelor's degree in any field with a credit average (equivalent to a UNSW WAM of 65), plus submission of a portfolio and personal statement. Twelve months of work experience in a closely related office is highly desirable.

2 year (96 UOC) program (Specialisation B)

- A bachelor's degree in a related field with a credit average (equivalent to a UNSW WAM of 65), plus submission of a portfolio and personal statement.

- A Graduate Diploma* (or equivalent qualification) in landscape design or a related field with a credit average (equivalent to a UNSW WAM of 65), plus submission of a portfolio and personal statement.

Students in this specialisation will receive 48 UOC in advanced standing upon entry to the program, and will complete a 2 year (96 UOC) program of study.

1 year (48 UOC) program (Specialisation C)

- A four year accredited Bachelor of Landscape Architecture degree with a credit average (equivalent to a UNSW WAM of 65), plus submission of a portfolio and personal statement.

Students in this specialisation will receive 96 UOC in advanced standing upon entry to the program, and will complete a 1 year (48 UOC) program of study.

Portfolio requirements

- Must be in clear digital format

- Must include sample works from various stages of the applicant’s first degree and text should accompany all drawings/images to explain the projects

- May also include other creative work

- May include professional work, but the applicant’s contribution and responsibility for the work must be clearly stated.

Related fields: Landscape architecture, architecture, urban design and urban planning, interior architecture/design, landscape design, environmental design (minimum 6 studios).

*It is expected that a Graduate Diploma will have been completed after an undergraduate degree.

### Landscape Architecture Stream B (LANDES)
<p>Stream B is one of three streams in the Master of Landscape Architecture Program. Stream B provides an opportunity for students from cognate degree backgrounds to undertake the Master of Landscape Architecture at UNSW. In Stream B you will complete a 2 year 96 UoC program of study.<br /><br />The program is structured into three streams: Stream A is available to students from non-cognate disciplines, and Streams B and C are available to students with bachelor&#39;s qualifications in cognate disciplines. In all streams in the program you will complete core courses fundamental to contemporary, professional practice of Landscape Architecture. To meet both the Australian Qualification Framework (AQF) and Australian Institute of Landscape Architect’s (AILA) criteria for Master’s degree level of study, each stream in the program provides you with the opportunity to undertake a sequence of capstone design studios, with research methods and projects core components of the curriculum.</p>
