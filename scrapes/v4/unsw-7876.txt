## Description
The fully online Graduate Certificate in Project Management is designed to meet the growing demand for skilled project professionals across a wide range of industries. Whether you are a recent graduate or an experienced professional seeking to enhance your capabilities, this program delivers essential, practical skills for a successful career in project management. Through a combination of core and elective courses, the program emphasises real-world application, preparing you to manage schedules, budgets and risks while aligning projects with broader organisational objectives.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

2. Apply best practices to identify risks and manage project scope, schedule and budget for optimal performance.
3. Integrate strategic thinking and stakeholder engagement to ensure projects support organisational goals.
1. Use project management frameworks, methodologies and tools to plan and deliver projects across diverse contexts.

## Career Opportunities & Employment Areas
Project Coordinator, Project Administrator, Project Analyst, Project Scheduler, Risk Analyst

## Program Structure & Degree Requirements
Students must complete 24 Units of Credit (UOC) structured across the following academic components:

### Core Courses (12 UOC)
Students must complete 12 UOC of the following courses.

- **ZZCA8303**: Project Management Body of Knowledge [6 UOC]
- **ZZCA8302**: Project Administration [6 UOC]

### Prescribed Electives (12 UOC)
Students must complete 12 UOC of the following courses.

- **ZZCA8147**: The Business of Managing Projects [6 UOC]
- **ZZCA8301**: Project Risk Management [6 UOC]
- **ZZCA8310**: Project Schedule and Budget Control [6 UOC]

## Admission Requirements
**Limitations on Recognition of Prior Learning**:
Students enrolled in a program that consists of 24 UOC or less are not eligible for RPL

**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
- Have completed a Bachelor degree or higher-level qualification (or equivalent); OR​

- Applicants who have not completed a Bachelor degree or higher-level qualification are required to have a minimum of 2 years relevant work experience in project management or related positions*​

​*Relevant work experience includes being responsible for tasks that are non-routine, unstructured or semi-structured, engaging in problem-solving activities. It can include experience of managing a small team, being a team leader, managing a project or being responsible for the management and allocation of resources. Alternatively, engaging in a professional role over time is relevant experience.

Further Study

Articulation pathways exist to the Graduate Diploma in Project Management and Master of Project Management programs if the student completes this program.

Subject: Project Risk Management (ZZCA8301)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Effective risk management is critical to the success of projects and organisations in an increasingly complex and uncertain global environment. This course provides students with a foundational understanding of risk management principles, frameworks, and best practices, with a focus on their application in project management and enterprise environments. The course discusses the complex nature of the words 'uncertainty' and 'risk', and how their many definitions and interpretations impact risk management. The course covers aspects of risk management including why individuals and organisations must manage risks; the identification, classification and measurement of risks; the fundamental principles and frameworks for risk management; the tools and techniques used in the risk management process; the important role of risk culture and communication in managing enterprise risks; and current issues in risk management.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically evaluate a case study application of project risk management theories, frameworks, and standards.
- Conduct advanced qualitative and quantitative risk analyses and design integrated risk response strategies.
- Explain and evaluate the impact of risks on individuals and organisations, and evaluate strategies to measure and treat them effectively.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of risk governance, risk appetite frameworks, and senior-level governance oversight in aligning risk management with organisational objectives.

Subject: Project Administration (ZZCA8302)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course equips students with the knowledge and skills to analyse and manage the complex environments in which major projects are defined and delivered. The course emphasises the organisational, political, financial, and legal dimensions of project management, including corporate governance, project viability and contract law. Through real-world case studies, students will develop advanced capabilities in project governance, procurement strategies, financial evaluation, and risk management. The course also explores contemporary challenges such as government regulations, stakeholder engagement, and dispute resolution strategies.
Learning Outcomes:
- Evaluate legislative and common law provisions impacting procurement and develop strategies to address legal and ethical challenges in project delivery.
- Analyse governance objectives, constraints, and delivery models to recommend effective solutions for managing project complexity.
- Develop a comprehensive Project Execution Strategy that effectively manages project risks and aligns with governance frameworks.
- Analyse the financial and economic aspects of projects and develop data-driven cost-benefit recommendations to support decision making.
- Design project organisations and procurement strategies that optimise performance while balancing buyer and supplier objectives.

Subject: Project Schedule and Budget Control (ZZCA8310)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course provides students with the knowledge, tools, and techniques to manage project scope, scheduling, budgeting, and control effectively. Students will learn to develop project plans and baselines using industry-standard techniques. The course emphasises the practical application of project scheduling and budgeting principles, resource management, and progress reporting. Students will also explore strategies for managing uncertainty and adapting to changing project requirements. By the end of the course, students will be equipped to plan, track, and control project performance to ensure successful delivery of time, cost, and scope objectives.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse project performance using Earned Value Management (EVM) to evaluate progress and control deviations.
- Develop project plans, schedules, and budget baselines using industry-standard techniques, such as PERT, CPM (AOA, AON), and EVM.
- Explain the principles and processes of project scheduling, budgeting, and control, and their importance in project success.
- Design strategies to manage project uncertainties and address changing requirements in project schedules.
