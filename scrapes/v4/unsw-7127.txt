## Description
The Graduate Certificate is the first tier of a sequence of postgraduate programs in Property and Development. It is undertaken on a part-time basis. Students learn about property and development principles and techniques needed to effectively manage property development projects.

The Graduate Certificate program is suited to practicing personnel in relevant fields including building, construction management, construction economics, civil engineering, mining engineering, project management, quantity surveying, real estate or equivalent, who wishes to enhance their career opportunities. This program also provides an opportunity to those who have relevant professional experience but limited formal qualifications to study at the graduate level.

This program can only be done on a part-time basis and is therefore not available to International students who require a student visa.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

2. Apply property development and investment principles and practices to new or complex environments.
3. Critically evaluate contemporary debates and literatures in property development and investment discipline.
1. Demonstrate an advanced understanding of the property development and investment body of knowledge, including established theories and recent developments relevant to the discipline or professional field.
4. Demonstrate an understanding of international perspectives relevant to property development and investment discipline and the property industry.

## Program Structure & Degree Requirements
Students must complete 24 Units of Credit (UOC) structured across the following academic components:

### Core Courses (24 UOC)
Students must take 24 UOC of the following courses.

- **REST0006**: Property Development and Feasibility Analysis [6 UOC]
- **REST0019**: Real Property Law [6 UOC]
- **REST0005**: Property Valuation [6 UOC]
- **REST0010**: Property Industry Application [6 UOC]
- **REST0004**: Property Investment and Finance [6 UOC]
- **REST0001**: Property Performance Analysis [6 UOC]

## Progression & Academic Rules
On completion of the program, students with grades of credit average or above may apply to upgrade to the Master of Property and Development program (8127) with full credits for the completed courses.

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
One of the following:

- A qualification equivalent to an Australian Bachelor's degree in a related field

- A qualification equivalent to an Australian university diploma (or equivalent) in a related field with a minimum of 3 years industry experience with some managerial responsibilities

- A recognised professional qualification in the property industry related fields, e.g. Associate membership of API or RICS, plus 5 years industry experience with some managerial responsibilities

- A qualification equivalent to an Australian recognised bachelor's degree or college or university diploma in any field, plus a minimum of 5 years of work experience in the property industry at a management level

Related fields: Built environment disciplines, engineering, building, construction management, construction economics, civil engineering, mining engineering, material engineering, architecture, interior architecture, landscape architecture, urban planning, quantity surveying, property development, real estate, law or business.

Accepted roles at management level: Portfolio Manager, Property Development Manager (Company: Lendlease, Mirvac,Stockland, etc), Property Investment Manager (Fund Management, Investment Bank, Private Equity), Valuer, Working at a Senior Level at a National/International Real Estate company (e.g. JLL, BK, Colliers; CBRE, Knight Frank), Senior Property Analysts (including Property Acquisitions), Asset Managers, Facilities Managers, Senior Property Finance Managers

Relevant Professional Qualification: Evidence of membership is required.

**Limitations on Recognition of Prior Learning**:
No advanced standing nor exemption will be given for the Graduate Certificate program.

Subject: Property Investment and Finance (REST0004)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Property Investment and Finance covers insight into property investment and finance in Australia and internationally. It introduces the concept of property investment and finance concepts. It explores the unique features of income-producing property, property development, and examines the performance of these properties through the application of a variety of investment techniques. You critically review equity and debt financing and examine the financing alternatives available, as well as methods for evaluating these alternatives. In addition, you gain both a theoretical and an applied understanding of basic accounting principles in this course. The role of real estate in a capital market is also examined through a portfolio analysis. The contemporary issues (e.g. sustainability in property investment, macroeconomic impacts, and real estate legal implications) are also addressed. This enables you to develop a high level of knowledge of the workings of modern-day real estate capital markets.
Learning Outcomes:
- Evaluate financing strategies for property investment.
- Apply the property investment analysis in assessing property assets for investment purposes.
- Evaluate practical applications of risk management, legal considerations and capital structure for property investment.
- Critically assess the profitability and risk of both income-producing and development property.

Subject: Property Valuation (REST0005)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Property Valuation introduces property valuation theory and practice. Property valuation remains one of the most important specialisations in the real estate profession, and thus valuers are specially educated and trained to carry out property valuations for the public due to the peculiarity and importance of real estate assets. You will learn about the concept of value, purposes and bases of property valuation, methods of property valuation (both traditional and advanced), property valuation process, property valuation report and an introduction to the application of computer programs in property valuation.
Learning Outcomes:

- Analyse the factors that affect property valuations.
- Apply different valuation methods to property valuation practice.

Subject: Property Development and Feasibility Analysis (REST0006)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Property Development and Feasibility Analysis examines the process of property development and feasibility analysis of property development projects. You will investigate the meaning and scope of real property and sustainable property development, development process and participants, as well as development appraisal procedures for producing good development outcomes. You will be introduced to computer software of property development feasibility analysis and use case studies to develop your skills in organising and solving feasibility analysis problems.

- Analyse the different phases of the property development process.
- Assess the viability of property development projects.
- Generate land value assessment and cash flow feasibility analysis using appropriate tools.
- Evaluate the implications of planning control, economic and market conditions on property development.

Subject: Real Property Law (REST0019)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: In this course, you will learn concepts of commercial law and real property. Material covered in this course includes foundational concepts in contract, tort and business law, examination of legal doctrines, principles and rules governing property, land, tenure, title, possession, estates, successive and concurrent interests, priorities, covenants, easements, mortgages. You will also learn about acquisition, sale, valuation, use and development of land as well as the resolution of disputes in relation to land to provide an overview of the operation of the Australian legal system.
Learning Outcomes:
- Discuss the historical and legal underpinnings of real property law and jurisprudence in NSW and Australia
- Evaluate land-related legal responsibilities and restrictions in NSW and Australia.
- Apply land-related legal principles and practices in the valuation, sale, use, and development of land.
- Explain land-related legal concepts and practices in NSW and Australia.
