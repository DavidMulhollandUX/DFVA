Subject: SCIE4000 - Research Methods
### SCIE4000 - Research Methods
Learning Outcomes:
- Develop a research-focussed approach to the solution of a new problem, beginning with the development of research questions.
- Apply project management techniques to plan and carry out a research project in the allocated time frame, within the available resources and in a safe and ethical manner.
- Competently search the scientific literature to critically evaluate previous research.
- Offer clear and logical explanations of scientific concepts that may be unfamiliar to the non-specialist through both oral and written communication.
- Incorporate Indigenous perspectives in research in a respectful and meaningful way.
- Apply fundamental data analysis tools to analyse quantitative and qualitative results.
- Be familiar with the contexts of scientific research, and the various research activities that take place within the Faculty’s schools and research centres.
Assessment Tasks:
- Worksheets Hurdle: 48% - This weight is indicative only. This is a pass/fail course.%
- 3MT-style presentation Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 18% - This weight is indicative only. This is a pass/fail course.%
- Research pitch Hurdle: 20% - This weight is indicative only. This is a pass/fail course.%
- Development documentation Hurdle Team or group-based: 14% - This weight is indicative only. This is a pass/fail course.%
Workload: Workshop 2 Hours/ Week This course contains a series of workshops (up to two hours each) and tasks requiring self-paced learning and group work

Subject: SCIE4010 - Honours Research Project
### SCIE4010 - Honours Research Project
Learning Outcomes:
- Contribute to the advancement of relevant scientific fields through original research
- Critically evaluate existing research and identify gaps in knowledge relevant to your project
- Conduct an independent research project including: documenting project progress, generating appropriate evidence through a reproducible method, objectively analysing data, developing evidence-based arguments to support conclusions, and critically evaluating the impact and limitations your research
- Explain your ideas effectively to a general audience by using effective audio-visual means
- Report the rationale and findings in a written document

Subject: SCIE4040 - Literature Review and Project Research Proposal
### SCIE4040 - Literature Review and Project Research Proposal
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate your ability to follow scientific procedure of writing in terms of following scientific writing structure and relevant literature citation
- Show evidence of command and understanding of relevant concepts within your writing
- Demonstrate an application of relevant principles to new information within your writing
- Demonstrate critical thinking/problem solving within your writing by evaluating other studies, being aware of knowledge gaps and provide ways to improve or fill in these gaps
- Show an ability to appropriately infer and make relevant conclusions within your writing
- Professionally present the literature in terms of style, formatting, presenting relevant tables and figures and editing.
- Develop and/or displaying an in-depth knowledge of the field of work being reviewed
Assessment Tasks:
- Project Research Proposal Hurdle: 40%%
- Literature Review Hurdle: 60%%
Workload: Workshop 2 Hours/ Ad Hoc Self-directed learning
