## Graduate Certificate in Marketing - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BC009
##### 60 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Build marketing knowledge and skills needed to expand your career opportunities and become a marketing leader with La Trobe's Graduate Certificate in Marketing.

To thrive in today’s digital and data-driven environment, marketers need more than creative instinct: they need strategic insight, technological fluency, and an understanding of the risks and opportunities shaping modern business. In six months, this short postgraduate course equips you with the foundational tools and emerging knowledge to navigate contemporary marketing practice with confidence.

By completing La Trobe’s Graduate Certificate in Marketing, you will:

  * develop a strong grounding in marketing management, including how to evaluate marketing strategies and make evidence-based decisions
  * gain insight into digital marketing tools, platforms and performance metrics to design and manage high-impact campaigns
  * explore the potential of artificial intelligence in transforming marketing practices and customer engagement
  * understand how cybersecurity and data governance shape trust, privacy, and brand integrity in digital marketplaces.
Successfully completing the Graduate Certificate in Marketing allows you to pursue further postgraduate study with credit, including our Master of Marketing.

Subject: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR BUSINESS (BUS4005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the nature and function of business. However, business leaders are challenged by the effective and competitive adoption of AI. This subject provides you with an industry-ready skillset to empower productivity, innovation and decision-making across the business ecosystem. - Design and develop in teams, AI chatbot solutions that address complex business challenges, demonstrating alignment with ethical principles, governance frameworks, and cybersecurity standards.

Subject: CYBERSECURITY FOR BUSINESS (BUS4006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Cybersecurity for Business provides a comprehensive overview of cybersecurity principles, practices, and frameworks for managing cybersecurity risks in organisations. Cybersecurity plays a critical role in an organisation's operations by safeguarding its assets, ensuring regulatory compliance, and enhancing overall business resilience. The subject covers key topics, including cybersecurity threats, attacks, governance frameworks, cybersecurity risk management, cybersecurity policies and procedures, and compliance. You will explore cybersecurity for businesses through a combination of tailor-made case studies and hands-on workshops, offering a comprehensive understanding of theoretical principles alongside practical application in real-world scenarios. It develops the skills necessary to craft effective cybersecurity strategies that align with organisational goals and regulatory requirements. The course will also examine the ethical, legal, and regulatory dimensions of cybersecurity.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse different types of cyber threats, attack methods, and AI-driven threats, to assess their potential impact on business operations, risk management, and compliance obligations.
- Design and justify ethical and governance-aligned cybersecurity strategies using appropriate frameworks, AI-enabled tools, and security models to strengthen threat detection, incident response, resilience, and regulatory compliance.
- Identify and apply fundamental cybersecurity principles to recognise and evaluate common organisational security challenges, including those arising from emerging technologies and AI.
- Communicate cybersecurity governance strategies and AI-informed risk mitigation plans clearly and appropriately to diverse technical and non-technical stakeholders in professional business contexts.

Subject: DIGITAL MARKETING (MKT4007)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: With the prominence of digital platforms, it is critical for companies to supplement and further advance their traditional activities by managing their marketing activities via digital platforms. Digital platforms allow companies to enhance their productivity in achieving their objectives. Digital Marketing offers overall knowledge of digital marketing strategies and principles to enhance firms' digital performance. This subject develops your knowledge of theories and principles utilised in digital marketing. You will learn to analyse the digital marketing practices of a firm. Further, you will apply digital marketing principles to develop solutions for the digital marketing problems of a company.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and recommend actionable, theory-driven digital marketing solutions such as search engine marketing and optimisation, inbound marketing strategies and customer engagement strategies for business challenges.
- Apply digital marketing concepts and theories to critically evaluate and improve company practices, individually and collaboratively.
- Critically analyse digital marketing practices including search, social media, content, and data-driven campaigns used by businesses across diverse industries and contexts.
