## Diploma of Information Technology
##### U0605
##### 96 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Diploma of Information Technology (IT) is the first step towards gaining your Monash IT-related degree. Information technology and computer science are central to every industry – creating more efficient ways to work, developing engaging ways to reach customers, and establishing powerful ways to communicate. By studying this pathway, you’ll have the skills you need to succeed in the fast-changing world of technology. 

In the first year, you’ll learn about the maths and science theory and practical skills you’ll need for University.

In the second year, you’ll study subjects equivalent to first-year Monash University students and develop your specialist knowledge of Information Technology or Computer Science.

### Structure
The course is structured in two parts: Part 1. Foundation knowledge and Part 2. Discipline knowledge.

Part 1. Foundation knowledge

These units will provide you with the foundational information technology and computer science concepts and theory and practical skills you’ll need for University.

Part 2. Discipline knowledge

These units are equivalent to first-year Monash University units that will provide you with specialist knowledge in information technology or computer science to enable progression to your destination bachelor's degree.

### Requirements
The course comprises 96 points structured in two parts: Parts 1. Foundation knowledge (48 points) and Part 2. Discipline knowledge (48 points).

Each unit is 6 credit points unless otherwise stated.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Identify and communicate advice in a variety of IT disciplines to address problems in accord with management requirements
**2.**
Critically apply theoretical and technical skills to solve problems in relation to a range of IT disciplines
**3.**
Manage work priorities and coordinate the work of others in accord with parameters set by management in a number of IT contexts.
**4.**
Utilise technical skills to demonstrate understanding and problem solving in relation to IT issues involving diverse stakeholders.
**5.**
Demonstrate knowledge of technical and theoretical issues in a variety of IT disciplines, as well as programming language skills.

### Progression to further studies
Once you have completed your Diploma with the required entry score and prerequisite requirements, you will have guaranteed entry into second year of a related undergraduate degree at Monash University.

### AHEGS
The Diploma of Information Technology is an undergraduate award. The course is taught in English.  Graduates have gained a range of technical and problem solving skills relevant to the IT sector. The course takes two years of full-time study or part-time equivalent to complete. The Diploma of Information Technology is a Level 5 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.
