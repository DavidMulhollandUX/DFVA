Subject: BIOE6100 - Fundamentals of Neuroengineering
### BIOE6100 - Fundamentals of Neuroengineering
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate how neurons generate and propagate electrical signals and the basic properties of signal transmission in neural circuits
- Critique and select relevant frameworks for analysing neural data
- Implement signal processing algorithms for practical problems involving neuroscience
- Demonstrate the need of engineering frameworks for large scale neural modelling
- Critically analyse information independently acquired from diverse sources (e.g., research articles, books, patents, company product data sheets) in providing solutions to real-world neuroscience problems.
- Effectively communicate (using oral and written communication and appropriate figures and visualizations) the real-world implementation of analytical frameworks in neuroscience
- Orally present and report a research project on neural signal processing
Assessment Tasks:
- Problem Sets In-person: 40%%
- Project proposal oral assessment Identity Verified In-person: 15%%
- Project Presentation Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 35%%
- Project Final Report: 10%%
Workload: Problem Based Learning 4.00 Hours/ Week 2 x 2 hour problem-based learning sessions

Subject: BIOE6403 - Biomedical Instrumentation
### BIOE6403 - Biomedical Instrumentation
Learning Outcomes:
- Describe the origins of biopotentials and physiological parameters for the purposes of diagnostic and therapeutic device design.
- Design and construct conditioning circuits for medical devices, particularly concerning human cardiopulmonary measurements.
- Apply appropriate means of mathematical analysis to biosignals for research and diagnostic purposes.
- Identify sources of noise and signals artifacts in instrumentation systems and execute corrective techniques in both hardware and software.
- Appraise micro and macro shock risks in electromedical equipment and be able to design equipment compliant with relevant international electromedical safety standards.
- Identify appropriate sensor and actuation solutions for a given measurement task, design an electronic front-end and signal processing chain.
Assessment Tasks:
- Initial Quiz: 5%%
- Instrumentation amplifier & filter Identity Verified: 15%%
- Digitisation and feature detection Identity Verified: 25%%
- Final Examination Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 55%%
Workload: Lecture 2 Hours/ Week Practical 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: ELEC7309 - Power System Planning and Reliability
### ELEC7309 - Power System Planning and Reliability
Learning Outcomes:
- Construct a basic modelling of power system components for reliability evaluation and planning
- Design methodologies to solve power system generation system reliability calculation and generation planning.
- Investigate calculation of reliability indices for combined generation and transmission systems.
- Formulate reliability concepts to transmission, distribution and interconnected systems, especially in planning.
- Design power systems reliability and planning in the context of the deregulated environment.
- Develop load forecasting models for short-term and long-term power system planning
- Construct transmission and generation expansion models for both regulated and deregulated power systems.
- Develop a simple peer-to-peer energy trading algorithm for distribution system planning analysis.
- Select suitable technology options for generation and transmission planning problems using cost-benefit analysis.
- Investigate challenges and tools for solving large-scale power system planning problems.
- Construct small-scale planning/reliability problems using power system packages and toolboxes.
Assessment Tasks:
- Assignments: 40% Individual%
- Final exam during exam period Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 60% Individual%
Workload: Lecture 3 Hours/ Week Applied Class 2 Hours/ Week

Subject: ELEC7310 - Electricity Market Operation and Security
### ELEC7310 - Electricity Market Operation and Security
Learning Outcomes:
- POWER SYSTEM DYNAMICS & STABILITY - Classify power system stability and describe definition and concepts.
- POWER SYSTEM DYNAMICS & STABILITY - Demonstrate essential knowledge and skills in mathematical modeling of power system and analytical techniques for stability studies.
- POWER SYSTEM DYNAMICS & STABILITY - Perform case studies on a given test system on transient, small signal and static voltage stability and interpret the results in meaningful ways.
- POWER SYSTEM DYNAMICS & STABILITY - Apply with commercial grade and research power system analysis software tools (e.g. PSS/E, DiGsilent Powerfactory and PSAT).
- Market Operations and Analysis - Define electricity market structure, including rules and codes.
- Market Operations and Analysis - Demonstrate the understanding of electricity market operation from generation, transmission and distribution and collaboratively apply that understanding to current challenges faced by energy sector globally.
- Market Operations and Analysis - Examine the major issues such as ancillary services and transmission pricing relevant to market operations and security.
- Market Operations and Analysis - Evaluate existing electricity market structures and mechanisms.
Assessment Tasks:
- Simulation Lab reports: 32%%
- In-semester exam Identity Verified In-person: 18%%
- Class Project Team or group-based: 12%%
- Final Exam Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 38%%
Workload: Lecture 2 Hours/ Week Tutorial 2 Hours/ Week
