## Professional Certificate of Business (Executive)
##### B9002
##### 12 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Professional Certificate of Business (Executive) provides you with the opportunity to seek advancement within or beyond your organisation. It is designed to develop your knowledge, competencies, and skills in the area of leadership. The course involves applied pedagogy and engagement with industry leaders.

This course is innovative and industry-focused, with a strong emphasis on professional development and excellence in business practice to help you assist businesses to succeed in complex and dynamic environments. Flexible in its delivery, the course aims to ensure that you have the toolkit to answer challenges faced by individuals, organisations, and communities. This course is suited to early-career professionals, as well as to senior leaders who want to extend their leadership, business skills, and knowledge.

### Post-nominals
ProfCertBus(Exec)

### Structure
The course comprises two units consisting of LEE (Leadership and Executive Education) coded units, offered by the Faculty of Business and Economics, where you develop your knowledge, competencies, and skills in the area of leadership.

### Requirements
The course comprises 12 credit points of study.

Units are 6 credit points unless otherwise stated.

You must complete the following two units (12 credit points):

  * LEE5001 Advanced leadership 1

  * LEE5002 Advanced leadership 2

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
be able to provide advanced discipline-based solutions relevant to the business, professional and/or public policy context
**2.**
be responsible and effective global citizens who:

engage in an internationalised world
exhibit cross-cultural competence
demonstrate ethical values
**3.**
apply advanced business knowledge in the practice of leadership, management and organisational roles.
**4.**
be critical and creative scholars who:

produce innovative solutions to problems
apply research skills to business challenges
communicate effectively and perceptively

### Progression to further studies
Successful completion of this course may provide a pathway to the Graduate Certificate of Business (Executive) (B4002) subject to satisfying the appropriate entry requirements. Credit may be granted towards other Faculty of Business and Economics postgraduate courses upon successful completion.

Subject: Advanced leadership 1 (LEE5001)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This unit is designed to develop and refine your leadership skills so you can survive and thrive in the new world of work. A combination of theory and practice will enable you to refine key leadership capabilities which can be immediately applied in commercial and organisational contexts. An applied approach will be taken involving in-class practice and assignments reflecting real-world challenges.
Learning Outcomes:
- evaluate personal learning and personal development in a complex business environment
- develop a deeper understanding of the difference between theory and practice
- communicate effectively and perceptively in a business environment.
- apply research skills and provide innovative solutions to complex business problems
- apply theory and ethical values to practice in a supervised environment
Assessment Tasks:
- 2 - Written: 60% (Hurdle: None)
- 1 - Presentation: 40% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: Minimum total expected workload to achieve the learning outcomes for this unit is 144 hours per semester typically comprising a mixture of scheduled learning activities and independent study. Independent study may include associated readings, assessment and preparation for scheduled activities. You are expected to complete all pre-class activities prior to your scheduled class, and post-class activities should be completed after your scheduled class. Learning activities may include a combination of teacher directed, peer directed and online engagement activities.
