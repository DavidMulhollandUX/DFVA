La Trobe University’s Master of Business Administration (MBA) is an AI-first, fully online degree and designed for mid-career professionals, managers and technical specialists from non-business fields who want to develop the skills to lead teams, projects and organisations in AI-enabled and data-driven global business environments.
The AI first MBA is a curriculum and learning model in which AI literacy is a core leadership capability embedded across strategy, operations, marketing, finance, people, and governance so graduates can lead AI adoption responsibly, make high quality decisions with AI support, and create value through human and AI collaboration.
Delivered 100% online in accelerated six-week study blocks, this MBA is designed for working professionals, giving you the flexibility to study anywhere while continuing your career.
The program’s structure supports tailored learning pathways, with specialisations in AI, Analytics and Cybersecurity; Digital Health; and Leadership and Governance. You can also create your own pathway from a range of elective subjects.
The La Trobe MBA develops practical management and leadership capability for organisations operating in technology-driven markets. You will design AI-enabled business strategies that create sustainable value and are grounded in responsible and inclusive business practices, then apply them to real business challenges through industry-aligned assessments and applied strategic analysis.
Through a curriculum co-designed with global industry leaders and enhanced with artificial intelligence tools and insights and supported by La Trobe University’s collaboration with OpenAI, you will graduate with a portfolio of AI-driven business solutions designed to support leadership in transforming organisations.
Whether you are pursuing career progression, a strategic pivot or entrepreneurial ventures, you will graduate with advanced business knowledge, leadership capability and a globally recognised MBA credential.
By studying the Master of Business Administration, you will learn how to:
  * use digital and AI technologies to support data-driven decision-making that enhances organisational performance and competitiveness in global markets


  * design human-centred, AI-enabled business strategies that create sustainable economic value and reflect responsible and inclusive business practices


  * lead and communicate business strategies effectively to diverse organisational stakeholders across global and cultural contexts


  * analyse financial and accounting information, assess risks and apply expert judgement to solve complex organisational challenges


  * integrate ethical, environmental, social and governance (ESG) and strategic leadership principles when applying AI technologies to organisational decision-making.

### 
Career outcomes 
Graduates of the La Trobe AI-first MBA are prepared for leadership and professional roles where artificial intelligence, analytics, and digital technologies inform strategy, decision‑making, and organisational transformation.
Career pathways can include:
**Strategy manager/senior strategy analyst**
  * Using AI‑enabled scenario analysis, forecasting, and market intelligence to support corporate and competitive strategy.


**Management consultant**
  * Advising organisations on responsible AI adoption, innovation strategy, and data‑driven decision‑making.


**Product manager/digital product lead**
  * Managing AI‑enabled products and services, translating business needs into data‑informed and AI‑informed solutions.


**Operations and performance manager**
  * Applying AI and analytics to improve operational efficiency, forecasting, and performance management.


**Business and management consultant**
  * Help businesses identify ways to improve their operations. Lead projects and manage stakeholders to deliver more efficient and effective business outcomes.


**General manager/business unit manager**
  * Leading teams and functions in technology‑rich environments using evidence‑based and AI‑supported decision‑making.


**People and performance analytics manager**
  * Applying AI and workforce analytics to talent management, performance, and organisational capability development.


**AI governance or risk manager**
  * Supporting organisations to manage AI risk, ethics, cybersecurity, and regulatory compliance.


**Responsible innovation lead**
  * Integrating ethical, ESG, and responsible AI principles into business strategy and transformation initiatives.


**Founder/startup Leader**
  * Launching or scaling ventures that use AI to create new business models, products, or services.


Graduates are not trained as data scientists or engineers; rather, they emerge as AI‑literate leaders who can critically evaluate, govern, and deploy AI tools to enhance organisational performance, innovation, and responsible decision‑making.

Subject: APPLIED ECONOMICS (BUA5004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Organisations must navigate the economic landscape both domestically and internationally. Key organisational decisions around the allocation of scarce resources are at the core of economics, and an understanding of economic concepts, frameworks and language can greatly enhance a leader’s capacity to make sound decisions and to influence the decisions of those around them. In this subject you will draw on key economic concepts and apply them to business, organisational and societal challenges. You will use digital technology and hone your critical thinking and problem-solving skills to examine the links between theory and practice, and the principles that underpin markets, economic policy and business decision making. You will gain the skills to interpret economic data, to apply economic frameworks to contemporary challenges and to construct sound economic arguments.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse economic challenges and opportunities facing organisations and generate and communicate recommendations to improve organisational outcomes.
- Using independent research predict and evaluate how changes in economic conditions and government policies may affect businesses and industries.
- Construct and present logical and persuasive economic arguments and communicate concepts professionally using digital technologies.
- Critique government policy options and evaluate the potential impact of alternative proposed solutions.

Subject: ACCOUNTING FOR MANAGERS (BUA5FMA)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will gain a critical understanding of the role and functions of accounting information in business decision making. These include the use of information in external financial reports as a basis for evidence-based financial judgements and how managers use a range of internal financial and non-financial information to make strategic decisions. Independently and with others, you will work on various activities including case studies to become a judicious user of accounting information. You will also explore the role of accounting and associated reporting of broader ethical and social issues.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse the basis and structure of conventional accounting reports, their constituent elements and the related user information needs.
- Examine how managers use a range of financial and non-financial information to make evidence-based strategic decisions.
- Analyse, interpret and critically evaluate the information in external financial reports to make financial judgements.
- Consider the role of accounting and associated reporting of broader ethical, social and environmental issues and related accountability expectations.

Subject: PEOPLE AND CULTURE STRATEGY (BUA5HER)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In a highly competitive and rapidly changing business environment, effectively managing human capital is essential for an organisation's success, and is increasingly part of an organisation's strategy development and implementation. People and Culture (P&C) and Human Resource Management (HRM) is often a distinct unit within an organisation, but it is also increasingly integrated within the general management role. Thus, effective general managers must be able to retain talent, manage staff wellbeing and enhance employees' productivity. In this subject, you will explore contemporary HRM concepts for leading and implementing people and culture strategies that contribute to organisational sustainability, productive employment and decent work for all (UN SDG 8). You will be encouraged to critically examine how current people and culture practices can be applied in the general management context.
Learning Outcomes:
- Formulate contemporary people and culture strategies to promote sustainable organisations, productive employment and decent work for all
- Evaluate the strategic contribution that human resource management can make to organisational performance and sustainability
- Critically analyse how local, national, and global trends affect HR management
- Investigate and contextualise current trends impacting people and culture strategies, employee wellbeing and human resource management

Subject: FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT (BUA5MFN)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will develop knowledge and skills in financial management. The subject will facilitate advanced understanding of financial management policy and application, and how financial management decision-making affects the risk, growth and profitability of firms and, combining these elements, firm valuation outcomes. You will consider financial management outcomes, and particularly the underlying goal of the maximisation of the valuation of the firm, through the key decision-making areas investment, financing and distribution decisions. In turn, this will incorporate the consideration of the requisite usage of debt and equity financing and the need for efficient management of working capital to ensure adequate liquidity and short-term resource and cash flow availability.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate knowledge of financial management theory, concepts and models and associated application by analysing issues involved in valuation, financing and dividend policy decisions.
- Demonstrate the ability to interpret and communicate financial information effectively.
- Apply financial management concepts and principles to company risk assessment, cost of capital estimation, corporate valuation and corporate strategic planning and analysis
- Apply quantitative and spreadsheeting models and techniques in completing investment decision-making tasks associated with assessing project risk, valuation and feasibility.

Subject: GLOBAL BUSINESS STRATEGY (MGT5STR)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will learn how strategy is formulated, articulated, and enacted in a global context. We will combine ideas, theories and tools from strategic management, international business and innovation to take an interdisciplinary approach to build a comprehensive understanding of the management of organisations operating and competing domestically and across national borders. The subject will enable you to understand the challenges of businesses competing in the global arena and will help you develop the knowledge and skills needed to design strategies that aim for sustainable competitive advantages and increased firm performance. Through this subject you will become aware of key political, socio-economic, and cultural dynamics and trends that characterise the globalised business environment and the ethical and social responsibility consideration when doing business and strategising in a global scale.
Learning Outcomes:
- Appraise and apply the concepts and tools of business strategy in a global context.
- Integrate theories and techniques in the analysis of the strategic management options available to a wide range of different types of organisations as the operate and compete in the global arena.
- Critically analyse and critique arguments for and against ideas on how global innovation may enable a firm to achieve positional advantage and superior performance.
- Demonstrate knowledge of the main theoretical approaches to global strategic management and understand the economic, social and ethical and contexts that give rise to their formulation.
