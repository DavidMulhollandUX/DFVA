## Diploma of Science
##### U0606
##### 96 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Diploma of Science provides a pathway into a Monash University Science degree. The Diploma will introduce you to the fundamentals of the scientific method in both theory and practice, and across a range of science and related disciplines including Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Mathematics, Psychology, Computing and Engineering.

The Diploma of Science will enable you to develop the skills you will need to prepare you for success at Monash University, including critical thinking, research techniques, academic writing and communication. This Diploma offers flexibility to select subjects depending on your career goals and areas of interest, to give you a head start in your science degree.

Part 2 of this Diploma enables you to study subjects equivalent to first-year Monash University subjects, which will enable you to progress to second year in your chosen field.

In the Diploma of Science, you can also select to undertake the Education stream, which upon completion allows you to progress to the Bachelor of Education (Honours) and Bachelor of Science double degree. During your studies in part 2, you will complete the same number of units as a single discipline diploma. You'll complete two education studies units and 10 days of professional work placement as part of your diploma.

### Structure
The course is structured in two parts: Part 1. Foundation knowledge and Part 2. Discipline knowledge.

Part 1: Foundation knowledge

In this part, across your first two trimesters, you will study eight core science units that provide you with foundational knowledge in science disciplines with the opportunity to develop focus and prepare you to progress to part 2.

Part 2: Discipline knowledge

In this part, across your final two trimesters, you will study units that are equivalent to first-year university units and develop your specialist knowledge in science.  In this part you will study eight units, comprising five core units and three elective units that align with your interests.

In this part you also have the opportunity to complete Education studies stream, which allows you to develop foundational knowledge in either primary or secondary education. In the Education stream you will complete two education studies units and 10 days of professional work placement.

The course comprises 96 points structured in two parts: Part 1. Foundation knowledge (48 points) and Part 2. Discipline knowledge (48 points).

Each unit is 6 credit points unless otherwise stated.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Demonstrate knowledge of technical and theoretical issues in a variety of science disciplines, underpinned by scientific and mathematical theory.
**2.**
Critically apply theoretical and technical skills to solve problems in relation to a range of science disciplines.
**3.**
Manage work priorities and coordinate the work of others in accord with parameters set by management in a number of science contexts.
**4.**
Utilise technical skills to demonstrate understanding and problem solving in relation to science issues involving diverse stakeholders.
**5.**
Identify and communicate advice in a variety of science disciplines to address technical problems in accord with management requirements.

### Progression to further studies
Once you have completed your Diploma with the required entry score and prerequisite requirements, you will have guaranteed entry into second year of a related undergraduate degree at Monash University.

### AHEGS
The Diploma of Science is an undergraduate award. The course is taught in English. Graduates have gained a range of fundamental knowledge and skills related to the scientific method in both theory and practice, and across a range of science and related disciplines. Students demonstrate their ability to successfully undertake study at the university level. The course takes two years of full-time study or part-time equivalent to complete. The Diploma of Science is a Level 5 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.
