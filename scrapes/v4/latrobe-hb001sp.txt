## Bachelor of Nursing - Singapore (PSB)
##### HB001SP
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Extend your passion for changing lives when you study a Bachelor of Nursing (Post Registration) in Singapore or Mauritius. As a La Trobe student, you'll gain the skills and confidence to care for people of all ages, making ethical, clinical decisions with compassion – and receive the same award as students in Australia.
Through our partnership with the Singapore Nurses Association – PSB or Polytechnics Mauritius, you'll expand your knowledge of nursing practice. A combination of online learning and face-to-face classroom intensives in your location.
After successfully completing the program, you'll be eligible to further enrich your career when you apply for postgrad studies at La Trobe.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Demonstrate therapeutic communication strategies in professional relationships with people, families, and colleagues.
**2.**
Employ health assessment data and use evidence to develop, document and modify evidence-based person-centred care plans in partnership with people across a variety of healthcare settings.
**3.**
Critically review and analyse professional nursing practice and other information to synthesise knowledge to advance the profession individually and as a community.
**4.**
Appraise and monitor the health and wellbeing of self and others and revise practice as needed.
**5.**
Demonstrate the ability to pursue ongoing professional learning and development for self and others through feedback, reflection, and action.
**6.**
Participate in or lead collaborative practice in intra-professional and interprofessional teams to promote optimal health outcomes.
**7.**
Employ evidence-based frameworks to inform systematic and comprehensive person-centred health and wellbeing assessments in partnership with people across various healthcare settings in Singapore.
**8.**
Exhibit inclusive and responsive professional nursing practice that respects individuals from diverse backgrounds with sensitivity to culture, values and beliefs.
**9.**
Demonstrate safe nursing practice in various health settings that is professional, accountable, ethical and legal, underpinned by a sound knowledge base as identified in the SNB Core Competency Domains of RNs and NMBA Registered Nurse standards.

### Course features
### Work based learning (placement) requirements
Compulsory placements (Work Based Learning)
During this course, you will participate in one or more supervised Work Based Learning (WBL) placements designed to allow you to practice newly acquired skills, behaviours and knowledge while integrating theory with practice. The placements will immerse you in professional work culture, enabling you to develop and further enhance your skills. WBL provides you with the opportunity to gain valuable industry experience at one or more of our local, interstate or international industry partners or agencies. WBL placements will generally be arranged by the Office of the Provost Placements and WBL Team at La Trobe University. The number of hours undertaken can vary considerably, depending on the requirements of the course. Students may incur additional costs and it may be necessary to travel or relocate to complete a specific placement. Please refer to the individual subject entries for more information.
### Work integrated learning opportunities
Career development learning, Professional simulations
### Other opportunities
Student exchange and overseas short programs offer a unique opportunity for a meaningful learning and educational experience abroad. La Trobe University encourages all students to make the world their campus by participating in an exchange or overseas short program that fits within your degree structure.
### Career outcomes
Advance your career as a registered nurse by developing your professional knowledge and skills allowing you to explore areas surrounding quality patient care, leadership and inter-professional team management and supervision. Take your career further through the development of nursing knowledge through research and publication. Upon completion, you will be eligible to apply for postgraduate studies at La Trobe University allowing you to develop these skills further.

### Additional costs
Students should allow approximately AUD$100 for a uniform, name badge and equipment. Students can purchase these items from any supplier; however La Trobe University branded uniform must be purchased from a university approved vendor. These items remain the student's personal property.
Prior to commencing a placement, students are required to meet all of the requirements of the degree in relation to immunisation. Students must visit a medical practitioner who will complete and sign the Immunisation and Health Record form.

Depending on a students immunisation history, the overall costs for vaccination may be up to AUD$1030. Students are responsible for all costs incurred. Further information will be provided post enrolment.
Applicants will be required to hold a valid Police Check which is renewed annually. Students will have access to a reduced fee when applying via a La Trobe University provider. For more information on what a police check entails, refer to the Victoria Police website.
First Aid and/or CPR certification is required to meet the professional requirements of the degree. Further information will be provided post enrolment.
Prior to commencing a placement, students will be required to complete a National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) worker screening check. For information please refer to the Services Victoria website. Further information will be provided post enrolment.
Applicants will be required to hold a valid Working with Children Check (WWCC). There is no cost for a volunteer WWCC. For more information and costs refer to the Working with Children Check Victoria website.

Subject: PRIMARY HEALTHCARE IN NURSING AND MIDWIFERY (NSG2005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject will provide you an introduction to primary health care. This subject will explore the role of the nurse in health promotion as a member of the primary healthcare team and look at models that support safe patient care. You will explore the evolution and role of digital health and education in primary health. You will analyse the role of the nurse in coordination of care and patient health education to prevent disease and manage illness. Contemporary developments and future challenges including priority populations health, communicable diseases and climate change will be examined. Using the concept of health priorities to inform health promotion activities.
Learning Outcomes:
- Evaluate the impact of primary health care principles on health outcomes locally, nationally, and internationally
- Analyse how health literacy and health seeking behaviors influence health outcomes in various health populations and regions.
- Critically examine how primary health care philosophies, frameworks and principles are applied in contemporary primary care nursing practice, including health promotion knowledge and skills
- Critically examine the contemporary evolution of primary health in Australia and globally with a focus on climate change and health, priority populations, communicable disease and social and environmental determinants.
- Demonstrate skills in applying health coaching models and principles of health education to work with individuals, groups, and populations to promote health and prevent disease.

Subject: PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE NURSE TRANSITION (NSG204D)
Credit Points: 30
Overview: In 2026 only, and as an interim measure stemming from contractual arrangements with PSB Academy, the delivery of this subject will differ slightly between the Victorian and Singapore instances. The differences are specified within the 2026 subject details. Students located at LTU Australia This is a transition subject to the Bachelor of Nursing as part of the accelerated degree. This subject is specifically for students who have a nursing qualification, either as an enrolled Nurse or a Registered Nurse outside Australia. In this subject, students will work in collaborative teams to develop their understanding of the foundation concepts that underpin effective nursing practice in interprofessional teams. There will be a strong focus on the transition from the role of the enrolled nurse to registered nurse. An introduction to ethics and law as it relates to nursing practice will also be explored. Students will be introduced to the concept of evidenced based practice and the use of research in the degree. Foundational academic skills and information literacy required for successful university study will be prioritised and developed. In addition, students will learn the skills, knowledge and attitudes required by Registered Nurses in the comprehensive assessment, planning, implementation, and evaluation of person-centred, evidence-based care. A lifelong approach to nursing care will be addressed. Students will also have opportunity to revise the theoretical basis of pharmacology including pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics and to the role of controlling the spread of antimicrobial resistance. Responsibilities, practical skills, and scope of practice of the registered nurse in medication administration will be examined and practised. The three-week clinical placement will allow you to integrate theoretical principles to the nursing practice of patients that is underpinned by nursing theory. Students located at PSB Singapore This is a transition subject to the Bachelor of Nursing and designed for students who have a nursing qualification as a Registered Nurse in Singapore. There will be a strong focus on the transition from the diploma qualification to a Bachelor degree. Building on previous knowledge, this subject emphasises the academic skills and information literacy required to find evidence-based information which enables students to use evidence in their assessments and day to day nursing practice. In addition, students will learn the knowledge and skills required by degree qualified Registered Nurses in the comprehensive assessment, recognition of patient deterioration, planning, implementation, and evaluation of person-centred, evidence-based care. This subject also introduces clinical reasoning cycle and reflective practice, which will prepare students to assess clinical situations and facilitate appropriate and timely clinical decision making. Using simulation-based learning, students will be provided an opportunity to practice and demonstrate comprehensive focused health assessments. This subject aims to support students in transitioning from diploma to degree-level nursing practice by developing academic skills, information literacy, and the ability to integrate evidence-based knowledge into clinical decision-making. Also, this subject aims to enhance students’ capability to undertake comprehensive and person-centred care through the development of clinical reasoning, reflective practice, and advanced health assessment skills using simulation-based learning.
Learning Outcomes:
- Singapore students: Employ clinical reasoning to plan, implement and evaluate person-centred care that is culturally relevant, and evidence based for persons across the lifespan
- Singapore students: Demonstrate suitable database searches in relation to nursing practice.
- Singapore students: Practice appropriate health assessment techniques that are systematic and person-centred to elicit subjective and objective health data that considers anatomical, physiological, cultural, and psychosocial factors.
- Singapore students: Incorporate principles of evidence-based research and literacy skills in academic writing.
- Singapore students: Apply the principles of reflective practice to further develop professional nursing practice.
- Singapore students: Critically examine the roles and responsibilities of a registered nurse as members of an intra and interprofessional team.
- LTU students: Employ clinical reasoning to plan, implement and evaluate person-centred care that is culturally relevant, and evidence based for persons across the lifespan
- LTU students: Critically examine the roles and responsibilities of a registered nurse as members of an intra and interprofessional team
- LTU students: Demonstrate suitable database searches in relation to nursing practice
- LTU students: Examine the relevant ethics and law as it relates to nursing practice
- LTU students: Demonstrate and practice safe use of medicines relating to storage, preparation, and administration in a range of settings
- Singapore students: Demonstrate safe nursing practice within scope of practice that is professional, accountable, ethical, and legal underpinned by evidence-based knowledge.
- LTU students: Apply the principles of reflective practice to further develop your professional nursing practice
- LTU students: Incorporate principles of evidence-based research and literacy skills in academic writing
- LTU students: Demonstrate safe nursing practice within scope of practice that is professional, accountable, ethical, and legal underpinned by evidence-based knowledge
- LTU students: Practice appropriate health assessment techniques that are systematic and person-centred to elicit important health data that considers anatomical, physiological, cultural, and psychosocial factors

Subject: HEALTHCARE ETHICS AND LAW (NSG2HEL)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Nursing practice is informed by, and framed within, the principles of law and ethics. This subject begins with an introduction to ethics, including an overview of the ethical theories and principles used in health care. Students are then introduced to the law and to the notion of legal systems while reviewing the sources of law and examining the difference between criminal and civil law. Concepts including 'duty of care' negligence, end of life issues, whistleblowing, resource allocation, and conscientious objection will also be explored as students examine the legal, ethical and professional responsibilities of nursing and accountability in nursing practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify and apply common ethical theories and principles.
- Critically examine contemporary legal and ethical issues within nursing practice.
- Explain the ethical and legal nature of nursing practice.
- Critically reflect upon individual values and how these may influence a nurse's practice.

Subject: LEADERSHIP AND CLINICAL GOVERNANCE IN NURSING AND MIDWIFERY (NSG3004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will extend your knowledge of the components that provide frameworks for accountable and responsible nursing practice. You will explore competency standards, decision making frameworks, direction and delegation guidelines and the Australian safety and quality framework in relation to professional conduct. You will be introduced to the role of contemporary theories of leadership and management, the concept of professional boundaries, health policy, organisational structures, and culture. You will explore the application of digital health in healthcare. Your capacity to engage in your professional role across all practice settings will be enhanced by completion of this subject.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critique contemporary leadership theories, and their importance to individuals, health teams and the nursing and midwifery profession
- Critically review the impact of corporate and clinical governance models in health services in relation to person-centred care
- Appraise contemporary health policy issues and current legislation as they relate to safety and quality in nursing and midwifery and application of digital health
- Critique how organisational structure, organisational culture and contextual variables influence the nursing and midwifery environment and the coordinated approach to person centred care

Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will be given an opportunity to further explore the importance of evidenced based care in health care and you will be encouraged to generate ways of thinking and critique wider health practice to form the foundation for future health practice evolutions. You will look at ways to use health care data in a systematic way and use quality improvement tools and techniques. - Analyse and recommend practice improvement opportunities based on contemporary research
- Conduct a critical review of the literature relevant to your chosen practice improvement topic
- Elicit strategies for individuals and groups to communicate results to a range of audiences for different purposes using a variety of modes
- Identify the components of a health research project using the principles of quality improvement in healthcare

Subject: NURSING PATIENTS WITH CHRONIC ILLNESS (NSG3NCI)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will be given an opportunity to generate ways of thinking that enable sustainable access and delivery of health services to people living with chronic illness. Through the readings, online learning activities, workshops and assessments, you will be consolidating the fundamental knowledge you have gained from previous semesters to be able to select appropriate public health strategies and models of chronic illness management, promote active engagement with health services among people living with chronic illness and recommend ways to improve the sustainability of health services for people living with chronic illness. As such, it is essential to have knowledge on the disease process of chronic illnesses, familiarity with public health approaches and understanding of interdisciplinary practice environments in the acute, sub-acute, residential and community settings to succeed in this subject.
Learning Outcomes:
- Generate evidence-based recommendations for how to improve the sustainability of chronic illness management approaches in terms of their impact on natural, economic, social, political and cultural systems.
- Evaluate chronic illness management methods to determine strategies that promote active engagement of persons, families and carers with health services.
- Design appropriate chronic disease management plans or interdisciplinary team care arrangements based on the lived experiences of persons with chronic illness.
