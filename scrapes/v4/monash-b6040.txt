## Master of Global Business
##### B6040
##### 72 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Master of Global Business is a multi-disciplinary program that advances your knowledge of business and the global environment in which it operates. This Master’s program provides you not only with a comprehensive understanding of the dynamic economic, political and social forces that affect and impact upon global business, but also with the skill set required to function and operate in a global environment. 

The Master of Global Business is designed to build upon your work experience and your undergraduate credentials by further developing and strengthening your business skills and acumen. This program advances your ability to address the global issues and challenges faced by business and society by improving your ability to critically analyse, problem solve and develop solutions. These skills are further enhanced and developed through an appreciation of the global context in which business operates and how it deals with issues of a geo-political nature, business-government relations, trade and its governance, global supply chain, sustainability, social responsibility and intercultural communication. If you are interested in further research within this domain, the Master of Global Business can also provide a pathway into a higher degree by research. 

The Master of Global Business, ultimately looks to produce graduates with a comprehensive and multi-disciplined perspective of global business, that enables them to not only better understand the contemporary global environment in which business operates but just as importantly create and produce innovative solutions for the business, professional, social and public policy communities which they serve.

As part of the Master of Global Business, you can choose to attend the Berkeley Haas Global Access Program for one semester, and receive a Berkeley Haas certificate. For more information refer to the Monash Business School site.

### Structure
The Master of Global Business is structured in three parts. Part A. Core studies, Part B. Capstone studies and Part C. Elective studies

Part A. Core studies

The focus of these studies is to develop your knowledge and skills as a critical and creative professional who can apply their understanding and knowledge of the global business and politics environments in order to contribute to solutions that address contemporary global issues

Part B. Capstone studies

These studies are designed to consolidate the knowledge and skills acquired throughout the study of your course.

Part C. Elective studies

The focus of these studies is to contribute to a portfolio of professional development that enables you to expand your knowledge and skills, in the realm of global business, politics, trade and related disciplines.

Master's entry points

Depending on prior qualifications you may receive entry level credit (a form of block credit) which determines your point of entry to the course:

  * If you are admitted at entry level 1 you complete 72 credit points, comprising Part A, Part B and Part C
  * If you are admitted at entry level 2 you complete 48 credit points, comprising Part A and B

Note: Students eligible for credit for prior studies may elect not to receive the credit and complete one of the higher credit-point options.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
have the ability to critically appraise and apply the knowledge gained to broad range of firms and organisations operating globally
**2.**
be able to incorporate a diversity of world views, to develop and build solutions that address the issues of sustainable development, social development, social responsibility and strategic decision making.
**3.**
be critical and creative scholars who:

  * produce innovative solutions to problems

  * apply research skills to business challenges

  * communicate effectively and perceptively
**4.**
be responsible and effective global citizens who:

  * engage in an internationalised world

  * exhibit cross-cultural competence

  * demonstrate ethical values
**5.**
have acquired the necessary reflective and analytical skills to engage in life-long learning.

### AHEGS
The Master of Global Business is a postgraduate award. The course is taught in English and normally takes one and a half years of full-time study to complete or part- time equivalent. The course involves an interdisciplinary program of study, including an optional experiential global business in practice component, which helps develop graduates' ability to critically appraise and apply the knowledge gained in the program, to a broad range of firms and organisations operating globally. Graduates with a better understanding of differing world views will be able to better develop and build solutions that address a broad range of issues including sustainable development, social responsibility and strategic decision making, while acquiring necessary skills to engage in life-long learning. The Master of Global Business is a Level 9 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.

Subject: Engaging with international business (BEX5722)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This unit requires you to examine a broad range of social, cultural, legal and political issues that can impact on the strategy and operations of businesses operating in a global business environment. Through the use of readings, case studies, and an analysis of current events you are confronted with some specific challenges of doing business in a global context. This unit focuses on developing skills, addressing various global issues and how they affect business, as well as aspects of global citizenship, cultural sensitivity and effective teamwork.
Learning Outcomes:
- recognise issues affecting businesses operating in a global context
- acquire team skills including team leadership, collaboration, problem-solving, decision-making, communication and presentation skills
- develop and present strategic responses to specific issues affecting businesses operating in a global context with due consideration to sustainable, responsible and ethical business practices
- develop reflective practice skills in relation to individual learning.

Subject: Business practice (BEX5861)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This is a practicum unit, part of the Master of Global Business which is underpinned by experiential learning that may take a number of forms including a work placement, a consulting project or a research project among others. You will undertake, either individually or as part of a group, a practicum project defined in consultation with the academic unit leader. The practicum project can be based either domestically or internationally and can be sourced from a diverse range of industries and sectors, including government, private industry and the not-for-profit sector. This practicum unit may also be based on an affiliated arrangement and carried out in association with an organisation although physical location at the organisation may not be required. You are required to satisfy a series of academic requirements that maximise your learning experience. Academic, and where applicable, host organisation requirements may vary. The aim of this unit is to expose you to the skill set relevant to the type of placement or project you are completing. Depending upon the specific arrangements, you may need to communicate your findings and or experience to the host organisation in the form of a report or presentation mutually agreed upon by the host organisation in consultation with the academic unit leader. This practical unit will provide you with the opportunity to engage in real-world business situations and in turn develop your ability to engage and communicate clearly and effectively as is fit for purpose with the intended audience.
Learning Outcomes:
- apply broad discipline knowledge to find innovative solutions to problems
- evaluate self-learning and personal development in an organisational environment
- plan, execute and reflect upon a project with a degree of independence and accountability
- exercise critical thinking and professional judgment in developing new understandings
- collaborate with others on a project in a workplace setting.
- show technical skill in designing, conducting and reporting on a research project
