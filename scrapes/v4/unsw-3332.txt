## Description
The Bachelor of Construction Management and Property (3332) is a three-year full-time (or equivalent part-time) program of study. The program prepares you for management careers in a broad range of construction and property-related organisations.  You will acquire sound foundational knowledge and skills in Construction Management, Property Development and Quantity Surveying, including but not limited to relevant construction and property industry regulations, systems and technologies for construction and property management, sustainability principles, and ethical and professional responsibilities. Coursework throughout the program emphasises innovative methods, technologies and processes specific to the construction and property industries.

The UNSW CMP program has a close relationship with industry. You have the option of enrolling in a dedicated work integrated learning course. The program may also provide real-world experiences by offering site visits, guest lectures and overseas study at various points in the curriculum.

The program is offered over 9 terms, leading to the award of the degree of Bachelor of Construction Management and Property (BCMP).  After completion of the BCMP, you may enroll in a one-year honours program where you have the option to continue your studies in construction management and property or to specialise in building, property development, or quantity surveying.

Subject: Construction Materials (BLDG1012)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Construction Materials covers the primary structural materials adopted in commercial and residential projects in Australia. The course will also introduce you to the concept of life cycle sustainability assessment (LCSA) to select and contrast applicable construction materials.
Learning Outcomes:
- Explain the fundamental material properties relevant to the selection of materials appropriate across various stages of the construction process.
- Discuss concrete mixture design for sustainable and structural requirements.
- Justify construction material selection based on life cycle sustainability assessment.
- Describe the properties and behavior of essential construction materials, including concrete, steel, timber and masonry.

Subject: ADA Professional Placement (FADA6700)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: ADA Work Integrated Learning (WIL) - Professional Placement provides you an opportunity to gain targeted professional work experience, and to explore the dynamics, requirements, and expectations of a workplace. Aligned with the UNSW’s emphasis on work integrated learning (WIL), this real-life experience in a supervised work setting allows you to apply and enhance your disciplinary knowledge and skills. The course supports your placement experience with a reading program that examines scholarly perspectives on professional practice, including the study of organisations and professional development. Placement opportunities can be either self-sourced or advertised by the School.
Learning Outcomes:
- Articulate reflections on university learning with links to the workplace context, with the opportunity to analyse the host using an organisational framework
- Engage in independent, collaborative, and reflective learning towards building student’s professional goals and identity
- Demonstrate capacity to contribute to and work within an organisation, taking responsibility for delegated project tasks in an accountable and ethical way
- Identify and develop a range of skills and knowledge needed to gain future disciplinary employment
