## Master of Professional Accounting - Bendigo
##### BM003B
##### 180 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Become an expert with in-demand skills when you study La Trobe’s Master of Professional Accounting.

In this accredited degree, you’ll build expertise in existing and emerging accounting information systems and data analytics tools. Investigate the fields underpinning modern accounting, including business law, taxation, finance and economics. Connect with industry and professional accounting bodies during your studies and prepare for career success.

Learn how to solve accounting problems across a diverse variety of business contexts. With lectures delivered by leading academics and guest industry experts, you’ll get a well-rounded perspective on contemporary accounting issues – ranging from industry-specific measurement issues to the impact of global harmonisation on financial reporting practices.

La Trobe’s Master of Professional Accounting is a direct pathway to becoming a qualified professional accountant. If you have limited exposure to accounting or are forging a new path, this degree will develop your theoretical and technical knowledge and skills to build your dream career.

You'll learn:

  * Accounting principles, information systems and analytics  * Understand how to record business transactions, prepare financial statements and evaluate business performance. Use the accounting and data analytics tools to analyse data and assist decision making.

  * Management accounting  * Understand how to provide organisational decision-makers with relevant accounting information to assist with strategic planning, controlling and performance management.

  * Financial accounting and reporting  * Apply international and Australian accounting standards involving measurement and professional judgement in a variety of practical scenarios.

  * Auditing and assurance  * Explore the nature and purpose of auditing and assurance. Understand the responsibilities of auditors in the context of reporting quality information.

  * Taxation  * Learn the fundamentals of the taxation system and its implications for individuals and businesses.

### Course features
### Work based learning (placement) requirements
Students have an opportunity to undertake a work placement subject BUS5010 (Business Work Placement) or a work-based project subject BUS5009 (Business Consulting Project) as core-choice.  These subjects are an opportunity to focus on the application of theoretical concepts to address workplace problems or development issues using problem-based enquiry and learning. Students are allocated a host organisation or can identify their own work placement and negotiate a work-based project with the host employer and subject coordinator. The themes of projects, enquiries and/or other work-based activities are individually tailored to the student's professional development needs and the employer's requirements.
### Work integrated learning opportunities
Industry presentations, Industry informed curriculum, Industry-informed projects, Career development learning
### Career outcomes
Accountants and accountancy expertise are vital for organisations in every industry – so after graduation, you could find roles in private enterprise, government departments, the not-for-profit sector and beyond.

Possible roles include:

  * Business analyst
  * Assist with start-up structuring, valuation and financing. Review existing business operations to help with strategy, business planning, and identifying and implementing performance improvements.

  * Financial accountant
  * Take responsibility for financial reporting, including end-of-year financial reports, reconciliations and statutory obligations. Help provide stakeholders with reliable information for decision-making.

  * Management accountant
  * Help your organisation evaluate past performance and plan for the future. Analyse financial and non-financial data to assist in decision-making and advise on the implications of business decisions.

  * Auditor
  * Use modern auditing and data analytics tools to give internal and external clients assurance about an organisation’s operations.

  * Taxation accountant
  * Advise on taxation issues for individuals and organisations. Manage, prepare and submit reports, including tax returns and other statements, and advise clients on how to manage their income.

Subject: PRINCIPLES OF FINANCE (BUA4005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will be introduced to the concepts, theories, and applications of finance. You will examine the role of the financial sector within the economy at large and the role of finance in the management of organisations. A particular focus will be on how investment and funding decisions are made within organisations, including an introduction to capital budgeting and project evaluation. This will lead to an introduction to a wide range of financial markets, including equity and debt markets. The mechanics of each market are examined, and the characteristics of the instruments traded in each market are explored. Consideration is then given to the concepts of risk and return and their application to portfolio theory and asset pricing models.
Learning Outcomes:
- Formulate the risk and return of individual financial assets and portfolios and describe the Capital Asset Pricing Model and its implications when evaluating financial assets' expected return and risk.
- Identify and review the fundamental concepts of capital structure theories and explain the critical factors which impact managerial decisions in developing capital structure and dividend policy
- Identify the financial manager's critical financial decisions, discuss the financial system's primary role, and explain how interest rates are determined.
- Compute bonds and share prices using the time value of money. Evaluate and explain the capital budget decision rules and apply these rules to a firm's investment decisions.

Subject: FOUNDATIONS OF COMPANY AND COMMERCIAL LAW (LST4001)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will explore some of the core principles of contract law, corporations law and the law of negligence. You will discuss various business structures including the law relating to the incorporation of companies and a strong emphasis will be placed on important company law topics such as directors' duties, member rights and remedies, financing of companies and company insolvency. You will examine the purposes of these legal frameworks and analyse the practical operation of these legal principles in the business environment. This subject adopts a skills-based learning approach through teaching the basic techniques of problem-solving and promoting an understanding of the dynamic nature of the law in business and corporations. This is a core subject accredited by CA/CPA.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify legal principles, including case law and statute, relevant to company law and commercial law, applying them appropriately to problem-solving scenarios.
- Identify the key purposes and principles of contract law, the law of negligence and corporations law, including the legal status of corporations, and explain how these purposes and principles apply to commercial transactions.
- Demonstrate advanced knowledge of the key features of the Australian legal system.
- Critically analyse the ethical frameworks for the laws relating to data protection and privacy regulation in business and corporations.
