## Bachelor of Sport Coaching and Development - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### HBSCD

You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Bachelor of Sport Coaching and Development prepares students for employment in coaching, coach education, sport science and sport development.

Students will learn to apply a range of skills in the areas of athlete performance, sport management, fundamental sport science and advanced coaching sciences. The course places a focus on sport management subjects along with subjects addressing more advanced aspects of sport coaching and performance analysis.

Students will also complete core subjects in:

  * human biosciences
  * physiology
  * biomechanics
  * skill acquisition
  * sport psychology
  * talent identification
  * and game development.
Sport coaching placements and internships will allow you to apply your theoretical knowledge to real-life situations. The Bachelor of Sport Coaching and Development also provides a pathway to a range of Allied Health Masters courses, including Sport Analytics, as well as Honours.

The Bachelor of Sport Coaching and Development is an alternative pathway for TAFE Diploma of Sport and Recreation Management/Diploma of Sport Development (Chisholm) to progress to university level study in Sport Coaching and Development.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Communicate and collaborate effectively with clients, mentors and peers, including those from culturally and linguistically diverse communities, using a variety of modes and media
**2.**
Appraise and critically analyse emerging developments in the field of sport coaching and development in order to maintain professional skills and the capacity to evaluate new evidence and applications
**3.**
Work independently and collaboratively to achieve evidence-based solutions to sometimes complex problems
**4.**
Apply theoretical knowledge and skills to professional practice within a range of sport coaching practice environments
**5.**
Demonstrate the application of a range of technical skills and their relationship to the theoretical concepts of sport coaching and development

### Work based learning (placement) requirements
Compulsory placements (Work Based Learning)
During this course, you will participate in one or more supervised Work Based Learning (WBL) placements designed to allow you to practice newly acquired skills, behaviours and knowledge while integrating theory with practice. The placements will immerse you in professional work culture, enabling you to develop and further enhance your skills. WBL provides you with the opportunity to gain valuable industry experience at one or more of our local, interstate or international industry partners or agencies. WBL placements will generally be arranged by the Office of the Provost Placements and WBL Team at La Trobe University. The number of hours undertaken can vary considerably, depending on the requirements of the course. Students may incur additional costs and it may be necessary to travel or relocate to complete a specific placement. Please refer to the individual subject entries for more information.

Industry presentations, Industry informed curriculum, Industry-informed projects, Career development learning
### Other opportunities
Student exchange and overseas short programs offer a unique opportunity for a meaningful learning and educational experience abroad. La Trobe University encourages all students to make the world their campus by participating in an exchange or overseas short program that fits within your degree structure.
### Career outcomes
As a graduate, you could seek employment within major participation sports such as:

  * Australian football
  * soccer and basketball
  * sports academies and schools
  * private sport-focused businesses
  * and in local, state and national governing bodies for sport.

Opportunities also exist internationally in sport development, coach education, and the rapidly expanding global sport ecosystem. Note that local, state and national governing bodies for sport will require additional entry requirements for coaching accreditation at all levels.

Subject: SPORT AND EXERCISE PSYCHOLOGY (PHE3SEP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will focus on the scope, aims and techniques of sport and exercise psychology. The scope of sport psychology is vast and expanding and goes beyond the sporting field. Sport and exercise psychology aims to enhance both performance and wellbeing through the application of psychological principles. This field goes beyond the goal of winning, focusing on improving various aspects of life, including sport, fitness, health and overall enjoyment. This subject combines theory with practical application, employing cognitive-behavioural techniques to boost motivation, reduce competition anxiety, foster teamwork and manage stress in various areas of life such as work, study and family. It also addresses issues such as injury management, promoting physical activity, substance use awareness, working with diverse populations and navigating life transitions. This subject is designed for third-year students. To maximise your chances of success in achieving the intended learning outcomes, it is recommended that you complete this subject in your third- year.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse performance scenarios and identify tailored solutions to influence positive behaviours in sport and exercise settings.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the psychological influences on injury risk, rehabilitation, and performance to improve outcomes for individuals and multi-disciplinary teams.
- Collaborate as part of a team to analyse the impact of psychological factors on athlete performance, and develop an evidence-based intervention to improve performance.
- Understand and apply Sport and Exercise Psychology theories and evidence to increase performance in sport and exercise settings.

Subject: TALENT IDENTIFICATION AND GAME DEVELOPMENT (SPE3TID)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: You will appraise and apply criteria of athlete talent identification from theoretical models and practical assessment. You will analyse and compare how athletes are identified in various sports through the key criteria of athlete development. You will be introduced to and discuss the theoretical frameworks and models used to identify and develop talented athletes. Taking a multidisciplinary approach, you will use a variety of assessments for the purpose of identifying talented athletes and measuring their development. You will be introduced to and appraise the potential outcomes of and the justifications for the use of talent identification and development.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically review factors which can have an impact on the development of talented athletes
- Review and assess potential outcomes and use of talent identification and game development with key stakeholders
- Justify and implement multidisciplinary approaches to talent identification and development
- Review and analyse the frameworks/models used within talent identification and development
- Develop talent identification and development protocols based on evidence-based studies
