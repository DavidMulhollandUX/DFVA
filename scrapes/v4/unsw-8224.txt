## Description
This program provides postgraduate students with the opportunity to study two complementary programs offered in UNSW Arts and Social Sciences to create a combined two year Masters program in professional and interdisciplinary areas.

The Combined Masters degree program offers students an interdisciplinary combination of programs that will substantially extend and deepen their existing skills, knowledge and understanding in professionally relevant areas of study and teach advanced skills in analytical thinking and research.

Upon completion, students may be awarded up to two of the following awards:

- Master of Education – MEd
- Master of Educational Leadership - MEdLead
- Master of Translation – MTran
- Master of Environmental Management – MEM
- Master of Applied Linguistics – MAppLing
- Master of Journalism and Communication – MJournComm
- Master of Public Relations and Advertising – MPRA
- Master of International Relations – MIR
- Master of Development Studies – MDS
- Master of Public Policy and Governance – MPPG

### Notes for Masters in International Relations/Development Studies/ Public Policy and Governance
If you combine two Social Sciences specialisations (i.e. International Relations A and/or Development Studies A and/or Public Policy and Governance A), you only need to complete one Research Core Course for your two Masters specialisations. You can complete additional Elective Courses to meet the minimum UOC requirement.

### Notes for Masters of Education
If you combine two Masters of Education specialisations, you only need to complete one research methodology and research project in your two Masters specialisations. You can complete any two additional MEd elective courses for your other specialisation.

If you combine a Masters of Education (TESOL) specialisation with a Master of Applied Linguistics you cannot take any LING electives in your MEd.

## Progression & Academic Rules
You may exit the program with only one of the constituent degrees if the requirements for that program have been met but if so, you will not be able to take out the combined degree at a later date.

## Admission Requirements
**Limitations on Recognition of Prior Learning**:
The maximum credit transfer for a Masters is 50% of the specialisation, that is, up to 24 UOC for each specialisation.

**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
A recognised Bachelor degree (or equivalent qualification) and requirements specific to the individual Master (one year, 48 UOC) programs chosen.

===== SPECIALISATION / AREA OF STUDY =====
### Public Policy & Governance A (SRAPES)
<p><strong>The following information applies to students commencing from 2022.</strong></p>
<p><strong>If you commenced prior to 2022, please refer to the specialisation requirement of the year you started.</strong></p>
<p>Policymaking plays a key role in the management and change of the economy and society. Policy is part of every field of practice, which has led to a rapid growth in the need for policy professionals in government, business, and social and political organisations.</p>
<p>The Master of Public Policy and Governance (MPPG) applies a social science perspective to questions of policy and management in modern organisations. You will acquire a solid grounding in policy analysis, policy evaluation and social research, the necessary skills and knowledge domains required for effective engagement within the policy process. You will be able to specialise in an area of direct relevance to your own interests, which may include Social Policy, International Development Policy or Policy Management.</p>
<p>The program will prepare you for professional practice, by fostering analytical skills and a practical appreciation of the processes of policymaking, the significance of research and evidence in driving policy change and the role of stakeholders in effective policy implementation. The Master of Public Policy and Governance places emphasis on developing the skills and perspectives needed for proficient assessment, evaluation and sensitivity to the effectiveness of policy programs in terms of the impact they have on clients, customers and the public.</p>
<p>Unique to the UNSW Master of Public Policy and Governance is the significant policy and research experience of the program&#39;s teaching staff. At UNSW, the staff involved in the MPPG have had direct experience either as policy makers or policy researchers in government, non-government organisations and, the business sectors. Combined, they have over 20 years direct experience working at high levels inside government, from providing policy support to the Council of Australian Governments through to conceptualising, designing and implementing large scale policy across the states. Staff are also actively involved with non-government organisations in providing effectively policy research and evidence to support their advocacy and lobbying activities. These teaching staff bring their real life experience to the classroom, revealing the relationship between theory, research and everyday practice.</p>
<p>The Master of Public Policy and Governance aims to prepare you for work which requires analytical skills and a practical appreciation of the processes of policy-making and implementation. The program is oriented to the practice of policy, and prospective students are required to have relevant work experience. This may be in the public sector, unions, business or community organisations.</p>
