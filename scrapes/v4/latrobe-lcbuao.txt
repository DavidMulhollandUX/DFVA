## Graduate Certificate in Business Administration - On-Line
##### LCBUAO
##### 60 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The La Trobe Graduate Certificate in Business Administration is an AI-first, fully online qualification from La Trobe University designed for professionals who want to develop contemporary management capability for an AI-driven economy. Delivered in flexible six-week study blocks, you can study from anywhere while building practical expertise in strategy, financial analysis, innovation and AI-enabled decision-making. The course provides a direct pathway into the La Trobe MBA.

You will complete four subjects from La Trobe University’s online MBA curriculum, studying alongside MBA students and gaining exposure to postgraduate-level management learning.

Through subjects including Generative AI for Business, Navigating the AI Economy, Entrepreneurial Thinking and Innovation, and Strategy, you will learn how to apply generative AI and analytical frameworks to managerial decision-making, innovation and organisational performance. You will develop the ability to evaluate opportunities, design responsible innovation initiatives and formulate value-creating strategies in global, technology-driven markets.

The course emphasises evidence-informed managerial judgement, ethical and responsible leadership, and the integration of environmental, social and governance (ESG) considerations into business transformation. Fully credited into the La Trobe MBA, the qualification allows you to progress with advanced standing while building practical leadership capability for organisations adopting AI and advanced analytics.

Through a curriculum co-designed with global industry leaders and supported by La Trobe University’s collaboration with OpenAI, you will develop human-centred leadership capability alongside AI-enabled business expertise.

By studying the Graduate Certificate of Business Administration, you’ll learn how to:

  * apply generative AI and analytical tools to interpret economic, financial and organisational information

  * use AI insights and evidence to support managerial decision-making in complex business environments

  * analyse opportunities and design strategic responses using structured frameworks, financial analysis and risk assessment

  * apply ethical, environmental, social and governance (ESG) and responsible AI principles in management decisions

  * communicate AI-informed strategies clearly to diverse organisational stakeholders.

Subject: NAVIGATING THE AI ECONOMY (BUA4006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Navigating the AI Economy develops advanced economic and analytical capability for organisational decision making in an AI-driven global economy. The subject applies key economic concepts and frameworks to business, organisational, and societal challenges shaped by artificial intelligence, data, and automation. You will use digital and AI-enabled tools to interpret economic data, analyse market behaviour, and evaluate financial and organisational performance. Emphasis is placed on critical thinking, problem solving, and the construction of sound economic arguments to support evidence-based strategic decisions in complex and uncertain environments.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse and interpret economic data, market behaviour, and organisational decision making using appropriate economic and financial frameworks to support evidence-based inquiry and decision making.
- Develop and communicate coherent economic arguments that apply AI-enabled analysis to contemporary business and policy challenges, drawing on relevant economic concepts, models, and evidence.
- Evaluate the economic, social, and ethical implications of AI-driven decision making for organisations, markets, and society, applying economic reasoning to assess responsible and sustainable practices and outcomes.

Subject: STRATEGY (BUA5005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Strategy develops your ability to design, assess, and implement value‑creating strategies for organisations operating in complex, technology‑infused and generative AI‑driven business environments. The subject integrates contemporary strategic management principles with insights from responsible AI leadership, preparing you to lead ethically and strategically as AI reshapes competitive landscapes. You will examine strategic tools and frameworks used by organisations ranging from startups to multinational enterprises and evaluate how strategic choices are influenced by global markets, technological disruption, and evolving regulatory expectations. The subject builds your capability to assess competitive position, evaluate strategic options, and support strategic decision making as AI and digital technologies transform industries. Emphasis is placed on aligning strategy with organisational goals, risk, and long‑term performance in dynamic and uncertain environments.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and justify value-creating strategies that align organisational goals, risk, and long-term performance, demonstrating ethical and socially responsible judgement.
- Apply leadership and teamwork skills to develop, communicate, and support the implementation of strategy in diverse organisational contexts.
- Critically analyse strategic positions and options in technology and AI-driven business environments to support informed and evidence-based decision making.

Subject: ENTREPRENEURSHIP AND INNOVATION (BUA5ENP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Entrepreneurial thinking and innovation develops an entrepreneurial mindset and advanced capability to identify, evaluate, and lead innovation initiatives in both business start‑ups and established organisations. The subject integrates creative problem‑solving with Artificial Intelligence (AI) initiatives to support new venture creation, business growth, digital transformation, and organisational change. You will explore how entrepreneurs and intrapreneurs recognise opportunities, develop and test business concepts, and apply innovation frameworks to the design of new products, services, processes, and ventures. Through practical analysis and applied activities, you will assess feasibility, risk, and scalability across start‑up and enterprise contexts.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design viable business concepts and innovation initiatives by integrating creative problem solving with strategic, financial, and risk analysis to justify recommendations for implementation.
- Justify strategic alignment decisions for innovation ventures by critically evaluating market opportunities and organisational context to support evidence-based entrepreneurial leadership and contemporary entrepreneurship issues.
- Critically evaluate entrepreneurial opportunities in start-up and organisational contexts using contemporary entrepreneurship, innovation, and AI frameworks to produce evidence-based assessment of viability and strategic fit.

Subject: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR BUSINESS (BUS4005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the nature and function of business. However, business leaders are challenged by the effective and competitive adoption of AI. This subject provides you with an industry-ready skillset to empower productivity, innovation and decision-making across the business ecosystem. You will learn to apply prompting techniques, build and evaluate AI chatbots, construct and articulate AI strategies. Alongside AI capabilities, you will recognise and address the risks of AI, ethics and cybersecurity through a deep understanding of local and international guidelines, regulations and governance. You will foster an AI-first mindset for the effective and responsible practice of AI in any business or industry environment.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply and critically evaluate prompting techniques and AI tools in diverse business contexts
- Analyse and critically evaluate AI strategies and policies to support responsible adoption in varied business environments
- Design and develop in teams, AI chatbot solutions that address complex business challenges, demonstrating alignment with ethical principles, governance frameworks, and cybersecurity standards.

Subject: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY MODULE (LTU0AIM)
Credit Points: 0
Overview: The academic integrity module introduces academic integrity standards to inform and support students in avoiding plagiarism and academic misconduct. Students complete four parts that encompass academic misconduct and academic integrity decisions, including cheating, plagiarism, and collusion. Students learn about Turnitin (the text-matching tool) used at La Trobe University, and how to get help and support their development of referencing skills.
Learning Outcomes:
- Understand the importance of the acknowledgment and the referencing of sources
- Locate, select, and utilise information about La Trobe's approach to academic integrity to support appropriate acknowledgement of references used.
- Recognise and understand academic integrity responsibilities.
