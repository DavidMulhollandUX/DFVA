## Graduate Certificate of Business (Executive)
##### B4002
##### 24 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Give yourself and your organisation a competitive edge with the Graduate Certificate of Business (Executive).

This program is innovative and industry-focused, with a strong emphasis on professional development and excellence in business practice to help you stay at the forefront of your field. Flexible in its delivery, we design our leadership and executive education studies to ensure you have the toolkit to answer challenges faced by individuals, organisations and communities. You will graduate from this program with an advanced set of professional and technical skills, which will enable you to assist businesses to succeed in complex and dynamic environments.

This program is suited to early-career professionals to senior leaders who want to extend their leadership and business skills and knowledge. It is available for individuals from organisations wanting to undertake executive education at Monash Business School, with programs specifically tailored to encourage better decision making, more creative thinking and to stay up-to-date with the latest developments in the field.

The course comprises 24 credit points of study.

Units are 6 credit points unless otherwise stated.

You must complete 24 credit points from any combination of the following units:

  * LEE5001 Advanced leadership 1

  * LEE5002 Advanced leadership 2

  * LEE5003 Advanced leadership 3

  * LEE5004 Advanced leadership 4

  * LEE5010 Advanced leadership 5 (12 credit points)

  * LEE5011 Advanced leadership 6 (12 credit points)

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
apply advanced business knowledge in the practice of leadership, management and organisational roles.
**2.**
be responsible and effective global citizens who:

  * engage in an internationalised world

  * exhibit cross-cultural competence

  * demonstrate ethical values
**3.**
be critical and creative scholars who:

  * produce innovative solutions to problems

  * apply research skills to business challenges

  * communicate effectively and perceptively
**4.**
be able to provide advanced discipline-based solutions relevant to the business, professional and/or public policy context

### Progression to further studies
Successful completion of this course may provide a pathway to the Graduate Diploma of Business (B5001) and/or a post-experience master course offered by the Faculty of Business and Economics subject to satisfying the appropriate entry requirements. Credit may be granted towards other Faculty of Business and Economics postgraduate courses upon successful completion.

### AHEGS
The Graduate Certificate of Business (Executive) is a postgraduate award. The course is taught in English and normally takes half a year of full-time study or part-time equivalent. Graduates develop advanced knowledge of leadership, management and organisational roles within various business contexts to enable them to provide appropriate solutions. The Graduate Certificate of Business (Executive) is a Level 8 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualifications.  The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.

Subject: Advanced leadership 1 (LEE5001)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This unit is designed to develop and refine your leadership skills so you can survive and thrive in the new world of work. A combination of theory and practice will enable you to refine key leadership capabilities which can be immediately applied in commercial and organisational contexts. An applied approach will be taken involving in-class practice and assignments reflecting real-world challenges.
Learning Outcomes:
- evaluate personal learning and personal development in a complex business environment
- develop a deeper understanding of the difference between theory and practice
- communicate effectively and perceptively in a business environment.
- apply research skills and provide innovative solutions to complex business problems
- apply theory and ethical values to practice in a supervised environment
Assessment Tasks:
- 2 - Written: 60% (Hurdle: None)
- 1 - Presentation: 40% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: Minimum total expected workload to achieve the learning outcomes for this unit is 144 hours per semester typically comprising a mixture of scheduled learning activities and independent study. Independent study may include associated readings, assessment and preparation for scheduled activities. You are expected to complete all pre-class activities prior to your scheduled class, and post-class activities should be completed after your scheduled class. Learning activities may include a combination of teacher directed, peer directed and online engagement activities.
