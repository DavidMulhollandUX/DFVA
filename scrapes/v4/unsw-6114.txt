## Description
The University Preparation Program (UPP) is an enabling program for adults aged 20 and over who either do not possess formal qualifications or whose academic qualifications do not satisfy the minimum entry requirements for admission to university study;

and for adults aged 17-19 (at the time of commencement) who have a min 50 ATAR and who satisfy any of the following criteria:

- Qualify under the UAC Educational Access Scheme (EAS) criteria, or
- Gateway Admissions Pathway (GAP) applicant, or
- Are registered as part of the Elite Athletes, Performers and Leaders (EAPL) Program, or
- Be an Indigenous applicant applying via UNSW's Nura Gili Indigenous Admissions Scheme (IAS).

The program includes a mix of academic/maths skills courses and faculty-based courses so that students can experience what it is like to study in a university environment and also gain a qualification to apply for university. The courses are designed to assess students' academic potential and to provide a realistic environment for students to effectively develop their skills.

Students must pass all courses in the relevant UPP stream to satisfactorily complete. Upon satisfactory completion of the UPP students will be assessed for entry into UNSW degree programs based on their WAM.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

1. Apply foundational academic skills needed for success in first-year university study.
3. Demonstrate discipline-specific knowledge relevant to the area of study.
4. Collaborate respectfully with peers and staff in a university environment.
2. Communicate clearly and confidently across a range of mediums.
5. Recognise ways in which university study can contribute to addressing future societal challenges.

## Progression & Academic Rules
Progression rules

- Students must take REGZ9000 in their first study period. Students must pass REGZ9000 before they can take a Faculty course.

- Students who are in the Science, Business and Engineering streams must pass REGZ9070, before REGZ9072. Students in the Engineering stream must pass REGZ9072 before enrolling in REGZ9073.

Program requirements

- Students can take a course up to 3 times (so long as they complete in the maximum allowable time).

- Maximum allowable time is 3 years.

- To complete the program, students must have a passing grade in all courses required for the stream.

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
Students must satisfy either of the following requirements:

be aged 20 years (at the time of commencement) or older, and not possess formal qualifications for university study or whose academic qualifications do not satisfy the minimum entry requirements for admission to university study; or

be aged 17-19 (at the time of commencement) who have a min 50 ATAR and who satisfy any of the following criteria:

- Qualify under the UAC Educational Access Scheme (EAS) criteria, or

- Gateway Admissions Pathway (GAP) applicant, or

- Are registered as part of the Elite Athletes, Performers and Leaders (EAPL) Program, or

- Be an Indigenous applicant applying via UNSW's Nura Gili Indigenous Admissions Scheme (IAS)

be aged 17-19 years (at beginning of program) and have min ATAR 50 and satisfy the Educational Access Schemes (EAS) criteria, including attending Gateway schools, the Elite Athletes, Performers and Leaders (EAPL) Program or be an Indigenous applicant applying via UNSW's Nura Gili Indigenous Admission Scheme (IAS)

Subject: Mathematics Skills 1 (REGZ9070)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: REGZ9070 Mathematics Skills 1 is designed to provide a level of competency in mathematics for students who have not studied HSC Mathematics (or equivalent) at high school or have not met the assumed level of knowledge of their intended degree and who are enrolled in the Science, Business or Engineering streams of the University Preparation Program (UPP). Students would normally be expected to already possess a level of knowledge equivalent to Year 10 Mathematics. REGZ9070 covers basic and further topics in arithmetic and algebra, coordinate geometry and introduces functions.
Learning Outcomes:
- Model, analyse and solve problems involving linear, quadratic and cubic functions.
- Manipulate and solve linear equations.
- Develop the skills to prepare and organise for university style of learning.
- Apply basic transformations to sketch graphs.
- Expand, factorise and simplify algebraic expressions.
- Manipulate complex algebraic expressions involving algebraic fractions.
- Identify features of exponential and hyperbolic curves.
- Recognise features of the graphs of circles.
- Solve measurement problems by using scientific notation to represent numbers and rounding to a given number of significant figures.

Subject: Mathematics Skills 2 (REGZ9072)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: REGZ9072 Mathematics Skills 2 allows students who have successfully completed REGZ9070 Mathematics Skills 1 to engage with the material at a deeper level and consolidate the understandings acquired in REGZ9070. Topics will include an introduction to differential calculus, trigonometry and an introduction to integration.
Learning Outcomes:
- Determine indefinite and definite integrals of simple functions
- Develop the skills to prepare and organise for university style of learning
- Solve problems involving financial decisions, such as calculating the present or future value of an annuity
- Communicate mathematical ideas effectively using correct terminology
- Apply the derivative to find the equation of a tangent or normal to a graph at a given point
- Analyse graphs of exponential functions and determine the relationship between exponential functions and their derivatives
- Demonstrate and apply the power, product, quotient and chain rules to differentiate functions
- Apply arithmetic and geometric sequences and series to solve problems
- Interpret the meaning of the derivative
- Apply concepts and techniques from probability to present and interpret data and solve basic problems

Subject: Mathematics Skills 3 (REGZ9073)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: REGZ9073 Mathematics Skills 3 is a continuation of Mathematics Skills 2 to broaden and expand student's mathematics knowledge in preparation for their intended degree. - Solve problems using appropriate statistical processes
- Apply algebraic and graphical techniques to analyse trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions
- Develop the skills to prepare and organise for university style of learning
