## Graduate Diploma in Business - On-Line
##### LGBO
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Graduate Diploma in Business is a comprehensive program designed for graduates and professionals seeking to deepen their understanding of core business principles and enhance their management capabilities. This program provides a solid foundation in key areas such as management, marketing, accounting and finance, economics, sustainability, and artificial intelligence for strategic decision-making, equipping students with the skills necessary to navigate today's dynamic business environment.

Ideal for those aiming to advance their careers, shift into a business-focused role, or pursue further postgraduate studies such as a Global Master of Business Administration, the diploma blends academic theory with practical application. Students will develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and leadership skills through case studies, group projects, and real-world business scenarios.

Whether you're looking to upskill, reskill, or build a pathway into a master’s program, the Graduate Diploma in Business offers the flexibility and professional relevance to support your goals.

You'll learn:

  * Financial management  * Acquiring financial skills, including financial analysis, introductory accounting, and investment strategies.

  * Global marketing and consumer behaviour  * Adapting marketing strategies to diverse cultural, demographic, and economic conditions.

  * Ethics, sustainability, and corporate social responsibility (CSR)  * Understanding the importance of ethical practices in a global business environment and analysing sustainable business practices and their implementation internationally.

  * Leadership and organisational behaviour  * Understanding organisational behaviour in a global context, including team dynamics, conflict resolution, and motivation strategies.

  * Digital transformation and technology  * Understanding the impact of emerging technologies on global business and exploring the role of artificial intelligence (AI) in modern business management.

  * Economics  * Apply economic concepts and frameworks to current issues of concern for organisations and society.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Apply emerging technologies and digital tools, including artificial intelligence, to inform evidence-based decision-making and strengthen alignment with strategic business priorities.
**2.**
Evaluate and apply management strategies, operational practices, and analytical tools to enhance organisational effectiveness and performance.
**3.**
Integrate ethical reasoning, sustainability principles, and Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) considerations to develop responsible business solutions with social, global, and environmental impact.
**4.**
Apply business, economic, financial, and marketing concepts to make informed, strategic decisions in diverse and dynamic organisational contexts.
**5.**
Demonstrate leadership, communication, and teamwork skills to address complex business challenges and engage internal and external stakeholders effectively.

### Career outcomes
A Graduate Diploma in Business is a versatile qualification that can open doors to a wide range of roles across industries. While it's often used as a stepping stone into more advanced business education like a GMBA, it also provides enough practical and theoretical knowledge to access mid-level and entry-level positions — or even pivot careers.

Possible roles include:

  * Corporate management and change consultant
  * Work with businesses to solve complex problems, improve performance, and implement strategy and change.

  * Business development officer
  * Identify new business opportunities, build client relationships, and help increase revenue.

  * Operations coordinator/manager

  * Ensure day-to-day business operations run smoothly and efficiently, often managing people, processes, and budgets.

  * Project coordinator
  * Manage the day-to-day tasks of a project to ensure it stays on schedule and within budget.

  * Marketing coordinator
  * Supports the planning and execution of marketing campaigns globally.

  * Corporate sustainability officer
  * Support organisations broader sustainability strategies.

  * AI Strategy associate or consultant
  * Help organisations become more AI-ready or AI-driven.

  * Business administrator
  * Manage the operational and administrative functions of a company or department.

  * Executive assistant
  * Support senior executives or managers, often acting as a gatekeeper and organiser.

Overview: You will be introduced to key dimensions of management and marketing in diverse organisational contexts. Informed by the need for sustainable value creation in contemporary organisations, you will be able to explore how external and internal environments influence management, sustainability and marketing behaviour and practice. You will engage with these issues by considering and analysing real world examples, informed by theoretical and analytical frameworks.

- Conduct a business environmental analysis and communicate findings effectively in writing for a variety of stakeholders.

- Critically analyse key management concepts and trends for use in contemporary business decision making.

Subject: ACCOUNTING AND FINANCE FOR MANAGERS (BUA4002)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Accounting and finance are critical skills for all professionals in the modern global environment. In this subject, you will be provided with an introduction to key concepts, theories and practical applications associated with accounting and finance in diverse organisational contexts. You will be exposed to content which will cover the nature of the accounting and financial environment and the types of accounting and financial practice and decision-making required from a business perspective.

- Construct, interpret, analyse and communicate information contained in a firms financial statements using appropriate digital and information tools.
- Examine and apply financial mathematics tools and techniques to determine cash flows, cost of capital, capital investments.

Subject: ECONOMICS FOR GLOBAL LEADERS (BUA4003)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Economics impacts on all aspects of business and society. Key individual, organisational and government decisions around the allocation of scarce resources are at the core of economics, and an understanding of economic concepts, frameworks and language can greatly enhance a leader’s capacity to make sound decisions and influence the decisions of those around them. This subject develops students’ knowledge of economic concepts and provides the opportunity to apply this knowledge to real world situations. Students will apply economic concepts and frameworks to current issues of concern for organisations and society. Students will learn how to evaluate and apply critical aspects of economics such as the main market forces of supply and demand; the choices producers make in relation to their costs; revenues and profit; competitive and monopoly market structures and how these affect market prices; the use of game theory to make more strategic economic decisions; measuring a nation's income and the cost of living; the drivers of economic growth and the causes of unemployment.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply economic frameworks to business and social problems for policy and decision-making.
- Construct and present logical and persuasive economic arguments and communicate concepts professionally.
- Research and apply economic concepts to predict how changes in economic conditions may impact individuals, businesses and industries within market structures.
- Critically analyse economic data to explain relationships between economic variables and their impacts on policy, business and individuals.

Subject: STATISTICS FOR BUSINESS AND FINANCE (BUA4004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop basic data-analysis skills to investigate real-life problems in business and finance. You will focus on basic concepts in statistics and probability theory, methods of statistical inference, measures of linear relationship between variables including linear regression analysis. There is a strong emphasis on the application of these techniques to practical problems in business and finance using Microsoft Excel. The contents of the subject are in line with the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) quantitative analysis curriculum. Successful completion of this subject will lay a solid foundation for the further study of data analysis in business and finance.
Learning Outcomes:
- Ability to present work in a professional manner and to communicate effectively to a professional audience
- Use statistical principles and research skills in a collaborative team environment to solve practical problems and communicate the findings to people with various viewpoints
- Apply probability theory in business and financial decision making
- Apply and interpret concepts of descriptive statistics to real life data and interpretation of results
- Conduct estimating linear regression models and testing hypothesis to aid decision-making

Subject: MANAGEMENT PRACTICE AND THEORY (MGT5MPT)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will examine the main characteristics of contemporary management practice and theory, and its importance to the ethical, efficient and effective operation of modern organisations. You will investigate the various organisation design options and draw on theoretical models, academic research and current thinking to determine the factors that influence the selection of an appropriate organisational design and management model for competitive advantage and sustainability. You will be guided by global and local case studies and organisational design principles to illustrate the practical application of people and operational management, and advance your analytical and management skills.
Learning Outcomes:
- Create innovative organisational designs and management models that ensure competitive advantage and sustainability in modern organisations.
- Assess the roles of management and change management in determining organisational processes and outcomes in dynamic environments.
- Collaborate effectively in diverse teams to complete complex tasks or structure projects in culturally diverse employment settings, fostering equality and well-being.
- Evaluate organisational problems and formulate evidence-based organisational design and people-management solutions.

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MGT5STR =====
Subject: GLOBAL BUSINESS STRATEGY (MGT5STR)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will learn how strategy is formulated, articulated, and enacted in a global context. We will combine ideas, theories and tools from strategic management, international business and innovation to take an interdisciplinary approach to build a comprehensive understanding of the management of organisations operating and competing domestically and across national borders. The subject will enable you to understand the challenges of businesses competing in the global arena and will help you develop the knowledge and skills needed to design strategies that aim for sustainable competitive advantages and increased firm performance. Through this subject you will become aware of key political, socio-economic, and cultural dynamics and trends that characterise the globalised business environment and the ethical and social responsibility consideration when doing business and strategising in a global scale.
Learning Outcomes:
- Appraise and apply the concepts and tools of business strategy in a global context.
- Integrate theories and techniques in the analysis of the strategic management options available to a wide range of different types of organisations as the operate and compete in the global arena.
- Critically analyse and critique arguments for and against ideas on how global innovation may enable a firm to achieve positional advantage and superior performance.
- Demonstrate knowledge of the main theoretical approaches to global strategic management and understand the economic, social and ethical and contexts that give rise to their formulation.
