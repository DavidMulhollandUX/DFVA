## Description
The double degree Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) / Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) (BE Hons) is a 5.7-year program (full-time).

The Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) is designed for aspiring students in creative fields who are open minded, curious, and inventive, and who wish to immerse themselves in their creative practice. The BFA is home to distinctive specialisations in Animation and Moving Image, Art Theory, Music, and Visual Arts, each of which is led by experts in your field and supported by specialist facilities.

The UNSW Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) is designed to equip you for a career as a professional engineer. We provide you with a solid background in mathematics, natural sciences and computing that will prepare you for learning knowledge and skills in an engineering discipline specialisation. You will get the chance to apply yourself to engineering design and enquiry projects, professional engineering practice, engineering and technical management and conduct research towards your thesis project.

## Professional Recognition & Accreditation
Accreditation

The Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) component of this double degree program has the same accreditation status as the Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) stand alone program. For details, please see 3707 Engineering (Honours).

Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) Progression Requirements

Students must make satisfactory progress while studying in the BE (Hons) program. Students must show cause not to be excluded from the program if any of the following occur:

- Fail a core course 2 or more times;
- Fail more than half of the courses attempted, after completing 84 UOC or more of the BE (Hons) major;
- Have an overall WAM less than 50.000 with 48 UOC or more of your BE (Hons) remaining.

Students must also satisfy UNSW progression requirements. For more information on university policy on progression requirements please visit Academic Progression.

Subject: Industrial Training (ENGG4999)
Credit Points: 0
Overview: " Exposure to professional engineering practice is a key element in differentiating a professional engineering degree from an applied science degree." (Engineers Australia). To satisfy the EA expectations of a graduating engineer, students need to have exposure to professional practice. At UNSW, this means the equivalent of 60 days of relevant Engineering Professional Practice. Students get first-hand experience in an engineering-practice environment. Students source their own placement to gain employability skills. Placements can be combination of traditional internships (minimum of 30 days) and UNSW industry collaborated projects. This course is to confirm that students have completed their Engineering Professional Practice requirements. Students must gain approval for each (up to 3 placements can be undertaken) placement by submitting the required information via InPlace. Once 60 days have been completed in InPlace, students are required to submit a Final Report via Teams Classroom. When all the requirements have been completed, a satisfactory grade will be awarded to ENGG4999. Opportunities, information and InPlace submission links can be found on the Engineering Professional Practice Student Portal .
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply professional and technical capabilities for industrial problem solving.
- Communicate with a broad range of stakeholders in a professional context.
- Demonstrate evaluative judgement, ethical conduct and accountability in professional practice.
- Work independently and collaboratively.
