## Description
The Graduate Certificate in Business Psychology provides a focused introduction to the application of psychological principles in organisational contexts. As workplaces face increasing complexity in decision making, leadership, and employee experience, professionals require a sound understanding of how behavioural factors influence performance and organisational outcomes.

This fully-online program develops foundational capability in analysing behaviour at individual and group levels, interpreting evidence, and applying established psychological frameworks in business settings. The Graduate Certificate is suited to professionals seeking to strengthen their understanding of workplace behaviour while also providing a pathway into the Graduate Diploma or Master of Business Psychology for students wishing to deepen their expertise.

## Program Structure & Degree Requirements
Students must complete 24 Units of Credit (UOC) structured across the following academic components:

### Core Courses (18 UOC)
Students must take 18 UOC of the following courses.

- **ZZSC5111**: Behaviour in Organisations [6 UOC]
- **ZZSC5112**: Research and Evidence in Organisations [6 UOC]
- **ZZSC5113**: Behavioural Science and Decision Making [6 UOC]

### Elective Courses (6 UOC)
Students must take 6 UOC of the following courses.

- **ZZBU6402**: Employee Wellbeing and Engagement [6 UOC]
- **ZZBU6409**: Change Management [6 UOC]
- **ZZBU6413**: Leadership Development [6 UOC]

## Progression & Academic Rules
Students who successfully complete the Graduate Certificate may articulate into the Graduate Diploma in Business Psychology or the Master of Business Psychology with full credit for courses completed.

## Admission Requirements
**Limitations on Recognition of Prior Learning**:
Advanced standing or RPL are not permitted for this program.

**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
- A Bachelor degree or higher-level qualification (or equivalent) in any discipline; OR

- Applicants without a Bachelor or higher-level qualification may be considered for entry to a Graduate Certificate based on a minimum of six years of relevant professional experience.

“Relevant professional experience” includes responsibility for tasks within a business or organisational context that involve understanding, influencing, or supporting individual and group behaviour. This may include experience in people management, human resources, recruitment and selection, organisational development, change initiatives, learning and development, employee wellbeing, customer or stakeholder engagement, or applied behavioural analysis. Experience may be gained through supervisory roles, participation in workplace change or improvement projects, or roles requiring the application of behavioural insights in commercial, public, or not-for-profit settings. Part-time roles and internships undertaken during undergraduate studies will not be counted.

Subject: Change Management (ZZBU6409)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Organisations are increasingly having to adapt and change in response to rapid changes in their environment. Yet, some estimates suggest that up to 75% of all organisational change fail to deliver the results that were anticipated. As there are many factors which influence the success and failure of organisational change initiatives, this course introduces you to different theories of change alongside the tools and techniques that are associated with effective change management. You will have the opportunity to apply these frameworks and insights to the development of a change management plan that can be applied to organisations today.
Learning Outcomes:
- Explain and Justify appropriate theories and models of organisational change
- Consider how interpersonal relationships and group dynamics influence the success and failure of organisational change
- Identify a range of application tools and techniques as part of effective change management
- Apply your understanding of change management theory alongside available tools for practice to implement changes for individuals and organisations

Subject: Leadership Development (ZZBU6413)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: The current global labour skills gap crisis has turned focus towards human resources. Growing complexity, uncertainty and increasing regulation in the labour market, has highlighted the importance of a purpose driven culture and the need for leadership skills, and qualities, at every level of an organisation. This course will equip students with the framework to implement leadership development programs to ensure contemporary and viable leadership within an organisation. Learning Outcomes:
- Identify and address challenges in developing and nurturing effective leadership at various organisational levels
- Develop strategies to communicate effectively with internal and external stakeholders
- Leverage ethical and responsible leadership to cultivate sustainable team and organisational excellence
- Critically evaluate different approaches to advance and implement effective leader development initiatives
