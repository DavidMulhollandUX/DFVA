### Course duration (full time)


### Structure
The Monash Advanced Preparation Program consists of four units. Each unit in the program is designed to build advanced skills needed to ensure a smooth transition into the first-year of university. The program runs for 16 weeks, including a week of orientation activities and one-week mid-course break.

### Requirements
You must complete:

  * MPP1010 Introduction to Academic Communication

AND

You must complete three of the following units:

  * MPP1040 Introductory Mathematics for Business or MPP1050 Applied Mathematics

  * MPP1140 Media Studies (not offered in Semester 1, 2026)

  * MPP1060 The Modern World (not offered in Semester 1, 2026)

  * MPP1030 Introductory Economics

  * MPP1070 Psychology

  * MPP1080 Physics Foundations (not offered in Semester 1, 2026)

  * MPP1090 Biology Foundations (not offered in Semester 1, 2026)

  * MPP1020 Accounting for Business

  * MPP1100 Chemistry Essentials

  * MPP1110 Cyber and Digital Foundations

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
demonstrate capability to responsibly engage in an internationalised world with cross- cultural competence exhibiting ethical, sustainable and inclusive values
**2.**
communicate confidently and effectively in the English language
**3.**
demonstrate capability to learn and work collaborating effectively to achieve common goals while demonstrating empathy and understanding of different perspectives
**4.**
demonstrate flexibility in different ways of thinking and learning
**5.**
demonstrate capability to learn and work independently with integrity and responsibility using reflective practice to shape your future learning
**6.**
demonstrate understanding and engagement with the world around you using multiple literacies
**7.**
analyses and evaluates information to solve problems by making judgements and producing innovative solutions

### Progression to further studies
Upon successful completion of this program, you will have guaranteed entry into the first year of an undergraduate degree at Monash University, provided that you meet the required entry score for your chosen course, including prerequisites.
