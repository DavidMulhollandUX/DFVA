This program introduces students to principles and practices of Immersive Media. Students will develop foundational skills and understandings in areas related to immersive media such as physiological and psychological impact, design principles and technical knowledge. They will develop key industry understandings, and will put their skills into practice in developing an applied project plan and designing and executing an immersive media project.
The Graduate Certificate in Diploma in Immersive Media Technologies is an AQF Level 8 qualification with a standard full-time duration of 1 year.

## Program Learning Outcomes
Differentiate between immersive media technology platforms.
Understand the physiological and psychological impact on users within immersive media frameworks.
Apply design theory and relevant methodologies to immersive media development.
Demonstrate knowledge of industry frameworks in relation to data, analytics and market trends.
Design and execute a project relevant to immersive media.
Demonstrate broad technical knowledge across a variety of hardware and software platforms and pipelines.
Construct an immersive media project management plan based on industry practices.

Subject: Immersive Media Design (MDIA5011)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: The design paradigms associated with immersive media technology are in a constant state of experimental change. Adequate design choices not only provide users with comfortable immersive technology experiences, but also have the potential to impact platform dependency and technological applicability. In this course, students will form deep contextual understanding of the physiological and technological design paradigms associated with immersive media development. Through facilitated hands-on experimentation with various platforms, software frameworks and game-engines, students will form critical analysis of existing systems of design and develop new knowledge in relation to next-generation technologies.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify and analyse physiological response characteristics associated with design paradigm implementation.
- Demonstrate comprehension via the application of context specific design terminology within immersive media technology frameworks.
- Critically differentiate between HMD (Head Mounted Display/Device) technologies and software in relation to design implementation.
- Develop and formulate a feasible design brief incorporating design paradigm understanding.
- Categorise the benefits/shortcomings of immersive media technology platforms from a design understanding perspective.

Subject: Immersive Media Asset Generation (MDIA5012)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: Without digital assets, immersive media content would not be immersive. Asset generation in this space can differ from other mediums, as robust optimisation is paramount to success. In this course, students will cover all aspects of asset creation/development for immersive media technologies everything from 3D modelling, photogrammetry, and texturing through to audio recording and sound design. Students will have the opportunity to become adept at using numerous industry standard software packages and platforms, as well as receiving guidance to experiment with their own artistic visions.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate development pipeline understanding in regard to core digital asset generation.
- Produce digital assets fit for immersive media technology platforms.
- Identify compatibility issues with digital asset generation.
- Synthesise immersive media design theory knowledge into practical output.
- Demonstrate technical knowledge across a variety of hardware and software platforms used for digital asset development.

Subject: Immersive Media Business (MDIA5013)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: The immersive media industry is one of the fastest growing, most applicably diverse media areas we have witnessed for decades. In this course, students will not only explore the historical milestones that have brought us to this level of technological feasibility, but also the business practices currently in use within both entertainment and simulation contexts. Students will additionally have the opportunity to explore the ethical controversies/concerns facing the immersive media industry as it matures.
Learning Outcomes:
- Define, categorise and critically differentiate between immersive media technologies and platforms.
- Identify and explain roles/responsibilities associated with immersive media technology development.
- Interpret and assess historical milestones.
- Make predictions associated with immersive media technology business models and technological advancements.
- Critically assess ethical controversies/concerns through understanding of immersive media technology business frameworks.

Subject: Immersive Media Development (MDIA5014)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: In this course, students will author and develop cutting-edge standalone immersive media experiences. Students will not only have the opportunity to explore managerial methods and techniques commonly used in the immersive media industry, but will also cover technical topics relevant to next-generation creative professionals. These topics include game-engine use and initial setup methods, SDK (Software Development Kit) integration, asset management, lighting techniques, rendering methods, coding and scripting differences, and platform-oriented final build optimisation.
Learning Outcomes:
- Construct and manage an immersive media development project in line with industry practices.
- Demonstrate development pipeline understanding in regard to digital asset infusion within game-engine frameworks.
- Integrate and adapt immersive media design paradigms within a fully-functioning immersive media experience.
- Demonstrate technical knowledge across a variety of hardware and software platforms used for immersive media content development.
