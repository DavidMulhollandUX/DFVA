### Overview
The Graduate Certificate in Business is a foundational postgraduate program designed for individuals seeking to develop essential business knowledge and skills. It is ideal for professionals aiming to broaden their understanding of core business disciplines or those considering a pathway into further postgraduate study, such as a Graduate Diploma in Business or Global Master of Business Administration.

This flexible and practical program introduces key areas including management, economics, accounting and finance, and artificial intelligence for business. It emphasises analytical thinking, problem-solving, and effective communication—skills that are highly valued across industries and sectors.

Whether you're looking to enhance your career prospects, transition into a new field, or gain a formal business qualification, the Graduate Certificate in Business offers a fast-track and focused option to achieve your goals.

You'll learn:

  * Management and organisational behaviour  * Understanding organisational behaviour in a global context, including team dynamics, conflict resolution, and motivation strategies

  * Financial management  * Acquiring financial skills, including financial analysis, introductory accounting, and investment strategies.

  * Artificial intelligence (AI) in business  * Apply prompting techniques, build and evaluate AI chatbots, construct and articulate AI strategies.

  * Economics  * Apply economic concepts and frameworks to current issues of concern for organisations and society.

### Course features
### Career outcomes
A Graduate Certificate in Business is a shorter, more foundational qualification than a Graduate Diploma or Master's degree. It’s often used for:

  * career changers wanting to enter the business field
  * professionals looking to upskill or specialize
  * a pathway into more advanced business study, like a GMBA.

Despite its shorter length, it can still open doors to a range of roles — especially if you already have work experience or a degree in another field. Possible roles include:

  * Business administrator
  * Manage the operational and administrative functions of a company or department.

  * Executive assistant
  * Support senior executives or managers, often acting as a gatekeeper and organizer.

  * Operations coordinator/manager

  * Ensure day-to-day business operations run smoothly and efficiently, often managing people, processes, and budgets.

  * Business development officer
  * Identify new business opportunities, build client relationships, and help increase revenue.

  * AI Strategy associate or consultant
  * Help organisations become more AI-ready or AI-driven.

Subject: SUSTAINABLE MANAGEMENT AND MARKETING (BUA4001)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: You will be introduced to key dimensions of management and marketing in diverse organisational contexts. Informed by the need for sustainable value creation in contemporary organisations, you will be able to explore how external and internal environments influence management, sustainability and marketing behaviour and practice. You will engage with these issues by considering and analysing real world examples, informed by theoretical and analytical frameworks.
Learning Outcomes:
- Work collaboratively to assess marketing problems and solutions from a value exchange perspective to promote sustainable value creation in real-life business situations.
- Conduct a business environmental analysis and communicate findings effectively in writing for a variety of stakeholders.
- Integrate sustainable management and marketing ideas and apply the conventions of attribution and citation accurately.
- Critically analyse key management concepts and trends for use in contemporary business decision making.

Subject: ACCOUNTING AND FINANCE FOR MANAGERS (BUA4002)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Accounting and finance are critical skills for all professionals in the modern global environment. In this subject, you will be provided with an introduction to key concepts, theories and practical applications associated with accounting and finance in diverse organisational contexts. You will be exposed to content which will cover the nature of the accounting and financial environment and the types of accounting and financial practice and decision-making required from a business perspective.
Learning Outcomes:
- Construct, interpret, analyse and communicate information contained in a firms financial statements using appropriate digital and information tools.
- Examine and apply financial mathematics tools and techniques to determine cash flows, cost of capital, capital investments.
- Prepare budgets, manage working capital, and make appropriate pricing and production decisions.
- Examine the importance of basic accounting and finance principles, and differentiate the appropriate sources of finance for a firm.

Subject: ECONOMICS FOR GLOBAL LEADERS (BUA4003)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Economics impacts on all aspects of business and society. Key individual, organisational and government decisions around the allocation of scarce resources are at the core of economics, and an understanding of economic concepts, frameworks and language can greatly enhance a leader’s capacity to make sound decisions and influence the decisions of those around them. This subject develops students’ knowledge of economic concepts and provides the opportunity to apply this knowledge to real world situations. Students will apply economic concepts and frameworks to current issues of concern for organisations and society. Students will learn how to evaluate and apply critical aspects of economics such as the main market forces of supply and demand; the choices producers make in relation to their costs; revenues and profit; competitive and monopoly market structures and how these affect market prices; the use of game theory to make more strategic economic decisions; measuring a nation's income and the cost of living; the drivers of economic growth and the causes of unemployment.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply economic frameworks to business and social problems for policy and decision-making.
- Construct and present logical and persuasive economic arguments and communicate concepts professionally.
- Research and apply economic concepts to predict how changes in economic conditions may impact individuals, businesses and industries within market structures.
- Critically analyse economic data to explain relationships between economic variables and their impacts on policy, business and individuals.

Subject: STATISTICS FOR BUSINESS AND FINANCE (BUA4004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop basic data-analysis skills to investigate real-life problems in business and finance. You will focus on basic concepts in statistics and probability theory, methods of statistical inference, measures of linear relationship between variables including linear regression analysis. There is a strong emphasis on the application of these techniques to practical problems in business and finance using Microsoft Excel. The contents of the subject are in line with the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) quantitative analysis curriculum. Successful completion of this subject will lay a solid foundation for the further study of data analysis in business and finance.
Learning Outcomes:
- Ability to present work in a professional manner and to communicate effectively to a professional audience
- Use statistical principles and research skills in a collaborative team environment to solve practical problems and communicate the findings to people with various viewpoints
- Apply probability theory in business and financial decision making
- Apply and interpret concepts of descriptive statistics to real life data and interpretation of results
- Conduct estimating linear regression models and testing hypothesis to aid decision-making
