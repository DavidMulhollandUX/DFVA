## Diploma of Business
##### U0603
##### 96 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Diploma of Business is your first step towards completing your Monash Business or Commerce related degree. You’ll learn how to work independently, think critically and make analytical decisions, while being introduced to the business concepts and theory you'll need for University.

You can choose your area of interest from day one – better preparing you for your chosen Monash bachelor degree. There are two streams to choose between:

Business stream – provides you with the foundational knowledge and skills you need to progress to a Bachelor of Business, where you will have the flexibility and opportunity to develop broad business knowledge as well as specialised expertise in one or two chosen areas of business practice.  Alternatively, if you have a specific area of interest, this stream also provides the opportunity to progress to an specialised business degree, for example accounting, marketing, digital business or banking and finance degree.

Commerce stream – requires a higher level of mathematics and leads to the Bachelor of Commerce where you can study in areas such as economics, finance, actuarial studies and accounting.

In part 2 of this Diploma you’ll study the subjects equivalent to first-year University students and develop your specialist knowledge of business or commerce.

In the Diploma of Business, you can also select to undertake the Education stream, which upon completion allows you to progress to the Bachelor of Education (Honours) and Bachelor of Business double degree. During your studies in part 2, you will complete the same number of units as a single discipline diploma. You'll complete two education studies units and 10 days of professional work placement as part of your diploma.

### Structure
The course is structured in two parts: Part 1. Foundation knowledge and Part 2. Discipline knowledge.

Part 1: Foundation knowledge

In this part, across the first two trimesters, you will study eight core business units that provide the foundational knowledge in business with the opportunity to develop focus and prepare you to progress to part 2 in your chosen stream.

Part 2: Discipline knowledge

In this part, across your final two trimesters, you will study units equivalent to first-year university students and develop your specialist knowledge of business or commerce.  In this part you will study eight units, comprising six core units and two elective units that align with your interests and the degree you wish to apply for.

In this part you also have the opportunity to complete Education studies stream, which allows you to develop foundational knowledge in either primary or secondary education. In the Education stream you will complete two education studies units and 10 days of professional work placement. Note: only the Business discipline in the Diploma of Business is available if you choose the Education stream.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Critically apply theoretical and technical skills to solve problems in relation to a range of Business disciplines.
**2.**
Identify and communicate advice in a variety of Business disciplines to address problems in accord with management requirements.
**3.**
Utilise technical skills to demonstrate understanding and problem solving in relation to Business issues involving diverse stakeholders.
**4.**
Demonstrate knowledge of technical and theoretical issues in a variety of Business theory and technical and language skills with depth in some areas.
**5.**
Manage work priorities and coordinate the work of others in accord with parameters set by management.

### Progression to further studies
Once you have completed your Diploma with the required entry score and prerequisite requirements, you will have guaranteed entry into second year of a related undergraduate degree at Monash University.

### AHEGS
The Diploma of Business is an undergraduate award. The course is taught in English. Graduates have gained a range of technical and problem solving skills relevant to the business sector. Students demonstrate their ability to successfully undertake study at the university level. The course takes two years of full-time study or part-time equivalent to complete. The Diploma of Business is a Level 5 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.
