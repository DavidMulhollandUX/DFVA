Overview

If you’re passionate about teaching and are keen to build a rewarding career in secondary school teaching, La Trobe's Master of Teaching (Secondary) is for you. Get the skills, experience and confidence you need to make a real difference in the classroom.
Feel completely prepared from your first day. Through a combination of practical and evidence-based learning, you’ll gain the confidence and knowledge to help shape the lives of our next generation.
You'll develop up to two curriculum specialisations that you'll take straight into the classroom when you graduate. Choose from science, languages, arts, psychology, physical education and many more. Or delve deeper in your area of interest with a specialisation in drama, mathematics, physical education, business management, commerce and business, science and visual arts.
Learn the most up-to-date skills for the modern classroom. Understand the needs of diverse learners and gain the skills and experience to succeed in any classroom. You’ll prepare for classroom teaching with extensive in-school experience and guidance from our highly experienced and dedicated teaching academics.
Become the best teacher you can be. With extensive real-world teaching experience, you'll learn how to manage classrooms, design class plans, guide students, and create a safe and engaging learning space for your students.
We focus on practical learning and our students learn about creating relationships from day one. You’ll be embedded in your community from your first semester and will be mentored by community leaders. Understand how to connect with every student as an individual and develop meaningful relationships with students, parents and the wider school community.
We are committed to social justice and diversity. With campuses across metropolitan, regional and rural Victoria, we collaborate with a range of communities on the things that matter most to them and us.
Your study experience will be flexible and suited to your needs. You’ll enjoy a combination of online, on campus and in-schools learning, making it easier to balance study and life commitments.
Study in a supportive learning environment. You’ll be mentored by our experienced and dedicated teaching staff. Learn from leading academics and researchers in the field of learning sciences and develop strong professional networks. Throughout your studies, you'll learn the latest evidence-based teaching techniques and explore innovative pedagogies and learning sciences.
Graduate ready to convert your love of learning into a rewarding career.
You’ll learn:
  * **Discipline expertise**
    * Learn essential skills to effectively teach up to two specialist areas of your choice.
  * **Classroom management**
    * Learn how to confidently manage a classroom. Prepare for classroom teaching with extensive in-school experience and guidance from our highly experienced and passionate teaching academics.
  * **Inclusion and diversity**
    * Learn how to create safe, diverse learning environments for people of different abilities and backgrounds. Develop skills to connect with parents and the wider school community.
  * **Indigenous perspectives**
    * Understand the histories, cultures, traditions, languages and diversity of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, and learn how to provide education that’s most appropriate to their needs.
  * **Digital learning**
    * Explore the world of digital learning in a way that's creative, collaborative and innovative.

### 
Additional explanatory information 
Students are allocated their discipline pedagogy area/s on application. This allocation is based on the studies previously undertaken. This allocation is not to be changed without approval from the course coordinator.
If your two approved discipline specialisations are combined within one subject area (for example, Health and Physical Education, or Visual Arts and Drama), or if you are completing a single approved discipline specialisation, you will not take a second pair of discipline pedagogy subjects. Instead, you will enrol in EDU4032 and EDU4033, as well as the two subjects for your approved discipline specialisation.

Work based learning (placement) requirements 
**_Compulsory placements (Work Based Learning)_**  
During this course, you will participate in one or more supervised Work Based Learning (WBL) placements designed to allow you to practice newly acquired skills, behaviours and knowledge while integrating theory with practice. The placements will immerse you in professional work culture, enabling you to develop and further enhance your skills. WBL provides you with the opportunity to gain valuable industry experience at one or more of our local, interstate or international industry partners or agencies. WBL placements will generally be arranged by the Partnerships Operations team at La Trobe University. The number of hours undertaken can vary considerably, depending on the requirements of the course. Students may incur additional costs and it may be necessary to travel or relocate to complete a specific placement. Please refer to the individual subject entries for more information.

Subject: ARTS SECONDARY PEDAGOGIES 1 (EDU4016)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, pre-service teachers (PSTs) will develop their understanding of what it means to be a secondary Arts (Drama and/or Media and/or Visual Arts) educator at levels 7-10. In this subject, PSTs will engage critically with approaches to curriculum, pedagogy, planning, designing, assessing and evaluating appropriate to Arts education as well as preparation for the senior secondary Arts subjects. PSTs will be introduced to relevant curriculum and pedagogical theories, developing, and justifying a pedagogical approach towards Arts education. They will engage in critique of a selected secondary Arts curriculum document and employ the Understanding by Design (UbD) planning model to design an appropriate secondary assessment task and rubric and to ‘backwards design’ a learning and teaching sequence appropriate to prepare secondary learners for the task.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the concepts, substance and structure of the content of relevant secondary Arts curriculum.
- Demonstrate ability to ‘backwards design’ an appropriate learning and teaching sequence using the Understanding by Design (UbD) planning model, knowledge of student learning, content and effective teaching strategies.
- Demonstrate ability to organise content into an effective learning and teaching sequence for summative assessment using knowledge of relevant secondary Arts curriculum and UbD planning model, including design and justification of an assessment task and rubric.
- Demonstrate ability to organise content into a sequence enabling various forms of diagnostic and formative assessments for the purposes of evaluation, reflection and goal setting, using knowledge of relevant secondary Arts curriculum.
- Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of various pedagogical approaches relevant to teaching secondary Arts curriculum.
- Demonstrate ability to organise content into an effective learning and teaching sequence using knowledge of relevant secondary Arts curriculum, SOLO taxonomy and Bloom’s Revised Taxonomy.

Subject: ARTS SECONDARY PEDAGOGIES 2 (EDU4017)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, pre-service teachers (PSTs) will develop their understanding of what it means to be a senior secondary Arts educator (Drama or Media or Visual Arts). - Demonstrate ability to ‘backwards design’ an appropriate learning and teaching sequence using the Understanding by Design (UbD) planning model, knowledge of student learning, content and effective teaching strategies (including literacy and numeracy teaching strategies appropriate to Arts).
- Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of various pedagogical approaches relevant to teaching senior secondary Arts curriculum.
- Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the concepts, substance and structure of the content of relevant senior secondary Arts curriculum.
- Demonstrate ability to organise content into an effective learning and teaching sequence for summative assessment using knowledge of relevant senior secondary Arts curriculum and UbD planning model, including design and justification of an assessment task and rubric.

Subject: ENGLISH SECONDARY PEDAGOGIES 1 (EDU4018)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, pre-service teachers (PSTs) will develop their understanding of what it means to be a secondary English educator at levels 7-10. PSTs will learn how to effectively deliver reading and writing instruction that will lead students to understand what mastery in English skills, including text analysis and creative writing, can look like. They will engage critically with approaches to curriculum, pedagogy, planning, designing, assessing, and evaluating appropriate to English and English as an Additional Language education as well as preparation for the senior secondary English subjects. PSTs will engage with a variety of worked examples to understand best practice for planning tasks and lessons and explicit modelling of key English skills that is curriculum-aligned. PSTs will be introduced to relevant theories about curriculum and pedagogy. They will engage in critique of a selected junior secondary English and English as an Additional Language curriculum document. The Understanding by Design (UbD) planning model will be used to design an appropriate English secondary assessment task and rubric and to ‘backwards design’ a learning and teaching sequence appropriate to prepare secondary learners for the task, with particular focus on appropriate sequencing and scaffolding of materials.
