## Bachelor of Exercise Science - Bendigo
##### HBESB
##### 360 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Turn your passion for physical health into a rewarding career with La Trobe’s Bachelor of Exercise Science. Understand the scientific foundations of human movement and fitness, then use your knowledge to help individuals reach peak health and performance. Work with real athletes and teams – from recreational to elite – through our extensive network of sports partners. You’ll graduate ready for a dynamic career in sport, exercise or rehabilitation.

Explore the field of human biosciences and understand the foundations of how we function. From there, you’ll build your knowledge in exercise physiology and psychology, as well as human anatomy, movement and learning.

Hone your ability to help athletes achieve sporting success by carefully fine tuning their individual training. You’ll also discover how to improve community health and behaviours by working with groups such as the elderly, adolescents and athletes undergoing rehabilitation.

Learn from lab sessions and case studies, as well as lectures, seminars and workshops. Furthermore, you’ll gain hands-on experience and build professional relationships through industry placements. You could have the opportunity to work with the Bendigo Spirit Women’s basketball team, the Bendigo Pioneers under-18 AFL team, high school elite athlete sports programs or the Bendigo Academy of Sport. You will also gain experience delivering exercise to apparently healthy clients in local gyms and hospitals.

Interested in taking your skillset even further? The Bachelor of Exercise Science offers you the strongest pathway to postgraduate degrees such as La Trobe’s Master of Exercise Physiology and Master of Physiotherapy Practice.

Graduate with the skills to develop training programs for a range of clients, including corporate employees, amateur sportspeople, soldiers and elite athletes.

You’ll learn:

  * Human biosciences  * Learn the fundamentals of human life by exploring the anatomical organisation of the body and the basics of cell structure and function.

  * Health and wellbeing  * Investigate the social, environmental and biomedical factors that influence health and wellbeing in Australia and around the world.

  * Nutrition  * Understand the principles of a healthy diet by looking at the major food sources, the functions of macronutrients and micronutrients, and the principles of energy balance.

  * Exercise fitness and health  *  Discover the vital role that exercise plays in our lives by examining the relationship between physical activity, sedentary behaviour and lifestyle-related disease.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Evaluate movement and devise safe and effective programs to improve athletic performance, correct improper technique, prevent injuries and regain physical fitness after injury through applied knowledge integrating the sub-disciplines of exercise science.
**2.**
Combine nutritional, physiological, biomechanical, motor and psychological principles with exercise advice to improve the effectiveness of health and wellbeing interventions.
**3.**
Adapt individualised exercise programs following pre-participation screening, risk appraisal, and exercise and performance assessments.
**4.**
Evaluate acute and chronic responses to exercise to develop effective programs that encourage individuals to incorporate regular physical activity into a healthy lifestyle.
**5.**
Determine the relationships between physical activity, sedentary behaviours and lifestyle-related diseases using evidence-based approaches.
**6.**
Work effectively in teams to assess the influence of personal and social factors on individuals’ exercise capacity, motivation to participate in regular physical activity; and influence on growth and development.
**7.**
Clearly, concisely and coherently communicate through various modalities, necessary ethical and professional practices.
**8.**
Critique physiological, psychological, social and environmental influences on participation and adherence to a physically active lifestyle.

Subject: ADVANCED EXERCISE BIOMECHANICS (EXS3AEB)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject will advance your theoretical and practical understanding of exercise and sports biomechanics. In this subject you will explore the relationship between biomechanical principles of human movement that underpin common activities of daily living to observe, describe, measure and analyse gross body movements relevant to performance in sport and exercise. This subject will examine the biomechanics of human walking and running gait and the changes which occur with development and ageing. This subject will introduce you to principles of tissue mechanics and will explore the contribution of biomechanics to the field of sports injury prevention.
Learning Outcomes:
- Interpret the biomechanical properties of human tissue and analyse adaptations to loading, over and under use, aging and pathological function.
- Evaluate injury occurrence and predisposing factors across sport and exercise performance including assessment of injury mechanisms.
- Analyse and evaluate (individually and in groups) biomechanical data in order to solve quantitative problems involving linear and angular kinematic and kinetic quantities.
- Critically assess appropriate measurement methods and perform and report biomechanical measurements involving temporal- spatial, kinematic and kinetic quantities.
- Evaluate the stages of development and patterns of muscular activity for normal gait, and identify biomechanical parameters that are commonly assessed in clinical gait analysis, including aging and pathological function.

This subject combines theory with practical application, employing cognitive-behavioural techniques to boost motivation, reduce competition anxiety, foster teamwork and manage stress in various areas of life such as work, study and family. It also addresses issues such as injury management, promoting physical activity, substance use awareness, working with diverse populations and navigating life transitions.
