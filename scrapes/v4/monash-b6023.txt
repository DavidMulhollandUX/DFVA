## Master of Commerce
##### B6023
##### 96 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Master of Commerce provides pre-doctoral training for high-achieving students who intend to pursue a Monash PhD. The course comprises advanced coursework and research methodology training designed to prepare you to undertake deep and effective doctoral research when you transition to the PhD. During the course, you will design and undertake independent research in your specialisation, working closely with a supervisor who will provide individual guidance and academic advice.

The Master of Commerce is the compulsory doctoral pathway for the Banking and finance specialisation.

The Master of Commerce is the preferred doctoral pathway for the following specialisations:

  * Accounting

  * Econometrics and business statistics

  * Economics

  * Health economics

  * Management

  * Marketing studies

Upon successful completion of the Master of Commerce, students may progress to the PhD program in the relevant discipline, subject to meeting selection standards.

### Structure
The course is structured in two parts: Part A. Research studies and Part B. Advanced research studies.

Part A. Core research studies

These studies will enable you to develop a deep understanding of the latest research approaches within the discipline and develop your skills in conducting research on complex business issues.

Part B. Advanced research studies

These units will equip you with foundational expertise and advanced theoretical and methodological knowledge necessary for successful doctoral research within the discipline, with the opportunity to work with a range of academic staff to further develop your research skills and aspirations.

Master's entry points

Depending on prior qualifications you may receive entry level credit (a form of block credit) which determines your point of entry to the course:

  * If you are admitted at entry level 1 you must complete 96 credit points, comprising Part A and Part B.
  * If you are admitted at entry level 2 you must complete 48 credit points, comprising Part B.

Note:

If you are eligible for credit for prior studies you may elect not to receive the credit and complete one of the higher credit-point options.

For Semester 2 commencement, only applicants who meet the Entry Level 2 requirements will be considered.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
demonstrate the ability to design relevant research-informed solutions that respond to business, professional and public policy communities
**2.**
be proficient at applying conceptual frameworks and methodologies to analyse complex problems arising in a variety of contexts, and be able to apply discipline-relevant theories and strategies.
**3.**
be responsible and effective global citizens who:

  * engage in an international world

  * exhibit cross cultural competence

  * demonstrate ethical values
**4.**
be critical and creative scholars who:

  * produce innovative solutions to problems

  * apply research skills to business challenges

  * communicate effectively and perceptively

### Progression to further studies
Successful completion of this course may provide a pathway to a graduate research degree.

### AHEGS
The Master of Commerce is a postgraduate award. The course is taught in English and normally takes two years of full-time study or part-time equivalent. Graduates develop advanced research, analytical and communication skills in their chosen specialisation. The Master of Commerce is a level 9 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.
