##### HC005O
##### 60 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Improve patient outcomes through faster and more effective prescribing.

La Trobe's Graduate Certificate in Registered Nurse Prescribing is designed to meet the growing demand for nurse prescribers in Australia, equipping registered nurses with the skills and knowledge to prescribe scheduled medicines. The course is developed by experienced educators with strong ties to La Trobe’s nursing alumni network.

Learn the practical skills and knowledge to prescribe safely and confidently within established collaborative frameworks – improving patient outcomes and increasing access to medications, especially in underserved areas and rural communities. Graduates of the Graduate Certificate in Registered Nurse Prescribing may be eligible for endorsement as a Registered Nurse Prescriber with the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA) provided they have evidence of the required clinical supervision following completion of their studies.

At La Trobe, you can make study fit around your life commitments with flexible and accredited online delivery, tailored to fit the busy schedules of working nurses across urban, regional, and remote areas – enabling experienced nurses to take their next step into advanced nursing roles while balancing their current careers.

You'll learn:

  * Therapeutic medication management
  *   * Develop and refine your understanding of medication management and its application to patient cohorts.
  * Explore the legislative and regulatory frameworks that underpin Registered Nurse Prescribing in Australia and explore funding systems such as the PBS.

  * Pharmacotherapy
  *   * Develop your knowledge and skills to assess the effects of medication on the human body.
  * Explore the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of medications and their relation to the treatment of illness and injury.

  * Health assessment
  *   * Develop advanced health assessment skills to support evidence-based nursing care of people across the lifespan in rural health care contexts.
  * Integrate knowledge of pathophysiology, assessment and diagnostic findings to make informed clinical decisions and prioritise nursing care.

  * Advanced pathophysiology
  *   * Explore the transition from health to illness through an in-depth understanding of cellular function, genetic and epigenetic influences, inflammation, ageing, and systemic physiological alterations.
  * Investigate key organ systems, and the pathophysiological changes associated with common and complex conditions encountered in clinical setting.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Formulate frameworks and models for partnered prescribing in the healthcare setting.
**2.**
Apply medicolegal and legislative principles of Quality Use of Medicines for the RN prescriber.
**3.**
Demonstrate competence in safe and ethical prescribing within the registered nurse scope of practice.
**4.**
Contextualise the roles and responsibilities of an RN prescriber in relation to scope of practice.
**5.**
Interpret pathophysiology and assessment findings in relation to pharmacotherapy management of a patient/client.

Subject: ADVANCED PATHOPHYSIOLOGY FOR HEALTH PROFESSIONALS (NRS4007)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject provides essential knowledge to support safe, evidence-based care within advanced scope of practice. The subject explores the transition from health to illness through an in-depth understanding of cellular function, genetic and epigenetic influences, inflammation, ageing, and systemic physiological alterations. You will investigate key organ systems, and the pathophysiological changes associated with common and complex conditions encountered in clinical settings. There is emphasis on the clinical relevance of altered physiological parameters and how these inform assessment, diagnosis, treatment, and prescribing decisions. You will develop advanced clinical reasoning skills and the ability to interpret assessment findings using appropriate scientific terminology. This course bridges theoretical knowledge with real-world application, enabling students to make informed, person-centred decisions in their advanced area of practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Communicate using precise scientific and clinical terminology to describe disease processes, analyse findings, and evaluate intervention outcomes.
- Analyse the cellular and systemic mechanisms underlying disease, including disruptions in homeostasis, nerve conduction, and physiological function.
- Critically evaluate the roles of genetics, epigenetics, inflammation, and ageing in the progression from health to complex disease states
- Interpret pathophysiological changes in clinical assessment data to support evidence-based practice.
- Integrate advanced clinical reasoning with pathophysiological knowledge to inform safe and effective decision-making in specialty practice.

Subject: THERAPEUTIC MEDICATION MANAGEMENT FOR RN PRESCRIBERS (NSM4020)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop and refine your understanding of medication management and its application to your patient cohorts. You will explore the legislative and regulatory frameworks that underpin RN Prescribing in Australia, as well as develop the skills and knowledge around partnered prescribing principles and relay this to your workplace context. You will revisit concepts learned in NSM5004 and apply these concepts to your patient context and link these to practical prescribing principles. You will identify the barriers and enablers to RN prescribing and explore funding systems such as the PBS and its application to RN prescribing and scope of practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply safe prescribing practices within applicable legislative and regulatory frameworks and compliance with the Quality Use of Medicines framework
- Justify safe and effective rationale for medication management to achieve therapeutic goals, based on review and analysis of relevant patient information (including comprehensive patient assessment, diagnostics and thorough patient history).
- Appraise credible and appropriate sources of medication information and evidence to implement an evidence-informed approach to RN prescribing.
- Demonstrate knowledge of partnered prescribing in relation to RN scope of practice.
- Implement partnered clinical decision-making processes to tailor appropriate medication plans to meet therapeutic goals, patient informed choices and holistic patient management
- Utilise clinical knowledge to rationalise the indications for prescribing and/or use of non-pharmacological therapies in the treatment of patients.

Subject: PHARMACOTHERAPY FOR RN PRESCRIBERS (NSM5004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject will develop your knowledge and skills to assess the effects of medication on the human body. It will explore the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of medications and their relation to the treatment of illness and injury in the human body. You will apply these principles to identify and illustrate the rationale and clinical reasoning skills, within an RN prescriber framework and scope of practice, to evidence your clinical decision making around pharmacotherapeutic patient management.
Learning Outcomes:
- Rationalise the alterations in pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics in patients across the lifespan of and within those of diverse ethnicities.
- Rationalise advanced clinical practice knowledge and assessment to the pharmacological management of pain.
- Articulate principles of pharmacology, including the differences between pharmacodynamics and pharmacokinetics (the manner in which medications are absorbed, distributed, metabolised and excreted in the body.
- 6 Critique available evidence to inform and support advanced RN clinical decision making in the management and delivery of pharmacological interventions.
- Evidence understanding of medications effects on each body system and utilise this understanding in the application of pharmacological therapies in RN prescribing models of care.
- Demonstrate safe prescribing practices within applicable legislative and regulatory frameworks in accordance with partnered RN prescribing frameworks.
