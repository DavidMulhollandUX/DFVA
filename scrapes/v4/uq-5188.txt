Subject: Statistical Methods in Business (BSAN7204)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Business Analytics involves the application of statistics to understand and solve business problems. This course provides the statistical foundation for the Master of Business Analytics program, by introducing students to relevant statistical concepts required for data analysis. In addition, through a practical component, the course introduces students to applying appropriate statistical analysis through R.
Learning Outcomes:
- Perform exploratory data analysis to describe data.
- Construct and evaluate models for inferential statistical analysis for real-world problem solving.
- Formulate data-driven insights and value propositions for stakeholders.
Assessment Tasks:
- Exploratory Data Analysis Online: 30%% (Hurdle: False)
- Analytics Practice Online: 20%% (Hurdle: False)
- Stakeholder Presentation and Q&A Identity Verified Online: 10%% (Hurdle: False)
- Technical Report Online: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Contact 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: Business Data Management (BSAN7206)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Business Data management concerns the organizational practice of managing and using data for running daily business transactions and generating analytical insights for business decision-making. The course focuses on transactional and analytical information systems covering topics in conceptual data modeling, creating and manipulating databases, data querying, integrating data, and developing an analytical data warehouse. During the course, students acquire Structured Query Language (SQL) skills and learn to use advanced tools for data modeling and integration.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply fundamental concepts to model, design, and manage organisational data.
- Manipulate and retrieve data using SQL.
- Develop skills to generate insights from organisational data.
- Solve challenges and leverage opportunities in dealing with Big Data.
- Implement data warehousing solutions and generate business insights that enable value creation from data.
Assessment Tasks:
- Data Warehousing Project - Part 1 Online: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
- Data Warehousing Project - Part 2 Online: 60%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Lecture 1.5 Hours/ Week Tutorial 1.5 Hours/ Week

Subject: Social Analytics (BSAN7207)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: With the advent of social media platforms, social data has been produced at an unprecedented rate in the last decade, offering organizations new avenues for data-driven value creation and capture. This course explores how organizations can develop a social media analytics capability to better listen, understand and interact with their customers in real-time. At the technical level, students will be introduced to concepts and methods of network analysis and natural language processing and will be able to use contemporary software tools to access, analyse and visualise real-world social network data.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate an understanding of advanced techniques for collecting, processing, and analysing social media data for different business applications.
- Critically evaluate and effectively communicate the outcomes of social media analysis to inform, generate, and justify strategic recommendations for business stakeholders.
- Actively apply skills using current and emerging tools in social analytics to tackle new business challenges and gain insights.
Assessment Tasks:
- Milestone 1: Project Proposal Online: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
- Milestone 2: Project Report and Presentation Online: 60%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Contact 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: Visual Analytics (BSAN7208)
Credit Points: 2

Subject: Managing Analytics Projects (BSAN7209)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: A business analytics project, like any ICT project, requires careful planning, analysis and management to ensure successful completion. This course introduces students to project management principles, in the context of large multi-disciplinary big data initiatives. It provides students with knowledge and skills in estimating risks, identifying stakeholders, understanding human and organisational factors, project leadership, and project controlling/management.
Learning Outcomes:
- Effectively appraise, judge and defend analytic projects and champion organisational change for a range of stakeholders.
- Design analytic projects aimed at high velocity industries and environments through practical approaches such as the business case, project plan, and test strategy.
- Manage project group dynamics to achieve agreed ethical, financial, temporal, and organisational objectives and expectations.
Assessment Tasks:
- Business Case with Pitch Team or group-based: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
- Individual Report: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
- Reflective Report: 20%% (Hurdle: False)

Subject: Responsible AI (BSAN7210)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Gathering, understanding, interpreting and making decisions based on collected data is an invaluable tool for business. However, concerns about privacy, consent, confidentiality, discrimination, ownership, commercialisation, intellectual property and the importance of fair benefit sharing need to be considered. Consideration is also required by those who access and make decisions about collected linked personal information. In this course students will critically analyse the ethical and legal foundations of data analytics governance that are relevant to data collection, storage, integration, exchange and access. Issues covered will include the ethical dimensions of data management, legal and regulatory frameworks in Australia and in relevant jurisdictions, data policy, data privacy, data ownership, legal liabilities regarding analytical decisions, and discrimination. The course will equip students to apply ethical and legal considerations to the core processes of business analytics.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify and reflect on key ethical, legal and social aspects related to the AI life cycle from design to deployment.
- Research and critically analyse the opportunities and risks that AI presents in various scenarios and for diverse stakeholders.
- Propose and evaluate organisational and technological solutions in collaboration with others that respond to ethical, legal and social implications of the responsible use of AI in business and society.
Assessment Tasks:
- Individual Reflective Essay Online: 45%% (Hurdle: False)
- Final Group Presentation and Individual Reflection Team or group-based Online: 55% (35% Final Group Presentation; 20% Individual Reflection)% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Contact 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: Business Analytics Applications (BSAN7213)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Data mining techniques enable the identification of patterns and relationships in a variety of data types. Identifying previously unknown patterns and relationships in data can help business make better decisions through, for example, identifying new trends, predicting customer loyalty, supporting targeted marketing campaigns. Subject: Competing with AI and Digital Technologies (BSAN7214)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: To remain competitive in the digital era, organisations need to undergo a digital transformation into data-driven organisational structures. Such transformation requires a careful strategy considering new structures and communities, culture, IT infrastructure (e.g. cloud) and legacy systems. This course explores the success factors for data driven transformation of business, and develops students' knowledge and skills in developing a transformation strategy.
Learning Outcomes:
- Describe and apply critical factors for using AI and digital technologies successfully.
- Analyse and evaluate the current state of an organisation's digital maturity to identify focus areas for transformation.
- Design and justify an organisational roadmap to elevate digital maturity and gain competitive advantage by identifying short, medium, and long term opportunities.
Assessment Tasks:
- Digital Transformation Priorities Online: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
- Digital Transformation Roadmap Online: 60%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Contact 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: Business Analytics Consulting Project (BSAN7215)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: BSAN7215 is the capstone course of the Master of Business Analytics to be completed in the final semester of study. It requires students to apply their business analytics skills to a real-world business problem. Students will identify a business problem and related data, solve the problem by applying various analytics and visualisation techniques, and present the findings in a report to senior management. To do this successfully, students will draw on skills gained throughout the program and their existing business knowledge.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply innovative analytics-based solutions for complex business problems in collaboration with industry partner and/or peers.
- Communicate effectively and professionally to justify project outcomes to relevant stakeholders.
- Evaluate and reflect on project implications to inform future business practices.
