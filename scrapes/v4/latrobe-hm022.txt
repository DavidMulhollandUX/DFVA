You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Start your journey to registering as an in-demand clinical neuropsychologist with La Trobe's Master of Clinical Neuropsychology.

Complete a contemporary, inclusive and evidence-based degree designed to address a critical skills gap across Australia and master competencies to assess and treat adults and children with conditions affecting the brain. Strike the balance between theoretical knowledge and practical skills in both assessment and intervention to employ a competency-based approach to neuropsychology.

By completing La Trobe's Master of Clinical Neuropsychology, you will:

  * learn the theoretical basis and clinical practice of neuropsychology by completing engaging, practical coursework subjects addressing neuropsychological syndromes, neuroanatomy, neuropsychological assessment, neuropsychological rehabilitation and more
  * apply your knowledge across three clinical placements in a range of hospital and community settings
  * be trained and mentored by experienced clinical neuropsychologists who are passionate and internationally recognised
  * conduct research relevant to clinical neuropsychology which has the potential to make a meaningful difference to people living with brain conditions
  * enjoy the flexibility of our multi-modal delivery mode, including online classes, condensed on-campus teaching, and metropolitan, regional and interstate placement options (where available). Complete all 10 coursework subjects online with just one three-day block of condensed in-person teaching, or study 6 out of 10 coursework subjects in person at our Bundoora campus.
*Please be aware that this course includes mandatory in-person placement requirements. Students are expected to complete these placements in Australia, and may be required to complete placements within Victoria.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Clearly and sensitively communicate outcomes of neuropsychological assessments and interventions, professional decisions and research in neuropsychology to specialist and non-specialist audiences.
**2.**
Demonstrate advanced understanding and mastery of the theories, models and evidence-base underpinning the practice of clinical neuropsychology, and apply this knowledge to clinical cases across the human lifespan.
**3.**
Exemplify high professional integrity by analysing, applying and complying with the principles and standards of professional practice, and legal and practical issues likely to be encountered in clinical neuropsychology practice.
**4.**
Apply skills in neuropsychological assessment, diagnosis and formulation of neuropsychological disorders with a wide range of clients, professional practice settings, multi-disciplinary teams and service delivery systems.
**5.**
Demonstrate a flexible, reflective, and culturally responsive approach to clinical neuropsychology practice, research and service development.
**6.**
Design, implement and meaningfully evaluate outcomes of individually tailored neuropsychological interventions and management recommendations.

### Course features
### Work based learning (placement) requirements
Compulsory placements (Work Based Learning)
During this course, you will participate in one or more supervised Work Based Learning (WBL) placements designed to allow you to practice newly acquired skills, behaviours and knowledge while integrating theory with practice. The placements will immerse you in professional work culture, enabling you to develop and further enhance your skills. WBL provides you with the opportunity to gain valuable industry experience at one or more of our local or interstate industry partners or agencies. WBL placements will be arranged by the university. You will complete three clinical placements, over which you will undergo a total of 1000 hours of clinical training at the placement agency. Students may incur additional costs and it may be necessary to travel to complete a specific placement. Please refer to the individual subject entries for more information.
### Work integrated learning opportunities
Industry presentations, Industry informed curriculum, Industry-informed projects, Interprofessional learning, Career development learning, Professional simulations
### Career outcomes
After graduation, you could work in a variety of settings, including hospitals, community organisations, private practices and research institutions. Possible roles include:

  * clinical neuropsychologist
  * neuropsychology researcher or educator
  * specialist consultation or advisory positions.

Subject: FOUNDATION SKILLS IN COGNITIVE BEHAVIOURAL THERAPY (PSY5COG)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject content is based on the Cognitive Behavioural Therapy model (CBT). CBT is a relatively short-term intervention aimed at solving current problems through the evaluation of unhelpful thinking and behaviour. CBT has been shown to have considerable empirical effectiveness for a range of psychological presentations. You will gain an overview of cognitive and behavioural models of psychological disorders, with a particular focus on interventions for high prevalence disorders such as anxiety and depressive disorders. The role of individual case formulation and treatment planning as ways to tailor interventions to account for cultural differences and client diversity, will be emphasised. You will focus on the development of intervention skills relating to CBT, and review process issues in CBT interventions.
Learning Outcomes:
- Construct and appraise case formulations and intervention plans based on cognitive and behavioural models, accounting for cultural differences and client diversity.
- Assess the empirical base of CBT interventions including indications and contraindications.
- Identify and apply evidence based cognitive and behavioural intervention strategies for common presenting problems such as mild-moderate anxiety and depressive disorders.
- Understand cognitive behavioural models of a range of depressive and anxiety disorders.

Subject: PSYCHOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT (PSY5PAX)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject provides in-depth training in the area of psychological assessment, integrating the scientist-practitioner roles of the professional psychologist. The focus is on concepts and techniques that are foundations for other subjects in the course. The assessment process, emphasising a problem-solving orientation, is examined, taking into account life-span and cultural issues. Psychometric theory, and its relationship to testing, is reviewed. Principles of assessment interviewing are outlined. The contribution of tests and testing to this process is considered in detail. Techniques of test administration and interpretation, with a particular emphasis on the Wechsler Intelligence Scales, are explored. Communication of psychological assessment outcomes, particularly through report writing, is given close attention. Lectures and seminar presentations will be augmented through self-directed experiential activities.
Learning Outcomes:
- Communicate effectively to clients, families, referral agencies, and other psychologists about the psychological assessment with understanding of how to incorporate assessment results into treatment planning.
- Write assessment reports to a professional standard and suitable for different referral agencies or contexts.
- Apply structured history- taking interview as the preliminary component of psychological assessments.
- Apply advanced understanding of the principles and theoretical background of psychological assessment with an understanding of issues relating to (i. reliability and validity, (ii. development and aging, and (iii. culture.
- Administer psychological tests taking into account issues of validity and reliability, and contextual issues related to development and aging, and cultural diversity.

Subject: PSYCHOPATHOLOGY (PSY5PPY)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject begins the process of building clinical competence in assessment, diagnosis, and treatment of major disorders. You will also develop a rich appreciation of their psychopathology, including the subjective experience of clients. The Diagnostic and Statistical Manual (DSM) and International Classification of Diseases (ICD) classification systems are critically appraised and used, and you will be guided in developing skills in administering a diagnostic interview.
Learning Outcomes:
- Gain insight into, and empathy for, the personal experience of those who are affected by psychological problems
- Develop skill independently and collaboratively in conducting and appraising semi-structured diagnostic interviews that routinely consider differential diagnoses, age specific features, and common comorbidities
- Demonstrate an understanding of the notion of a taxonomy of mental disorders, with particular reference to the rationale, structure, content, and limitations of the DSM system
- Develop and apply a practical understanding of the symptoms and psychopathology of major psychiatric disorders
- Integrate cultural considerations in assessment and diagnosis

Subject: RESEARCH MASTERS A (PSY5RMA)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will discuss a range of approaches to conducting research, with a particular focus on research methods most commonly used in clinical and applied psychological research. This knowledge will support you to work collaboratively with your research supervisor to develop a research proposal for your research project. Presentation and discussion of your research project with your peers within the workshop setting will also facilitate the development of your project and proposal. The research project must address a research question relevant to the field of clinical psychology.
Learning Outcomes:
- Evaluate and apply different approaches to research methods to a variety of clinical or applied research topics.
- Use knowledge of research methods and importance of cultural context to inform interpretation and critical analysis of previous research studies and results.
- Formulate a research question and design and write an appropriate research proposal to address the question.
