## Career Opportunities & Employment Outcomes
Many employers, especially in large organisations, value the skills an Arts (Honours) degree provides and will specify an honours degree as desirable in their selection criteria.
Apart from content knowledge, employers and professionals value both the reliability and capacity for independent work, and the skill at writing substantial reports which are demonstrated by successful completion of Honours.
In the face of unprecedented change and uncertainty at both a local and global level, organisations are seeking graduates who can think critically and react positively to these changes.
Students with a BA (Honours) are in the ideal position to meet this challenge with their ability to think independently, reason logically, and communicate clearly.

The BA (Honours) degree develops core skills and knowledge in theory and research in the humanities and social sciences.
The program is based on a combination of coursework and a research project in the chosen field of study. Its objective is to produce graduates who will be able to think clearly, flexibly and critically, and be able to weigh up evidence and arguments and make rational choices.
Graduates should be able to express themselves effectively, both orally and in writing; be able to work cooperatively in a team; and be able to demonstrate a depth of understanding in one or more fields of knowledge.
The Honours Program also prepares students for further study at the Master of Philosophy (MPhil) and Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) level.
Please note that some specialisations have an intake in Semester 1 only. Please check the details for specialisations (under "What you can study") or contact the relevant school.

### HUMN6014 - Honours Research Thesis
Learning Outcomes:
- Understand at a deeper level the complex nature of problems and methods in your discipline.
- Reflect critically on arguments presented in contemporary and historical debates.
- Conduct advanced independent research.
- Demonstrate reasoned thought, creativity, and a high level of written expression.
Assessment Tasks:
- Honours thesis: 100%%
- Oral defence Hurdle In-person: Pass/fail (hurdle)%
Workload: Contact 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: HHSS6020 - Research Design in the Social Sciences
### HHSS6020 - Research Design in the Social Sciences
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate an understanding and application of research design principles in the social sciences.
- Demonstrate an understanding and application of methodological approaches to research in the social sciences.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the role of evidence in social science research and its application to your project.
- Demonstrate the capacity to plan and manage a complex project.
- Demonstrate the capacity to communicate your research design in written and oral form.
- Demonstrate a capacity to communicate research benefit to a range of audiences.
- Demonstrate the capacity to engage with evidence and literature relevant to your discipline.
Assessment Tasks:
- Thesis outline/structure document submission and peer feedback participation Hurdle In-person: 10%%
- Research module completion Online: 20%%
- Discipline specific research proposal on thesis topic Hurdle: 40%%
- Presentation on honours research topic Identity Verified In-person: 30%%
Workload: Seminar 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: HHSS6030 - Research Design in Humanities
### HHSS6030 - Research Design in Humanities
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and undertake advanced independent research.
- Collect and analyse appropriate information to answer important research questions.
- Demonstrate reasoned thought, creativity, and a high quality of written expression.
- Develop skills in oral presentation and communicating research findings.
- Express an understanding of the nature and processes of academic research in the Humanities.
- Have an appreciation of theoretical and methodological issues specific to the chosen area of research.
- Reflect critically on complex arguments and problems.
- Achieve an advanced understanding of a relevant Humanities discipline (classics and ancient history, history, philosophy, or religious studies), including its ethical and social contexts.
Assessment Tasks:
- Research Project 1 Hurdle In-person: 45%%
- Research project 2 Hurdle In-person: 45%%
- Participation In-person: 10%%
- Three-Minute Thesis Presentation Hurdle In-person: Hurdle%
- Research Design Modules Hurdle Online: Hurdle%
Workload: Lecture 1 Hour/ Week Tutorial 1 Hour/ Semester
