## Professional Certificate in Supply Chain Management - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BP003
##### 30 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Professional Certificate in Logistics and Supply Chain Management is designed for professionals seeking rapid upskilling or exploring a career in logistics, procurement or operations roles. The course enables you to build your skills in a practical, credible, and flexible learning experience without committing to a full degree.

The course is delivered in a flexible blended format, enabling you to study part-time over six to twelve months while balancing life and career commitments. You'll study two core subjects from La Trobe's Master of Logistics and Supply Chain Management, giving you the option to continue your studies into La Trobe's Graduate Certificate or Master of Logistics and Supply Chain Management.

You'll gain the practical expertise in logistics and supply chain strategies to enhance organisational efficiency and responsiveness, including how to apply core supply chain concepts to optimise operations and leverage data analytics tools to interpret supply chain data, generate insights, and support strategic decision-making across the value chain.

You'll learn:

  * the fundamentals of logistics and supply chain management, along with their implications for operational and strategic business decisions.
  * essential theories and concepts related to demand and supply planning, distribution network design, sustainable procurement, service supply chain and operations, international transport and logistics, as well as supply chain sustainability and the circular economy.
  * strategic sourcing, procurement, and supply planning to contribute effectively to sustainable procurement and enhance supply chain performance.
  * critical analysis of environmental and social sustainability in procurement, focusing on ethical sourcing aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Subject: FOUNDATION OF LOGISTICS AND SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT (MGT4001)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will be introduced to the fundamentals of logistics and supply chain management, along with their implications for operational and strategic business decisions. You will explore essential theories and concepts related to demand and supply planning, distribution network design, sustainable procurement, service supply chain and operations, international transport and logistics, as well as supply chain sustainability and the circular economy. Throughout the subject, you will have the opportunity to learn these concepts in a range of business contexts, preparing you to advance your knowledge in each of those specialised areas. Additionally, you will cultivate critical and creative thinking, quantitative, and interpersonal skills essential for success in the logistics and supply chain profession.
Learning Outcomes:
- Propose informed solutions to real-world logistics and supply chain challenges, through both individual and collaborative efforts.
- Critically analyse foundational concepts in logistics and supply chain management across various business settings.
- Examine and evaluate the principles of logistics and supply chain management.

Subject: SUSTAINABLE PROCUREMENT AND SUPPLY PLANNING (MGT4004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject provides students with a comprehensive understanding of strategic sourcing, procurement, and supply planning, empowering them to contribute effectively to sustainable procurement and enhance supply chain performance. Students will develop practical expertise and proficiency in using industry-leading tools and data-driven decision-making methods for real-world procurement challenges. The subject also emphasizes critical analysis of environmental and social sustainability in procurement, focusing on ethical sourcing aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), preparing students to make informed decisions for a sustainable future. Hands-on training covers essential business and employability skills such as negotiation, RFP development, contract management, and supplier auditing, applicable to procurement and supply planning roles.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design a procurement plan that addresses environmental and social sustainability, and ethical sourcing aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
- Critically analyse the role of strategic procurement and supply planning in enhancing the firm competitive advantage.
- Identify and apply a diverse range of digital tools to make informed procurement and supply planning problems decisions.
- Exercise professional judgement applicable to procurement and supply planning.
