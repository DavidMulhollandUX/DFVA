## Undergraduate Certificate in Health Sciences - On-Line
##### HU001O
##### 60 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
If you're passionate about making a difference to the wellbeing of others, La Trobe's Undergraduate Certificate provides you with the foundational knowledge to upskill or pursue a new career in health sciences. Our health sciences curriculum is uniquely aligned with industry trends, focusing on real-world and data-driven health solutions to equip students with the in-demand skills in today’s evolving health sector.

You'll develop the foundational knowledge in the health sciences and the interdisciplinary skills to prepare you for future studies and employment in the field of health sciences. Learn to communicate, think critically, problem solve and collaborate – essential skills valued across all industries.

Make study work around work and life commitments with flexible online learning, giving you the freedom to study anywhere. You'll gain the confidence to directly pathway to the Diploma of Health Sciences or Bachelor of Health Sciences, where you'll continue your studies and choose to major in areas like public health, health promotion, rehabilitation counselling, health wellbeing and performance, environmental health, digital health, or psychological science.

You'll learn:

  * Health and wellbeing  * Investigate the complex range of interactions that influence the health and wellbeing of individuals, communities and populations.

  * Human structure and function  * Explore concepts and principles of the structure (anatomy) and function (physiology) of the human body.

  * Data-driven health solutions  * Learn about modern data analytics, visualisation and statistics. Gain the skills required for the analysis and visualisation of complex data used in data-based decision making.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Describe basic physiological and anatomical principles relevant to health sciences.
**2.**
Identify and apply basic descriptive and inferential statistical methods to support decision-making in health sciences.
**3.**
Identify and explain the determinants of health and how they influence health and wellbeing of individuals, communities and populations.

### Career outcomes
The Undergraduate Certificate in Health Sciences is a pathway into La Trobe's Bachelor of Health Sciences, providing students with the foundational knowledge and interdisciplinary skills in the health sciences.

With further study in La Trobe's Bachelor of Health Sciences, students can pursue a range of career opportunities in public health, rehabilitation, health promotion, environmental health and digital health.

Subject: UNDERSTANDING HEALTH AND WELLBEING (PHE1UHW)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop a broad understanding of health and wellbeing, examining key theories that underpin concepts in contemporary health and wellbeing. You will investigate the complex range of interactions that influence the health and wellbeing of individuals, communities and populations. As health is a dynamic concept, you will further examine the social, environmental and biomedical determinants of health and wellbeing within an Australian and global context.
Learning Outcomes:
- Discuss the contemporary issues of health and wellbeing in an Australian and global context.
- Describe the key perspectives and theories of health and wellbeing.
- Analyse the role of various health practitioners in health care systems and health promotion settings to facilitate optimal health and wellbeing.
- Identify and explain the determinants of health and how they influence health and wellbeing of individuals, communities and populations.

Subject: MAKING SENSE OF DATA (STM1001)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject introduces students to modern data analytics, visualisation, and statistics. It equips students with the skills required to take advantage of powerful computing for the analysis and visualisation of complex data. These skills are used to solve problems in areas such as the biological sciences, medical sciences, agricultural sciences, nutrition, health sciences, education, and business. Students will become familiar with data visualisation and computing, descriptive statistics, statistical modelling, and data-based decision making. Using statistical computing packages is an integral part of this subject. This subject allows further study in second-year subjects in statistics and data science.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify and apply appropriate statistical inference methods for decision making.
- Calculate probabilities and other quantities from discrete and continuous probability distributions.
- Convert data into useful information by using appropriate numerical and graphical summaries.
- Apply data visualisation skills and/or statistical knowledge in a chosen applied field of study.
- Compute, display, and interpret numerical and graphical summaries, probabilities and various statistical inference procedures using one or more statistical software package(s).
