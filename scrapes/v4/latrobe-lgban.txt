## Graduate Diploma in Business Analytics - City Campus
##### LGBAN
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Carefully designed to keep you ahead of the trends, La Trobe’s Graduate Diploma in Business Analytics will help you grow your career in this fast-moving industry. This course equips you with the technical, analytical, and business skills necessary to build data-based solutions that address the complexity of data and information in modern businesses. In this course, disciplinary knowledge in data, analytics and AI are aligned with industry leading tools and technologies to provide a hands-on learning experience of practical skills for solving real-world business problems. The teaching team draw on their own industry and research expertise in building AI and analytics solutions to deliver a future-focused learning experience. They teach in small, interactive classes and use Gen AI teaching tools to ensure the student learning journey is personalised and fully supported.

You will learn:

  * 
Data Analytics:

  * 
Professional knowledge and skills to build and use analytics dashboards, and predictive models for real-world business problems.

  * 
Business Innovation:

  * 
AI, analytics and data-driven innovations in business settings to create value, solve problems, and improve performance.

  * 
ICT Ethics and Cybersecurity:

  * 
Knowledge of local and international regulatory frameworks and guidelines for addressing enterprise ethics and cybersecurity challenges.

  * 
Professional ICT Practice:

  * 
Power skills essentials for collaboration and teamwork in modern workplaces, including communication with a wide range of stakeholders.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Analyse and interpret business datasets using data transformations, visualisation, modelling and management techniques to support organisational decision-making.
**2.**
Use AI prompting, strategies, tools, and AI-assisted coding platforms to design and develop AI models and prototypes to address complex industrial challenges.
**3.**
Develop and communicate analytics strategies, actionable insights, and AI-supported recommendations clearly to diverse organisational stakeholders.
**4.**
Apply and evaluate predictive analytics models and AI algorithms to solve complex business problems.
**5.**
Apply principles of cybersecurity, ethical practice, and data privacy to ensure responsible handling of information in analytics and AI contexts.

Subject: STATISTICS FOR BUSINESS AND FINANCE (BUA4004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop basic data-analysis skills to investigate real-life problems in business and finance. You will focus on basic concepts in statistics and probability theory, methods of statistical inference, measures of linear relationship between variables including linear regression analysis. There is a strong emphasis on the application of these techniques to practical problems in business and finance using Microsoft Excel. The contents of the subject are in line with the Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) quantitative analysis curriculum. Successful completion of this subject will lay a solid foundation for the further study of data analysis in business and finance.
Learning Outcomes:
- Ability to present work in a professional manner and to communicate effectively to a professional audience
- Use statistical principles and research skills in a collaborative team environment to solve practical problems and communicate the findings to people with various viewpoints
- Apply probability theory in business and financial decision making
- Apply and interpret concepts of descriptive statistics to real life data and interpretation of results
- Conduct estimating linear regression models and testing hypothesis to aid decision-making

Subject: CLOUD PLATFORMS AND ANALYTICS (BUS5001)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Organisations are rapidly migrating technology infrastructure and applications into cloud platforms, due to increased availability, security, reliability and cost-effectiveness. Moreover, cloud platforms are crucial in enabling artificial intelligence, big data analytics and data engineering of Internet of Things, social media data streams and multimedia data. In response, these platforms are gradually becoming user-centric and customisable to suit the technology requirements of small to large scale organisations. Technical knowledge, practical skills, and IT security implements in cloud platforms are a strategic advantage in the modern workplace. You will develop a range of technical knowledge and practical skills required to instantiate, configure, secure and manage a cloud platform for organisational needs. You will learn how to leverage a cloud platform and its components for business automation, security risk management, robotic process automation, data integration and data analytics and artificial intelligence applications.
Learning Outcomes:
- Assess and apply cloud adoption frameworks to justify, plan, implement, and govern cloud platforms, in alignment with organisational culture, business objectives, sustainability, and ethical practice.
- Critically evaluate and apply cloud-based products and services to meet organisational needs for data, networking, and computing, while ensuring cybersecurity, privacy, and ethical compliance.
- Design and synthesise end-to-end cloud-based platforms that deliver secure and ethical data, analytics, and AI capabilities aligned with organisational objectives and industry standards.
- Configure and critically explain the components, capabilities, deployment, and cybersecurity features of modern cloud computing platforms.

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/BUS5DWR =====
Subject: DATA WRANGLING (BUS5DWR)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject introduces you to the various techniques of data wrangling with a strong focus on hands-on experience in programming using Structured Query Language (SQL) and the Python programming language. It will cover the basic concepts in relational database design including Entity Relationship (ER) modelling and SQL as a tool for basic data wrangling. You will also learn various types of data sources and common data formats. The subject equips you with skills in data wrangling, including data import and export, basic data integration, assessment and transformation. Upon completion, you will be able to perform a variety of data wrangling tasks using SQL and a programming language for different kinds of data types.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and implement database models that meet business requirements using advanced data modelling and querying techniques to enable analytics initiatives.
- Analyse data by developing SQL queries and data wrangling scripts using programming tools with responsible use of generative AI to support data transformation and insight generation for data-driven business decisions.
- Evaluate and compare data sources, formats, and structures to prepare and transform data for analysis, considering ethical, legal, and cybersecurity risks in business analytics.
- Formulate data and data-wrangling requirements that address the technical challenges of analytics tasks, incorporating data governance, ethical principles, and cybersecurity constraints.

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/BUS5MIP =====
Subject: MANAGING IT PROJECTS (BUS5MIP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: IT projects involve multiple stakeholders with competing interests, operate in dynamic business environments and must deliver projects in increasingly short time-frames. In this subject you will examine the foundations of project management involving scope, cost, time, human resources, procurement, risk, quality, communication and integration in the context of information systems projects and methodologies. Management techniques to effectively respond to the organisational, political and cultural barriers IT projects typically face will be discussed. Risk and stakeholder analysis, and critical chain scheduling will be taught. You will also consider advanced topics like management of cross-organisational projects, societal issues, teamwork issues, project office functions and project management toolsets. The agile project management concepts and development are also discussed.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically examine the methodologies for managing IT project requirements across the full project lifecycle
- Assess the processes required for IT project initiation to incorporate scope, time, cost, and quality management
- Demonstrate leadership, collaboration, and accountability in managing project teams and producing professional-standard project documentation and reports
- Apply advanced techniques in executing, monitoring, and controlling IT projects, with consideration for risk, communication, and stakeholder engagement

Subject: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY MODULE (LTU0AIM)
Credit Points: 0
Overview: The academic integrity module introduces academic integrity standards to inform and support students in avoiding plagiarism and academic misconduct. Students complete four parts that encompass academic misconduct and academic integrity decisions, including cheating, plagiarism, and collusion. Students learn about Turnitin (the text-matching tool) used at La Trobe University, and how to get help and support their development of referencing skills.
Learning Outcomes:
- Understand the importance of the acknowledgment and the referencing of sources
- Locate, select, and utilise information about La Trobe's approach to academic integrity to support appropriate acknowledgement of references used.
- Recognise and understand academic integrity responsibilities.
