### Overview
Every industry needs people with strong business skills to manage and improve the day-to-day operations of organisations. To keep up with growing industry needs, companies are looking for graduates with strong business administration skills. Business Administration is the coordination of business functions and activities. In the Bachelor of Business Administration, you will build a sound conceptual and theoretical foundation from which you can build essential skills for a career in business. You will learn about core business functions and how they are coordinated to achieve business goals whilst having the opportunity to specialise in more than one business discipline. The BBA provides a unique environment with a close connection to teaching staff and other students and opportunities to develop ethical and socially responsible communication, decision-making, and problem-solving skills to succeed in a competitive and diverse world. Many subjects provide practical experience, real-world case-based projects, collaborative learning, interaction with experts from industry, and job-ready skills. This degree will equip you for careers in public-sector and private enterprises.

### Structure
This is a comprehensive course, structured in four parts:

Part A. Core studies

These studies will provide you with a broad foundation for your study of business and expose you to several business disciplines. It will contribute breadth to your knowledge of business and develop your understanding of multi-disciplinary decision making in organisations. It will also give you the opportunity to learn more about each discipline before finalising your choice of major.

Part B. Major studies

Your major studies will provide you with a focused program of study that will develop your expertise. You will develop, apply and communicate an advanced level of understanding of the concepts and theoretical frameworks that constitute the knowledge base of your major area of study.

Part C. Capstone studies

This is designed to consolidate the knowledge and skills acquired throughout the study of your major.

Part D. Elective studies

This will enable you to further develop your knowledge of your chosen major, or business more broadly, or study a second business major. Alternatively you can select units from across the University in which you are eligible to enrol.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
embed ethical considerations into decision-making processes and propose solutions that result in sustainable outcomes for stakeholders and the environment.
**2.**
demonstrate an inclusive mindset that embeds equity, diversity and inclusion in business practices by incorporating differing perspectives, cultures, identities, skills, and abilities.
**3.**
demonstrate a working knowledge of core business activities and their interrelationships within organisations
**4.**
integrate global contexts in business analysis and decision-making.
**5.**
gather, evaluate and analyse data to generate relevant information and insights that support business decision making
**6.**
exhibit effective collaboration in teams to address business challenges.
**7.**
identify and implement ethical, evidence-based, discipline-specific practices to meet the needs of business.
**8.**
communicate appropriately and effectively in business contexts.

### Progression to further studies
Successful completion of this course may provide a pathway to the one year honours program Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) (B3701). To be eligible to apply for entry into the Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) (B3701), you must obtain a distinction grade average (70%) or above in 24 credit points of studies in relevant discipline units at Level 3.

### AHEGS
The Bachelor of Business Administration is an undergraduate degree. The course is taught in English and normally takes three years of full-time study. The course provides a solid foundation in a range of key business disciplines. Graduates will be job-ready, independent, critical and creative scholars, who have broad knowledge, as well as technical skills, in their chosen discipline. The Bachelor of Business Administration is a Level 7 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.

Subject: Accounting in business (ACB1020)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This unit introduces basic accounting concepts to non-accountants. The information requirements of two main groups of information users are examined - external users such as current and potential investors and internal users such as managers. This unit provides an introduction to the structure, meaning, analysis and interpretation of financial statements, in addition to exploring financial issues confronting managers, such as cost and performance measurement and budgeting.
Learning Outcomes:
- demonstrate an understanding of various forms of business organisations
- measure and interpret information relating to financial performance, financial position, liquidity and risk indicators of businesses
- measure and interpret financial and non-financial information for managers to use in planning, decision making and control
- apply financial and management accounting principles in the preparation of financial statements
- develop the ability to work effectively in a team and/or independently and demonstrate the development of judgement, and skills in problem solving, critical thinking and analysis.
Assessment Tasks:
- 3 - Examination: 40% (Hurdle: None)
- 2 - Exercise: 30% (Hurdle: None)
- 1 - Project: 30% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: Minimum total expected workload to achieve the learning outcomes for this unit is 144 hours per semester typically comprising a mixture of scheduled learning activities and independent study. Independent study may include associated readings, assessment and preparation for scheduled activities. You are expected to complete all pre-class activities prior to your scheduled class, and post-class activities should be completed after your scheduled class. Learning activities may include a combination of teacher directed, peer directed and online engagement activities.

Subject: Global study programs in marketing (MKX3521)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: The Global Study Programs in Marketing (GSPM) offers you a hands-on learning experience that deepens your understanding of marketing in a global context through exposure to some of the world’s leading marketers. Building on your existing marketing knowledge, the program sharpens your critical thinking, cross-cultural communication, and global awareness. On the study trip, you will directly engage with marketing managers during company visits. You will also have the opportunity to experience diverse marketplaces and consumers, gaining valuable insights into how marketing drives business success and shapes society.
Learning Outcomes:
- critically evaluate the impact of marketing on business and society
- identify and critically appraise diverse marketplaces and consumer behaviours to inform marketing decision-making across global contexts
- demonstrate effective cross-cultural communication through oral, visual, and written forms in marketing settings
- compare, contrast, and analyse global senior marketing manager practices and strategic approaches
- apply marketing theories and strategies to real-world contexts, using in-country insights to develop actionable and culturally informed marketing solutions.
