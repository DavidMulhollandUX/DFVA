## Bachelor of Education (Honours) and Bachelor of Arts
##### D3002
##### 204 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Bachelor of Education (Honours) double degree course will prepare you for a career as a skilled, critical and effective teacher and educator in the primary or secondary sector. Participants build a strong base for decision making in relation to their practice and establish themselves as future educational leaders in a global workforce. You will build a high level of expertise in one of two specialisations:

  * Primary education

  * Secondary education

The course will prepare you for teaching in an increasingly diverse, complex and changing world. You will engage with contemporary ideas and practices and learn how to foster the learning of children and young people with varied needs. This will enable you to develop your students’ creative, intellectual, physical, personal and social capabilities.

While learning to navigate the complex nature of teaching, you will develop the specific professional knowledge and teaching skills needed to engage students of various ages and developmental stages with particular school subjects. Depending upon your specialisation, you will develop the skills and knowledge needed to teach in learning areas such as the arts and music, English, mathematics, humanities, physical education, health and wellbeing, science, and digital technologies. Professional experience offered throughout the course in urban, rural, remote or international locations and education settings, will build your knowledge of teaching and learning. As a result, you will become a thoughtful, effective and inclusive educator, able to design learning experiences that ensure that your future students have the right balance of challenge and support.

The Bachelor of Education (Honours) prepares you for the global workforce. You will have a strong foundation for decision making about your practice and you will be in a position to establish yourself as an educational leader of the future.

The course is available for either primary or secondary school teaching. Secondary education students will prepare as specialist teachers in two secondary teaching areas, while primary education students will be prepared to teach across the school curriculum with specialist expertise in either, English and literacy, or mathematics and numeracy.

Arts at Monash provides a comprehensive gateway to a wide range of fascinating areas of study across the arts, humanities and social sciences. Pair this with the degree in education and you can share that fascination with the next generation of young people, inspiring and engaging young developing minds.

### Structure
Double degree courses include the features of the component degree courses, except that electives may be reduced.

Education

The course develops through the five themes of Part A. Core studies, Part B. Education studies, Part C. Curriculum studies, Part D. Discipline studies and Part E. Professional experience. The nature and proportion of units required among these themes will differ depending upon your specialisation and discipline studies. In this double degree you will complete: 

Part A. Core studies

These studies include essential units that are part of every Bachelor of Education (Honours) specialisation. These units are designed to ground you in evidence-based practices, preparing you to navigate diverse classroom environments and enhance student learning effectively. A key focus of this theme is developing your knowledge and skills to choose strategies that support students to learn, retain, and apply knowledge effectively.

You will explore effective teaching practices, such as sequencing for learning, modelling expected outcomes, assessing and reporting student progress, and providing constructive feedback to support student growth. Classroom management is another major component where you will learn to create structured, supportive environments using clear rules, routines, and high expectations. You will also develop skills to build positive relationships with students, engaging them actively in their learning journey. Inclusivity is embedded throughout the units and you will gain foundational knowledge of First Nations cultures and perspectives, along with other enabling factors that promote cultural responsiveness and support for diverse learners. These units will equip you to become an adaptable, culturally aware, and skilled teacher, ready to apply evidence-based practices for impactful and inclusive teaching and learning.

Part B. Education studies    

These units cover essential aspects of learning to teach and are tailored to each specialisation to prepare you appropriately to enter the profession in your chosen sector (early childhood, primary, secondary, or a combination of these). These studies include an examination of the learning and teaching relationship, child and/or adolescent development, alongside classroom culture and practice. You will build your professional skills, knowledge and understanding of teaching through a process of personal inquiry into effective teaching practices, informed by current research. These units will challenge you to consider global and local influences on education as well as the effective and innovative incorporation of emerging technologies to ensure the relevance of your teaching. You will build a deep understanding of learners, families, schools and communities. Societal trends are explored as well as how to effectively engage local communities, parents and carers.

Part C. Curriculum studies

With a focus on the Victorian Curriculum, these units will provide you with the content-specific knowledge pedagogies, covering the early years framework, Foundation–10 curriculum, as well as specific pedagogy appropriate for teaching VCE in a particular discipline or disciplines. You will develop the understanding and skills needed to become a skilled, innovative and effective educator who is able to draw on a repertoire of strategies to suit particular students, educational contexts and learning outcomes. You will learn how to design, plan and implement engaging learning experiences to meet diverse learners' needs. You will develop your knowledge and skills in key learning areas relevant to the educational level of your specialisation, deepening your understanding of teaching and learning, assessment strategies, theories and practices related to the subjects you will be teaching.

If you are enrolled in the Bachelor of Education (Honours) in Secondary Health and Physical Education, you will be prepared to teach in three secondary teaching areas: health, physical education and one other area of your choice.

If you are enrolled in a primary/secondary specialisation, you will be prepared to teach in one secondary teaching area as well as the curriculum areas required for teaching in primary schools.

If you are enrolled in a Bachelor of Education (Honours) in Secondary Education double degree course, you will be prepared to teach in two secondary teaching areas. You will also be required to successfully complete a unit that develops skills in teaching literacy and numeracy across the curriculum, ensuring you have the skills and expertise to share in the responsibility of educating literate and numerate learners.

Part D. Discipline studies

These studies are designed to develop your content knowledge in the key learning areas that you will be teaching in schools or other education settings. These units are offered as integrated curriculum/discipline studies in the early childhood and primary, and primary specialisations, and as a standalone discipline studies sequence in all other specialisations. They will provide you with a focused sequence of study that will deepen and advance your knowledge in your teaching specialist areas. The number of units completed in this sequence is unique to each specialisation and accordingly aligns and reflects the different needs of the early childhood, primary and secondary school sectors.

Part E. Professional experience 

Integral to the course, the professional experience opportunities have been designed as a developmental learning sequence that you will engage with at every stage of your course. This component is an opportunity for you to build connections between the core, education, curriculum and discipline themed unit content and apply these in a professional context. You will operate within a particular social and legislative environment unique to your placement setting, and interact with sector professionals as you work collaboratively with them whilst on placement. An opportunity to further explore these professional elements is facilitated through the Teaching Performance Assessment (TPA) unit.

Arts

A2000 Bachelor of Arts is a comprehensive course, structured in three equal parts. In this double degree course you will complete:

Part A. Major studies

These units will provide you with a focused program of study that will develop your practical and theoretical skills and knowledge in one Faculty of Arts listed major area of study. You will learn to critically analyse, apply and communicate an advanced level of understanding of the concepts and theoretical frameworks that constitute the knowledge base of the area of study.

Part B. Specified studies

These units will expose you to several arts disciplines contributing breadth to your knowledge of the arts, humanities and social sciences. You will have the flexibility of  learning about several areas of study before finalising your choice of major.  You will also build skills and abilities that employers value and seek by completing units from the Professional Futures domains. 

Course progression map 

The course progression maps provide guidance on unit enrolment for each semester of study.

### Requirements
You must complete 204 points, of which 108 points are from the Bachelor of Education (Honours) and 96 points are from the Bachelor of Arts.

The 108 points from the Bachelor of Education (Honours) must include all of the requirements in Parts A, B, C, D and E and any special course requirements. 

The 96 points from the Bachelor of Arts will include all the requirements from Parts A and B as described in the Bachelor of Arts degree.  At least 24 credit points must be completed at level 3.

Notes:

For Part B Arts Specified study, you must complete 12 credit points of units from the Professional Futures domains and the remaining 12 credit points should be selected from level 1, 2 or 3 Arts' elective units.

For your discipline studies in the double degree secondary education specialisation, you must complete a major and minor study that qualifies you to teach in two secondary teaching areas. Your major will be your first teaching area. You must also complete a Victorian Institute of Teaching minor study that will be your second teaching area. The minor study must consist of four units, with no more than two units at first year level. Space for the minor study units will come from elective unit allocations on the course map. Approved disciplines can be found on the secondary specialist areas page.

===== SPECIALISATION / AREA OF STUDY =====
### Secondary education (SECONDRY01)
<p>This specialisation will prepare you to teach adolescents (12 – 18 years of age) in secondary schools from Year 7 to Year 12 as a specialist teacher in two teaching areas. Your two teaching areas will be determined by your unit choices within your partner degree. In Years 1 – 3 of this specialisation, you will gain the discipline content knowledge for your teaching areas, whereas in Year 4 you will learn how to teach effectively within your discipline areas. Throughout the course, you will focus on understanding how adolescents learn, which is as varied as the individual learner themselves, and you will develop adaptive and inclusive teaching practices to respond effectively to learner needs. You will be challenged to develop the capacity to make considered and effective professional decisions that are learner-focused. Responding to the ever-changing environment, you will explore ways to effectively integrate emerging and innovative digital technologies within the contemporary classroom.</p>
<p><strong>Availability</strong><strong><br /></strong>Secondary education is listed in D3001 Bachelor of Education (Honours) double degrees as an undergraduate specialisation at Clayton.</p>
