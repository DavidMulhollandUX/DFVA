## Description
The Graduate Certificate in Business Law provides professionals with a foundational understanding of the Australian legal system, an ability to identify sources of law, an understanding of legal reasoning and processes and subject-specific knowledge about key areas of law that impact on business practice.

Delivered 100% online in an accelerated format, this program offers a range of courses tailored for individuals from non-legal backgrounds.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

4. Communicate complex legal concepts and issues in a business context with clarity to both legal and non-legal audiences
3. Apply legal principles, legal reasoning and research to real-world business scenarios/issues, addressing legal risks, disputes, and obligations
2. Analyse business law caselaw, legislation, and regulatory developments impacting Australian businesses
1. Examine key principles and frameworks of business law, including contracts, corporate governance, and regulatory compliance
6. Monitor and respond to changes in business laws and regulations
5. Apply the ethical and legal responsibilities of businesses, ensuring compliance with relevant laws and regulations while promoting corporate social responsibility and practice

Subject: Introduction to Employment Law (ZZBU6414)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Employment Law deals with the law governing the formation, content and termination of employment relationships. This course will examine the complex integration of the body of federal and state statutes, industrial instruments and the common law contract of employment. Students will gain insight to the formation, duration and termination of an employment contract and the ability to comprehend terms expressed, implied or otherwise incorporated into the contract of employment. Students will be assessed on their understanding of the rights and liabilities of employers and employees under contract, legislation, awards and industrial instruments and what remedies are available to parties for breach of employment contracts, regulations or awards.
Learning Outcomes:
- Discuss ways to avoid breaches of an employer’s legal obligations
- Recommend practices that promote high performance and productivity within the legal framework
- Identify the legal risks throughout the employment lifecycle and explain the consequences of non-compliance and inadequate employee management
- Demonstrate understanding of the legal framework and compliance requirements pertaining to employment

Subject: Law, Regulation and Ethics (ZZLJ8100)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course will provide students with a foundational understanding of the Australian legal system and the ethical frameworks within which businesses operate. Students will examine the nature of the Australian legal system, key institutions and principles, the evolution of that system over time and its place in the broader international context. Additionally, students will explore the main areas of law regulating business, environmental, social and governance (ESG) impacts and understandings of ethical business practice. Topics will include the sources and hierarchies of law in Australia, systems of government and the separation of powers, Australia’s federal system, and the role of courts, tribunals and regulatory bodies. Students will also be guided to develop legal reasoning, and in the application of legal principles to fact scenarios. The course will be delivered online through asynchronous modules, online forums and synchronous online classes.
Learning Outcomes:
- Interpret legislation with reference to the rules of statutory interpretation and find and understand caselaw (PLO 2, 3, 4)
- Evaluate the ethical frameworks for the conduct of business (PLO 3, 4, 5)
- Articulate legal and ethical concepts clearly to a broad audience (PLO 4, 5)
- Analyse the evolution and key components of law of the Australian legal system (PLO 1, 6)
- Examine the sources of law regulating business (PLO 1, 2, 5, 6)

Subject: Corporate Governance and Leadership (ZZLJ8211)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course explores the primary legislative rules and legal principles that regulate the operation and governance of corporations, focusing on the legal responsibilities of leadership roles. Students will also be guided to develop legal reasoning, and in the application of legal principles to fact scenarios. Topics include foundational principles such as legal personhood and limited liability, the rights of shareholders and other stakeholders, decision-making, the appointment and removal of office holders, duties and remedies. The course also examines the community and regulatory context in which corporations operate and contemporary issues in corporate governance. The course will be delivered online through asynchronous modules, online forums and synchronous online classes.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse key principles of corporations law and governance.
- Evaluate the duties of directors and other leaders and the availability of remedies.
- Interrogate the regulatory and social responsibilities of corporations.
- Apply key principles of corporations law and governance to fact scenarios.

Subject: Commercial Contract Law (ZZLJ8212)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Contracts are an essential part of the daily conduct of business. In this course students will examine key elements of contract law and practice, including how and when contracts are formed, terms (express and implied), performance, what happens when contracts are breached, how they can be terminated, and remedies. Focusing on agreements in a commercial context, this course also explores the relationship between regulation and contractual freedom, and the interplay between contract law and related forms of legal obligation. Through exposure to case law and legislation, students will develop an understanding of sources of law and how to interpret primary legal materials. Students will also be guided to develop legal reasoning, and in the application of legal principles to fact scenarios. The course will be delivered online through asynchronous modules, online forums and synchronous online classes.
Learning Outcomes:
- Evaluate the regulation of contracts in the context of business law.
- Analyse the key principles of contract law as they relate to the conduct of business.
- Identify the availability of remedies in statute, common law and equity.
- Apply key principles of contract law to hypothetical fact scenarios.

Subject: Cyber and the Law (ZZLJ9223)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: The goal of the course is to provide a legal orientation for cyber security professionals. Students are not assumed to have a legal background. The course offers an introduction to legal and policy issues in cyber security. It introduces students to relevant core legal concepts, including understanding the role of legislation and case law as well as other forms of regulation, including technical standards. It will guide students in understanding the ‘map’ of law and regulation relevant to cyber security and the role of these in Australia’s cyber security policy. The course helps cyber security professionals become familiar with legal issues they are likely to encounter and how to manage them in different circumstances - during normal operations, in responding to an incident, and in the aftermath of an incident. After completing the course, students will be aware of and be able to manage core compliance obligations as well as be in a good position to interact meaningfully with legal advisers in more complex scenarios. The focus is on Australian law, but comparative and international material is included where relevant, particularly where it impacts Australian industry.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify key legal requirements and how these and other forms of regulation impact on cyber security professional practice.
- Discuss the limits of law, both generally and in specific instances, and be able to argue for reform in particular areas.
- Explain the role of key players in regulation for cyber security and be aware of the circumstances in which engagement with regulators is required or advisable.
- Analyse and respond to legal issues arising in the contexts of cyber security operations, incident response and incident fall-out.
