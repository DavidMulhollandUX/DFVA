## Graduate Certificate in Service Co-Ordination and Rehabilitation - City Campus
##### HCSCR
##### 60 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
This course aims to give professionals working in a wide variety of settings (including health, education and welfare, occupational and industrial arenas) a comprehensive introduction to rehabilitation concepts and practices.

It's designed to prepare graduates from a range of health and human service delivery backgrounds to work with populations characterised by chronic illness or disability. You'll develop an approach to rehabilitation service delivery focused on client involvement, coordination of services and the maintenance of individuals in the community.

The content reflects recent developments in rehabilitation at Commonwealth and state levels, including occupational and community-based rehabilitation systems. This course provides a flexible option for students not proceeding to higher level studies to obtain a valuable qualification.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Critically evaluate and apply emerging research or evidence-based practices to meet the complex and emerging health care needs of clients to produce better rehabilitation outcomes
**2.**
Critically analyse how mental health,  disability, disadvantage, or disability policy and legislation can impact on rehabilitation service provision and outcomes
**3.**
Critically analyse and interpret rehabilitation theory and research

Subject: REHABILITATION COUNSELLING PRACTICUM (PHE5RCP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject provides practicum experience in which you have the opportunity to develop and practice rehabilitation counselling skills and to demonstrate competency in specified rehabilitation areas. This may include placement in a specialist rehabilitation facility, other rehabilitation-related health facility or human service agency. In addition to the workplace placement, you are required to participate in classroom-based activities relevant to professional issues in rehabilitation counselling practice. You will develop the knowledge and attitudes necessary for ethical and professional approaches in the delivery of rehabilitation services.
Learning Outcomes:
- Communicate and apply core rehabilitation evidence-based counselling knowledge, organisational practices and client and stakeholder feedback to facilitate client outcomes in complex settings .
- Effectively design and implement components of team-based rehabilitation services.
- Design, implement and revise services to remain compliant with current organisational values, legislation, government policy and funding influencing rehabilitation practice.
- Implement core rehabilitation counselling skills through development of clear, logical and concise reports and communications for effective client service delivery.
- Identify and analyse client and stakeholder requirements to develop, implement, monitor, and revise client goals, rehabilitation plans and strategies based on the evidence available.
- Identify and analyse client psychosocial barriers to change and develop and implement counselling and technical skills to facilitate meaningful community integration for clients (e.g. secure and retain work).

Subject: REHABILITATION COUNSELLING (PHE5REC)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will increase your theoretical knowledge and develop your basic skills in the interpersonal helping process, especially as it applies to rehabilitation. This learning will occur in experiential workshops and online content. Topics include: counselling models, theories and assumptions; basic counselling techniques; counselling clients on problems commonly encountered in rehabilitation settings.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply specialised technical and creative skills to engage a client in a counselling session directed towards behaviour change and demonstrate the capacity to form a durable working alliance, and assist the client to clarify their presenting issues and the associated feelings
- Apply specialised core motivational interviewing counselling skills such as active (reflective) listening, summarising, affirmations, and apply specialised evidence- based knowledge and advanced technical skills to deliver other counselling strategies within a motivational interviewing framework.
- Analyse rehabilitation interactions to identify barriers to change to support self-critique of your own performance and facilitate engagement in undertaking reflective practice to address your learning needs.
- Analyse and synthesise evidence-based knowledge to inform high level independent judgements to identify core presenting themes or relational issues in a counselling session.

Subject: SERVICE COORDINATION AND CASE MANAGEMENT (PHE5SCC)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop: (i) an understanding of basic concepts that underpin care coordination in community settings; and (ii) the opportunity to examine current models of service coordination and case management as these have been applied to meet service needs of individuals with chronic conditions or disabilities in these settings.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify, review, analyse, and synthesise the background, rationale, objectives and key influences on the development of service coordination and case management approaches to the provision of care.
- Reflect and describe about how case management should be explained to a target audience within a group of peers.
- Analyse models of case management application to clients with disability or chronic conditions, and evaluate concepts of complex assessment and implications for management and referral to other services.
- Critically interpret and analyse common procedures and tools used for the assessment of individuals with disability and/or chronic conditions.
- Initiate, plan and implement evidence-based strategies to manage services for optimal outcomes and client-focused goals.
- Analyse, consolidate and synthesise knowledge of case management models within the disability, rehabilitation and aged care sectors.
- Critically evaluate research into case management effectiveness
- Identify, review and critique models of case management used by health and human health service organisations.

Subject: COMMUNITY BASED HEALTH SERVICE DELIVERY SYSTEMS (PHE5SDS)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you are introduced to a range of community-based service delivery systems for those living with permanent impairments or chronic conditions. Populations studied include those suffering traumatic injury, post-traumatic stress disorder/injury, lifelong physical impairments, and those with mental health conditions living in the community.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse and critique the philosophy, structure and effectiveness of community-based aged care services
- Initiate, plan and implement evaluations of rehabilitation services, programs and policy.
- Analyse and critique the philosophy, scope and effectiveness of the community-based Australian based service delivery sector for traumatic injury including rehabilitation for veterans and those in the military
- Analyse and critique the philosophy, scope and effectiveness of Australian based service delivery models for mental health conditions

Subject: THE SERVICE USER PERSPECTIVE IN HEALTH CARE (PHE5SUP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you focus on the development and implementation of client-centred services for people with disability or chronic illness. You are given the opportunity to explore disability and chronic illness from the perspective of the person with the disability or chronic illness and how this perspective can be reflected in effective support services. Content is organised into three modules: (i) client related psychological and social dimensions of disability and chronic illness, and their relationship to effective service provision, (ii) self-management models of service delivery: theoretical approaches, strategies and interventions which facilitate involvement of the person in the management of their own condition, and (iii) the service user as a consumer: models and strategies regarding service user participation in health service planning and delivery.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse, generate and implement solutions to sSolve complex problems related to how social support can impact on behaviour in rehabilitation settings and implementation of social support needs assessments
- Identify and evaluate concepts of personal control impacts on behaviour in rehabilitation settings and apply to provision of effective client-centred services in these settings.
- Identify, analyse and critically assess the concept of adjustment to chronic conditions and a client's coping style and attitudes can impact on behaviour in rehabilitation settings and demonstrate evidence-based practice to provide more effective practice, i.e. client-centred services, in these settings
- Critique and apply an advanced and specialised body of conceptual, theoretical and practical evidence base to the rehabilitation counselling environment within a changing health care environment
- Identify, analyse and synthesise application of contemporary evidence-based theories, concepts and practices for service user involvement.

Subject: VOCATIONAL REHABILITATION (PHE5VOR)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject will provide you with the knowledge and skills to (i) assist rehabilitation clients achieve optimal vocational outcomes and (b) advise employing organisations about the best way to achieve optimal rehabilitation outcomes for their employees. You will examine topics including: (i) the psychology of work and unemployment; (ii) occupational rehabilitation: systems and practice; (iii) workplace disability management; and (iv) vocational assessment.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse and apply an evidence-based assessment process in order to prepare a client for securing and retaining work.
- Identify, evaluate and apply client centred vocational assessment to select suitable vocational options and make appropriate modifications where required.
- Identify, critique and systhesise resources and supports within relevant return to work schemes and workplace disability management research.
- Identify, analyse and critique core theories of vocational assessment and disability.
- Initiate, justify, plan and implement the vocational assessment process to cater to the client needs in relevant legislative context.
- Identify, critique and apply general labour market information relating to client vocational needs using evidence based models.
- Critique and synthesise theoretical knowledge and evidence-based professional judgment to assess the suitability of vocational assessment and practice of occupational rehabilitation
