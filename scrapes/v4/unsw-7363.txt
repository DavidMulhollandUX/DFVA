## Description
The Graduate Certificate in Business Management is designed for professional managers wishing to gain an understanding of the concepts and principles that underpin effective management, business decision making and leadership in organisations. It is particularly appropriate for those entering or aspiring to a middle management position in their career. Students will have appropriate undergraduate qualifications in management or a related discipline and/or relevant professional experience.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

2. Synthesise disciplinary knowledge and skills to achieve organisational objectives in a range of business contexts.
1. Communicate effectively to business knowledge to a range of audiences and be capable of independent and collaborative inquiry.
3. Analyse business issues and develop appropriate solutions, through critical thinking and problem solving skills that ensure efficient and effective outcomes.
4. Support business issues in a wider management context so as to ensure the best outcomes organisation wide.

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
To gain entry into a Graduate Certificate in Business Management, an applicant must meet one of the following entry requirements:

- Completion of a Bachelor degree with honours in the same or a related discipline* from a recognised institution; or

- Completion of a Graduate Diploma or Graduate Certificate in a related discipline* from a recognised institution; or

- Completion of a Bachelor degree in the same or a related discipline* from a recognised institution; and completion of at least three years relevant full-time professional experience; or

- Completion of a Bachelor degree in a non-related discipline; and completion of at least four years relevant full-time professional experience; or

- Evidence of other qualifications and professional experience to be assessed as acceptable grounds for admission into the program by the relevant Program Authority. In certain circumstances, students may be required to undertake and successfully complete a relevant non-award course as a condition for admission into the program. This non-award course may also be credited towards the program upon admission.

*Related Discipline/s: Business, Management, Economics, Accounting, Information Technology, Information Systems, Engineering, Human Resource Management, Project Management, Public Policy, Marketing Management, Law.

This Graduate Certificate is also an exit program from the Master of Business (8388).

Subject: Strategic Management (ZBUS8101)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: The objective of this course is to investigate the role of strategy in achieving organisational success in commercial environments. It examines the nature of organisational objectives, capabilities and strategies and, in particular, the role of corporate and business strategies as determinants of superior performance. This course also addresses corporate social responsibility in strategic management.
Learning Outcomes:
- Describe potential strategic directions and plans-of-action for an organisation
- Describe and analyse the situation of an organisation from a strategic situation
- Critically comment on an organisation's strategy in light of its strategic situation
- Recommend and justify an optimal strategic direction and associated plan-of-action for an organisation

Subject: Organisational Behaviour (ZBUS8102)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Organisational behaviour (OB) is an interdisciplinary course that integrates insights from psychology, anthropology, philosophy, and sociology to explain human behaviour in organisational settings. Grounded on established concepts and theories, learners will develop knowledge of the patterns of human behaviour in organisations from the individual, group, and organisational levels. They will also be exposed to contemporary research on important OB topics to establish the dynamic nature of organisational phenomena. With the ultimate goal of developing an evidence-based approach to problem-solving, this course provides learners with opportunities to critically reflect, formulate, and recommend evidence-based solutions to real-world organisational problems.
Learning Outcomes:
- Reflect on the social and ethical implications of organisational phenomena on individuals, groups, and organisations
- Formulate and recommend evidence-based solutions and practical strategies to address organisational problems
- Explain the interplay of individual, group, and organisational mechanisms of human behaviour in the context of organisations using key OB concepts and theories

Subject: Accounting for Management Decisions (ZBUS8108)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course provides an introduction to accounting information, terminology and techniques. This applied course is designed to give students confidence in using financial information in the workplace. It covers the identification of accounting information, the preparation and analysis of financial accounting reports - including company financial statements, and a practical understanding of the key management accounting techniques used to inform business decisions.
Learning Outcomes:
- Classify financial information for the purposes of financial statement preparation.
- Analyse financial reports from the perspective of internal and external users.
- Critically analyse financial and management accounting information to support decision making.
- Apply accounting knowledge and techniques to inform management decisions.

Subject: Business Law (ZBUS8109)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: Business Law aims to provide an introduction to, and general understanding of, the law in key legal areas applying within a private or public sector context. Topics covered in the course can include: the Australian legal system, contract law, agency law, ethics, privacy law, legal governance in the public sector, employment law and emerging topics in legal governance. The purpose of the course is to equip students with sufficient knowledge of the legal system and law to understand the legal implications of business actions within a private or public sector context. Legal analysis and problem-solving skills are developed throughout the course. The course is intended for managers who are not lawyers and no prior knowledge of law is required.
Learning Outcomes:
- Compose concise, accurate, clear and compelling arguments during legal reasoning activities.
- Evaluate legal, ethical and social issues affecting business, the public sector and external stakeholders.
- Apply legal reasoning techniques that provide solutions to problems that may arise in business and public sector scenarios.
- Interpret legal principles and policies affecting the operation of commercial and public sector enterprises.
