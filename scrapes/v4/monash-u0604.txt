## Diploma of Engineering
##### U0604
##### 102 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Diploma of Engineering will prepare you for success in your Monash Engineering degree.

In the first year of this course you will learn about the maths and science theory and skills you will need for University. In the second year, you will study the same subjects as first-year University students and develop your specialist knowledge of engineering and the practical skills you will need for University. Students will be able to select from one of the following specialisations: 

  * Aerospace Engineering

  * Biomedical Engineering

  * Chemical Engineering 

  * Civil Engineering 

  * Electrical and Computer Systems Engineering 

  * Environmental Engineering

  * Materials Engineering

  * Mechanical Engineering

  * Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering 

  * Software Engineering

### Structure
This course is structured in two parts Part 1. Foundation knowledge and Part 2. Discipline knowledge.

Part 1. Foundation knowledge

These units will provide you with the foundational maths and science theory and practical skills you’ll need for University and to progress to your discipline studies.

Part 2. Discipline knowledge

These units are equivalent to first-year University units and will provide you with discipline specific knowledge in engineering to enable progression to your destination bachelor degree.

### Requirements
The course comprises 96 points structured in two parts: Parts 1. Foundation knowledge (48 points) and Part 2. Discipline knowledge (48 points).

Units are 6 points unless otherwise stated.

Part 1: Foundation knowledge (48 points)

You must complete the following eight units (48 points):

  * MCD1160 Introductory Engineering Computing

  * MCD1180 Introductory Physics

  * MCD1700 Introductory Mathematics

  * MCD1170 Introductory Chemistry

  * MCD1190 Chemistry A

  * MCD1520 Introduction to Academic Communication

  * MCD1200 Physics A

  * MCD1530 Functions and their Applications

Part 2: Discipline knowledge (48 points)

You must complete the following eight units:

  * MCD4390 Chemistry 1

  * MCD4160 Physics for Engineering or MCD4600 Intermediate Physics# or MCD2130 Functions and their Applications+

  * MCD4490 Advanced Mathematics

  * MCD4500 Engineering Mathematics

  * MCD4520 Engineering Methods

  * MCD4530 Engineering Design

  * MCD4540 Engineering Smart Systems

  * MCD4550 Engineering Numerical Analysis

#Students entering directly into Part 2 who have not completed Physics (Australian Year 12 equivalent) must have completed Australia Year 11 Physics (or equivalent) and enrol in MCD4600 Intermediate Physics

+ Students entering directly into Part 2 who have not completed Mathematics (Australian Year 12 equivalent) must have completed Year11 Mathematics (or equivalent) and enrol in MCD2130 Functions and their Applications.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Utilise technical skills to demonstrate understanding and problem solving in relation to engineering issues involving diverse stakeholders
**2.**
Manage work priorities and coordinate the work of others in accord with parameters set by management in a number of engineering contexts.
**3.**
Identify and communicate advice in a variety of engineering disciplines to address technical problems in accord with management requirements
**4.**
Demonstrate knowledge of technical and theoretical issues in a variety of engineering disciplines, underpinned by scientific and mathematical theory.
**5.**
Critically apply theoretical and technical skills to solve problems in relation to a range of engineering disciplines

### Progression to further studies
This diploma leads to the destination degree courses listed below. To be eligible to apply for an internal course transfer to gain direct admission into the second year of a destination course, you must achieve the average mark required for the destination course:

Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in one the following specialisations:

  * Aerospace Engineering
  * Chemical Engineering 
  * Civil Engineering 
  * Electrical and Computer Systems Engineering 
  * Environmental Engineering
  * Materials Engineering
  * Mechanical Engineering
  * Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering 
  * Software Engineering

### AHEGS
The Diploma of Engineering is an undergraduate award. The course is taught in English. It requires the successful completion of units that develop study skills in engineering. Graduates have gained a range of technical and theoretical skills relevant to the Engineering sector. Students demonstrate their ability to successfully undertake study at the university level. The course takes two years of full-time study to complete. The Diploma of Engineering is a Level 5 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.
