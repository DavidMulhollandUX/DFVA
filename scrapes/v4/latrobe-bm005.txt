## Master of Business Information Systems - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BM005
##### 240 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
If you’re keen to learn invaluable business skills and invest in your future, La Trobe’s Master of Business Information Systems (MBIS) will help you advance your career in an exciting sector. Designed in collaboration with industry experts, our Master of Business Information Systems will prepare you for professional practice.

Learn how to design, operate and analyse business information systems. You’ll understand the latest techniques and templates, so you’ll graduate with the skills to start your own enterprise or thrive in the world of start-ups.

Use the latest tools to analyse data, turn it into insights, then use this information to drive real business value. You'll learn through practical, team-based business intelligence projects and use industry tools such as SAP HANA, Design Thinking and R Studio. Plus, you'll collaborate with industry when you build and deploy a real-world business intelligence system.

You’ll also gain high-level skills when you choose a specialisation in your area of interest, including artificial intelligence in business, data science, applied digital health and more. Graduate with your specialisation on your academic transcript, so future employers can recognise your expertise.

Develop advanced analytical skills and understand how to use data to build information systems and databases. Learn how to create enterprise-wide computing systems and grow business intelligence. You’ll can also put these skills to the test – working in small teams, you’ll design a real-world business intelligence system. 

Learn from world-class academics and be immersed in research at La Trobe's Centre for Data Analytics and Cognition (CDAC). The CDAC staff also teach in the Master of Business Information Systems and work with international research groups to develop industry-leading research into artificial intelligence and advanced analytics.

Get an added edge with optional industry partnerships, such as the SAS work placement program.

You’ll learn:

  * Integrated information systems and business  * Learn how information systems are effectively designed, planned, analysed and managed in business contexts.

  * Business intelligence  * Understand how to use intelligent data-mining technologies to improve decision-making in a wide range of sectors, including retail, banking and finance.

  * Business analytics  * Understand the unique needs of businesses and learn how to improve their processes

  * Enterprise information systems  * Learn about enterprise-wide computing systems and understand how organisations use them to develop customer-centric business strategies and integrate business processes in accounting, finance, human resources, sales and marketing.

  * Artificial intelligence fundamentals   * Explore the fundamental concepts and different applications of AI. Design and develop AI models for real-world problems in our labs.

  * Predictive analytics  * Learn how to use data to predict future events. You’ll build and use predictive models in real business scenarios and learn how to use one of the most widely used predictive analytics software tools.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Apply data analysis insights to the design and implementation of the business and IT alignment to meet organisation requirements and support its business decision making system
**2.**
Apply advanced knowledge and skills in information management and systems principles in a business analytics context.
**3.**
Employ effective teamwork strategies to collaboratively develop solutions to complex business problems using traditional or agile project management methodology
**4.**
Critically evaluate and analyse data using complex problem-solving techniques such as business intelligence and enterprise resource planning
**5.**
Design, evaluate and apply advanced techniques of Analytics in an industry-appropriate manner

Critically formulate a considered position, employing a range of theoretical frameworks and data analysis to address business issues and create appropriate solutions

### Career outcomes
Skilled business information specialists can gain employment in many industries, including finance, health and medicine, cybersecurity, defence and agriculture. After graduation, you could work across a wide range of sectors in roles such as:

  * Business analyst
  * Understand how businesses run and use data to solve problems and improve processes.

  * Business-IT strategy consultant
  * Work with both senior management and business units to create business-IT solutions.

  * Enterprise resource planning (ERP) analyst
  * Help organisations to use ERP solutions to solve their business problems.

  * Data scientist
  * Understand complex data and leverage it to the advantage of businesses and organisations.

  * ICT entrepreneurial and innovation manager
  * Create robust business-IT strategies. Implement ICT products and services, and manage their ongoing development.

Subject: BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE PROJECT IMPLEMENTATION (BUS5BPI)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Conducting a Business Intelligence (BI) project is part of the day-to-day operation of the business analysts which involves project planning, client's requirements analysis, solution design, implementation, and deployment. An effective BI project produces a solution for corporate leaders to make more informed, strategic decisions, improve operational efficiency, gain a competitive edge, and drive sustainable, data-driven growth for their organisations. In this unit, students will have the opportunity to apply many of the aspects covered in this program through undertaking project preparation, construction, testing, and deployment of a real-world BI system. Working in small groups/project teams for a simulated consulting firm, students will implement a project solution design in the form of a BI dashboard for a real client. Students will fully develop the design, and then present it in a demo to the client. Students will also document a blueprint of the final product for the client and plan for the deployment, client training, post-implementation review, and maintenance.
Learning Outcomes:
- Assess the suitability of business intelligence frameworks and apply them effectively to generate data-driven insights in varying business environments.
- Demonstrate professional competence in managing client relationships and stakeholder expectations within diverse, real-world business contexts
- Lead and collaborate in multidisciplinary teams to research, produce, and deliver professional-standard reports and presentations for industry stakeholders.
- Critically evaluate, design, and apply advanced business intelligence project methodologies to address complex industry problems.

Subject: DATA WRANGLING (BUS5DWR)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject introduces you to the various techniques of data wrangling with a strong focus on hands-on experience in programming using Structured Query Language (SQL) and the Python programming language. It will cover the basic concepts in relational database design including Entity Relationship (ER) modelling and SQL as a tool for basic data wrangling. You will also learn various types of data sources and common data formats. The subject equips you with skills in data wrangling, including data import and export, basic data integration, assessment and transformation. Upon completion, you will be able to perform a variety of data wrangling tasks using SQL and a programming language for different kinds of data types.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and implement database models that meet business requirements using advanced data modelling and querying techniques to enable analytics initiatives.
- Analyse data by developing SQL queries and data wrangling scripts using programming tools with responsible use of generative AI to support data transformation and insight generation for data-driven business decisions.
- Evaluate and compare data sources, formats, and structures to prepare and transform data for analysis, considering ethical, legal, and cybersecurity risks in business analytics.
- Formulate data and data-wrangling requirements that address the technical challenges of analytics tasks, incorporating data governance, ethical principles, and cybersecurity constraints.

Subject: ENTERPRISE INFORMATION SYSTEMS (BUS5EIS)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Organisations today are employing many enterprise information systems such as Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) for developing customer-centric business strategies and integration of business processes in accounting, finance, human resources, sales and marketing. In this subject, you will be provided with an overview of enterprise-wide computing and integrated enterprise systems concepts. You will be introduced to the skills needed to become an ERP consultant and have the opportunity to develop your capacity to analyse and reengineer business processes based on ERP life cycle using well known ERP tools like SAP S/4 HANA. You will critique business process re-engineering strategies of organisations in the context of ERP systems. Case studies and software packages such as SAP S/4 HANA will be used to provide you with hands-on experience.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse and evaluate business process reengineering strategies and their alignment with enterprise resource planning(ERP) systems to support organisational transformation
- Collaboratively assess organisational size, complexity, and context to inform the strategic selection, design, and implementation of ERP systems
- Manage business data and information in ERP systems by applying advanced ERP knowledge and technical skills using the latest technology, such as SAP S/4HANA.
- Analyse the role of AI and intelligent enterprise technologies in enhancing ERP capabilities, including data-mining, CRM, and data-driven decision-making

Subject: MANAGING IT PROJECTS (BUS5MIP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: IT projects involve multiple stakeholders with competing interests, operate in dynamic business environments and must deliver projects in increasingly short time-frames. In this subject you will examine the foundations of project management involving scope, cost, time, human resources, procurement, risk, quality, communication and integration in the context of information systems projects and methodologies. Management techniques to effectively respond to the organisational, political and cultural barriers IT projects typically face will be discussed. Risk and stakeholder analysis, and critical chain scheduling will be taught. You will also consider advanced topics like management of cross-organisational projects, societal issues, teamwork issues, project office functions and project management toolsets. The agile project management concepts and development are also discussed.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically examine the methodologies for managing IT project requirements across the full project lifecycle
- Assess the processes required for IT project initiation to incorporate scope, time, cost, and quality management
- Demonstrate leadership, collaboration, and accountability in managing project teams and producing professional-standard project documentation and reports
- Apply advanced techniques in executing, monitoring, and controlling IT projects, with consideration for risk, communication, and stakeholder engagement

Subject: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FUNDAMENTALS   (CSE4002)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Semester 1/Term 2: subject description: The field of Artificial Intelligence (AI) engages computers for reasoning and decision-making. In this subject, you will be introduced to the fundamental concepts and different applications of AI. The main topics include searching, knowledge representation and reasoning, expert system design and development, and responsible AI principles and applications. Practice in the design and development of AI models for real world problems will be offered in laboratory classes. (Includes subject intended learning outcomes 1-4) Semester 2/Term 4: subject description: This subject gives you an introduction to AI and how AI transforms business and society. You will learn principal AI concepts, explore a variety of AI techniques for solving different types of problems, and examine ethical issues and concerns associated with AI. Upon completion of the subject, you will be equipped to recognise, select, and apply existing AI methods in both computer science and non-computer science fields that engage with AI technologies. (Includes subject intended learning outcomes 5-8)
Learning Outcomes:
- Semester 2/Term 4 - Assess ethical, social, and technical implications of AI systems to inform responsible decision-making.
- Semester 1/Term 2 - Analyse and design basic machine learning algorithms to model a practical case.
- Semester 2/Term 4 - Evaluate the suitability of different AI approaches for real-world problems and justify their selection in practical scenarios.
- Semester 2/Term 4 - Apply AI approaches to solve business or technology challenges, considering constraints and objectives.
- Semester 1/Term 2 - Represent knowledge using semantic networks, scripts, frames and logic-based mechanisms and write simple Prolog programs to enable automated reasoning.
- Semester 1/Term 2 - Construct simple expert systems to solve a real-world problem.
- Semester 1/Term 2 - Devise appropriate representations for state space search and game playing to solve practical problems.
- Semester 2/Term 4 - Critically interpret and explain the core concepts of artificial intelligence and evaluate how common AI models are designed to operate and generate outputs in varied contexts.
