### Overview
With the ongoing expansion and transformation of international markets, there is a rising demand for professionals who can navigate complex business environments across borders. If you are looking to accelerate your career in international business, La Trobe’s Graduate Certificate in International Business is designed to give you a competitive advantage.

Throughout the duration of this course, you will develop effective communication skills for cross-cultural settings and formulate strategies to lead diverse teams and organisations across various international business contexts. Alongside core business competencies, you will examine how emerging technologies are shaping the international landscape. The course provides key insights into artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity, helping you understand the opportunities and challenges presented by digital transformation.

You will also benefit from opportunities to engage with industry professionals and gain practical perspectives from experienced experts. By the end of the course, you will be equipped with a solid understanding of international business, digital consciousness and cross-cultural leadership, positioning you to lead competently in today’s interconnected business environment.

Upon successful completion of the course, you will be awarded the Graduate Certificate in International Business. If you wish to pursue further postgraduate study, you can continue your education with credit by joining our Master of International Business.

Subject: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR BUSINESS (BUS4005)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the nature and function of business. However, business leaders are challenged by the effective and competitive adoption of AI. This subject provides you with an industry-ready skillset to empower productivity, innovation and decision-making across the business ecosystem. You will learn to apply prompting techniques, build and evaluate AI chatbots, construct and articulate AI strategies. Learning Outcomes:
- Apply and critically evaluate prompting techniques and AI tools in diverse business contexts
- Analyse and critically evaluate AI strategies and policies to support responsible adoption in varied business environments
- Design and develop in teams, AI chatbot solutions that address complex business challenges, demonstrating alignment with ethical principles, governance frameworks, and cybersecurity standards.

Subject: CYBERSECURITY FOR BUSINESS (BUS4006)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Cybersecurity for Business provides a comprehensive overview of cybersecurity principles, practices, and frameworks for managing cybersecurity risks in organisations. Cybersecurity plays a critical role in an organisation's operations by safeguarding its assets, ensuring regulatory compliance, and enhancing overall business resilience. The subject covers key topics, including cybersecurity threats, attacks, governance frameworks, cybersecurity risk management, cybersecurity policies and procedures, and compliance. You will explore cybersecurity for businesses through a combination of tailor-made case studies and hands-on workshops, offering a comprehensive understanding of theoretical principles alongside practical application in real-world scenarios. It develops the skills necessary to craft effective cybersecurity strategies that align with organisational goals and regulatory requirements. The course will also examine the ethical, legal, and regulatory dimensions of cybersecurity.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse different types of cyber threats, attack methods, and AI-driven threats, to assess their potential impact on business operations, risk management, and compliance obligations.
- Design and justify ethical and governance-aligned cybersecurity strategies using appropriate frameworks, AI-enabled tools, and security models to strengthen threat detection, incident response, resilience, and regulatory compliance.
- Identify and apply fundamental cybersecurity principles to recognise and evaluate common organisational security challenges, including those arising from emerging technologies and AI.
- Communicate cybersecurity governance strategies and AI-informed risk mitigation plans clearly and appropriately to diverse technical and non-technical stakeholders in professional business contexts.

Subject: MANAGING INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS (IBU4003)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: As the international landscape grows increasingly complex and interconnected, effective management of international business has become a vital capability for organisations. This subject provides a comprehensive introduction to managing international business, examining key strategic approaches to cross-border operations, regionalisation, and international competition. You will analyse how organisations engage with external environments, navigate political and social dynamics, and address ethical considerations and social responsibility within international contexts. Through critical study of international business functions, you will explore how organisations adapt structures and strategies to gain competitive advantage in international markets. This subject highlights how cross-national planning, organisational design, and people management interrelate in internationally oriented organisations, equipping you to lead effectively in a rapidly evolving international business arena.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply contemporary theories to align talent management with the strategic and operational demands of international business.
- Examine the role of business ethics and organisational governance in international business management.
- Evaluate management challenges by analysing cultural dynamics, regulatory environments, and the complexities of cross-border operations.
- Critically analyse how regional and international developments influence managerial approaches and strategic decision-making in international business environments.
