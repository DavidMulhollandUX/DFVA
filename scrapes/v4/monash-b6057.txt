## Master of Business Management and Master of Project Management
##### B6057
##### 96 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The double degree in Master of Business Management and Master of Project Management provides emerging and established professionals with advanced business knowledge combined with the practical skills you’ll need to deliver successful outcomes. The Master of Business Management is an integrated and practical introduction to the practice of business management in a contemporary, dynamic and digital context. In this degree, you will learn how organisations can thrive in the context of digital disruption, transformation and accelerating technological change. Combined with the Master of Project Management where you’ll learn to think creatively, build and sustain productive stakeholder relationships and mitigate risks for successful project outcomes, you’ll be ready for the future. With its part-time and online delivery, you can also fit study around your busy work and family commitments.

In the Master of Business Management, a broad-based business degree, you will develop core business management knowledge and skills, while also gaining the foundation of business leadership knowledge aligned to contemporary employability criteria. As part of the degree, you will also have the opportunity to develop your professional presence and behavioural attributes to equip you for a managerial career and apply the latest thinking and models for implementing transformational change. Ultimately, you will apply all of the skills you have developed by completing industry projects in a sector or business of your choice.  

The Master of Project Management integrates the knowledge required to manage major projects and programs across multiple sectors. The course provides foundational knowledge and skills in delivering projects including the organisational and dynamic context of project delivery, learning and planning.  You’ll learn how to critically analyse project environments from managing people and stakeholders to communications, culture, and politics, as well as identify adaptive approaches to complex problems and use innovative practices to deliver successful project outcomes. You’ll also gain specialist expertise in the way organisation projects function, to deliver value through your ability to manage complex projects and knowledge and discovering ways to generate innovation in the project management process. You will have the opportunity to work on real-life projects where theory and practice are applied to problems situated in local and international, corporate, government and social contexts.

As a Master of Business Management and Master of Project Management graduate, you’ll be strongly placed to step into a managerial career with advanced business knowledge and skills, and you’ll also be ready for effective project leadership, managing resources and changing expectations in team environments, to deliver successful project outcomes.

### Structure
Double degree courses include all the features of the component degree courses, except that electives may be reduced or redirected.

Master of Business Management 

This course is structured in three parts Part A. Core studies, Part B. Discipline studies, and Part C. Application studies. In this double degree you complete:

Part A. Core studies

These studies will introduce you to business management practice at an advanced graduate level. They are designed to extend your ability to manage the organisation while building on existing professional work experience.

Part C. Application studies

The focus of these studies is professional and project work that can contribute to a portfolio of professional development.

Master of Project Management

This course is structured in three parts: Part A. Core studies, Part B. Discipline studies and Part C. Application studies. In this double degree you complete:

Part A. Core studies

In these studies, you will develop an understanding of the principles and techniques of delivering projects using different methods, processes and knowledge frameworks. You will explore the techniques to manage the project context, including people, stakeholders, communications, culture, and politics.

These studies will also introduce you to project management studies at advanced graduate level. They are intended for students whose previous qualification is not in a cognate field, and build on existing professional work experience.

Part B. Discipline studies

In these studies you will develop specialist expertise in organising the project function to deliver value through your ability to manage complex projects and knowledge. These studies will introduce you to advanced graduate level concepts in project management within allied disciplines. In this part you will also develop specialist expertise to critically analyse complex problems and to identify adaptive approaches so agreed project outcomes are delivered across multiple boundaries, utilising innovative project practices.

### Requirements
You must complete 96 points, of which 48 points are from the Master of Business Management (including all the requirements in Part A and Part C for the single degree) and 48 points from the Master of Project Management (including all of the requirements in Part A and Part B for the single degree).

Master of Business Management component (48 credit points)

Units are 6 points unless otherwise stated.

You must complete the following four units (24 credit points):

  * LEO5001 Leadership for organisational advancement

  * LEO5002 Strategic management for agile organisations

  * LEO5004 Financial management and resource utilisation

Subject: The learning approach to project management (OPO5903)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This unit focuses on the learning approach to project management. You will explore a range of contemporary methods, tools and techniques to support continuous learning spanning the project life cycle. By critically applying agile approaches, you will work in a team to develop your ability to adapt, respond to and drive project innovation through reflective and evidence-based practices from project development through to delivery.
Learning Outcomes:
- critically reflect on agile project learning approaches for improved project outcomes in complex organisational contexts.
- analyse project learning methods, techniques, tools, strategies and protocols in complex organisational contexts
- evaluate agile project learning decisions related to scope, tasks, stakeholders, resources and impact in complex organisational contexts
- design agile project learning approaches in response to project life cycle activities in complex organisational contexts
Assessment Tasks:
- 3 - Demonstration: 30% (Hurdle: None)
- 2 - Written: 40% (Hurdle: None)
- 1 - Written: 30% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: Minimum total expected workload to achieve the learning outcomes for this unit is 144 hours per semester typically comprising a mixture of scheduled learning activities and independent study. Independent study may include associated readings, assessment and preparation for scheduled activities. You are expected to complete all pre-class activities prior to your scheduled class, and post-class activities should be completed after your scheduled class. Learning activities may include a combination of teacher directed, peer directed and online engagement activities.
