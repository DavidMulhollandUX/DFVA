The Master of Entrepreneurship and Innovation is a 1.5 year degree for students seeking to improve their understanding of entrepreneurship and innovation in modern business, thereby improving business productivity, economic growth and global competitiveness. The program will provide students with the skills to encourage innovation and entrepreneurship within their organisation, be it a corporation, a start-up, a research organisation or in the public sector.
The program undertakes an interdisciplinary approach that utilises UQ's leading position in sciences and the commercialisation of those sciences, and allows for students to learn how to manage new ideas to maximise growth impact within a specialist field of study.

Subject: Lean Start up for Entrepreneurship and Innovation (EIBS7311)
Credit Points: 4
Overview: The Lean Start-up method provides a structured approach for developing new ideas into successful innovations that solve real customer problems. Failure to solve real problems is why 95% of start-ups and more than 80% percent of new products fail. As a result, not only start-ups but also organisations from small non-profits to large multinational enterprises are increasingly adopting the Lean Start-up method to test and refine their ideas. In this capstone course, you will learn the Lean LaunchPad, a specific variation of the Lean Start-up, as a structured approach to minimise the risks of failure and increase the chances of success when transforming ideas into reality. You can work on your own idea or on ground-breaking technologies developed by partner organisations. You will create a business model based on in-depth customer interviews, rapid prototyping and agile development. You will also gain a holistic understanding of the Lean LaunchPad approach in different contexts by sharing learnings with your peers.
Learning Outcomes:
- Create a validated Business Model Canvas.
- Evaluate the potential for a new business opportunity.
- Create a compelling presentation to convince a wide range of customers, investors and stakeholder of the potential for a new business opportunity.
- Create and articulate a new business opportunity by exploiting unaddressed customer problems and opportunities.
- Evaluate and validate or invalidate assumptions and hypotheses with a diverse range of customers about a range of problems and solutions.
Assessment Tasks:
- Assignment 1 – Humble Discovery - Big and Thick Data (Presentation)- (30%) Team or group-based In-person: 30%% (Hurdle: False)
- Assignment 2 - Stakeholder Journey and Reflection Presentation (30%) Team or group-based In-person: 30%% (Hurdle: False)
- Assignment 3 - Final Business Model Report (40%) Team or group-based: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Seminar 3 Hours/ Week

Subject: Business Industry Placement (RBUS7999)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This course aims to provide final year students with the opportunity to apply conceptual knowledge to the real world via a professional placement. As such, this course strongly links discipline learnings, personal employability skills and attributes developed by students across the duration of their studies with the challenges and expectations of working in industry. In doing so, students have the opportunity to gain valuable work experience in their chosen profession and to connect this experience to the development of a workplace ready skill-set. Successful completion of the course will provide students with the employability skills required for transition to the world of work. Further information about how to apply for RBUS7999 can be found on the BEL Faculty's work-integrated learning website .
Learning Outcomes:
- Reflect on and evaluate your employability skillset and attributes.
- Communicate your employability skillset and attributes.
- Create a professional development plan to further build upon your employability skills.
- Critically reflect on and articulate key business practices, processes and challenges within an organisation.
- Demonstrate the ability to think strategically from the perspective of a manager.
- Explain your industry employment requirements and trends.
- Clarify your career goals and pathways.
Assessment Tasks:
- A1: Internship Hurdle: Pass/Fail% (Hurdle: True)
- A2. Workplace reflection Identity Verified Online: 50%% (Hurdle: False)
- A3. Professional Development Plan: 50%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Seminar 3 Hours/ Week
