## Professional Certificate in Marketing - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### BP001
##### 30 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Professional Certificate in Marketing is designed for professionals seeking rapid upskilling or exploring a career in marketing. The course enables you to build your marketing skills in a practical, credible, and flexible learning experience, without committing to a full degree.

The course is delivered in a flexible blended format, enabling you to study part-time over six to twelve months while balancing life and career commitments. You'll study two core subjects from La Trobe's Master of Marketing, giving you the option to continue your studies into La Trobe's Graduate Certificate in Marketing or Master of Marketing.

You'll explore digital marketing practices, critically analyse marketing concepts across environments and contexts, and apply marketing principles to develop solutions for an organisation's marketing problems.

You'll learn:

  * theories and principles utilised in digital marketing and how to use digital marketing strategies and analysis to enhance digital performance.
  * marketing management, including marketing concepts, theories, and frameworks, and how to communicate the value of a specific product or service to target customers.
  * valuable skills for career progression and to meet professional development expectations.

Subject: DIGITAL MARKETING (MKT4007)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: With the prominence of digital platforms, it is critical for companies to supplement and further advance their traditional activities by managing their marketing activities via digital platforms. Digital platforms allow companies to enhance their productivity in achieving their objectives. Digital Marketing offers overall knowledge of digital marketing strategies and principles to enhance firms' digital performance. This subject develops your knowledge of theories and principles utilised in digital marketing. You will learn to analyse the digital marketing practices of a firm. Further, you will apply digital marketing principles to develop solutions for the digital marketing problems of a company.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design and recommend actionable, theory-driven digital marketing solutions such as search engine marketing and optimisation, inbound marketing strategies and customer engagement strategies for business challenges.
- Apply digital marketing concepts and theories to critically evaluate and improve company practices, individually and collaboratively.
- Critically analyse digital marketing practices including search, social media, content, and data-driven campaigns used by businesses across diverse industries and contexts.
