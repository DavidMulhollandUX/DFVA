## Career Opportunities & Employment Outcomes
As a graduate, students will fulfil requirements for titled membership of Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy Australia or Sports Physiotherapy Australia within the Australian Physiotherapy Association. Graduates will also be eligible to pursue the final stage training for clinical specialisation within the Australian College of Physiotherapists.

Choose the Master of Physiotherapy if you are a physiotherapist wanting to gain advanced skills in musculoskeletal physiotherapy or sports physiotherapy. Subject: Pain and clinical sciences in musculoskeletal and sports and exercise physiotherapy (PHTY7951)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Advances student's knowledge and clinical reasoning skills in pain and clinical sciences in musculoskeletal and sport and exercise physiotherapy.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate an understanding of evidence-informed practice and advanced clinical reasoning in musculoskeletal and sports and exercise physiotherapy.
- Understand different types of pain and underlying mechanisms.
- Describe and demonstrate the use of patient-reported and clinical outcome measures of pain, function and disability in the assessment and treatment of sports and musculoskeletal injuries.
- Demonstrate an in depth understanding and awareness of person-centred care/practice.
- Demonstrate an in depth understanding of when a client should be referred to other health care professionals.
Assessment Tasks:
- Written Assignment: Pain (Module 1) Online: 35%% (Hurdle: False)
- 5x5 presentation: Spinal condition (Module 2) Identity Verified Online: 30%% (Hurdle: False)
- Written assignment: Peripheral condition (Module 3) Online: 35%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Lecture 18 Hours/ Semester Contact 9 Hours/ Semester

===== SOURCE: https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=PHTY7953 =====
Subject: Exercise-based rehabilitation, strength & conditioning in musculoskeletal, sports & exercise physio (PHTY7953)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Exercise-based rehabilitation, strength and conditioning in musculoskeletal, sports and exercise physiotherapy
Learning Outcomes:
- Understand the acute and long‑term physiological adaptations and maladaptations to different types of exercise across the neuromuscular, sensorimotor, cardiorespiratory, and metabolic systems.
- Design individualised, safe and progressive evidence‑based exercise programs for diverse populations with musculoskeletal, occupational, sports, and chronic health conditions.
- Select appropriate assessment, monitoring, periodisation, workload management and criteria‑driven progression to optimise health outcomes at individual and group levels.
- Apply advanced clinical reasoning and behaviour‑change strategies to enhance engagement with exercise programs and support sustainable physical activity participation.
- Evaluate and critically reflect on the effectiveness of exercise‑based rehabilitation to improve client outcomes and refine clinical practice.
Assessment Tasks:
- In-semester Written Exam Online: 25%% (Hurdle: False)
- Exercise Program Written Assignment Hurdle Online: 35% Must Pass% (Hurdle: True)
- End of Semester Viva Voce Hurdle Identity Verified Online: 40% Must Pass% (Hurdle: True)
Workload: Lecture 14 Hours/ Semester Tutorial 6 Hours/ Semester

Subject: Research in musculoskeletal, sports and exercise physiotherapy (PHTY7954)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Advances student's understanding of and capacity to undertake research in musculoskeletal and sports and exercise physiotherapy.
Learning Outcomes:
- Understand key research methodologies in physiotherapy and health literature.
- Interpret and apply research findings to inform clinical practice.
- Understand the role of consumers and the public in research design and translation.
- Recognise the benefits of quality improvement projects in health services.
- Evaluate research quality using contemporary methodological frameworks.
- Conduct a research project demonstrating effective project management and data analysis.
- Create an original research manuscript suitable for scientific publication.
Assessment Tasks:
- Oral Examination Identity Verified Online: 30%% (Hurdle: False)
- Critical Appraisal Assignment Online: 30%% (Hurdle: False)
- Case Report Online: 40%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Clinical Teaching 60 Hours/ Semester

Subject: Advanced clinical practicum in musculoskeletal physiotherapy (PHTY7964)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Advances student¿s clinical expertise, reasoning and skills in evidence-informed musculoskeletal physiotherapy practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate advanced clinical reasoning and skills in the assessment, differential diagnoses and management of patients with musculoskeletal conditions and chronic health diseases.
- Demonstrate advanced communication skills when communicating with colleagues and clients.
- Demonstrate an advanced understanding of client needs, multiprofessional options and indications for referred in the management of clients with musculoskeletal conditions and disorders.
- Demonstrate advanced problem-solving abilities and goal setting for immediate management and planned treatment for restoration of the client's optimal function for work, recreation and activities of daily living.
- Demonstrate awareness and expertise in preventive programs for musculoskeletal health across the lifespan.
Assessment Tasks:
- Progressive Clinical Assessment Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 60% MUST PASS% (Hurdle: True)
- Clinical Consultation VIVAs Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 40% MUST PASS% (Hurdle: True)

Subject: Advanced clinical practicum in sports and exercise physiotherapy (PHTY7968)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Advances student's clinical reasoning skills and evidence-informed sports and exercise physiotherapy practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate advanced clinical reasoning, clinical skills and exercise testing and programming in the assessment, differential diagnoses and management of clients with musculoskeletal and sports injuries and conditions.
- Demonstrate advanced treatment planning and goal setting for immediate and ongoing management to optimise the client's function for work, exercise and sport performance.
- Demonstrate high-level understanding of client needs, inter-professional practice and indications for referral in the management of clients with musculoskeletal and sports injuries and conditions.
- Integrate feedback and self-reflect on strengths and weaknesses in clinical skills and reasoning in a sports and exercise physiotherapy clinical environment.
Assessment Tasks:
- Progressive Clinical Assessment Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 30% (MUST PASS)% (Hurdle: True)
- Clinical Consultation VIVAs Hurdle Identity Verified In-person: 50% (MUST PASS)% (Hurdle: True)
- Clinical Reasoning Form Online: 20%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Clinical Teaching 75 Hours/ Semester
