Subject: Microeconomic Policy (ECON2030)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: Extends microeconomic theory and demonstrates application to microeconomic policy issues; welfare economics, trade practices legislation, tariff policy and public enterprises.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate knowledge of market failures and the justifications for government policy.
- Understand and apply the economic concepts and frameworks used in the study of microeconomic policy.
- Critically evaluate the economic and social outcomes, and ramifications of microeconomic policy changes.
Assessment Tasks:
- Written assessment: 50% 2 Assessments (25% each)% (Hurdle: False)
- End-of-Semester Exam In-person: 50%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Lecture 2 Hours/ Week Tutorial 1 Hour/ Week

===== SOURCE: https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=FINM3414 =====
Subject: Financial Institutions Management (FINM3414)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This course will consider the financial management of financial institutions and the assessment of credit risk as applied to bank lending. Considerable industry involvement through guest lecturers and presenters will result in a practical understanding of risks assessment, financial distress prediction, asset/liability management, securitisation, bank regulation, bank profitability and the international banking system.
Learning Outcomes:
- Investigate the operations of financial institutions including ethical practices and the role of regulators.
- Analyse risk management in finance institutions.
- Measure the performance of financial institutions.
- Appraise securitisation, corporate capital raising, credit ratings and international banking.
Assessment Tasks:
- In-Semester Examination Identity Verified In-person: 20%% (Hurdle: False)
- Individual Research Assignment: 50%% (Hurdle: False)
- End of Semester Exam Identity Verified In-person: 30%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Lecture 2 Hours/ Week Tutorial 1 Hour/ Week

===== SOURCE: https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=RBUS4411 =====
Subject: Applied Research Project (RBUS4411)
Credit Points: 2
Overview: This course provides students with an opportunity to apply the tools and skills that they have developed over the course of the program to research. It involves a significant element of research and analysis, and students will be exposed to both industry and academic best practice in research.
Learning Outcomes:
- Evaluate and analyse information relevant to applied research.
- Analyse financial data using state of the art research techniques.
- Critically evaluate existing studies.
- Analyse research by and relating to Indigenous peoples.
- Demonstrate effective oral presentation skills that showcase knowledge, verbal and non-verbal skills and professionalism.
Assessment Tasks:
- Analysis of Indigenous research paper Identity Verified In-person: 15%% (Hurdle: False)
- Presentation and analysis of academic research method Identity Verified In-person: 20%% (Hurdle: False)
- Written critique of industry research Online: 15%% (Hurdle: False)
- Data analysis assignment - Part 1 Identity Verified In-person: 25%% (Hurdle: False)
- Data analysis assignment - Part 2 Identity Verified In-person: 25%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Lecture 2 Hours/ Week Tutorial 2 Hours/ Week

===== SOURCE: https://my.uq.edu.au/programs-courses/course.html?course_code=RBUS6996 =====
Subject: Honours Thesis A (RBUS6996)
Credit Points: 8
Overview: Independent research thesis on a selected topic within the area of discipline interest. Research is supervised by one or two academic staff members. Students commencing in Semester 1 enrol in RBUS6996 in both semesters. Students commencing in Semester 2 enrol in RBUS6997 in both semesters.
Learning Outcomes:
- Explain advanced knowledge of the field of study, informed by current research, theory, and scholarly debate.
- Propose justified research problems within the field and rigorous research questions and/or hypotheses.
- Conduct a substantial research project by applying appropriate methodologies in a systematic, critical, and evidence-based manner.
- Evaluate appropriate analysis techniques and software to analyse research data, synthesising findings to draw coherent and well-justified conclusions.
- Communicate research rationale, methods, and outcomes at an advanced scholarly level using discipline-appropriate conventions and technologies.
- Address social and ethical considerations throughout the research process by applying relevant ethical standards.
Assessment Tasks:
- Honours Thesis Topic/Supervisor Details: N/A% (Hurdle: False)
- Honours Thesis Topic/Supervisor Agreement: N/A% (Hurdle: False)
- Research Progress Check-ins In-person Online: 10% 5 check-ins, each 2% (total 10%).% (Hurdle: False)
- Research Proposal Presentation: 20%% (Hurdle: False)
- Honours Thesis: 70%% (Hurdle: False)
Workload: Supervision
