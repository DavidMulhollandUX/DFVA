## Master of Business Administration - On-Line

You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
La Trobe University’s Master of Business Administration (MBA) is an AI-first, fully online degree and designed for mid-career professionals, managers and technical specialists from non-business fields who want to develop the skills to lead teams, projects and organisations in AI-enabled and data-driven global business environments.

The AI first MBA is a curriculum and learning model in which AI literacy is a core leadership capability embedded across strategy, operations, marketing, finance, people, and governance so graduates can lead AI adoption responsibly, make high quality decisions with AI support, and create value through human and AI collaboration.

Delivered 100% online in accelerated six-week study blocks, this MBA is designed for working professionals, giving you the flexibility to study anywhere while continuing your career.

The program’s structure supports tailored learning pathways, with specialisations in AI, Analytics and Cybersecurity; Digital Health; and Leadership and Governance. You can also create your own pathway from a range of elective subjects.

The La Trobe MBA develops practical management and leadership capability for organisations operating in technology-driven markets. You will design AI-enabled business strategies that create sustainable value and are grounded in responsible and inclusive business practices, then apply them to real business challenges through industry-aligned assessments and applied strategic analysis.

Through a curriculum co-designed with global industry leaders and enhanced with artificial intelligence tools and insights and supported by La Trobe University’s collaboration with OpenAI, you will graduate with a portfolio of AI-driven business solutions designed to support leadership in transforming organisations.

Whether you are pursuing career progression, a strategic pivot or entrepreneurial ventures, you will graduate with advanced business knowledge, leadership capability and a globally recognised MBA credential.

By studying the Master of Business Administration, you will learn how to:

  * use digital and AI technologies to support data-driven decision-making that enhances organisational performance and competitiveness in global markets

  * design human-centred, AI-enabled business strategies that create sustainable economic value and reflect responsible and inclusive business practices

  * lead and communicate business strategies effectively to diverse organisational stakeholders across global and cultural contexts

  * analyse financial and accounting information, assess risks and apply expert judgement to solve complex organisational challenges

  * integrate ethical, environmental, social and governance (ESG) and strategic leadership principles when applying AI technologies to organisational decision-making.

### Course features
### Career outcomes
Graduates of the La Trobe AI-first MBA are prepared for leadership and professional roles where artificial intelligence, analytics, and digital technologies inform strategy, decision‑making, and organisational transformation.

Career pathways can include:

Strategy manager/senior strategy analyst  

  * Using AI‑enabled scenario analysis, forecasting, and market intelligence to support corporate and competitive strategy.

Management consultant 

  * Advising organisations on responsible AI adoption, innovation strategy, and data‑driven decision‑making.

Product manager/digital product lead

  * Managing AI‑enabled products and services, translating business needs into data‑informed and AI‑informed solutions.

Operations and performance manager

  * Applying AI and analytics to improve operational efficiency, forecasting, and performance management.

Business and management consultant

  * Help businesses identify ways to improve their operations. Lead projects and manage stakeholders to deliver more efficient and effective business outcomes.

General manager/business unit manager  

  * Leading teams and functions in technology‑rich environments using evidence‑based and AI‑supported decision‑making.

People and performance analytics manager  

  * Applying AI and workforce analytics to talent management, performance, and organisational capability development.

AI governance or risk manager 

  * Supporting organisations to manage AI risk, ethics, cybersecurity, and regulatory compliance.

Responsible innovation lead 

  * Integrating ethical, ESG, and responsible AI principles into business strategy and transformation initiatives.

Founder/startup Leader 

  * Launching or scaling ventures that use AI to create new business models, products, or services.

Graduates are not trained as data scientists or engineers; rather, they emerge as AI‑literate leaders who can critically evaluate, govern, and deploy AI tools to enhance organisational performance, innovation, and responsible decision‑making.
