## Diploma of Art and Design
##### U0602
##### 96 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Diploma of Art and Design is your first step towards completing your Monash Art, Design or Architecture degree. 

You'll learn how to work independently and develop your problem solving, team work and time management skills to prepare you for success at Monash University. You will also enhance your creative thinking, and develop your conceptual and technical skills in this field. This Diploma will also enable you to further develop your portfolio of work.

In Part 1 of the course you will develop your personal folio, which will show your creative work.

In Part 2 of the course you can choose your area of specialisation to prepare you for your Monash bachelor degree. There are five streams to choose between: Architectural Design, Fine Art, Communication Design, Industrial Design, or Spatial Design.

During part 2 you will study the equivalent curriculum and complete the equivalent assessments as first year undergraduate students.

Note: Only students who complete the two trimesters of part 2 will be eligible to receive the diploma qualification.

### Structure
This Diploma course is structured in two parts:

Part 1. Foundation knowledge

These studies develop your creative skills and understanding of relevant theory and principles, as well as provide the opportunity to develop your personal folio, which will show your creative work. 

Part 2. Discipline knowledge

You will study the equivalent curriculum and complete the assessments that are equivalent to first year undergraduate students. Your classes will help you develop your critical thinking skills and university research techniques. These skills will prepare you for success at Monash University.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Utilise technical skills to demonstrate understanding and problem solving in relation to Art and Design issues involving diverse stakeholders
**2.**
Manage work priorities and coordinate the work of others in accord with parameters set by management.
**3.**
Demonstrate knowledge of technical and theoretical issues in a variety of Art and Design theory and technical and language skills with depth in some areas.
**4.**
Identify and communicate advice in a variety of Art and Design disciplines to address problems in accord with management requirements.
**5.**
Critically apply theoretical and technical skills to solve problems in relation to a range of Art and Design disciplines.

### Progression to further studies
Once you have completed your Diploma with the required entry score and prerequisite requirements, you will have guaranteed entry into second year of a related undergraduate degree at Monash University.

### AHEGS
The Diploma of Art and Design is an undergraduate award. The course is taught in English. Graduates have gained a strong foundation in a range of creative art and design skills and developed a folio of creative work. Graduates will also gain technical, conceptual and problem solving skills relevant to the arts sector. Students demonstrate their ability to successfully undertake study at the university level. The course takes two years of full-time study or part-time equivalent to complete. The Diploma of Art and Design is a Level 5 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.
