The Master of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages (M TESOL) is designed for English language teachers who seek to develop their professional expertise and further their understanding in the areas of language education, particularly in teaching English to children, adolescents, or adults.

The program explores a range of issues relating to language teaching, learning, and assessment, and the socio-cultural contexts of education. It also develops themes relating to practical language teaching and learning issues in second and foreign language contexts. This program will equip you with the advanced skills and knowledge to teach diverse learner populations effectively whether you are working in a local or international context.

The M TESOL employs a comprehensive pedagogical approach to facilitate student learning, integrating traditional lectures and tutorial classes alongside experience-based and work-integrated learning to provide you with a well-rounded educational experience.

The M TESOL also offers a Graduate Certificate and Graduate Diploma option as a nested qualification – either as an initial entry option or as an exit option from the M TESOL.

5 Admission to candidature
(1) Available places will be offered to qualified applicants based on merit, according to the following admissions criteria. In exceptional circumstances the Dean may admit applicants without these qualifications who, in the opinion of the Faculty, have qualifications and evidence of experience and achievement sufficient to successfully undertake the award.
(2) Admission to candidature for the Graduate Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages requires:
(a) a bachelor degree or higher in a cognate discipline from the University of Sydney or equivalent qualification, AND professional experience equivalent to one year full-time in English language teaching; OR
(b) a bachelor honours degree or postgraduate qualification in a non-cognate discipline from the University of Sydney or equivalent qualification, AND professional experience equivalent to one year full-time in English language teaching; OR
(c) a bachelor degree in a non-cognate discipline from the University of Sydney or an equivalent qualification, AND professional experience equivalent to two years full-time in English language teaching
(3) Admission to candidature for the Graduate Diploma in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages requires:
(a) a bachelor degree or higher in a cognate discipline from the University of Sydney or equivalent qualification, AND professional experience equivalent to one year full-time in English language teaching; OR
(b) a bachelor honours degree or postgraduate qualification in a non-cognate discipline from the University of Sydney or equivalent qualification, AND professional experience equivalent to one year full-time in English language teaching; OR
(c) completion of the requirements for the embedded Graduate Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages, or an equivalent qualification.
(4) Admission to candidature for the Master of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages requires:
(a) a bachelor degree or higher in a cognate discipline from the University of Sydney or equivalent qualification, AND professional experience equivalent to one year full-time in English language teaching; OR
(b) a bachelor honours degree or postgraduate qualification in a non-cognate discipline from the University of Sydney or equivalent qualification, AND professional experience equivalent to one year full-time in English language teaching; OR
(c) completion of the requirements for the embedded Graduate Certificate or Graduate Diploma in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages, or an equivalent qualification.
6 Requirements for award
(1) The units of study that may be taken for these courses are set out in the Graduate Certificate, Graduate Diploma and Master of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages Tables of Units of Study.
(2) To qualify for the award of the Graduate Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages a candidate must complete 24 credit points of units of study including
(a) 12 credit points from the Master of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages core units of study; AND
(b) 12 credit points of selective units of study.
(4) To qualify for the award of the Graduate Diploma in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages a candidate must complete 48 credit points of units of study set out in the Graduate Diploma of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages Table of Units of Study including:
(a) 24 credit points of core units of study; AND
(b) a minimum of 18 credit points of selective units of study; AND
(c) optionally, a maximum of 6 credit points of elective units of study.
(5) To qualify for the award of the Master of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages a candidate must complete 72 credit points of units of study including:
(a) 24 credit points of core units of study; AND
(b) a minimum of 30 and maximum of 36 credit points of selective units of study; AND
(c) a minimum of 6 and maximum of 12 credit points of capstone units of study; AND
(d) optionally, a maximum of 12 credit points of elective units of study listed.
7 Recognition of prior learning
(1) Waivers and credit may be granted or the volume of learning may be reduced in recognition of prior learning.
(2) Credit may be granted for up to 24 credit points for relevant incomplete postgraduate qualifications.
(3) Candidates offered direct admission to the Master of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages may be eligible for a reduction in the volume of learning of up to 24 credit points, subject to the following:
(a) completion of a bachelor honours degree or higher in a cognate discipline.
(4) The maximum combined credit and reduction in the volume of learning for prior study granted to a
candidate will not exceed 24 credit points.
(5) When granting a reduced volume of learning, the Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences may specify particular units of study against which the reduction is to be applied.
8 Course transfer
A candidate for the Master of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages or the Graduate Diploma of Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages may elect to discontinue study and graduate with a shorter award from this embedded sequence, provided the requirements of the shorter award have been met.
9 Commencement date of these resolutions
(1) These resolutions apply to students who commenced their candidature after 1 January 2026.

Subject: Structure and Function of Language (LNGS7505)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This unit will focus on a functional approach to language, including its words, structures and sounds. The course considers linguistic resources for mapping reality, enacting interpersonal relations and managing information flow, alongside the expression of grammar as sound (phonetics, phonology, rhythm and intonation) or script.
Learning Outcomes:
- LO1 . understand the distinctive nature of a functional perspective as applied to the study of grammar and phonology
- LO2 . acquire basic grammar and phonology analysis skills, including argumentation motivated decisions about competing alternatives
- LO3 . begin to understand how to use linguistic analysis to distinguish texts from different social contexts
- LO4 . undertake collaborative text analysis projects with peers
- LO5 . begin to understand the ways in which phonology and grammar enact social values and identities.
