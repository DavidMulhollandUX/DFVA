### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Generate effective communication skills to effectively and sensitively work with colleagues, people accessing mental health services and the public in accordance with professional ethical standards
**2.**
Practice professional person centred care and advocacy independently as a team member in mental health settings in accordance with professional ethical standards
**3.**
Perform and evaluate clinical nursing assessments appropriate for mental health care
**4.**
Critically evaluate models of care to achieve personalized care strategies that meet the life and health needs of mental health consumers
**5.**
Develop and evaluate learning strategies that are responsive to reflective practice
**6.**
Critically analyse information to enable informed clinical decision making based to solve problems
**7.**
Develop and evaluate evidence-based nursing plans that support therapeutic relationships with mental health consumers and their families and carers that are characterized by co-constructive care and collaborative engagement
**8.**
Apply and evaluate advanced knowledge and skills to complex clinical nursing practice in mental health care

Subject: COMPREHENSIVE MENTAL HEALTH ASSESSMENT (MMH4CMA)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will develop fundamental knowledge and skills required in clinical mental health practice. Students will develop comprehensive assessment and management skills and recognise the interrelationship between a person's medical, social and psychological health. This subject provides a theoretical and practical foundation that prepares students for ethically oriented professional practice. This subject enables students to develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills, and specialised clinical proficiency. Using independent judgment, you will plan, implement and evaluate practice in mental health contexts.
Learning Outcomes:
- Develop and evaluate learning strategies that are responsive to reflective practice.
- Perform and evaluate clinical assessments appropriate for mental health care.
- Critically analyse information to enable informed clinical decision making based to solve problems.
- Develop and evaluate evidence-based nursing plans that support therapeutic relationships with mental health consumers and their families and carers that are characterized by co-constructive care and collaborative engagement.

Subject: EXPERIENCES AND PERCEPTIONS OF MENTAL ILLNESS (MMH4EPM)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, students will explore the complexities that exercising choice and control poses for people with mental illness and develop knowledge and skills such as supported decision making and recovery orientated practice. It will also explore the challenges and strategies for ensuring the collective voices of people with mental illness are heard about systemic issues.
Learning Outcomes:
- Develop and evaluate evidence-based interventions that support therapeutic relationships with mental health consumers and their families and carers that are characterised by co-constructive care and collaborative engagement.
- Practice professional person centered care and advocacy independently as a team member in mental health and educational settings in accordance with professional ethical standards.
- Develop and evaluate learning strategies that are responsive to reflective practice.
- Critically evaluate models of care to achieve personalized care strategies that meet the life and health needs of mental health consumers.
Workload: 

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH4MHD =====
Subject: MENTAL HEALTH DIAGNOSIS (MMH4MHD)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will critically examine psychopathology, mental health diagnosis and associated co-morbidities. This subject enables students to develop critical thinking and problem solving skills to be clinically knowledgeable in assessment and diagnosis of mental illness. The Diagnostic and Statistical Manual (DSM)and International Classification of Diseases (ICD) classification systems are critically evaluated.
Learning Outcomes:
- Consolidate and synthesise knowledge of mental health diagnosis to understand symptoms and psychopathology.
- Critically evaluate and apply available evidence to inform best practice in mental health.
- Make accurate assessments and informed judgements to develop reasonable and justifiable clinical formulations and plans.
- Recognise ongoing learning needs and use effective learning strategies for professional practice.
Workload:

- Critically analyse information to enable informed decision making aimed to solve problems.

Subject: ADVANCED THERAPEUTIC SKILLS FOR MENTAL HEALTH NURSING (MMH5ATS)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will critically examine theoretical frameworks for conducting advanced therapeutic skills in mental health nursing practice. Students will extend their understanding of therapeutic approaches including talk based therapy and working with people in crisis, using a problem-solving approach in the context of mental health care. Students will gain enhanced knowledge and skills required to undertake therapy with a specific focus on advanced communication and language.
Learning Outcomes:
- Develop and evaluate learning strategies that are responsive to reflective practice.
- Engage creatively in therapeutic relationships with mental health consumers their families/carers and significant others, which are characterized by co-construction of care and collaborative engagement.
- Extend the range of psycho-therapeutic interventions applied to recovery in different situations and contexts of care.
- Extend your current knowledge and use advanced therapeutic skills in mental health nursing, for common mental health and illness issues.
- Generate effective communication skills to effectively and sensitively work with colleagues, people accessing mental health services and the public in accordance with professional ethical standards.

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH5CNP =====
Subject: MENTAL HEALTH NURSING: CONSIDERATION IN TREATMENT AND RECOVERY (MMH5CNP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will analyse the nature and extent of social, political, economic and organisational influences on mental health nursing care. Students will engage in collaborative critical reflection on mental health nursing practice and address constraints in negotiation of options for mental health care. Students will explore the role of mental health nursing in the context of mental health legislation, advocacy and recovery. Students will demonstrate the role of the mental health nurse in a multidisciplinary and multi-professional approach to treatment and recovery
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate the role of the mental health nurse in a multidisciplinary and multi professional approach to treatment and recovery.
- Critically appraise the nature and extent of influences on mental health to address constraints in negotiation of options for mental health care.
- Engage in collaborative critical reflection on mental health nursing practice.
- Critically evaluate models of care relevant to the care continuum, to inform co-constructed personalised care strategies which meet the consumer’s life needs as well as health needs.
Workload: 

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH5MHN =====
Subject: ADVANCED MENTAL HEALTH NURSING PRACTICE (MMH5MHN)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will build on their knowledge and skills in mental health nursing practice. The subject emphasises advanced and specialist practice in mental health nursing. Students will develop cognitive and technical skills that demonstrate a broad understanding of a body of knowledge of underlying principles and theoretical concepts in mental health nursing., and an advanced understanding of mental health conditions and treatment planning in the context of recovery orientated practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Utilise analytical, problem solving, planning & evaluation skills to inform your advanced mental health nursing practice.
- Generate effective communication skills to effectively and sensitively work with colleagues, people accessing mental health services and the public in accordance with professional ethical standards.
- Critically reflect on your mental health nursing practice and determine how to reframe this practice to meet mental health nursing practice requirements.
- Make accurate mental health nursing assessments & develop reasonable and justifiable inferences in complex scenarios.
- Critically evaluate available evidence to inform your mental health nursing practice.
Workload: 

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/MMH5MHP =====
Subject: MENTAL HEALTH NURSING AND THERAPEUTIC PHARMACOLOGY (MMH5MHP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject students will develop knowledge and skills to identify the effects of medications on physiological, biochemical and pathophysiological body systems. Students will apply systematic clinical assessment and evidence based critical thinking skills to justify rationales for the initiation/instigation of therapeutic medications and treatment plans in mental health nursing practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Describe principles of pharmacology, including the differences between pharmacodynamics and pharmacokinetics, the manner in which medications are absorbed, distributed, metabolised and excreted in the body.
- Demonstrate evidence of collaborative care and communicate treatment plan clearly to patients, families and other health professionals.
- Expand your knowledge and use of psycho-pharmacology, including medication management, for common mental health and illness issues.
- Critically analyse information to enable informed clinical decision making to solve problems in the management and of delivery pharmacological interventions.
