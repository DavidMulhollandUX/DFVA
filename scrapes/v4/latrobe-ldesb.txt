## Diploma of Elite Sport Business - Carlton (Carlton College Sport)
##### LDESB
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
La Trobe’s Diploma of Elite Sport Business lets you turn your passion for elite sport into a career. You’ll gain the business expertise and practical skills to help sporting organisations and teams achieve success. Immerse yourself in a real elite sport environment by studying at the home of the Carlton Football Club – the Carlton College of Sport campus at Ikon Park.

Through 100 hours of placement with the club, you’ll work closely with industry experts, coaches and elite athletes. You’ll build your professional experience and network, while gaining firsthand business insights.

Alongside this, you’ll develop your strengths as a business leader and find out how organisations reach their professional goals through strategy and marketing. You’ll also discover the ways that business data can be harnessed to enhance the performance of sports businesses and teams.

Graduate with job-ready skills suited to entry-level roles in sport administration. Furthermore, you’ll have the opportunity to continue growing your business skillset. You’ll be eligible to gain direct entry into a La Trobe business degree where you can specialise in areas like sport management, marketing and digital business.

You’ll learn:

  * Sport management  * Learn how to use strategy, organisational structures and policies to reach professional goals.

  * Media and marketing in sport  * Examine the marketing of elite sporting organisations and develop the tools used to manage the relationship between sport and the media.

  * Business data analysis  * Discover how to apply statistical concepts and use this information for business solutions and decision making.

### Work based learning (placement) requirements
During this course, you will participate in one or more supervised Work Based Learning (WBL) placements designed to allow you to practice newly acquired skills, behaviours and knowledge while integrating theory with practice. The placements will immerse you in professional work culture, enabling you to develop and further enhance your skills.
### Career outcomes
Graduates with elite sport business expertise can pursue career opportunities in administration, project management, marketing and more.

Possible roles include:

  * Carlton Football Club graduate role
  * The Carlton Football Club has committed to hiring four Carlton College of Sport graduates into graduate employment roles.*

  * Sport administration
  * Take charge of the fundraising, procurement, supply chain management and general administration tasks of a sport organisation.

  * Sport marketing
  * Play a central role in how a team, sport or organisation is managed, presented and promoted.

*A total of four roles are available for graduates of both the Diploma of Elite Sport Business and the Diploma of Sport Coaching and Development.

Subject: DATA ANALYTICS CONCEPTS (BUS1001)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Modern organisations depend on data‑driven insights to inform strategic decisions. In this subject, you’ll learn how to prepare, analyse and model data using two complementary toolsets. First, you’ll build competence in Excel: cleaning and shaping datasets, creating dashboards with PivotTables and charts, applying probability distributions and regression techniques, and developing simple forecasts. You’ll also explore emerging AI‑assisted features that automate data cleaning and trend detection. Second, you’ll develop proficiency with Structured Query Language (SQL), progressing from basic retrieval commands through joins, subqueries, common table expressions and window functions to extract and transform data from relational databases. By the end of the subject, you’ll be able to combine Excel and SQL to build data‑driven solutions, communicate findings effectively and approach business problems with a structured, analytical mindset.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply probability distributions and regression analysis to business data.
- Import, clean and model data in Excel using automation tools.
- Use Structured Query Language (SQL) proficiently to retrieve and manipulate data.
- Create insightful data visualisations and dashboards using Excel ensuring ethical principles in the collection, analysis, and reporting of data.

Subject: DISRUPTION IN BUSINESS (BUS1DIB)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Traditional business models are being disrupted by numerous factors. Chief among them are new technologies that change how businesses operate. One commonality observed among these disruptive trends is the role of data. Data has become a highly valued business asset, forming a strong foundation to high quality decision processes. This subject will introduce you to the role of data in disrupting traditional business operations and formulating business strategy. You will gain an appreciation of how data can be turned into business insights through a process called analytics, using examples across a range of business problems. On completion of this subject, you will appreciate the pivotal role that data and analytics play in various strategic initiatives within organisations, and the strategies that link analytics to business performance while balancing issues such as ethical and data governance.
Learning Outcomes:
- Discuss how business performance management can be linked to any data strategy and data analytics initiatives.
- Understand the value of analytics from various case examples across key business functions and industries.
- Recognise the importance of data ethics and governance to business success and longevity.
- Explain the role of data and analytics in disrupting traditional business models.

Subject: SPORT REPORTING (JRN2SRP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Sports reporting is news reporting. It requires an understanding of sports-specific news values. This subject will introduce you to sports-specific news values through some of the most common forms of reporting in sport, press conference reporting, match reporting, profile pieces and analysis writing. This subject also explores the context in which sports journalists work, focusing on the major changes in the relationship between sports organisations and the media that cover them in the 21st century. In particular, it explores the implications of sports organisations' own media platforms and public relations staff on sports journalists' work.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate an understanding of the current Australian and global context of sport media, and identify opportunities and challenges to sport journalism practice in the digital age.
- Apply sports-specific news values to write sport news pieces, such as a match report and press conference report, to an industry standard level, to deadline.
- Apply the key fundamentals of professional writing such as correct grammar, punctuation and attribution as they apply to different media styles.
- Conduct an interview and create profile style piece using interview quotes and other relevant sources, written to a publishable standard.

Subject: PRINCIPLES AND PRACTICES OF MARKETING (MKT1MDP)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Marketing refers to the activity, set of institutions, and processes for creating, communicating, delivering, and exchanging offerings that have value for consumers, customers, clients, partners, and society at large. In today’s competitive business environment, it is important to understand the role of marketing in society, the marketing process and the marketing related decisions that businesses must take to satisfy the needs and wants of customers, and create value for stakeholders. This subject covers the principles of marketing. You will learn the role of marketing in business and society, and how marketing is based on an understanding of customer value. You will gain knowledge of core marketing principles including scoping and analysing the marketing environment, marketing research, consumer behaviour, and managing the marketing mix to influence consumers. In addition to marketing-specific knowledge you will also develop transferable employability skills in areas including communication, critical analysis and creative thinking, and working collaboratively.
Learning Outcomes:
- Individually and/or collaboratively develop marketing strategies in different business environments and contexts
- Evaluate market information, individually and/or collaboratively, to develop segmentation, targeting, differentiation and positioning strategies within culturally diverse business environments
- Explain marketing concepts and interpret their application, individually and/or collaboratively, within real-world business scenarios
