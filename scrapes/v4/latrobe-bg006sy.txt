## Graduate Diploma in Business - Sydney
##### BG006SY
##### 120 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
The Graduate Diploma in Business is a comprehensive program designed for graduates and professionals seeking to deepen their understanding of core business principles and enhance their management capabilities. This program provides a solid foundation in key areas such as management, marketing, accounting and finance, economics, sustainability, and artificial intelligence for strategic decision-making, equipping students with the skills necessary to navigate today's dynamic business environment.

Ideal for those aiming to advance their careers, shift into a business-focused role, or pursue further postgraduate studies such as a Global Master of Business Administration, the diploma blends academic theory with practical application. Students will develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and leadership skills through case studies, group projects, and real-world business scenarios.

Whether you're looking to upskill, reskill, or build a pathway into a master’s program, the Graduate Diploma in Business offers the flexibility and professional relevance to support your goals.

You'll learn:

  * Financial management  * Acquiring financial skills, including financial analysis, introductory accounting, and investment strategies.

  * Global marketing and consumer behaviour  * Adapting marketing strategies to diverse cultural, demographic, and economic conditions.

  * Ethics, sustainability, and corporate social responsibility (CSR)  * Understanding the importance of ethical practices in a global business environment and analysing sustainable business practices and their implementation internationally.

  * Leadership and organisational behaviour  * Understanding organisational behaviour in a global context, including team dynamics, conflict resolution, and motivation strategies.

  * Digital transformation and technology  * Understanding the impact of emerging technologies on global business and exploring the role of artificial intelligence (AI) in modern business management.

  * Economics  * Apply economic concepts and frameworks to current issues of concern for organisations and society.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Apply emerging technologies and digital tools, including artificial intelligence, to inform evidence-based decision-making and strengthen alignment with strategic business priorities.
**2.**
Evaluate and apply management strategies, operational practices, and analytical tools to enhance organisational effectiveness and performance.
**3.**
Integrate ethical reasoning, sustainability principles, and Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) considerations to develop responsible business solutions with social, global, and environmental impact.
**4.**
Apply business, economic, financial, and marketing concepts to make informed, strategic decisions in diverse and dynamic organisational contexts.
**5.**
Demonstrate leadership, communication, and teamwork skills to address complex business challenges and engage internal and external stakeholders effectively.

### Career outcomes
A Graduate Diploma in Business is a versatile qualification that can open doors to a wide range of roles across industries. While it's often used as a stepping stone into more advanced business education like a GMBA, it also provides enough practical and theoretical knowledge to access mid-level and entry-level positions — or even pivot careers.

Possible roles include:

  * Corporate management and change consultant
  * Work with businesses to solve complex problems, improve performance, and implement strategy and change.

  * Business development officer
  * Identify new business opportunities, build client relationships, and help increase revenue.

  * Operations coordinator/manager

  * Ensure day-to-day business operations run smoothly and efficiently, often managing people, processes, and budgets.

  * Project coordinator
  * Manage the day-to-day tasks of a project to ensure it stays on schedule and within budget.

  * Marketing coordinator
  * Supports the planning and execution of marketing campaigns globally.

  * Corporate sustainability officer
  * Support organisations broader sustainability strategies.

  * AI Strategy associate or consultant
  * Help organisations become more AI-ready or AI-driven.

  * Business administrator
  * Manage the operational and administrative functions of a company or department.

  * Executive assistant
  * Support senior executives or managers, often acting as a gatekeeper and organiser.

Subject: SUSTAINABILITY AND ESG REPORTING (ACC5008)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject provides a comprehensive introduction to sustainability reporting within the context of contemporary business practices. You will explore the principles of 'Triple Bottom Line Accounting' and develop expertise in both external and internal reporting aspects of corporate social and environmental sustainability. The subject examines how sustainability information can be integrated into organisational decision-making processes and explores the implications for assurance functions in modern accounting practice. Sustainability Reporting addresses the growing demand for accounting professionals with expertise in Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) frameworks, climate-related financial disclosures, and sustainability metrics. Through a combination of theoretical foundations and practical applications, you will develop the skills needed to navigate the rapidly evolving landscape of sustainability reporting.
Learning Outcomes:
- Collaborate effectively in teams to analyse organisational sustainability reporting practices
- Communicate sustainability reporting findings clearly and persuasively to professional and academic audiences using appropriate formats and conventions
- Critically evaluate the value of sustainability reporting, accountability, and reporting in contemporary business contexts
- Apply relevant theoretical concepts to analyse complex sustainability reporting issues with consideration of ethical, social, and environmental consequences

Subject: ACCOUNTING AND FINANCE FOR MANAGERS (BUA4002)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Accounting and finance are critical skills for all professionals in the modern global environment. In this subject, you will be provided with an introduction to key concepts, theories and practical applications associated with accounting and finance in diverse organisational contexts. You will be exposed to content which will cover the nature of the accounting and financial environment and the types of accounting and financial practice and decision-making required from a business perspective.

- Construct, interpret, analyse and communicate information contained in a firms financial statements using appropriate digital and information tools.
- Examine and apply financial mathematics tools and techniques to determine cash flows, cost of capital, capital investments.

- Examine the importance of basic accounting and finance principles, and differentiate the appropriate sources of finance for a firm.

Subject: ECONOMICS FOR GLOBAL LEADERS (BUA4003)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Economics impacts on all aspects of business and society. Key individual, organisational and government decisions around the allocation of scarce resources are at the core of economics, and an understanding of economic concepts, frameworks and language can greatly enhance a leader’s capacity to make sound decisions and influence the decisions of those around them. This subject develops students’ knowledge of economic concepts and provides the opportunity to apply this knowledge to real world situations. Students will apply economic concepts and frameworks to current issues of concern for organisations and society. Students will learn how to evaluate and apply critical aspects of economics such as the main market forces of supply and demand; the choices producers make in relation to their costs; revenues and profit; competitive and monopoly market structures and how these affect market prices; the use of game theory to make more strategic economic decisions; measuring a nation's income and the cost of living; the drivers of economic growth and the causes of unemployment.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply economic frameworks to business and social problems for policy and decision-making.
- Construct and present logical and persuasive economic arguments and communicate concepts professionally.
- Research and apply economic concepts to predict how changes in economic conditions may impact individuals, businesses and industries within market structures.
- Critically analyse economic data to explain relationships between economic variables and their impacts on policy, business and individuals.

Subject: INTERNATIONAL FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT (FIN5IFM)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: Financial globalisation, a heightened geopolitical environment, economic uncertainties, and increased ESG focus present unique challenges and opportunities for investors and corporations worldwide. International Financial Management develops your conceptual and applied knowledge and skills to manage the critical value drivers of firms operating globally. You will examine the structure and operations of global financial architecture, evaluate the economics, institutions, policies, and uncertainties governing exchange rate behavior, forecast exchange rates, and measure and manage exchange rate risk exposures. You will be trained to critically evaluate the relative merits and risks associated with the strategic financial management decisions of multinational companies, including financing, investment, and asset allocation decisions in a competitive global economic setting.
Learning Outcomes:
- Assess foreign exchange behaviour - exchange rate regimes, exchange rate determination, international parity relations - and their implications for forecasting exchange rates and managing exposures.
- Design and manage strategic financial decisions, including country risk analysis, foreign direct investment, financing, asset allocation, and tax management in international settings to deliver sustainable value.
- Critically evaluate contemporary global financial systems and current issues in international finance that affect the global flow of funds and financial management functions of global firms.

Subject: INTERNATIONAL TRANSPORT AND LOGISTICS (MGT5004)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: When cargo flows and supply chains extend beyond national borders, the complexity of international transport and logistics activities increases. This subject focuses on the global transport of goods, which heavily relies on ships, aircraft, and trains. As a result, more stakeholders become involved, additional documentation is generated and transferred, financial payment risks intensify, and disruptions and uncertainties can impact supply chains. In this subject, you will acquire essential knowledge and develop job-ready skills necessary for roles such as international transport and logistics coordinators and freight forwarders. This includes gaining familiarity with trade and customs policies, understanding documentation requirements for efficient cargo flows and physical distribution, developing the ability to design international transport and logistics plans for various business scenarios, creating recovery plans to address changes and disruptions in international transport and logistics, and effectively communicating information and potential changes to stakeholders. Additionally, the subject explores sustainability concerns, data analytics, and emerging technologies related to digitalisation.
Learning Outcomes:
- Design international transport and logistics plans for efficient, profitable, sustainable and resilient supply chains.
- Exercise professional judgement and evaluate international logistics management practices for a collaborative supply chain.
- Critically analyse the impact of international transport and logistics on a company's performance and collaborations within global supply chains.
- Critically analyse the implications of trade policies, international conventions, sustainability commitments, and digitalisation on global transport, logistics, and supply chains.

Subject: ETHICAL LEADERSHIP AND CHANGE (MGT5LIC)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: This subject examines the interrelated roles of leadership, and ethics in shaping effective and sustainable organisations. You will gain advanced knowledge and critical skills to lead with integrity, drive transformational change, and navigate complex ethical challenges in contemporary organisational environments. You will explore leadership theories and styles, the psychology of change, resistance and engagement strategies, as well as ethical frameworks for responsible decision-making. Emphasis is placed on understanding the social, cultural, and global dimensions of ethical leadership and managing change in diverse and dynamic contexts. Through case studies, simulations, and reflective learning, you will develop the capacity to lead ethically under pressure, influence stakeholders, and implement strategic change initiatives that align with organisational values and long-term objectives.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically analyse and communicate evaluations of contemporary leadership theories, models and their effectiveness in driving organisational performance and change.
- Collaboratively design and apply an ethical leadership approach that considers organisational context, stakeholder engagement, and barriers to change.
- Evaluate complex ethical issues in leadership and management and justify responsible and values-driven decision-making in diverse organisational settings.
- Reflect critically on personal leadership style and ethical values, and demonstrate the ability to lead change with integrity, accountability, and cultural sensitivity individually and in teams.
