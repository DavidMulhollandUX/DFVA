## Description
The Master of Biomedical Engineering (8660) is available only to students enrolled in the Engineering (Honours) / Biomedical Engineering (3768) or Materials Science and Engineering (Honours) / Biomedical Engineering (3133) double degree programs and is not offered as a standalone degree.

Biomedical Engineering is the application of engineering principles to developing technologies and solving problems in a diverse range of health care related fields e.g. implantable bionics, drug delivery systems, medical imaging, radiotherapies, orthopedic devices, telemedicine, robotic surgery, cell and tissue engineering, records management, physical rehabilitation and others.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

3. Utilise engineering techniques, tools, and resources to develop solutions for complex medical problems.
2. Interpret and explain the impact of contextual factors, such as regulatory, ethical, and societal considerations, on biomedical engineering practice.
4. Critically analyse and evaluate biomedical engineering designs and projects, identifying potential improvements and optimisations.
6. Demonstrate a deep understanding of professional and ethical responsibilities in biomedical engineering, exhibiting respect for industry standards and practices.
5. Assess and critique the effectiveness and ethical implications of biomedical engineering solutions in real-world applications.
7. Effectively communicate complex biomedical engineering concepts and solutions to diverse stakeholders, including medical professionals, patients, and regulatory bodies.
1. Apply comprehensive and specialised knowledge in biomedical engineering, integrating principles from both engineering and biological sciences.
