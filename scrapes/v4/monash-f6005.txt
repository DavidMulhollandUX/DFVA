## Master of Industrial Design
##### F6005
##### 72 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
This international double masters program enables you to complete the requirements of the Monash Master of Industrial Design as well as the second degree with Southeast University (SEU) located in Suzhou, Jiangsu Province, China.

This program allows designers to sharpen their practice and further their industrial design skills at an advanced level. Graduates of the Master of Industrial Design will have enhanced, highly developed capabilities, which will place you at the forefront of their field of design practice. Through the completion of sustained design projects, you are required to demonstrate high-level planning, resourcing, implementation, completion and evaluation, culminating in an expert understanding of the professional design process.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
apply advanced cognitive abilities, technical skills, business and marketing strategies, cultural enquiry and conceptual resolution to independent professional practice at the forefront of your contemporary industrial practice
**2.**
apply advanced understanding of the conventions of the discipline to engage collaboratievely and autonomously in the resolution and communication of design concepts
**3.**
identify and apply occupational health and safety measures appropriate to the area of study.
**4.**
analyse critically, reflect on and synthesise complex theories, concepts and propositions, communicate specific design strategies and methodologies with a high level of personal autonomy
**5.**
demonstrate a synthesis of complex theory and design practice through the execution and evaluation of creative endeavours, research, critical and social context

### AHEGS
The Master of Industrial Design is a postgraduate award. The course is taught in English and takes one and a half years of full-time study. The program of study consists of a series of supervised projects culminating in a design, product, including design systems, process or artefact. Graduates have had the opportunity to specialise in industrial design. Complementing these studios are a study in the formation of design research strategies, professional practice and other studies which extend their relevant skills and knowledge. Graduates demonstrate high-level planning, resourcing, implementation and evaluation skills and an expert understanding of the professional design process, as well as advanced marketing, business and analytical skills. The Master of industrial Design is a Level 9 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.

Subject: Advanced design communication (MDC5010)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This unit develops the practice of advanced design communication by combining techniques and media, as well as experimental methods. You will be required to examine current methods to help develop your design visualisation proficiency.
Learning Outcomes:
- Use drawing for persuasion and communication in a design context;
- Communicate your ideas through concept presentation sketches and renderings;
- Demonstrate advanced proficiency in the use of drawing as a means of communication to show salient features of an intended design;
- Demonstrate proficiency in a variety of techniques: traditional, electronic and experimental;
- Demonstrate an advanced practical and critical understanding of drawing as a method of externalising thoughts and developing ideas;
- Select appropriate techniques to suit the nature of the design or the communicative purpose.
Assessment Tasks:
- Single project / folio: 100% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: Block mode: 36 hours plus 108 hours independent study; On campus: 4 hours plus 8 hours of independent study per week.

Subject: Design studio practice (MDC5020)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: The unit provides an opportunity to undertake design projects and to develop design studio practice. You will be required to develop your own personal design philosophy, and consider the ways in which it responds to social needs and its visual and social expression as a preliminary to its development in your major project. The contemporary discourse in design and related theory is actively discussed and debated, and you are encouraged to develop a personal position relative to it. Both your intentions and the results of your work are debated in group discussions and critical sessions. This unit is only available to students enrolled in the double award Master of Industrial Design with Southeast University, China.
Learning Outcomes:
- Relate and critically analyse your design work against contemporary discourse and seek to extend its boundaries;
- Defend your chosen direction in the project against informed criticism, through the synthesis of complex theories, concepts, and design strategies;
- Apply advanced cognitive abilities, technical skills, and the practice of cultural inquiry to undertake your project;
- Practice design to a standard of professional competency; engaging collaboratively and independently in the resolution and communication of design concepts;
- Organise, develop and plan methodologies, for a project to successfully determine a viable outcome.
Assessment Tasks:
- Single project / folio: 100% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: Block mode: 48 studio hours plus 240 hours independent study. On campus: 8 studio hours plus 16 hours of independent study per week or equivalent.

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.monash.edu/2026/units/MDC5100 =====
Subject: Major design project (MDC5100)
Credit Points: 24
Overview: This unit represents the final stage of a multi-part design project and you are expected to continue with the work initiated in Design project (part one). Defined designs are expected to be detailed resulting in an assessable design outcome. Regular group and individual critiques will be undertaken as the project develops. Both your intentions and the results of your work are debated in group discussions and critical sessions. You will be expected to demonstrate a strong capability to detail design issues. This unit is only available to students enrolled in the double award Master of Industrial Design with Southeast University, China.
Learning Outcomes:
- Independently select and apply relevant design methodology/process to resolve and detail your design project;
- Lead, manage, plan and implement complex design projects within your selected field.
- Demonstrate an extensive understanding of your design discipline and its professional practices, including business strategies and ethical standards, within the scope of your investigation;
- Debate and/or defend your outcomes in an informed critical appraisal; demonstrating a high level understanding and analysis of complex theories, concepts, and design strategies;
Assessment Tasks:
- Portfolio: 100% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: Block mode: 96 studio hours plus 384 hours independent study. On campus: 16 studio hours plus 32 hours of independent study per week or equivalent.

Subject: Introduction to art and design health and safety (OHS1000)
Credit Points: 0
Overview: This is an online module in Moodle. A quick and easy introduction to occupational health, safety and environmental training within the context of courses and units offered by the Faculty of Art, Design and Architecture.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify the main types of hazard which may be encountered in studios and workshops - chemical, physical, and biological;
- Demonstrate an understanding of the nature of health and safety in the workplace, community expectations, and the relevant legislation;
- Demonstrate an understanding of the importance of controlling risk and how this is done.
- Understand the terms hazard, risk, risk assessment, and risk management, and have an understanding of the procedures for assessing and managing risk;
- Demonstrate that you are conversant with procedures for dealing with emergencies;
Assessment Tasks:
- Short quiz: 100% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: There are no classes - the unit is a short online module in Moodle, which will take approximately 15 minutes to complete. You should complete it as soon as possible after commencing study.

Subject: Research methods in industrial design (TDN5001)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: The unit develops the capacity to confidently engage in current design research practice and methods. Regular group and individual critiques of the design research applied to a project will be undertaken. Both your intentions and the results of your work are debated in group discussions and critical sessions. You will be expected to demonstrate a capability to investigate design research as part of design practice.
Learning Outcomes:
- Evaluate and apply design research methods to a design project, demonstrating a synthesis of complex theories, concepts and propositions;
- Investigate a range of current design research methods for design practice;
- Communicate specific design strategies and approaches reflecting on the outcomes of design research in design practice;
- Clearly articulate a design methodology and its critical application to the discourse of a design practice.
Assessment Tasks:
- Single project / folio: 100% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: Block mode: 36 contact hours and 108 hours independent study. On campus: 4 contact hours and 8 hours of independent study per week.
