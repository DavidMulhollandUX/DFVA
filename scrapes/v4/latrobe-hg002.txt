### Overview
Advance your career with La Trobe’s Graduate Diploma in Nursing and develop specialist knowledge and skills. Choose from a wide range of clinical and non-clinical specialisations and build your dream career in nursing.

Registered nurses working in Australia can access our clinical specialisations and complete advanced clinical training in a hospital work setting. Both domestic and international students can enrol in our non-clinical specialisations in leadership, health administration and public health.

Enjoy a flexible, blended learning approach that combines face-to-face workshops with online study that you can fit around your work commitments and access from anywhere.

Graduate ready to forge your unique professional path in the healthcare system.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Critique relevant research and utilise evidence based knowledge to evaluate clinical practice and outcomes
**2.**
Synthesise knowledge and develop critical thinking skills to optimise clinical outcomes.
**3.**
Exemplify professionalism through application of knowledge and skills with responsibility and accountability.
**4.**
Apply specialist theoretical knowledge and advanced clinical competence to facilitate professional patient centered care.
**5.**
Demonstrate competent and effective communication to ensure safe clinical practice within the health care environment.

### Course features
### Work based learning (placement) requirements
Compulsory placements (Work Based Learning)

During this course, you will participate in one or more supervised Work Based Learning (WBL) placements designed to allow you to practice newly acquired skills, behaviours and knowledge while integrating theory with practice. The placements will immerse you in professional work culture, enabling you to develop and further enhance your skills. WBL provides you with the opportunity to gain valuable industry experience at one or more of our local, interstate or international industry partners or agencies. WBL placements will generally be arranged by the Office of the Provost Placements and WBL Team at La Trobe University. The number of hours undertaken can vary considerably, depending on the requirements of the course. Students may incur additional costs and it may be necessary to travel or relocate to complete a specific placement. Please refer to the individual subject entries for more information.

Industry informed curriculum, Industry-informed projects, Interprofessional learning, Career development learning
### Career outcomes
Depending on your specialisation, you could be eligible for a range of clinical or non-clinical healthcare roles.

These may include:

  * Cardiac nurse
  * Deliver specialised advanced nursing care to patients before, during and after cardiac procedures, to patients suffering from acute and chronic cardiac conditions, manage complex cardiac supports and assist the interventional team during cardiac procedures.

  * Intensive care nurse
  * Graduates of this course will be recognised as having advanced knowledge relevant to intensive care nursing, so there are increased opportunities to advance your career and increase your scope of practice. Many intensive care nurses also work in education, administration, acute pain service, liaison roles, research and as nurse consultants.

  * Critical care nurse
  * Provide complex and multi-disciplinary care to a broad range of patients from a variety of critical care areas, including intensive care, cardiac care and emergency departments.

  * Neonatal nurse
  * Provide supportive health care to newborn infants up to four-weeks-old, including those born with prematurity, birth defects, infection, cardiac malformations, and following surgical procedures.

Subject: CRITICAL NURSING ESSENTIALS (NSM4CNE)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop fundamental knowledge and skills required to successfully transition to and engage in post graduate higher education nursing studies. The student will develop appropriate academic literacy skills applied to case based acute clinical nursing care of patients and their families. This subject introduces the student to nursing in the Australian context addressing professional standards, and healthcare organisational systems. This subject covers: recognition and responding to the deteriorating patient; teamwork and communication; legal and ethical issues, healthcare organisational environments and culture and professional standards . At the completion of this subject, you will be prepared for ethically oriented professional practice, and have the foundations to undertake further advanced study relevant to your professional interest.
Learning Outcomes:
- Accurately interpret scenario-based data to identify clinical and non-clinical priorities for patients.
- Determine and justify appropriate professional practice requirements relative to patient care scenarios.
- Critically assess ethical requirements related to specific clinical and professional scenarios.
- Accurately relate theoretical knowledge to practice in relation to caring for patients who are at risk and/or deteriorating.

===== SOURCE: https://handbook.latrobe.edu.au/subjects/2026/NSM5CQI =====
Subject: CLINICAL QUALITY IMPROVEMENT (NSM5CQI)
Credit Points: 30
Overview: In this subject, you will explore the implementation of clinical best practice in healthcare and the ethical implications of clinical quality improvements for the profession and patients. You will review specific clinical practice from their own clinical specialty and create a plan for a clinical audit in order to provide recommendations for best practice. The ethical implications of changes to practice or quality improvements are considered, with particular reference to informed consent and 'benefits versus harms' for patients. Clinical audit poster presentations are delivered online.
Learning Outcomes:
- Synthesise evidence to generate recommendations for best practice.
- Design a suitable clinical audit to determine the influence of a specific nursing or midwifery intervention in practice.
- Plan a clinical audit and gather appropriate evidence to evaluate the influence of a specific nursing or midwifery intervention on patient outcomes in practice.
- Develop an action plan to disseminate findings and support quality improvement implementation.
