## Description
Rapid changes in work, technology, and organisational design have increased demand for professionals who can understand and influence behaviour using evidence-based approaches. Organisations are facing complex challenges related to decision making, leadership, wellbeing, and change, requiring practitioners who can integrate psychological insight with sound business judgment and ethical practice.

The fully-online Master of Business Psychology is designed to meet this need by developing professionals who can systematically analyse behaviour and decision making in organisational contexts. The program emphasises the use of psychological evidence, data, and contextual analysis to diagnose organisational issues, evaluate alternative courses of action, and inform practical recommendations. Students build capability in applying behavioural and business perspectives to real-world problems, using rigorous analysis and clear professional communication to support effective organisational decision making.

This program is suitable for professionals working in, or seeking to move into, people-focused, advisory, and leadership roles across business, consulting, and organisational settings. Graduates may pursue career pathways in areas such as organisational development, people and culture, behavioural insights, change and transformation, consulting, and related specialist or leadership roles requiring analytical and behavioural capability.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

1. Critically apply an integrated understanding of psychological and business knowledge to address challenges across different organisational settings.
2. Articulate complex business and organisational problems and use professional judgement to propose or evaluate evidence-informed solutions.
4. Synthesise and communicate evidence-informed psychological and business insights and recommendations to diverse stakeholders in professional and context-appropriate formats.
5. Apply ethical standards and awareness of social, cultural, and organisational contexts to make and justify professional decisions in organisational practice.
3. Source, analyse, and interpret data and contextual information to generate insights to support organisational decision making.
6. Demonstrate the capacity to influence, implement, and improve practices in professional and organisational settings.
7. Synthesise psychological, business, and ethical perspectives to analyse complex organisational challenges, and critically reflect on own professional judgment and development.

## Progression & Academic Rules
Students who wish to exit this program early may nominate to transfer to and graduate from the Graduate Certificate in Business Psychology or Graduate Diploma in Business Psychology provided the requirements for the relevant program have been met.

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
- A Bachelor degree or higher-level qualification (or equivalent) in Psychology, Business, Commerce, Human Resources or related discipline*;

- A Bachelor degree or higher-level qualification (or equivalent) in any discipline plus a minimum of 2 years’ relevant work experience**; OR

- The UNSW Graduate Certificate in Business Psychology or UNSW Graduate Diploma in Business Psychology.

*Related disciplines may include Behavioural Science, Management, Marketing, Sociology, Anthropology, Social Science, Human Services, Organisational Studies, and other similar areas.

**Relevant professional experience includes responsibility for tasks within a business or organisational context that involve understanding, influencing, or supporting individual and group behaviour. This may include experience in people management, human resources, recruitment and selection, organisational development, change initiatives, learning and development, employee wellbeing, customer or stakeholder engagement, or applied behavioural analysis. Experience may be gained through supervisory roles, participation in workplace change or improvement projects, or roles requiring the application of behavioural insights in commercial, public, or not-for-profit settings. Part-time roles and internships undertaken during undergraduate studies will not be counted.

**Limitations on Recognition of Prior Learning**:
Students may be eligible to receive up to 24 UOC (equivalent to four courses) as Recognition of Prior Learning, subject to approval and provided the eligible courses were completed within the last ten years.
