##### HMN

You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
Our Master of Nursing helps registered nurses build their careers through qualifying in one of our nine specialisations. The degree is flexible and easily accessible for working nurses through online study. You'll need to work at a hospital that focuses on your specialisation subject (like emergency or neonatal care) so that you can do advanced clinical training on site.

You'll do some subjects wholly online, so broadband internet access is essential for taking part in this degree. Depending on your specialisation, you may also have face-to-face study days at La Trobe or at one of our affiliated hospitals.

Many of our teaching staff are clinical nurses in demanding areas like emergency care, cardiac and intensive care, so you'll get high quality training you can apply directly into your practice.

In each specialisation you'll study the latest evidence-based research developments, undertake advanced clinical studies, study both theory and clinical practice, explore the physiological, psychosocial, ethical and rehabilitation aspects of care, and take part in supervised clinical practice.

You also have the option to take an early exit from the Master of Nursing and get awarded with a Graduate Certificate or a Graduate Diploma.

The qualification awarded on graduation is recognised in the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) as Level 9.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Generate and evaluate complex ideas and concepts to inform practice in a specialist nursing field
**2.**
Critically reflect on theory and professional practice and scholarship, adapting established theories and evidence to knowledge and practice
**3.**
Apply nursing knowledge and skills with a high level of personal autonomy/accountability
**4.**
Utilise critical thinking to synthesize complex information, problems, concepts and theories in professional nursing practice
**5.**
Utilise research, literacies and communication skills to justify and interpret theoretical propositions, methodologies, conclusions and professional decisions to specialist and non-specialist audiences
**6.**
Utilise advanced clinical skills and knowledge in situation management, biomedical and psychosocial concepts and person-centred caring in a systematic and coherent manner to provide professional specialist care
**7.**
Communicate effectively and sensitively with colleagues, clients/patients and the public in a variety of professional and social contexts in accordance with the ethical standards of the profession
**8.**
Design, evaluate, implement, analyse and theorise about developments that contribute to professional practice or scholarship
**9.**
Critically apply knowledge and skills in research principles and methods to plan and execute substantial nursing scholarship
**10.**
Critique and synthesize developments in a specialized area of professional nursing practice

### Course features
### Work based learning (placement) requirements
Compulsory placements (Work Based Learning)
During this course, you will participate in one or more supervised Work Based Learning (WBL) placements designed to allow you to practice newly acquired skills, behaviours and knowledge while integrating theory with practice. The placements will immerse you in professional work culture, enabling you to develop and further enhance your skills. WBL provides you with the opportunity to gain valuable industry experience at one or more of our local, interstate or international industry partners or agencies. WBL placements will generally be arranged by the College based Placements Operation Team or Experiential Learning Team at La Trobe University. The number of hours undertaken can vary considerably, depending on the requirements of the course. Students may incur additional costs and it may be necessary to travel or relocate to complete a specific placement. Please refer to the individual subject entries for more information.
### Work integrated learning opportunities
Industry informed curriculum, Industry-informed projects, Interprofessional learning, Career development learning

Subject: CRITICAL NURSING ESSENTIALS (NSM4CNE)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop fundamental knowledge and skills required to successfully transition to and engage in post graduate higher education nursing studies. The student will develop appropriate academic literacy skills applied to case based acute clinical nursing care of patients and their families. This subject introduces the student to nursing in the Australian context addressing professional standards, and healthcare organisational systems. This subject covers: recognition and responding to the deteriorating patient; teamwork and communication; legal and ethical issues, healthcare organisational environments and culture and professional standards . At the completion of this subject, you will be prepared for ethically oriented professional practice, and have the foundations to undertake further advanced study relevant to your professional interest.
Learning Outcomes:
- Accurately interpret scenario-based data to identify clinical and non-clinical priorities for patients.
- Determine and justify appropriate professional practice requirements relative to patient care scenarios.
- Critically assess ethical requirements related to specific clinical and professional scenarios.
- Accurately relate theoretical knowledge to practice in relation to caring for patients who are at risk and/or deteriorating.

Overview: In this subject you will complete the work (commenced in NSM5THA and continued in NSM5THB) associated with your research project. This work will be undertaken in conjunction with an academic supervisor and in consultation with the subject coordinator. The final product of your research study will be a written thesis. While not a requirement, the thesis may include drafted papers prepared for publication in high impact Scopus or Web of Science listed journals.
Learning Outcomes:
- Implement practical skills in writing-up the research involved in your project.
- Demonstrate high order skills in professional research through the execution and completion of the agreed research project.
- Derive a comprehensive report (as a thesis) of the research project undertaken and knowledge outcomes attained and submit a report.
