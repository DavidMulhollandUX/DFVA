## Master of Midwifery Practice - On-Line
##### HM010O
##### 180 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
La Trobe’s Master of Midwifery Practice gives midwives the opportunity to move into a new phase of their professional lives.

Delivered entirely online, you’ll have the flexibility to study as you work. Taking your industry experience into consideration, the Master of Midwifery Practice helps you focus on your areas of interest, and work towards the changes you want to see. 

Explore more than just the clinical side of midwifery through a range of specialisations and pathways affording you the opportunity to diversify your skillset and broaden your professional prospects. With specialisations in advanced clinical, education, management and leadership, and research, you can tailor the course to your individual needs and passions.

Working with our passionate educators, research leaders and industry experts, you'll graduate ready to demonstrate leadership in your domain of practice and move confidently into the next phase of your career.

You'll learn:

  * Continuity of care  * Learn how to summarise, interpret and critically evaluate the existing evidence on midwifery-led continuity of care models.
  * Develop the skills to inform changes to practices that improve health outcomes for women and babies.

  * Advocacy  * Improve the effectiveness of care and the experiences of midwives and mothers, through your specialist knowledge and decision-making skills.

  * Collaborative maternity care  * Appraise the role of midwifery in intra and inter-disciplinary collaborations.

  * Legal accountability  * Understand midwifery practice within legal parameters and professional standards, codes and guidelines.

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Synthesise advanced midwifery knowledge, skills, and scholarship to make evidence-based decisions in complex midwifery scenarios and professional midwifery contexts
**2.**
Demonstrate capability and accountability for midwifery practice within relevant legal parameters and professional standards, codes and guidelines
**3.**
Appraise the role of the midwife in intra and inter-disciplinary collaboration to provide comprehensive maternity care
**4.**
Critically reflect on and apply knowledge and skills in research principles and methods to plan and execute research-based projects or evidence-based scholarship
**5.**
Critically reflect on and apply knowledge of continuity of midwifery care to increase access to and improve effectiveness of this care
**6.**
Utilise research to generate and evaluate complex ideas and concepts to inform practice

### Career outcomes
After graduation you could work across a wide range of sectors including health, education, government, and research. Possible roles include:

  * Clinical midwife specialist 
  * Provide advanced patient care and consultation services, optimise patient care by supervising midwifery staff and develop midwifery practices, policies, and procedures.

  * Clinical educator 
  * Provide clinical training at a ward/unit level by formulating and delivering in-service educational programs, assessment and demonstration of clinical procedures.

  * Clinical unit manager
  * Supervise and manage a midwifery team, and work closely and form partnerships with interdisciplinary medical teams, including doctors, specialists and therapists.

Subject: FIRST NATIONS PERINATAL HEALTH (NSM4IPH)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will reflect on aspects of your own cultural self-awareness to advance your knowledge and skills in providing effective and safe care to First Nations Australian families in the maternity context. You will explore Australian history, concepts related to traditional and contemporary Indigenous cultures, the rights of individuals and groups to self-determination, and consider how culturally safe clinical practices can contribute to closing the gap in health outcomes. The skills gained in this subject will enable you to recognise how socio-cultural diversity may impact maternity outcomes equity and access. This subject is delivered online using online activities, and synchronous lectures and workshops to support your learning.
Learning Outcomes:
- Evaluate health inequity, the factors contributing to this, and analyse ways to close the gap in birth outcomes between First Nations and non-First Nations Australians
- Evaluate the interconnections between physical, social, historical, political and cultural factors and their impact on perinatal health outcomes by understanding First Nations concepts of health and healing.
- Development of culturally competent communication skills to facilitate therapeutic relationships, and support partnerships with First Nations families, health professionals, organisations and communities.
- Critically analyse the role of cultural safety in improving maternal and child health outcomes for First Nations Australians and how cultural safety influences practice.
- Critically reflect on personal and professional beliefs and attitudes in relation to working alongside First Nations women, babies and families.

Subject: THERAPEUTIC MEDICATION MANAGEMENT A (NSM5TMA)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will gain an understanding of the clinical perspectives of therapeutic medication management. The principles of Quality Use of Medicines will be covered, along with how legislative and professional issues inform judicious, safe, and appropriate prescribing and medication management in the Australian healthcare setting. Your learning will be assessed through a series of case studies, a comprehensive clinical case presentation, online examinations, and work-based learning.
Learning Outcomes:
- Justify safe and effective individualised patient medication profiles to achieve therapeutic goals based on review and analysis of relevant patient information (including comprehensive patient assessment, diagnostics and thorough patient history).
- Implement critical decision-making processes to tailor appropriate medication plans to meet therapeutic goals, patient informed choices and holistic patient management.
- Exemplify safe prescribing practices within applicable legislative and regulatory frameworks and compliance with the Quality Use of Medicines framework.
- Appraise credible and appropriate sources of medication information and evidence to implement an evidence informed approach into care planning.
- Rationalise advanced clinical practice knowledge and assessment to the pharmacological management of pain in own specialty area of practice.

Subject: THERAPEUTIC MEDICATION MANAGEMENT B (NSM5TMB)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject, you will develop knowledge and skills to identify the effects of medications on physiological, biochemical, and pathophysiological body systems. Systematic clinical assessment and evidence-based critical thinking skills will be applied to justify rationales for the initiation and instigation of therapeutic medications and treatment plans. Your learning will be assessed through a systems-based case study, a comprehensive clinical case presentation, online examinations, and work-based learning.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critique available evidence to inform and support clinical decision making in the management and delivery of pharmacological interventions.
- Exemplify safe prescribing practices within applicable legislative and regulatory frameworks to meet the clinical needs of healthcare consumers.
- Utilise advanced clinical knowledge to rationalise the indications for prescribing, de-prescribing and/or use of non-pharmacological therapies in the treatment of patients.
- Rationalise the differences in pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics in patients of diverse ethnicities and alterations noted to these processes across the lifespan.
- Articulate principles of pharmacology, including the differences between pharmacodynamics and pharmacokinetics, the manner in which medications are absorbed, distributed, metabolised and excreted in the body.
- Evidence understanding of medications effects on each body system and utilise this understanding in the application of pharmacological therapies in relevant area of practice.
