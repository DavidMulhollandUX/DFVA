The Graduate Certificate in Business Analytics is designed to provide o provide students with specialist knowledge in business analytics. Students will gain the ability and skills to recognise, understand and apply the theory, concepts, and models of business analytics to contemporary business environments. Students will develop the skills necessary to apply modern business analytics techniques to their chosen field of expertise including handling Big Data, an ability to code in the most popular analytics platforms and present formative reports that interpret and present data to enable decision-making within the firm.
The Graduate Certificate in Business Analytics is an AQF level 8 program with a standard full-time duration of 0.5 years.

## Program Learning Outcomes
Advanced and integrated understanding of the theory and practice of business analytics.
Ability to promote business sustainability through innovation and strategic thinking.
Effective communication skills to interpret and transmit specialised technical advice, knowledge and ideas.
Expert, specialised cognitive and technical skills in business analytics.

Subject: Customer Analytics (UoA) (BAFI5028)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course will cover a broad range of topics that will equip students to solve some of the most common customer analytics problems, such as customer segmentation, understanding customer retention and customer lifetime value analysis. A large part of the course will be dedicated to digital marketing analytics, which is becoming increasingly crucial given the rapid digitalisation of the marketplace. Students will learn about digital marketing pipelines and the business intelligence tools that support modern reporting practices (with an emphasis on Google Data Studio). Students will also learn how to analyse paid and organic digital marketing channels, design and analyse A/B tests, and perform sentiment analysis on social media data to understand what content resonates with customers the most.
Learning Outcomes:
- Transform qualitative queries into testable, quantifiable hypotheses.
- Demonstrate data scrapping techniques to collect and collate large sets of social media data.
- Relate ethical principles to the collection, storage and use of customer service data.
- Prepare and present high-value reports for business in a both a technical and non-technical manner.

Subject: Customer Analytics (MARK5001)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course aims to equip learners with advanced knowledge and practical skills in customer analytics. The course is not just about coding, but also about theory and practical hands-on application. The Foundations module introduces key topics, including customer segmentation with machine learning and customer lifetime value analysis. Building on these topics, the digital and social media module examines customer analytics for digital platforms, social media performance, and sentiment analysis. The advanced module extends these capabilities to demand forecasting, churn prediction, and customer experience experimentation, while also addressing essential ethical, privacy, and managerial issues. Throughout the course, students will have the opportunity to apply their learning using industry tools (e.g., Python, Google Analytics, Google Looker Studio), thereby developing the expertise needed to apply customer analytics to data-driven decision-making and long-term value creation.
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply advanced analytical techniques to customer data.
- Analyse customer data to generate actionable insights and support decision-making.
- Critically evaluate ethical, privacy, and managerial considerations.
- Communicate and collaborate effectively in customer analytics projects that drive value creation.

Credit Points: 6
Overview: The purpose of this course is to provide an introduction to both basic and advanced analytical tools for business disciplines.
