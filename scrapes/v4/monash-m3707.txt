### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Communicate ideas and results perceptively and effectively to professional audiences in oral and written formats
**2.**
Apply advanced knowledge and technical skills including theoretical, experimental and computational approaches as appropriate for the field of research to demonstrate autonomy, well-developed judgement, adaptability and responsibility as a learner
**3.**
Demonstrate cognitive skills to identify, retrieve, interpret, critically review and synthesise the relevant literature in the field
**4.**
Apply cognitive and technical skills to a range of complex problems including developing and executing a research project and collecting, organising, analysing and interpreting data with some independence
**5.**
Identify a gap in knowledge or evidence and develop critical, creative and innovative solutions to address the research problem
**6.**
Demonstrate ethical, professional and personal responsibility and behaviours consistent with good research practice and professional codes of practice

### Progression to further studies
Successful completion of this course may provide a pathway to a graduate research degree.

### AHEGS
The Bachelor of Paramedicine (Honours) is an undergraduate award.  The course is taught in English. Graduates of the course have demonstrated advanced theoretical knowledge and techniques in community-based paramedicine and relevant research skills including the capacity to undertake an independent research project and to communicate their findings to a high standard. The course takes one year of full-time study or two years part-time study following completion of a Bachelor of Paramedicine. The Bachelor of Paramedicine (Honours) is a Level 8 Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) qualification. The minimum requirements for admission are set out in the Monash University (Academic Board) Regulations.

Subject: Literature review (PAR4001)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: In this unit you will develop research skills, analytical abilities, communication skills, and acquire advanced knowledge in a specific area relevant to paramedicine. Under academic guidance, you will undertake an advanced research program, to design and develop a plan for a structured, comprehensive review of the literature in a topic area of interest to you. Further to this you will develop communication skills in both written and oral formats to convey your research process and findings to professional audiences.
Learning Outcomes:
- Formulate a comprehensive literature search strategy to identify relevant literature.
- Formulate effective communication strategies for peers and professional audiences using various formats.
- Consolidate and appraise the literature, and synthesise evidence.
- Design a structured, answerable research question.
- Identify an area of knowledge deficit relevant to paramedicine.
- Integrate professional attitudes and attributes in the process of research.
Assessment Tasks:
- Literature review report (3,500 words): 60% (Hurdle: None)
- Oral presentation (20 mins): 30% (Hurdle: None)
- Literature search plan (250 words): 10% (Hurdle: None)

Subject: Research proposal (PAR4002)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: In this unit you will further develop your research skills, analytical abilities, communication skills, and acquire advanced knowledge in a specific area relevant to paramedicine. You will undertake guided studies in research methodology and complete a research proposal to address an identified research question. You will acquire knowledge of the ethical principles of research and develop an ethics application for submission to the relevant research ethics committee/s.
Learning Outcomes:
- Integrate professional attitudes and attributes in the process of research.
- Formulate effective communication strategies for a variety of audiences using various formats.
- Propose the use of appropriate research methodologies and methods to address an identified research question.
- Design a structured, answerable research question.
- Identify an area of knowledge deficit relevant to paramedicine.
- Apply ethical principles in the design of a proposed research project.
Assessment Tasks:
- Oral presentation (20 mins): 30% (Hurdle: None)
- Plan (1,000 words): 25% (Hurdle: None)
- Research Proposal (2,500 words): 45% (Hurdle: None)
