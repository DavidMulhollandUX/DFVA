##### M9001

### Overview
The Professional Certificate of Health Professions Education provides you with foundational principles and theories that inform educational practice for health professionals.

### Post-nominals
ProfCertHlthProfEd

### Structure
The course comprises a foundation studies unit where you will be introduced to health professions education at a graduate level.

### Requirements
The course comprises 12 credit points of study.

You must complete:

  * HPE5100 Foundations of health professions education

### Course intended learning outcomes
On successful completion you will be able to:
**1.**
Describe and apply theories that inform educational practice
**2.**
Review contemporary teaching methods for a range of relevant contexts
**3.**
Develop a strategy for further learning as a health professions educator
**4.**
Plan, design, implement, assess and evaluate an education programme
**5.**
Describe and practice feedback strategies to support learning

### Progression to further studies
Successful completion of this course may provide a pathway to the Graduate Certificate of Health Professions Education (M4009) subject to satisfying the appropriate entry requirements. Credit may be granted towards other Faculty of Medicine, Nursing and Health Science postgraduate courses upon successful completion.

Subject: Foundations of health professions education (HPE5100)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: In this unit, you will develop knowledge and skills to enhance your education career, whether you are teaching at pre-registration level, preceptoring students on clinical placement, or supporting clinical education in your workplace. You will gain knowledge of educational theories and teaching strategies to optimise learning in the health professions across many and varied contexts. You will develop and apply skills in curriculum and lesson planning, teaching, assessment, feedback, and evaluation for learners and programs. On completion of the unit, you will be able to articulate your own philosophy of education in the health professions and have a plan for further learning in health professions education.
Learning Outcomes:
- Describe and apply theories and concepts that inform educational practice in the health professions.
- Critique contemporary educational methods in teaching and training health professionals.
- Design, implement, assess, evaluate and reflect on an educational programme for adult learners.
- Describe effective feedback and evaluation approaches to support learners.
- Develop a personal teaching philosophy in health professions education.
- Identify and apply principles of curriculum design and assessment in health professions curricula.
Assessment Tasks:
- Personal reflection (1,500 words): 15% (Hurdle: None)
- Critical appraisal (2,000 words): 20% (Hurdle: None)
- Oral presentation (500 word lesson plan; 15 mins teaching session; reflection-1,500 words): 35% (Hurdle: None)
- Critical appraisal (3,000 words): 30% (Hurdle: None)
Workload: This unit will be delivered entirely online through the Monash Moodle learning management system. Activities include readings, pre-recorded lectures, online discussions, and interactive sessions via Zoom cloud conferencing, self-directed learning and completion of assignments. There will be 12 hours of teacher directed study per week, including pre-recorded lectures, online interactive workshops and tutorial classes. 12 hours per week of self-directed study including readings, engagement of online discussions, and reflective written work. Total per week = 24 hours.
