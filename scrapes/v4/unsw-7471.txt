## Description
This program/specialisation is for experienced managers and professionals working in a broad range of industries that either wish to move into cyber security (or an adjacent discipline) or wish to improve their understanding of, and leadership skills specific to cyber security.

Cyber security is a fast-paced sector, even more so than the related IT discipline. Unlike IT, where the stakes for organisations who fail to keep pace might result in low satisfaction with certain systems, or slow response times (unless technical innovation is a market differentiator). With Cyber security, a failure to maintain pace with the rate of change can have devastating results; ranging from denial of services, loss of IP, or significant negative financial impact (not to mention reputational damage).

This program will equip managers and leaders with a broad understanding of the fundamental technical concepts with Cyber Security (and relevant adjacent disciplines), to enable them to communicate effectively vertically and horizontally across their organisation.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

5. Communicate effectively with diverse audiences to drive change, foster culture, and lead in large organisations, demonstrating independent and collaborative inquiry skills.
4. Assess the business and leadership aspects of the current and future state of cyber security
3. Analyse and apply critical thinking and problem-solving skills to issues in cyber security.
1. Demonstrate foundational skills and understanding of the current and future state of cyber security.
2. Possess a comprehensive and globally informed understanding of the field of cyber security, including ethical considerations, current challenges, and the history of its development.

Students must complete 24 UOC of the following courses.

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
To gain entry into this Graduate Certificate of Cyber Security Leadership, an applicant must meet one of the following entry requirements:

- A recognised undergraduate degree (or equivalent qualification) and a minimum of 2 years professional or managerial work experience, or

- At least 6 years professional work experience

Credit Points: 6

- Analyse the interconnectedness between cyber security and broader organisational concepts.

Subject: Cyber Security Governance, Risk and Compliance (ZSPS9001)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course focuses on Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) in the field of cyber security. Students will learn about governance structures, managing risk, audits for compliance, including legal obligations that organisations must meet. By the end of the course, students will be equipped with the skills and knowledge necessary to effectively manage cyber security risk and ensure compliance with relevant laws and standards. The emphasis will be on understanding and implementing effective risk assessment and management processes to ensure the security and resilience of organisational systems.
Learning Outcomes:
- Analyse the potential impact of different organisational priorities in the context of compliance.
- Describe the effectiveness of risk assessment methods and apply these methods in real-world scenarios.
- Communicate Governance, Risk, and Compliance concepts to a range of stakeholders, including technical and non-technical audiences.
- Evaluate risk profiles and make governance recommendations that enhance cyber resilience.
- Describe the principles and best practices of Governance, Risk, and Compliance in the field of cyber security.

Subject: Cyber Security Data Governance and Privacy (ZSPS9002)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course will explore data protection, privacy, and the law in the field of cyber security. Students will learn about the principles and best practices of data management, and how to navigate the complex landscape of privacy laws and regulations. The course will also touch on the skills required to navigate these complexities in the context of cyber security, data governance and privacy. Students will gain a deeper understanding of the challenges and opportunities presented by these critical issues in cyber security.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify the principles and best practices of data governance in relation to privacy and cyber security.
- Apply knowledge of data governance to categorise and treat data according to privacy, security and regulatory requirements.
- Describe the link between privacy and data governance, including ethical considerations.
- Analyse the impact on data governance and privacy, within regulatory landscapes.

Subject: Cyber Security Leadership and Strategy (ZSPS9003)
Credit Points: 6
Overview: This course on cyber security strategy and leadership aims to equip students with the necessary skills to lead and guide organisations in the constantly evolving field of cyber security. Students will learn how to evaluate and navigate the impact of new and emerging technologies in the organisation. This course will provide the tools required to inspire change, engage with executives, build teams, and create a strategy to drive the organisation's direction in cyber security.
Learning Outcomes:
- Demonstrate the ability to adapt strategic initiatives to different contexts.
- Demonstrate effective communication appropriate to a range of organisations, inclusive of technical and non-technical audiences.
- Apply concepts of strategy and leadership to cyber security.
- Analyse the impact of emerging technologies on an organisation's cyber security strategy.
- Analyse situations and trade-offs required for successful implementation of strategic goals.
