## Master of Strength and Conditioning - Melbourne (Bundoora)
##### HM007
##### 180 credit points
You are viewing the 2026 version

### Overview
If you’ve always wanted to work with athletes – or you’re a current practitioner who’d like to enhance your skills – La Trobe’s Master of Strength and Conditioning offers a unique opportunity to excel in this high-demand field as a qualified strength and conditioning coach.

Enhance your career as you learn the latest skills and knowledge on strength, speed and endurance. You’ll study with expert academics and industry leaders as you learn how to work with elite, professional and amateur athletes – and help enhance their performance. You’ll investigate and work with diverse athlete populations, including para-athletes, able-bodied athletes, and athletes of different ages and cultural and gender identities.

This course was developed in collaboration with the Australian Strength and Conditioning Association (ASCA), so you’ll learn the latest best practice. Our academic staff have a wealth of recent industry experience, and many have held positions with professional sporting teams, and state or national institutions. They have worked closely with development athletes through to Olympic medallists.

Immerse yourself in La Trobe’s state-of-the-art sport and exercise facilities in Melbourne, including our Strength and Conditioning, Biomechanics, Physiology and Performance Analysis Labs. Located in La Trobe Sports Park, they boast the latest technology and research facilities. La Trobe Sports Park is also the new home to Rugby Victoria, Football Federation Victoria and Australia’s women’s national soccer team, the Matildas.

You’ll benefit from both in-person and online learning. This flexible course includes four separate week-long blocks of intensive face-to-face classes and experiences in the first year , two of which are timed to coincide with the Australian Open and the AFL Grand Final. If you live interstate, it’s a fantastic time to visit Melbourne – one of the world’s top sporting cities – and meet likeminded professionals while you strengthen your skills and experience.

Take advantage of La Trobe’s connections to Melbourne’s top sporting organisations and work directly with athletes from professional clubs and our extensive partner network in your second semester. You’ll also complete an applied industry project or research thesis as part of your studies, which will open up a pathway to further research and study in this exciting field.

You’ll graduate confident, experienced and ready to take your coaching career to the next level – both here in Australia and sporting organisations around the world.

You’ll learn:

  * How to assess and develop strength, power, speed, agility and endurance qualities  * Learn how to design and implement training plans to improve the physical characteristics of athletes in preparation for competition.

  * High-performance management  * Learn how to work in high-performance environments and explore the principles underpinning an athlete’s success. You’ll learn how to design and deliver training plans across a range of sports and performance levels.

  * Coaching  * Learn how to develop meaningful relationships with the athletes you work with and understand how to work with other stakeholders who can help improve their performance.

  * Research methods  * Plan and execute research projects that focus on strength and conditioning practice.

On successful completion you will be able to:

Critically appraise evidence to make informed decisions and enhance human physical performance.

### Work based learning (placement) requirements
Compulsory placements (Work Based Learning)
During this course, you will participate in one or more supervised Work Based Learning (WBL) placements designed to allow you to practice newly acquired skills, behaviours and knowledge while integrating theory with practice. The placements will immerse you in professional work culture, enabling you to develop and further enhance your skills. WBL provides you with the opportunity to gain valuable industry experience at one or more of our local, interstate or international industry partners or agencies. WBL placements will generally be arranged by the Office of the Provost Placements and WBL Team at La Trobe University. The number of hours undertaken can vary considerably, depending on the requirements of the course. Students may incur additional costs and it may be necessary to travel or relocate to complete a specific placement. Please

### Additional costs
Prior to commencing a placement, students are required to meet all of the requirements of the degree in relation to immunisation. Students must visit a medical practitioner who will complete and sign the Immunisation and Health Record form.

Depending on a students immunisation history, the overall costs for vaccination may be up to AUD$1030. Students are responsible for all costs incurred. Further information will be provided post enrolment.
Clinical placements may incur accommodation, meals and travel costs and these will vary depending on the placement venue (occasionally interstate) and duration. Payment of these costs are the students responsibility.
Applicants will be required to hold a valid Police Check which is renewed annually. Students will have access to a reduced fee when applying via a La Trobe University provider. For more information on what a police check entails, refer to the Victoria Police website.
Applicants will be required to hold a valid Working with Children Check (WWCC). There is no cost for a volunteer WWCC. For more information and costs refer to the Working with Children Check Victoria website.
First Aid and/or CPR certification is required to meet the professional requirements of the degree. Further information will be provided post enrolment.

Subject: DEVELOPING STRENGTH AND POWER (SPO4001)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will be provided with the tools needed to design and monitor the impact of periodised strength training plans to improve sports performance across a range of contexts. This subject will also give you the opportunity to perform and coach weightlifting and powerlifting actions in addition to their variants. Further, you will explore the acute and chronic response to strength training programs. Advanced methods of resistance training will also be discussed. This subject includes professional learning, practical experiences or portfolio activities which contribute to the completion of the Silver Career Ready AdvantageTM award.
Learning Outcomes:
- Explain and critique the effects of manipulating strength training program variables on the acute and chronic physiological responses to training.
- Design and implement methods of testing strength qualities to improve athletic performance
- Perform and evaluate the correct technique for weightlifting and powerlifting actions, in addition to their variations.
- Design and critique periodised resistance training programs to improve sport performance across a range of sporting contexts for developing strength qualities.
- Justify the integration of advanced methods of resistance training into training programs and demonstrate its effects.

Subject: DEVELOPING SPEED, AGILITY AND ENDURANCE (SPO4002)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will explore, both practically and theoretically, the various speed and endurance qualities along with change of direction and agility. You will learn to design and implement training plans to improve and assess speed, agility and endurance performance across several sporting contexts. You will engage in practical scenarios where you will learn to coach the mechanics of running and apply training tasks to improve speed, agility and endurance performance.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critique the effects of manipulating speed, agility and endurance program variables on the acute and chronic physiological responses to training.
- Perform and evaluate the correct technique for sprinting and related actions
- Design and implement methods of testing forms of agility, speed and endurance and to improve athletic performance
- Explain advanced methods of developing speed, agility and endurance, and justify how they can be integrated into the training program.
- Design and critique periodised training programs to improve speed, agility and endurance across a range of sporting contexts.

Overview: In this subject you will be given the opportunity to develop advanced knowledge in relation to how overall training workload is manipulated over time to achieve competition goals. This subject includes professional learning, practical experiences or portfolio activities which contribute to the completion of the Career Ready AdvantageTM award.

- Organise overall training workload across range of sporting contexts and monitor athlete responses
- Critique, create and integrate flexibility and mobility programs for athletes
- Classify the physical characteristics and determinants of sport-specific performance and understand the role of the strength and conditioning coach in this process
- Identify and benchmark aspects of sports performance

Subject: HIGH PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT (SPO5002)
Credit Points: 15
Overview: In this subject you will develop an understanding of what is required to work in a high-performance environment and the principles that underpin athlete, program and system success in sport. Your role and effectiveness as a practitioner will be considered within the context of an integrated and multidisciplinary program, including concepts related to role clarity, project management and teamwork, leadership, organisational structure and culture. Case studies and problem-based learning approaches will be used to explore contemporary theory and practice of athlete development, performance pathways, monitoring and evaluation, strategic planning, high performance programs and frameworks, and sport system development. This subject includes professional learning, practical experiences or portfolio activities which contribute to the completion of the Silver Career Ready AdvantageTM award.
Learning Outcomes:
- Critically review the athlete development pathway of a sport.
- Identify principles of high performance in sport, and incorporate and evaluate these in a program or sport context.
- Reflect and assess current personal status in regard to accreditation criteria of a Strength and Conditioning Coach and High Performance Manager to plan ongoing professional development.
- Work independently and collaborate with others to solve complex problems in high performance sport and complete tasks and projects.
- Prepare and deliver a short business case for a program initiative or intervention within a high performance program.

- Design, develop and implement appropriate study methodologies and designs to gather data.
