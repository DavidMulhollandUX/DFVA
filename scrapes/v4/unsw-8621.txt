## Description
The Faculty of Engineering offers a two-year Masters Degree program. The program comprises specialisations leading to a Master of Engineering. Flexibility and choice are maintained throughout the program by providing many elective courses. It serves as an entry point for students to move into the engineering profession.

This degree is offered in the following specialisations:

- Civil Engineering
- Biomedical Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Environmental Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Telecommunications
- Mining Engineering
- Renewable Energy

The Masters Degree program is designed to enable students to fulfil a number of needs including:

- Acquiring in-depth knowledge within a chosen discipline.
- Deeper project experience.
- Increased technical confidence across selected research discipline(s).
- Developing and expanding knowledge and skills in engineering management.
- Providing opportunities for continuing professional development.
- Providing students with flexible pathways for completion of courses and a master degree qualification.
- Updating qualifications and knowledge to meet/ maintain professional accreditation standards.

## Professional Recognition & Accreditation
The following specialisations within the Master of Engineering program are fully accredited with Engineers Australia at the level of Professional Engineer. Graduates are recognised internationally through the Washington Accord.

Civil Engineering
Electrical Engineering
Environmental Engineering
Mechanical Engineering
Telecommunications

The following specialisations within the Master of Engineering program are provisionally accredited with Engineers Australia at the level of Professional Engineer. Graduates are not recognised internationally through the Washington Accord until the specialisation successfully transitions to full accreditation status.

Biomedical Engineering
Mining Engineering
Renewable Energy

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
Entry into the Master of Engineering program requires either completion of:

• 4-year Bachelor of Engineering degree (or equivalent) in a cognate discipline that is not completed in Australia with a minimum credit average (equivalent to a UNSW WAM of 65)

OR

• 3-year Engineering Science degree in a cognate discipline with a minimum credit average (equivalent to a UNSW WAM of 65)

OR

• a candidate applying for the following Master of Engineering specialisations must have completed a 3-year (or more) Science degree in the majors listed with a minimum credit average (equivalent to a UNSW WAM of 65):

Environmental Engineering specialisation -
Science major in environmental science, earth science, or chemistry.

Mining Engineering specialisation -
Science major in geology, geoscience, environmental science, materials science, hydrogeology, chemistry, physics, earth science, or mineral processing.

Renewable Energy specialisation -
Science major in chemistry, physics, climate science, climate systems science, physical oceanography, environmental science, or materials science.

OR

• a candidate applying for the Biomedical Engineering specialisation must have completed a 3-year (or more) degree in the below cognate disciplines with a minimum credit average (equivalent to a UNSW WAM of 65):
Medicine
Medical Science
Physiotherapy
Exercise Physiology
Dentistry
Science (majoring in biology, biotechnology, anatomy, physiology, microbiology, mathematics, statistics, bioinformatics, chemistry, data science, genetics, pathology, neuroscience, pharmacology, psychology, materials science, physics, or vision science.)

**Limitations on Recognition of Prior Learning**:
No advanced standing (Recognition of Prior Learning) will be allowed in this program.

Subject: Industrial Training (ENGG4999)
Credit Points: 0
Overview: " Exposure to professional engineering practice is a key element in differentiating a professional engineering degree from an applied science degree." (Engineers Australia). To satisfy the EA expectations of a graduating engineer, students need to have exposure to professional practice. At UNSW, this means the equivalent of 60 days of relevant Engineering Professional Practice. Students get first-hand experience in an engineering-practice environment. Students source their own placement to gain employability skills. Placements can be combination of traditional internships (minimum of 30 days) and UNSW industry collaborated projects. This course is to confirm that students have completed their Engineering Professional Practice requirements. Students must gain approval for each (up to 3 placements can be undertaken) placement by submitting the required information via InPlace. Once 60 days have been completed in InPlace, students are required to submit a Final Report via Teams Classroom. When all the requirements have been completed, a satisfactory grade will be awarded to ENGG4999. Opportunities, information and InPlace submission links can be found on the Engineering Professional Practice Student Portal .
Learning Outcomes:
- Apply professional and technical capabilities for industrial problem solving.
- Communicate with a broad range of stakeholders in a professional context.
- Demonstrate evaluative judgement, ethical conduct and accountability in professional practice.
- Work independently and collaboratively.
