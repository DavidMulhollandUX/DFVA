2   Attendance pattern
(1)   The attendance pattern for this course may be full-time or part-time according to candidate choice.
3   Streams
(1)
Bachelor of Music
(a)  The Bachelor of Music is available without a stream or with the following streams:
(i)     Composition;
(ii)    Music Education; or
(iii)   Performance.
(b)  Completion of a stream is not a requirement for the Bachelor of Music.
(c)  Candidates not undertaking a stream must complete a program or a major.
(2)
Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies
(a)  The Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies is available with the following streams:
(i)     Composition; or
(ii)    Performance
(b)  Completion of a stream is a requirement of the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies.
(3)   The requirements for each stream are specified in Table A for the Bachelor of Music, and the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies
4   Cross-faculty management
(1)   The Head of School and Dean of the Sydney Conservatorium of Music has authority over any matter concerned with the Bachelor of Music and the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies not otherwise dealt with in these resolutions.
5   Admission to candidature
(1)   All candidates for admission must:
(a)  present a portfolio of work and attend an interview, or
(b)  undertake a practical audition in a nominated instrument or voice.
(2)   Except for students admitted under the Creative Arts Special Admission Scheme, admission is on the basis of:
(a)  a secondary school leaving qualification such as the NSW Higher School Certificate (including national and international equivalents);
(b)  tertiary study; or
(c)  an approved preparation program.
(3)   English language requirements must be met where these are not demonstrated by sufficient qualifications taught in English.
(4)   Special admission pathways are open for mature aged applicants who do not possess a school leaving qualification, educationally disadvantaged applicants and for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people.
(5)   Applicants are ranked by merit and offers for available places are issued according to the ranking.  Details of admission policies are found in the Coursework Rule and the Coursework Policy.
(6)   Offers to the Bachelor of Music, Bachelor of Music (Composition), and Bachelor of Music (Performance) may be made to exceptional students under the Creative Arts Special Admission Scheme. Details of the requirements for this scheme are found in the Coursework Policy.

8   Requirements for Honours
(1)   Bachelor of Music
(a)  Honours is available to candidates who complete an alternative set of units of study in the final year.
(b)  Admission to honours is by permission of the Program or Honours Coordinator after candidates have completed 144 credit points of study.
(c)  Admission to honours requires:
(i)     a minimum Weighted Average Mark (WAM) of 75 in relevant 2000-level and above units;
(ii)    a minimum average mark of 75 in Analysis, History and Culture Studies units; and
(iii)   for candidates in a stream, the completion of stream specific requirements.
(d)  Specific stream requirements include:
(i)      for the Bachelor of Music (Composition), a minimum average mark of 75 in Composition 5 and 6;
(ii)      for the Bachelor of Music (Performance), a minimum average mark of 75 in Principal Study 4 and 6.
(e)  To qualify for honours, candidates must complete 192 credit points including 48 credit points of Honours units of study.
(f)   The grade of honours will be determined by an honours mark calculated from work in the honours units of study as specified in:
(i)     these resolutions; and
(ii)    the resolutions of the Sydney Conservatorium of Music; or
(iii)   the resolutions of the relevant faculty.
(2)   Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies
(a)  Honours is available for candidates who:
(i)  meet the admission requirements for a Bachelor Honours degree after completing requirements for the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies provided  in section 6; or
(ii) transfer to the Bachelor of Music before completing requirements for the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies as specified in section 6, and meet the honours admission requirements provided in section 8(1).
9     Award of the degree
(1)   Bachelor of Music
(a)  Candidates who have completed requirements in section 6, but who have not completed requirements for honours in section 8, will be awarded the Bachelor of Music.
(b)  Candidates who have completed requirements in section 6 and requirements for honours in section 8, will be awarded honours in classes ranging from First Class to Third Class as set out in the Coursework Policy and the Resolutions of the Sydney Conservatorium of Music.
(2)   Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies
(a)  Candidates for the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies will be awarded a separate testamur for each degree completed.
(i)  Candidates who have completed requirements for the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies (Composition) will be awarded a Bachelor of Music (Composition) and a Bachelor of Advanced Studies.
(ii) Candidates who have completed requirements for the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies (Performance)  will be awarded a Bachelor of Music (Performance) and a Bachelor of Advanced Studies.
10   Cross-institutional study
(1)   Cross-institutional study is available in this course under conditions specified in the Resolutions of the Sydney Conservatorium of Music.
11   International exchange
(1)   The Sydney Conservatorium of Music encourages candidates in this course to participate in international exchange programs as set out in the resolutions of the Sydney Conservatorium of Music.
12   Credit for previous study
(1)   Credit transfer is subject to the provisions of the Coursework Policy and the Resolutions of the Sydney Conservatorium of Music or, in the case of a major or minor offered by another faculty in Table S, any relevant resolutions of that faculty.
13   Course transfer
(1)   A candidate may transfer from the Bachelor of Music (Performance) and elect to complete the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies (Performance) or transfer from the Bachelor of Music (Composition) and complete the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies (Composition).
(a) Candidates wishing to transfer must do so before the start of year 2 of their candidature to receive full credit for work completed in the Bachelor of Music.
(2)   A candidate may transfer from the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies and complete the Bachelor of Music with the Performance or Composition stream.
(a)  Candidates who commenced before 1 July 2024 may transfer up to the end of year 4 of their candidature.  They will receive full credit, including 60 credit points for work taken in the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies, towards Principal Study and Core in the Bachelor of Music.
(b)  Candidates who commenced after 1 July 2024 may transfer at any time.  They will receive a maximum of 144 credit points of credit for work taken in the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies.
(3)   A candidate may transfer from the Bachelor of Music, Bachelor of Music (Performance) or Bachelor of Music (Composition) or the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies (Performance) or Bachelor of Music/Bachelor of Advanced Studies (Composition)and complete the equivalent program, major or principal study area in the Bachelor of Music (Music Education).
(a)  Candidates wishing to transfer must attend an interview with the Bachelor of Music (Music Education) program leader.
(b)  Candidates who are permitted to transfer:
(i)  must complete at least 48 credit points; and
(ii)  will receive credit for work completed in equivalent units of study in the Bachelor of Music , Bachelor of Music (Performance) or Bachelor of Music (Composition), or Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies(Performance) or the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies (Composition).
(4)   A candidate may transfer from the Bachelor of Music (Music Education) and complete the equivalent program, major or principal study area of the Bachelor of Music, Bachelor of Music (Performance) or Bachelor of Music (Composition), or the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies (Performance) or Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies (Composition).
(a)  Candidates wishing to transfer must attend a practical audition and/or an interview.
(b)  Candidates who are permitted to transfer:
(i)  must complete at least 48 credit points;
(ii) will receive credit for work completed in equivalent units of study in the Bachelor of Music (Music Education)
14   Transitional provisions
(1)
Sections 8 and 9 of these resolutions apply to all students in the Bachelor of Music and the Bachelor of Music and Bachelor of Advanced Studies, regardless of when they commenced their candidature.
(2)
All other sections of these resolutions apply to students who commenced after 1 July 2024.

(a)  Honours is available to candidates who complete an alternative set of units of study in the final year.

(c)  Admission to honours requires:

(i)     a minimum Weighted Average Mark (WAM) of 75 in relevant 2000-level and above units;

(ii)    a minimum average mark of 75 in Analysis, History and Culture Studies units; and

(iii)   for candidates in a stream, the completion of stream specific requirements.

(d)  Specific stream requirements include:

(i)      for the Bachelor of Music (Composition), a minimum average mark of 75 in Composition 5 and 6;

(ii)      for the Bachelor of Music (Performance), a minimum average mark of 75 in Principal Study 4 and 6.

(e)  To qualify for honours, candidates must complete 192 credit points including 48 credit points of Honours units of study.

(f)   The grade of honours will be determined by an honours mark calculated from work in the honours units of study as specified in:

(i)     these resolutions; and

(ii)    the resolutions of the Sydney Conservatorium of Music; or

(iii)   the resolutions of the relevant faculty.
