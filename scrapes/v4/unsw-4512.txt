## Description
The purpose of 4512 Business (Honours) is to enable students who have performed well at undergraduate level to deepen their knowledge of approaches, perspectives and traditions in their chosen scientific discipline and undertake a significant research project. Honours is a means for connecting undergraduate study with supervised independent research by consolidating and extending work completed in the undergraduate program and providing an academic foundation for students continuing on to higher degree research.

Students who complete an Honours program and achieve an Honours Class 1 are well placed to apply for higher degree research, such as a Masters by Research, a Master of Philosophy (MPhil) or a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD), and scholarships such as the Research Training Program (RTP). Further details on career opportunities for students who complete an Honours degree can be obtained from the School of Business.

## Program Learning Outcomes (CILOs)
On successful completion of this program, students will be able to:

1. apply cognitive skills to review, analyse, communicate and synthesise business knowledge to identify and provide solutions to complex problems with intellectual independence.
2. demonstrate the application of business knowledge and skills to plan and execute project work and/or a piece of research and scholarship in a business area with some independence.
3. apply advanced knowledge of the principles and concepts in one or more disciplines in business and knowledge of research principles and methods appropriate to the area of business and related disciplines.

## Program Structure & Degree Requirements
Students must complete 48 Units of Credit (UOC) structured across the following academic components:

### Core Courses (12 UOC)
Students must take 12 UOC of the following courses.

- **ZBUS4002**: Research Methods (Honours) [6 UOC]
- **ZBUS4001**: Literature Review (Honours) [6 UOC]

### Research Project (36 UOC)
Students must take 36 UOC of the following courses.

- **ZBUS4101**: Business Research 1 (Honours) [12 UOC]
- **ZBUS4102**: Business Research 2 (Honours) [24 UOC]

## Admission Requirements
**Minimum Entry Requirements**:
To gain entry into 4512 Business (Honours), an applicant must:

- Be an Australian citizen, New Zealand citizen, or Permanent Resident of Australia, and

- Have successfully completed, or are in the process of completing, a relevant pass-degree program in the same or a related discipline* from a recognised institution, and

- Have achieved at least a credit average (65%) across the entire undergraduate degree, including a credit average over the Level 3 courses in the discipline in which Honours is proposed, and

- Have an approved research proposal (see note below).

*Related discipline/s: Business, Management, Economics, Accounting, Information Technology, Information Systems, Human Resource Management, Project Management, Public Policy, Marketing Management, Law.

Please note, entry into 4512 Business (Honours) is subject to an approved research proposal. It is recommended that applicants discuss their research proposal with their proposed Honours supervisor. Applicants should submit their research proposal with their program application. Please contact the School of Business for further information.
