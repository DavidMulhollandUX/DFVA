This program introduces students to principles and practices of Immersive Media. Students will develop skills and understandings in areas related to immersive media such as physiological and psychological impact, design principles and technical knowledge. They will engage critically with industry frameworks and will demonstrate technical knowledge across a range of hardware and software platforms. They will put their skills into practice in developing an immersive media project, and will engage in a research project relevant to immersive media.
The Master of Immersive Media Technologies is an AQF Level 9 Masters qualification with a standard full-time duration of 2 years. Students may be eligible to complete the program in a full-time standard duration of 1.5 years on the basis of prior learning.

## Program Learning Outcomes
Critically differentiate between immersive media technology platforms.
Analyse the physiological and psychological impact on users within immersive media frameworks.
Synthesise and apply design theory knowledge to immersive media development.
Critically evaluate industry frameworks in relation to data, analytics and market trends.
Design and execute a research project relevant to immersive media.
Demonstrate advanced technical knowledge across a variety of hardware and software platforms and pipelines.
Construct an immersive media project management plan based on advanced industry practices.

Subject: Immersive Media Design (MDIA5011)
Credit Points: 12
Overview: The design paradigms associated with immersive media technology are in a constant state of experimental change. Adequate design choices not only provide users with comfortable immersive technology experiences, but also have the potential to impact platform dependency and technological applicability. In this course, students will form deep contextual understanding of the physiological and technological design paradigms associated with immersive media development. Through facilitated hands-on experimentation with various platforms, software frameworks and game-engines, students will form critical analysis of existing systems of design and develop new knowledge in relation to next-generation technologies.
Learning Outcomes:
- Identify and analyse physiological response characteristics associated with design paradigm implementation.
- Demonstrate comprehension via the application of context specific design terminology within immersive media technology frameworks.
- Critically differentiate between HMD (Head Mounted Display/Device) technologies and software in relation to design implementation.
- Develop and formulate a feasible design brief incorporating design paradigm understanding.
- Categorise the benefits/shortcomings of immersive media technology platforms from a design understanding perspective.
