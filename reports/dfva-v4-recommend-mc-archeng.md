## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Master of Architectural Engineering scores lowest on work-situated learning (W3, 0/3) and digital and AI literacy (C3, 1/3), the two items carrying the widest gaps to the instrument's top anchor. The market evidence for this program's destination occupations rests on a field-of-education basis rather than the program's own graduates (§6), so the interventions below lean on the signal with a direct occupational match: construction managers and quantity surveyors, the largest advertised destination in §2, now carry explicit work-health-and-safety duties for AI-enabled site tools and continuing licensing responsibility for the accuracy of AI-generated quotes (§3 Themes 3 and 4), while 'project delivery' is the single largest recurring skill keyword across 6,711 live postings and is rising (§2 Signal 2; §4).
