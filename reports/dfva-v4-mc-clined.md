**Workplace Practice: 3 / 9.** Scored on sub-scale W [[19]](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2021L00488) in the same pass, from the same extract. The position axis remains adaptiveness alone; the workplace sub-score is reported beside it, not folded into the axis, and the two are never added together.

**Position: Low exposure · high adaptiveness.** Assigned against the v4 medians (exposure 83.21 on the field basis, adaptiveness 9), computed from all 34 reference-cohort programs re-scored on this instrument. This is the deterministic median rule the site applies, not a probabilistic stability class.

## 3. GATES — Basis: scored

- **G1 Disciplinary foundation:** PASS — The program is an all-compulsory specialist core with a staged prerequisite chain, meeting the PASS anchor ('an all-compulsory specialist core, an accredited sequence, or a staged prerequisite chain'): every one of the 12 subjects across 3 years is listed Compulsory, the course explicitly builds on the Graduate Certificate and Graduate Diploma of Clinical Education, and individual subjects state they build on earlier ones (Assessment Design and Quality Assurance on Assessing Clinical Learners; Theory & Practice in Education Research on Clinical Education Research & Evaluation).
- **G2 Decision-making under uncertainty:** PASS — PASS anchor met ('simulations/capstones/live projects with real uncertainty and accountability'): the required, ethics-approved minor-thesis project (EDUC90837/EDUC90838) is a live research project on a self-selected, genuinely uncertain question, with accountability enforced via a hurdle requirement, staged feedback, and assessed oral presentations; EDUC90804 additionally requires a defended, literature-referenced judgement of the strengths and weaknesses of a real assessment program, rather than recall or scripted response.

## 6. EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE & LIMITATIONS — Basis: reported

**Panel A metadata.** Basis tier **field** (field grain), n = 0, 14 destination titles, 2 at medium mapping confidence. Index variant AIOE-2021; destination titles mapped to the published Felten index. Basis note: field-of-education occupation list (ATO-linked), not this program's own graduates

**Panel C provenance.** Scored from the 2026 handbook capture (27 pages) and verified on 2026-08-29: adversarial refutation of every level-3 score and every gate PASS (run), verbatim evidence check (run), and the construct-boundary pass.

**Ambiguities and construct-boundary notes** (5):

- C3: 'use of technology in clinical education' is named as a theme the assessments incorporate but no actual AI/digital content, capability, limitation, ethics or operational-tool element is ever described — ambiguous between Level 0 and a thin Level 1; resolved to the lower level, 0.
- C5, levels 2 vs 3: whether the EDUC90837 proposal-feedback-plus-ethics-hurdle sequence and EDUC90838's two oral presentations rise to methodology 'defended under scrutiny' (viva/defence) or are simply staged coursework is not stated explicitly. The original scoring resolved this ambiguity upward to Level 3 ('staged supervised review'); on review that violates the instrument's scoring-direction rule (ambiguity resolves to the lower level), so this is demoted to Level 2.
- W2: the recurring 'your own context/setting' framing (EDUC90804, EDUC91355, EDUC90838) makes real-context tasks recur across the program, arguably an assessment spine, but no single assessment documents a genuine constraint of practice (externally supplied problem, real resource/time limits, consequential external audience) required for Level 3; resolved to Level 2, the lower level.
- W3: several core assessments direct students to apply frameworks or evaluate programs 'in your own context/educational setting', and the cohort is documented as already-practising, suggestive of the in-practice route the instrument added in v4.2 — but no assessment documents delivery of an activity in the student's own practice with real recipients and a documented outcome measure, as distinct from a proposal, plan, review, or research project already scored under C4/C5/W2; resolved to Level 0 under 'one construct, one home' and R4.
- C1/W2: EDUC90744's 'Folio of sample assessment items with marking guides' (group-marked) evidences both collaborative practice and an authentic professional artefact; scored only in C1 for its explicit group-assessment feature, per 'one construct, one home'.

**Standing v4 limitations.**

- Scores describe **documented curriculum intent**, not demonstrated graduate
  capability; the extrapolation warrant is constructive alignment, stated as an
  assumption (Kane [17]).
- Indigenous data governance is not scored as a distinct construct; C3 level 3
  counts it where taught (CARE Principles; Lodge et al. 2025 Principle 4 [[2]](https://www.acses.edu.au/publication/australian-framework-for-artificial-intelligence-in-higher-education/)).
- Perception/manipulation (Frey & Osborne's third bottleneck [[7]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.techfore.2016.08.019)) is unscored.
- A high W3 score describes curriculum design, not student welfare: required
  placements can disadvantage students who cannot afford unpaid work or
  relocation (Accord [[30]](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-universities-accord/resources/final-report); Fawns et al. [[24]](https://doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2024.2404634) on ableism and elitism).
- W2 is scored against each discipline's own criterion situation [[22]](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02504676), so
  cross-disciplinary W2 comparison is weaker than within-discipline comparison.
- W3 excludes simulation by design (simulation scores in W2), so programs whose
  professional norm is high-fidelity simulation may score low on W3 for a
  defensible pedagogic choice. Read the two sub-scores together.
- The authentic-assessment employability evidence rests largely on student
  self-report [[25]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stueduc.2021.101030); W2's warrant is documented design fidelity plus the
  assessment-drives-learning mechanism [26], not demonstrated employment effects.
- Work-integrated learning is NOT claimed to raise employment rates — Jackson &
  Collings [[28]](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-017-0216-z) found it does not. W3's warrant is skill development, employment
  relevance and professional identity formation [[29]](https://doi.org/10.1080/07294360.2016.1139551).
