## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is interpretation,
not observation.*

Two facts frame everything below. The destination set averages **94.14 exposure with no
low-exposure tail** — eleven of fifteen titles sit between 95 and 96, and the tasks that
define those occupations are the ones current systems already do. And the program's
compulsory core assesses **no AI content at all**, while stating AI capability as a
graduate outcome.

That is not a generic gap. Three regulatory instruments now place governance of automated
employment decisions on HR practitioners specifically: the EU AI Act's Annex III high-risk
classification, NYC Local Law 144's bias-audit regime, and — binding in Australia — the
Work Health and Safety Amendment (Digital Work Systems) Act 2026. The market is requiring
the exact capability the curriculum does not assess.

**If only one thing is done: P2.** It is the only intervention that moves two items, it is
low effort, and it requires no new teaching — only that the assessment ask for what the
subject is already built around. Whether it is available at all depends on facts about
MGMT90140 the handbook does not record, so check the subject guide first.

**If only one thing is done for the market: P1.** P2 is the cheapest; P1 is the one the
destination evidence actually demands.
