## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Bachelor of Fine Arts (Acting) (B-FAACTING) is a 300-point intensive conservatory program offered by the Faculty of Fine Arts and Music at the Victorian College of the Arts (VCA). The degree demonstrates strong authentic studio practice (W2 = 2/3), industry-facing showcase presentations (W1 = 2/3), and recurring ensemble collaboration (C1 = 2/3). Both gates pass decisively: G1 on a rigid 250-point sequential specialist acting core across all three years, and G2 on live public theatre performances under professional guest directors with real artistic and audience accountability.

However, the program exhibits significant adaptiveness and workplace headroom (Adaptiveness: 4 / 15, Workplace Practice: 4 / 9). Specifically, digital and AI literacy is absent from the core curriculum (C3 = 0/3) despite emerging industry demands for virtual production, motion capture (mocap), and performer digital likeness governance. Primary empirical inquiry is not assessed as a formal research methodology (C5 = 0/3), self-reflections lack criterion-referenced peer review rubrics (C2 = 1/3), independent projects are heavily instructor-guided (C4 = 1/3), and no external industry secondment or placement exists within the course structure (W3 = 0/3).
