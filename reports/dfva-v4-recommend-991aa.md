## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Biostatistics provides advanced specialist mathematical and epidemiological training accredited by the Statistical Society of Australia (SSA). G1 passes on an accredited, staged progression from regression foundations to advanced non-linear modeling and inference, and G2 passes on defended parametric model validation and causal effect estimation under sampling uncertainty in a compulsory capstone Research Project.

However, under substantial exposure (93.92), the degree exhibits major headroom across both sub-scales: **6 / 15 on Adaptiveness** and **2 / 9 on Workplace**. Most acutely, W3 (0/3) documents that work-situated learning is entirely absent from the degree structure, despite strong demand for biostatisticians embedded in hospital clinical trial units and medical research institutes. On workplace communication and task design, W1 (1/3) and W2 (1/3) assess academic exam scripts and practical exercise sheets rather than authentic GCP-compliant Statistical Analysis Plans (SAPs) or interprofessional investigator consults. On adaptiveness, C1 (1/3), C2 (1/3), C3 (1/3), and C4 (1/3) lack assessed group collaboration with individual grading, criterion-referenced rubrics, AI scripting verification, and cross-context transfer.
