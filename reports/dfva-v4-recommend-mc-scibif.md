## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The two sub-scales tell opposite stories, and the split is the finding. On the adaptive
side the program is strong where research training is strong: C4 sits at the instrument
maximum on documented structure rather than on an outcome claim — 50 credit points of
Research Project subjects taken consecutively by every student, scoped with a project
coordinator, gated behind a progression rule requiring 37.5 points of core first — and
C5 reaches 2 on the same required inquiry. Both gates pass, G1 on a staged prerequisite
chain (COMP90059 → COMP90038, MAST90044 → BINF90017, MAST90044 or MAST20005 → BINF90001)
and G2 on inquiry whose result is not known in advance. C1 reaches 2 on BINF90018's
hurdled group project, assessed twice with individual accountability.

The workplace sub-scale scores 1 on all three items, and C2 and C3 score 1 alongside
them. The reason is structural and it is the same reason five times over. The documented
core assessment profile is hurdled examinations (BINF90002 50%, BINF90017 60%,
COMP90038 60%, COMP90059 60%), essays, written assignments, quizzes and take-home
programming tasks — academic genres addressed to the marker. Everything the market report
names as the decisive shift sits outside that core: the machine-learning subjects
(COMP90049, COMP90089, COMP90090) and the internship (SCIE90017) are all in the All
Streams General Elective list, from which students take exactly one 12.5-point subject.
Two coverage gaps bound this reading and are declared in the scoring: SCIE90013
Communication for Research Scientists and the research-project subject pages
(BINF90008–BINF90016) are absent from the captured extract, so W1's and C5's ceilings
rest partly on missing documentation rather than on a positive finding of absence. Where
that is the case, the cheapest intervention below is publication, not redesign.

P8 and P12 are justified by skill development, employment relevance and professional
identity formed through participation in a community of practice [[29]](https://doi.org/10.1080/07294360.2016.1139551), not by
employment outcomes: work-integrated learning was not found to raise full-time employment
rates [[28]](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-017-0216-z). The equity cost is real and larger here than in most programs, because the
research project is 50 credit points — an unpaid or relocation-dependent host placement
of that length is a serious access barrier, and the Commonwealth Prac Payment does not
extend to it [[30]](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-universities-accord/resources/final-report). Host-sited placement in the student's existing city, with paid or
stipended arrangements, is the form that avoids it. The instrument's own-practice route
[[35]](https://doi.org/10.1080/03075070903216635) is not available: nothing documents this cohort as already practising, and the
course is delivered on campus at Parkville.

The two columns are tracked separately and are never added: a plan that moved
adaptiveness to 15 and left workplace practice at 3 has done something different from a
plan that moved both, and a single total would hide which. C4 appears in no row because
it is already at the maximum, which is why the adaptive column starts at 9 rather than
lower and reaches 15 in nine steps.

The full plan reaches the instrument maximum on both sub-scales, which is a statement
about the number of open steps rather than a claim about how achievable it is. The
realistic near-term subset is the first three levers: P1, P2 and P3 are low or
low–medium effort, none requires a course-design decision, and two of them are largely
documentation of pages the capture did not retrieve. Together they reach an adaptiveness
of 11 and a workplace practice of 4 inside a single teaching year. Everything past P5
depends on a decision the plan cannot make for the program: the credit-point budget is
full, so P8's required internship costs the general-elective slot, and once that slot is
gone the elective machine-learning subjects are unreachable and P6 becomes not merely
desirable but the only remaining route to any AI content at all. P11 and P12 are
course-design decisions of a different order and should not be attempted before P5 and
P8 have shown that the host relationships and the practice criteria work at small scale.

Four categories of change would not move any score, and are recorded so that effort is
not directed toward them.

Two dependencies bind the sequence and are worth stating plainly. P9 is the highest-value
intervention against the market evidence — clinical governance is what separates the
LOW-substitution clinical roles from the MEDIUM-substitution analyst roles in the report's
own job-family map — and it is unreachable until P6 puts AI content into the core at all.
And P3 is the cheapest lever in the plan and may already be half-done: SCIE90013
Communication for Research Scientists is a required Professional Skills subject whose
assessment simply was not captured, so the first action is to look before redesigning.

Re-scoring after implementation follows the harness without modification: capture the new
handbook vintage — including the subject pages missing from this one — score, verify
adversarially, and publish the change. Because the instrument is unchanged, v4-to-v4
comparison is valid; comparison with v3.1 values is not.
