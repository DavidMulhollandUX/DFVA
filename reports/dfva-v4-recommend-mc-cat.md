## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Creative Arts Therapy documents a coherent, accredited specialist core (G1 Disciplinary foundation — PASS) and requires defended decisions under real accountability: CREA90012 requires an interactive oral defence of a written proposal, and the core placement subjects place students in real settings working to an external partner's own objectives under practitioner supervision (G2 Decision-making under uncertainty — PASS). W3 (3/3) already reaches the instrument ceiling: CREA90008, CREA90013 and CREA90018 form a required sequence of supervised, assessed core placements with a written report to the host and structured reflection.

The program holds substantial headroom on both sub-scales — 6/15 on Adaptive capabilities and 6/9 on Workplace practice. The largest gap is C3 (0/3): no core unit names artificial intelligence, tool operation or governance, even as the market report's own automation-exposure pattern (§3 Theme 6; §4) marks the entry-level administrative and clerical tasks this program's field-wide destination basis could otherwise fall back on as falling or falling sharply. C1, C5 and W1 each sit one level below what their next anchor requires; C2, C4 and W2 need one further step each to reach the ceiling.
