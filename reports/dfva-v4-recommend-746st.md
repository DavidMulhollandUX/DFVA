## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Engineering Structures prepares specialized structural design engineers, bridge engineers, and temporary works consultants across Australia's major infrastructure builders (Arup, WSP, Aurecon, CPB Contractors). Both gates pass: G1 passes on a coherent structural engineering core constrained by 62.5 points of compulsory and selective structural units; G2 passes on defended conceptual structural choices under lateral wind/earthquake loads and code-compliant design reports.

Operating in a moderate AI exposure domain (**83.18**, below the portfolio median of 90.9), the program exhibits considerable headroom on Adaptiveness (**6 / 15**) and Workplace Practice (**3 / 9**). The adaptiveness gap is driven by C2 (1/3), C3 (1/3), C4 (1/3), and C5 (1/3): while core units assess group design reports with individual workload accounting (C1: 2/3), digital content is restricted to commercial FEA package operation without AI optimization or data governance, reflection is asserted in learning outcomes without assessed peer review, and primary experimental research is an elective rather than a required capstone. In workplace practice, W2 (2/3) reflects code-compliant design reports, but W3 scores 0/3 because no internship or work-situated project exists in the 746ST course structure, and W1 (1/3) lacks spoken presentation assessment.
