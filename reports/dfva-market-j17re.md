### Theme 1 — the AASW Accredited Mental Health Social Worker (AMHSW) credential is required to access Medicare Better Ac…

The AASW Accredited Mental Health Social Worker (AMHSW) credential is required to access Medicare Better Access and Chronic Disease Management funding; eligibility requires current AASW membership and at least 2 years full-time-equivalent (3,360 hours) supervised mental health practice in the last 5 years. (Mental Health and Substance Abuse Social Workers; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [AASW, AASW Accredited Mental Health Social Worker Application Criteria](https://aasw.asn.au/credentials/aasw-accredited-mental-health-social-worker-application-criteria).

Scope: AMHSW sub-set of 21-1023; Medicare accreditation framework.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 2 — under Medicare, to provide Focused Psychological Strategies a practitioner must be an AASW member accredite…

Under Medicare, to provide Focused Psychological Strategies a practitioner must be an AASW member accredited to the AMHSW criteria in force from 1 July 2022. (Mental Health and Substance Abuse Social Workers; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [Services Australia, Specific criteria for allied health and primary care professionals](https://servicesaustralia.gov.au/specific-criteria-for-allied-health-professionals?context=20).

Scope: Medicare funding rule for FPS delivery.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 3 — the Australian Privacy Act's automated decision-making (ADM) transparency obligation, effective 10 December…

The Australian Privacy Act's automated decision-making (ADM) transparency obligation, effective 10 December 2026, requires charities and NGOs making decisions substantially based on automated processing (about beneficiaries, donors, employees) to notify affected individuals — directly relevant to AI casework, eligibility and triage tools in community services. (Social and Human Service Assistants; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [AI Risk Aware (citing the Privacy Act), AI Governance for Australian Non-Profits and Charities](https://airiskaware.com/insights/ai-governance-australian-non-profits-charities) (2026).

Scope: Charities/NGOs, a major employer of social and human service assistants; obligation is forward-dated (Dec 2026) and applies to automated decisions, not all AI assistance. Stated down to what the source measured.

**Bearing:** C3 and W2.

### Theme 4 — social workers are self-regulated through the Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW); only Accredi…

Social workers are self-regulated through the Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW); only Accredited Mental Health Social Workers gain a Medicare provider number to deliver Focused Psychological Strategies under Better Access. The broader 'Social and Human Service Assistants' occupation is not statutorily registered. (Social and Human Service Assistants; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [Australian Association of Social Workers, Mental Health (AASW Accredited Mental Health Social Worker)](https://aasw.asn.au/mental-health) (2025).

Scope: AASW member social workers; the SOC also covers assistants and non-AASW roles. Registration status is self-regulatory, not government licensing.

**Bearing:** C3 and W2.
