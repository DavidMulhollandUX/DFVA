## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

All eight adaptive and workplace items score 1 out of 3, and the pattern has one structural cause: BP001 requires 144 credit points, a level distribution and a degree-specific major chosen from 49 options, but no unit is core to the degree itself, only to whichever major a student picks. Collaborative practice, criterion-referenced appraisal, AI-capability content, research-methods training, professional communication and work-situated learning all exist somewhere in the 512-unit prescribed list — GEOG1107's collaborative project, HART3030's peer review, LING2000's AI-ethics outcome, ANTH3001's ethnographic project, GEOG1106's policy brief, ARCY3100's placement — but each sits inside one major's sequence and is elective for every other BP001 student. G1 passes on the guarantee of a coherent specialist core, whichever major supplies it; G2 fails because the degree itself prescribes no capstone, live project or assessment type at all.

The market evidence points the redesign toward the compulsory ACE, CARS and ISE modules rather than toward any one major, because those are the only components every BP001 student takes. The field's largest recorded entry destinations sit in retail, general clerical work and secondary teaching (JSA HEO field 0009 Society and Culture), each named exposed or gated in the discussion signals; Jobs and Skills Australia's Generative AI Capacity Study frames augmentation, not replacement, as the dominant pattern, which is what most of the actions below target: judgement over AI-drafted material, transfer of a disciplinary method into a non-disciplinary setting, and a professional communication genre built on a real statutory obligation.
