## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Bachelor of Arts fails both regression gates. G1 fails because the documented core is one prescribed unit — a mathematics foundations course — with no all-compulsory specialist core, accredited sequence or staged prerequisite chain shown anywhere in the extract; the program text describes the degree as broad and flexible, with the major left to student choice and no major structure documented. G2 fails because 75% of that unit's assessed weight sits in two identity-verified exams, with the remainder split between online assignments and an applied-class item, none of which is a defended trade-off decision, simulation, capstone or live project.

Operating on an estimated field-of-education exposure of 81.93 (Society and Culture, field grain, not program grain, per JSA HEO), the program shows headroom on both sub-scales: 3/15 on Adaptiveness and 0/9 on Workplace. C2, C3, C5, W1, W2 and W3 all sit at the floor (0/3): the sole prescribed unit carries no criterion-referenced appraisal, no digital or AI content, no methods training, no professional genre, no situational framing and no work-situated learning. C4 (1/3) scores only on an outcomes-level transfer claim, and C1 (2/3) scores only on a group-assessed applied-class item that does not extend to coordinating people and tools together.
