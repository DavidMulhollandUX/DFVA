### Theme 1 — environmental impact assessment in Australia is governed by the Environment Protection and Biodiversity Con…

Environmental impact assessment in Australia is governed by the Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 (EPBC Act), administered by DCCEEW. Actions likely to significantly impact a Matter of National Environmental Significance require Commonwealth referral, assessment and approval; environmental scientists prepare the referrals, field surveys and assessment reports the process depends on. The Act is being reformed through 2026 (National Environmental Standards; a new federal EPA). (Environmental Scientists and Specialists, Including Health; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [DCCEEW, Environmental Impact Assessment Client Service Charter](https://dcceew.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/eia-client-service-charter.pdf) (1999).

Scope: Commonwealth environmental approvals; state/territory systems run parallel EIA. Binds consultants preparing EPBC referrals/assessments.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 2 — jobs and Skills Australia's Generative AI Capacity Study (August 2025) scores 358 ANZSCO occupations on aug…

Jobs and Skills Australia's Generative AI Capacity Study (August 2025) scores 358 ANZSCO occupations on augmentability and automatability using an ILO/Felten-derived task-level framework. It finds that augmentation outweighs automation across the workforce: 79% of Australian workers face low or very low automation risk, while routine clerical and administrative roles carry the highest automation exposure. The study reports professionals and managers (ANZSCO skill levels 1-2) see the highest augmentation potential. (Environmental Scientists and Specialists, Including Health; lane L2, scholarly and grey-literature studies.)

Sources: [Jobs and Skills Australia, Australia's AI Transition: Jobs, Skills and the Future of Work (Generative AI Capacity Study)](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/studies/generative-artificial-intelligence-capacity-study) (2025-08).

Scope: Australian occupations nationally; model-derived task-level estimates.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

The Nature Repair Act 2023 establishes the Nature Repair Market, a national legislated biodiversity market issuing biodiversity certificates for projects that protect, manage or restore biodiversity, and environment protection reforms passed on 28 November 2025 allow those certificates to be used as environmental offsets where a method permits. (Conservation Scientists; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)
