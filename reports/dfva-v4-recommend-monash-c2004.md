## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This document argues from evidence; it is not itself evidence. Monash's Bachelor of Information Technology passes both gates: G1, on the six compulsory FIT core units, two required applied-studies units and the single eight-unit software development major with an explicit FIT3047→FIT3048 prerequisite; and G2, on the defended design and process decisions already assessed in FIT3077, FIT2001 and FIT1049. Neither gate constrains the redesign below; both need only to be preserved, not created.

AI exposure rests on a field-of-education basis (83.3, field grain, estimated) — the destinations are the whole 0201 Computer Science field's, not this program's own graduates — so it is sound to say the field is highly exposed and no more. The program already clears level 2 on seven of the eight items (C1, C2, C4, C5, W1, W2, W3); Digital & AI literacy (C3) is the outlier, still at level 1 on the operational tool-use anchor. Headroom is therefore 2/15 on Adaptiveness (9/15) and 3/9 on Workplace (6/9) — modest and concentrated, not a program in need of rebuilding.
