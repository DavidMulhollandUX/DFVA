## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Bachelor of Engineering shows a strong disciplinary foundation and sound decision-making assessment: G1 passes on an Engineers Australia-accredited sequence organised into named specialist majors, with prescribed units documenting a staged prerequisite chain against named prior and later courses; G2 passes on defended trade-off decisions under real uncertainty — materials-selection trade-off analysis, the highway design project's public-facing trade-offs, control units' justified open-ended responses, and the first-year design project's hurdle team demonstration. C1, C2, C4, C5, W1 and W2 each clear the program's level-2 bar — assessed collaborative practice, criterion-referenced appraisal, application to novel problems, methodology-selected inquiry, a recognised professional genre, and an authentic end-to-end design task — but stop short of the level-3 anchors requiring coordination across people AND tools, documented reliance-and-override decisions, a documented progression toward independent learning, a required (not elective) primary-evidence project, an externally judged professional item, and a genuine constraint of practice repeated across the assessment spine.

C3 and W3 sit at level 1, two steps below the instrument ceiling: AI content (the Artificial Intelligence Minor, DECO2801) and work-situated learning (the optional live-client project, occasional in-workplace international units) both exist only outside the guaranteed core. §3 and §5 of the market report record state-based professional registration becoming mandatory or rolling out across prescribed engineering areas (Theme 2, Theme 3, Theme 5) — putting a named human legally answerable for engineering work whatever produced it — and design, engineering, science and transport professionals among the most AI-exposed occupational groups (Theme 6); evidence that bears most directly on C3's digital-and-AI-governance gap, W1's professional-conduct gap, and W3's work-situated-learning gap.
