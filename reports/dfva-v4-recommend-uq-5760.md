## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Six of the eight items score 2 of 3 and both gates pass. The documented curriculum records a staged epidemiology/biostatistics/research-methods prerequisite chain, assessed peer-feedback and reflexivity tasks, a hurdle economic-evaluation viva, and a spine of professional-genre assessments (policy brief, advocacy letter, mock senate hearing). Two items score 1 of 3. C3 (Digital and AI literacy, including governance) records digital-health content only at the describe/explain level of information systems, with no core unit addressing AI capabilities or limitations. W3 (Work-situated learning) exists only as a final-semester, expression-of-interest placement or an optional consultancy "opportunity" — not a core requirement.

C3 and W3 rank first and second on the same ground recorded in the table below: each sits two anchor levels below the maximum. This program's destinations rest on a field-of-education basis rather than its own graduates (estimated; see the market report's §6), so every market-linked action below is scoped to what the field-level signal actually measured, not to a claim about this program's own graduates.
