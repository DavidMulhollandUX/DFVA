## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Psychology (Educational and Developmental) is an APAC-accredited fifth-and-sixth-year professional sequence — nine compulsory core subjects, four compulsory field practica and a compulsory research project — preparing graduates for practice as registered psychologists (Destination AI Exposure 95.57, field-of-education basis). The program clears both gates: a coherent, all-compulsory specialist core (G1) and assessed decision-making under real client uncertainty across the field practica and Intervention in Problems of Young People (G2). On the adaptive capabilities sub-scale (10 / 15), the degree reaches Level 3 on Life-long Learning & Transfer (C4, through the required year-long Research Project) and Level 2 on four items — collaborative practice (C1, the assessed group presentation in EDUC90227), evaluative judgement (C2, the assessed evaluation of intervention and taped-interview self-analysis in EDUC90226/EDUC90224), primary inquiry (C5, the Research Project's assessed proposal and mandatory ethics hurdle) and authentic task design (W2, the consultation package and live test-administration demonstration) — while Digital & AI literacy (C3) sits at Level 1, the lowest-scoring item and the priority target. On workplace practice (7 / 9), the four required, year-long field practica place Work-situated Learning (W3) at the maximum Level 3, while Professional Communication & Conduct (W1) sits at Level 2 pending explicit, progressive assessment of conduct against a real external audience.

Strategic curriculum headroom concentrates on Digital & AI literacy: Ahpra and the Psychology Board's 2024 AI-in-healthcare principles and refreshed Code of Conduct (2025) put verification of AI-generated content, contemporaneous record-keeping and consent before AI-assisted documentation on the registered practitioner, but no core unit assesses AI capabilities, limits or governance beyond the operational, tool-training level already present in Research Methods. The remaining headroom is one level each on evaluative judgement, inquiry, professional communication, authentic task design and collaborative practice, reachable through changes to existing assessed tasks rather than new core content.
