## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Finance (Enhanced) sits under very high destination exposure (97.04, variant basis) and holds broad headroom on both sub-scales: 7/15 on Adaptiveness and 3/9 on Workplace practice. Both gates pass: the compulsory-plus-core-finance-plus-capstone sequence gives the program a coherent specialist foundation (G1), and FNCE90082's live risk-management cases and FNCE90012's valuation and mergers-and-acquisitions case work already assess defended judgement under real uncertainty (G2).

The primary gap is C2 (0/3): no assessed task in the compulsory or core pool asks students to appraise the quality of their own, a peer's, or a tool's output, not even at the outcome-statement level — a shortfall the market data sharpens, since APESB now requires auditors to verify automated anomaly-detection algorithms and keep documented audit-trail logs for AI-generated financial estimates (§3 Theme 1), a governance duty for the program's largest destination family. C3 (1/3) sits close behind: the program's one AI-related core subject, FNCE90084, teaches machine-learning technique without an assessed limitations or governance component. On workplace practice, W1, W2 and W3 each sit at 1/3 — professional communication, authentic task design and work-situated learning are all present in single-instance or elective form, but none is yet the guaranteed, progressive core experience the top anchor levels require.
