## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Australian Institute for Teaching and School Leadership ([AITSL](https://www.aitsl.edu.au/)), Australian Council for Educational Research ([ACER](https://www.acer.org/au)), Victorian Institute of Teaching ([VIT](https://www.vit.vic.edu.au/)), and peer-reviewed educational technology scholarship ([British Journal of Educational Technology](https://bera-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/14678535), [February 2025](https://bera-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/14678535)). Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no social media forum was sampled — where this section refers to discourse, it denotes published AITSL Australian Professional Standards for Teachers, ACER research briefs, and documented peer-reviewed educational literature by named bodies and authors. Dates are stated where available.

### Theme 1 — generative AI integration in assessment and pedagogical design

Policy papers from [AITSL](https://www.aitsl.edu.au/) (November 2024) and the *British Journal of Educational Technology* (February 2025) underline that while generative AI automates routine lesson plan drafting and quiz generation, it presents major risks for academic integrity and student cognitive engagement. Educational leaders must move beyond superficial tool usage to critically audit AI limitations, student data privacy, and authentic assessment redesign.

**Bearing:** C2, C3, and W2.

### Theme 2 — collaborative knowledge mobilisation and stakeholder engagement

Reports from [ACER](https://www.acer.org/au/) emphasize that modern educational reform relies on structured collaborative inquiry between teachers, school executives, parents, and community partners. Designing protocols for interprofessional collaboration and human-AI tool management is becoming a key leadership capability.

**Bearing:** C1, W1, and W3.

### Theme 3 — practitioner inquiry, teacher-as-researcher, and evidence translation

Standards from [AITSL](https://www.aitsl.edu.au/) highlight that educational advancement requires teachers to formulate empirical practitioner inquiry questions, evaluate classroom interventions, and defend evidence-informed curriculum strategies under formal peer and panel review.

**Bearing:** C4, C5, and G1.

## 4. SKILL SHIFT SUMMARY

### Declining Demand
- Standardized transmission-based curriculum delivery
- Low-level recall-based worksheet and test design
- Isolated classroom teaching without collaborative data analysis

### Rising Demand
- Evaluative verification and critique of AI-generated educational content
- Authentic, process-focused performance assessment design
- Data-informed school improvement and mixed-methods program evaluation
- First Nations culturally responsive pedagogy and trauma-informed practice
