## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — HIGH CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Regulatory and statutory instruments, scholarly and grey-literature studies, drawn from the committed profession records for the occupations in §1, each with its publisher, date and link. Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no professional forum was sampled for this report; the profession records declare their own practitioner-discourse sample — linkedin (10 items, linkedin-mcp-search (unofficial scrape), Australia); practitioner_forums (45 items, National Computer Occupations, All Other Association discourse) — which is a declared sample, not a scrape. Themes are the claims as recorded; nothing is paraphrased, and a claim without a source was not carried. Dates are given where the source carries one.

### Theme 1 — iCT occupations in Australia, including Web Developers (ANZSCO unit group 2612, Multimedia Specialists and…

ICT occupations in Australia, including Web Developers (ANZSCO unit group 2612, Multimedia Specialists and Web Developers), are not subject to statutory licensure. The Australian Computer Society (ACS) is the peak professional body for the ICT sector, the authorised assessing authority for ICT skilled migration, and sets voluntary professional standards through its Certified Professional (CP) / Certified Technologist (CT) scheme. (Web Developers; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [Australian Computer Society, ACS — The Professional Association for Australia's ICT sector](https://www.acs.org.au/); [Australian Computer Society, Get ACS Certified](https://www.acs.org.au/professionalrecognition/certification-landing-page.html); [Australian Computer Society, IT occupations and ANZSCO codes](https://www.acs.org.au/msa/information-for-applicants/occupations-anzsco-codes/information-technology.html).

Scope: Australian ICT occupations. ACS standards are voluntary; migration skills assessment is mandatory only for visa purposes, not for domestic employment of Web Developers.

**Bearing:** C3 and G2.

### Theme 2 — jobs and Skills Australia's Generative AI Capacity Study (August 2025) finds generative AI is more likely t…

Jobs and Skills Australia's Generative AI Capacity Study (August 2025) finds generative AI is more likely to augment than automate work: augmentation outweighs automation, and the highest automation potential is concentrated in routine roles. At task-category grain, Technical Development & Engineering tasks show high augmentability (~0.69 on the 0–1 scale). (Web Developers; lane L2, scholarly and grey-literature studies.)

Sources: [Jobs and Skills Australia, Our Gen AI Transition – Exposure](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/studies/generative-artificial-intelligence-capacity-study/our-gen-ai-transition-exposure) (2025-08); [Jobs and Skills Australia, Our Gen AI Transition: Exposures, Adaptation, Dynamism (occupation data)](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/studies/generative-artificial-intelligence-capacity-study/occupation-data-on-ai-exposure) (2025-08).

Scope: Australian labour market; scores are model-derived task-level exposure estimates, not employment-change forecasts. Per-occupation ICT unit scores sit in the high-augmentation band but this claim reports the study-level finding and the task-category figure.

**Bearing:** C4 and W2 and G2.

### Theme 3 — computer Occupations, All Other (a residual O*NET group covering Cloud Solution Architects, QA Analysts, IC…

Computer Occupations, All Other (a residual O*NET group covering Cloud Solution Architects, QA Analysts, ICT Project Managers and similar) have no single statutory licence. ACS is the peak professional body (Certified Professional/Technologist under the Professional Standards Scheme, Code of Conduct + CPD). Cloud and security roles typically require vendor certs (e.g. AWS) and, where they handle Commonwealth data, adhere to the ASD Essential Eight. (Computer Occupations, All Other; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [Australian Computer Society, Get ACS Certified](https://acs.org.au/professionalrecognition/certification-landing-page.html) (2026).

Scope: Voluntary professional certification; residual group spans roles beyond any one regime. Population = AU computer occupations not elsewhere classified.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 4 — jSA's Gen AI Capacity Study (Aug 2025) places 'programming' among roles with repeated automation exposure a…

JSA's Gen AI Capacity Study (Aug 2025) places 'programming' among roles with repeated automation exposure and states the technology sector 'may be among the first to restructure its entry-level intake', while professionals and managers see high augmentation and low automation risk overall. (Computer Occupations, All Other; lane L2, scholarly and grey-literature studies.)

Sources: [Jobs and Skills Australia, Our Gen AI Transition: Exposures, Adaptation, Dynamism (Generative AI Capacity Study)](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/studies/generative-artificial-intelligence-capacity-study) (2025-08); [ACS / Information Age, Aussie jobs most vulnerable to AI outlined in govt study](https://ia.acs.org.au/article/2025/aussie-jobs-most-vulnerable-to-ai-outlined-in-govt-study.html) (2025).

Scope: Australian occupations; entry-level coding compression specifically, not the whole residual group.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 5 — australia's December 2025 National AI Plan abandoned the proposed mandatory AI guardrails; AI is governed t…

Australia's December 2025 National AI Plan abandoned the proposed mandatory AI guardrails; AI is governed through existing technology-neutral legal frameworks, so application developers face no new AI-specific statutory obligation, though existing privacy and consumer law still apply to the products they build. (Software Developers, Applications; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [ABC News, Artificial intelligence to be managed through existing laws under National AI Plan](https://abc.net.au/news/2025-12-02/national-artificial-intelligence-plan-growth-existing-laws/106086474) (2025-12-02).

Scope: Australian federal policy; affects product-building obligations indirectly via existing law, not via AI-specific statute.

**Bearing:** C3 and W1.

### Theme 6 — the ACS Code of Professional Conduct binds all Australian ICT practitioners (including software developers)…

The ACS Code of Professional Conduct binds all Australian ICT practitioners (including software developers), requiring competence, honesty and primacy of the public interest — the professional-standards backdrop against which developers deploy AI coding and agentic tooling. (Software Developers, Applications; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [Australian Computer Society, ACS Code of Professional Conduct (V.2.1, April 2014)](https://acs.org.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0014/4901/Code-of-Professional-Conduct_v2.1.pdf) (2014-04-04).

Scope: Australian ICT practitioners who are ACS members; voluntary professional standard, scoped as such.

**Bearing:** C3 and W1 and G1.
