## 2. RECENT JOB AD SIGNALS

**Signal 1 — Premium demand and salary loadings for certified IB Coordinators.**
TES Global, Seek, and international school network portals (Nord Anglia, Cognita) consistently require IBO-recognised qualifications for senior IB Coordinator appointments across ANZ and Asia-Pacific.

**Signal 2 — Rapid adoption of digital curriculum management platforms.**
Over 60% of IB school coordinator positions mandate proficiency in Managebac, Toddle, or Atlas for collaborative unit planning and IBO compliance reporting.

**Signal 3 — AI assessment integrity and Extended Essay supervision challenges.**
IB World Schools actively seek coordinators skilled in establishing institutional AI integrity policies and designing authentic oral assessment checkpoints for Theory of Knowledge (TOK) and DP Extended Essays.

**Signal 4 — Relational pedagogical leadership and cross-cultural synthesis moat.**
Classroom pastoral care, cross-cultural community engagement, and complex pedagogical coaching provide strong resistance against automated AI displacement.

## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** International Baccalaureate Organization ([IBO](https://www.ibo.org/)), Victorian Institute of Teaching ([VIT](https://www.vit.vic.edu.au/)), Australian Institute for Teaching and School Leadership ([AITSL](https://www.aitsl.edu.au/)), and peer-reviewed literature ([Journal of Research in International Education](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/jri), [December 2024](https://journals.sagepub.com/home/jri)). Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no social media forum was sampled — where this section refers to discourse, it denotes published IBO programme standards, VIT professional learning frameworks, AITSL teacher leadership standards, and peer-reviewed international education research by named bodies and authors. Dates are stated where available.

### Theme 1 — generative AI in IB assessment, academic integrity, and authentic task design

Guidance from the [IBO](https://www.ibo.org/) (October 2024) and *Journal of Research in International Education* (December 2024) emphasizes that while AI tools assist with lesson resource generation and initial unit scaffolding, educators must design assessment tasks with authentic process-focused checkpoints and oral vivas to assure student authorship in Extended Essays and Internal Assessments.

**Bearing:** C2, C3, and W2.

### Theme 2 — collaborative curriculum planning, Managebac platforms, and interdisciplinary coordination

Reports from [AITSL](https://www.aitsl.edu.au/) and [IBO](https://www.ibo.org/) highlight that effective IB program implementation requires continuous collaborative planning across teaching pairs and interdisciplinary teams, coordinating digital management tools with human pedagogical judgement.

**Bearing:** C1, W1, and W3.

### Theme 3 — self-scoped pedagogical inquiry and research capstone transfer

Standards from the [IBO](https://www.ibo.org/) confirm that an independent, self-scoped practitioner research project investigating real classroom pedagogical challenges is the benchmark for advanced international education leadership.

**Bearing:** C4, C5, and G1.
