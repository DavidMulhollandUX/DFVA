## 2. RECENT JOB AD SIGNALS

**Signal 1 — Acute nationwide shortage of registered specialist prosthodontists.**
Australian Dental Council (ADC) and Dental Board of Australia (DBA) workforce registers confirm that prosthodontics represents one of the smallest and most undersupplied specialist dental registers in Australia.

**Signal 2 — Rapid adoption of digital CAD/CAM restorative workflows.**
Specialist private practices and major public dental services mandate proficiency in digital impression workflows (3Shape Trios, iTero) and CAD/CAM prosthodontic design (exocad).

**Signal 3 — 540+ hours of compulsory clinical hospital residency.**
Specialist recruitment criteria across major Victorian hospital dental networks require extensive supervised clinical training at the Royal Dental Hospital of Melbourne.

**Signal 4 — Statutory specialist registration monopoly and physical procedural moat.**
AHPRA specialist dental registration and complex intraoral microsurgical / prosthetic reconstructions provide complete insulation against automated AI substitution.

## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Australian Dental Council ([ADC](https://www.adc.org.au/)), Dental Board of Australia ([DBA](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au/)), Australian Dental Association ([ADA](https://www.ada.org.au/)), and peer-reviewed literature ([Australian Dental Journal](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/18347819), [December 2024](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/18347819)). Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no social media forum was sampled — where this section refers to discourse, it denotes published ADC accreditation standards, DBA specialist practice guidelines, ADA clinical workforce reports, and peer-reviewed prosthodontics research by named bodies and authors. Dates are stated where available.

### Theme 1 — digital CAD/CAM prosthodontics and AI diagnostic imaging validation

Guidance from the [ADC](https://www.adc.org.au/) (November 2024) and *Australian Dental Journal* (December 2024) emphasizes that while AI tools (Overjet, Diagnocat) and automated CAD/CAM software accelerate smile design and margin detection, specialist prosthodontists retain sole medico-legal liability for occlusal harmony, material longevity, and patient consent.

**Bearing:** C2, C3, and W2.

### Theme 2 — interprofessional clinical collaboration, team leadership, and case defence

Reports from the [DBA](https://www.dentalboard.gov.au/) and [ADA](https://www.ada.org.au/) highlight that prosthodontic specialists lead multidisciplinary clinical teams across periodontists, oral surgeons, and dental technicians, requiring defended clinical case presentations and comprehensive patient handover.

**Bearing:** C1, W1, and W3.

### Theme 3 — evidence-based treatment planning and authentic clinical portfolio

Standards from the [ADC](https://www.adc.org.au/) confirm that a rigorous clinical portfolio documenting over 500 hours of complex patient care, backed by pre-clinical gating and defended clinical case presentations, is the definitive benchmark for advanced prosthodontic mastery.

**Bearing:** C4, W2, and G1.

## 4. SKILL SHIFT SUMMARY

### Declining Demand
- Analogue polyvinyl siloxane impressions and manual plaster articulations
- Unverified reliance on dental laboratory milling algorithms without specialist review
- Single-specialty isolated practice without multidisciplinary referral pathways

### Rising Demand
- Intraoral digital scanning (STL/PLY workflows) and CAD/CAM crown/bridge design
- Cone-beam CT (CBCT) 3D interpretation and guided implant surgical planning
- Interprofessional clinical team leadership and complex oncology rehabilitation
- Evaluative verification of AI-assisted dental margin detection and diagnostic triage
