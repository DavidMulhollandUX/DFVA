## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Human Resource Management trains HR generalists, recruitment consultants and people-and-culture advisers for the field's largest graduate destinations — Human Resource Advisers, Human Resource Managers and Recruitment Consultants. Both gates pass: G1 on an all-compulsory core accredited by the Australian HR Institute (AHRI), with a compulsory Industry Project capstone, and G2 on that capstone's requirement to investigate three alternatives against industry evidence before recommending one.

Exposure sits at 94.14 on an estimated cognate basis, pending the program's own graduate-destination data. Adaptiveness is low at 4/15: C2 and W3 sit at the floor (0/3), and C1, C3, C4 and C5 each clear only the first anchor (1/3). Workplace practice is comparatively stronger at 4/9, carried by W1 and W2 (2/3 each) on the strength of the Law and Human Resource Management memo and tribunal-submission tasks and the Industry Project capstone presentation.
