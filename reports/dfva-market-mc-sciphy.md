Dates are stated where available.

### Theme 1 — physics-informed machine learning and surrogate simulation models

Guidance from the [AIP](https://aip.org.au/) (December 2024) and *Physical Review Letters* (February 2025) emphasizes that while AI emulators accelerate large-scale PDE solving and particle tracking, physicists must maintain foundational mathematical derivations to identify unphysical hallucinations and validate conservation laws.

**Bearing:** C2, C3, and G1.

### Theme 2 — collaborative laboratory experimentation and industry technology pitches

Reports from [DSTG](https://www.dst.defence.gov.au/) and commercial quantum firms highlight that frontier hardware development requires structured team coordination across experimental physicists, optical engineers, and software developers, communicating complex findings to multi-stakeholder project boards.

**Bearing:** C1, W1, and W3.

### Theme 3 — substantial independent research thesis and defended inquiry

Standards from [AIP](https://aip.org.au/) confirm that a multi-semester 100-point research thesis evaluated against publishable scientific standards is the primary indicator of independent analytical capability for both doctoral admission and industry R&D recruitment.

**Bearing:** C4, C5, and G2.
