## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This program scores 8 of 15 on curriculum adaptiveness and 4 of 9 on workplace practice, against an estimated destination exposure of 76.5 on a field-of-education basis (not this program's own graduates). Both structural gates pass — an accredited, staged specialist core (G1) and assessment that already requires defended trade-off decisions under uncertainty (G2) — so redesign should preserve those two features rather than open them. Four items sit at the operational floor (C1, C3, W1, W2): collaborative practice, AI literacy, professional communication and authentic task design are named in learning outcomes or attempted in individual units, but none is documented as assessed to the anchor's level-2 bar in a core unit. The market report for this program locates the same gap at the field's largest, unregulated entry destination — health promotion — where no statutory credential exists and the transcript is the only signal an employer reads.
