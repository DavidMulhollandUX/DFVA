## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The program has no destination record of its own; job families rest on a field-of-education basis for field 100101 Music, weighted toward two mapped occupations — Secondary School Teachers, Except Special and Vocational Education and Office Clerks, General (§1 market report). C3 (Digital & AI literacy, including governance) sits at Level 0 with no assessed AI content anywhere in the core, the widest gap on the instrument, against a market picture (§3 Theme 2) that places the teaching occupation toward the higher-exposure end of the Jobs and Skills Australia Gen AI Capacity Study because its core tasks — lesson planning, resource creation, feedback, assessment drafting — are language-intensive and readily AI-supported.

C1, C4 and C5 sit one level below the ceiling. Two market drivers bear directly on the gap: entry to teaching is gated nationally by AITSL accreditation and state or territory registration (§3 Theme 1), and the administration, clerical and data-entry skills falling in the Office Clerks, General advertisement sample (§4) mark the program's secondary destination, not its target one, as the more automation-exposed path. C2, W1, W2 and W3 are each one step from the ceiling. The plan below sequences the C3 steps first, since that gap is both the largest and the one the market evidence most directly supports.
