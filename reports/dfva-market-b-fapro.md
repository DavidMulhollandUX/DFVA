## 2. RECENT JOB AD SIGNALS
> **Confidence: MEDIUM** — based on peer-reviewed and trade commentary on theatre/event production AI; no live 90-day scrape of Seek/ LinkedIn was conducted for this field, and Australian-specific hiring volumes are **not** asserted here.

**Signal 1 — scholarly work now maps AI across theatre creation, production and management.**
[Horváth (2025), "Curtain call for AI: Transforming theatre through technology"](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666188825003132) (ScienceDirect) examines AI's multifaceted role in theatre encompassing artistic creation, production and management processes.

**Signal 2 — practitioner outlets report stage managers adopting AI for organisation.**
[American Theatre, "What Can AI Do for the Arts?"](https://www.americantheatre.org/2025/06/19/what-can-ai-do-for-the-arts/) (19 June 2025) reports stage managers using AI to organise notes and streamline scheduling, and production teams using AI for idea generation.

**Signal 3 — replacement and rights are the contested frontier of live production.**
[TechPolicy.Press, "AI and the Future of Artistic Labor"](https://techpolicy.press/ai-and-the-future-of-artistic-labor) (2025) frames digital replacement on stage and training-pipeline collapse as the live-performance risks, with hybrid performance already being explored.

## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Peer-reviewed theatre-technology literature (ScienceDirect), US practitioner trade coverage (American Theatre), and policy commentary (TechPolicy.Press). Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed; a practitioner forum (Reddit r/techtheatre) was reviewed for signal only and is flagged as self-selected, low-confidence, and **not** used as a sourced claim. Dates are given where the source carries one. Claims about Australian graduate hiring volumes are **not** sourced here and have been omitted rather than restated.

### Theme 1 — AI is entering technical and production workflows as assistive tooling

[Horváth (2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666188825003132) examines AI across theatre artistic creation, production and management; [American Theatre (19 June 2025)](https://www.americantheatre.org/2025/06/19/what-can-ai-do-for-the-arts/) reports stage managers using AI to organise notes and streamline scheduling. The compressible work is documentation, search and scheduling; the resilient skill is live technical and coordination judgement.

**Bearing:** W2 and C2. Authentic task design should let students operate the tools, not just be operated by them.

### Theme 2 — digital replacement and rights are the live-production debate

[TechPolicy.Press (2025)](https://techpolicy.press/ai-and-the-future-of-artistic-labor) argues theatre is not immune to digital replacement and training-pipeline collapse, with hybrid performance already explored. The piece frames the choice as tool-versus-exploit.

**Bearing:** W3 and C4. Placement into a producing company should teach replica-consent and rights as a workplace competency.

### Theme 3 — practitioner forums show curiosity, not consensus (low confidence)

A review of Reddit r/techtheatre surfaced practitioners asking about current and future AI applications in technical theatre (lighting, AV, automation). This is self-selected and low-confidence; it is reported as a signal of interest, **not** as a measured claim about adoption, and carries no attribution suitable for a sourced theme.

**Bearing:** C1. Indicates demand for tooling literacy among early-career technicians; not evidence of displacement.

**Confidence restated:** MEDIUM. The scholarly and trade sources are real and attributable, but the discourse is overwhelmingly US/UK-centric and the forum signal is low-confidence; Australian production-employer practice and local hiring are not sourced here and should not be inferred from it.

## 4. SKILL SHIFT SUMMARY

### Declining Demand
- Manual cue sheet re-typing, routine call sheet formatting, and repetitive paperwork
- Routine lighting patch transcription and manual equipment inventory counting
- Disconnected CAD drafting without integrated show-control data linking

### Rising Demand
- Live, multi-system show control, network protocol management (sACN, Dante, OSC), and technical troubleshooting under show conditions
- AI-assisted production planning, scheduling, and digital stage management tooling
- Evaluative verification of generative design references against physical rigging, electrical load, and WHS limits
- Cross-disciplinary production team leadership and safety risk governance
