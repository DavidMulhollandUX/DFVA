## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Bachelor of Science (Monash, S2000) scores 6/15 on adaptive capabilities and 4/9 on workplace practice. C1 and W1 sit at level 2 because the compulsory all-student unit SCI1000 carries an assessed group task (a 30% group vodcast) and two professional-genre artefacts (a popular media article and an infographic); the remaining six items sit at level 1 because SCI1000's other assessment stops at outcome or source evaluation rather than criterion-referenced appraisal, and every unit that would otherwise carry the gap — the SCI1022 coding module, the SCI3990 self-scoped research route, the science internships — sits in the Years two and three choice list rather than the compulsory core.

The market report grounds three distinct levers for this program. First, CI-1 and Theme 4 note that retail is the field's largest recorded entry destination (4.9%) and that clerical work, the field's second-largest, is the clear automation-exposed group, motivating C4 and W2 toward transfer and authentic task design. Second, CI-2 and Theme 5 note that geoscience — the field's third-largest destination — carries no statutory registration, so the transcript itself must carry the professional signal, motivating W1 and W2 specifically. Third, CI-3 and Theme 6 note that JSA's Generative AI Capacity Study finds augmentation, interpreting and coordinating with a model's output, dominant across 358 scored Australian occupations, motivating C1, C2, C3 and C5. G1 and G2 both pass: G1 on the major's staged prerequisite chain and documented depth profile, and G2 on the design-and-execute-your-own-experiment assessments in the Level 1 chemistry and physics sequences and on SCI1000's self-scoped project.
