## 2. RECENT JOB AD SIGNALS

**Signal 1 — Mandatory BIM and Revit documentation proficiency.**
Major architectural practices (Woods Bagot, HASSELL, Architectus, Cox Architecture) require graduate architects to have functional BIM/Revit drafting and OpenBIM/IFC coordination skills from day one.

**Signal 2 — Stringent NCC 2025 energy efficiency and embodied carbon compliance.**
Following the commencement of NCC 2025 Section J revisions and Green Star Buildings 2.0, hiring firms demand graduates capable of calculating embodied carbon and conducting life cycle assessments.

**Signal 3 — Scarcity of heritage and adaptive reuse specialists.**
Victorian government housing targets and urban densification drive sustained demand for heritage-trained architects capable of navigating statutory heritage registers and adaptive reuse frameworks.

**Signal 4 — AI option generation elevating the demand for critical design curation.**
While generative AI tools (Midjourney, Forma) rapidly produce conceptual visualisations, firms demand architects who can rigorously evaluate building performance, structural constructability, and planning compliance.

## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Australian Institute of Architects ([AIA](https://www.architecture.com.au/)), Architects Accreditation Council of Australia ([AACA](https://www.aaca.org.au/)), Architects Registration Board of Victoria ([ARBV](https://www.arbv.vic.gov.au/)), and peer-reviewed architectural design scholarship ([Design Studies](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/design-studies), [February 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/design-studies)). Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no social media forum was sampled — where this section refers to discourse, it denotes published National Standard of Competency for Architects (NSCA) benchmarks, AIA practice notes, and documented technological reviews by named bodies and authors. Dates are stated where available.

### Theme 1 — generative AI design curation vs statutory constructability and compliance

Policy papers from the [AIA](https://www.architecture.com.au/) (November 2024) and *Design Studies* (February 2025) underline that AI image generators and parametric layout engines automate early-stage visual ideation, but frequently output unbuildable geometries that violate the National Construction Code (NCC) or Disability Discrimination Act (DDA) access standards. Architects carry statutory legal liability for building life safety and must critically evaluate and override AI-generated design concepts.

**Bearing:** C2, C3, and W2.

### Theme 2 — collaborative multidisciplinary coordination and client engagement

Reports from the [AACA](https://www.aaca.org.au/) emphasize that modern building delivery requires complex interpersonal coordination between architects, structural/services engineers, quantity surveyors, building surveyors, and community stakeholders. Designing protocols for role allocation and human-AI tool management is becoming a key practice competence.

**Bearing:** C1, W1, and W3.

### Theme 3 — self-directed design inquiry and primary site-specific research

Accreditation frameworks from [AACA](https://www.aaca.org.au/) highlight that architectural leadership depends on rigorous design-as-research methods, where architects formulate original design research questions, conduct empirical site investigations, and defend their design thesis under formal peer and industry panel critique.

**Bearing:** C4, C5, and G1.

## 4. SKILL SHIFT SUMMARY

### Declining Demand
- Manual 2D CAD drafting without 3D BIM integration
- Standalone conceptual rendering without constructability analysis
- Unintegrated sustainability treated as an afterthought

### Rising Demand
- Evaluative verification of AI-assisted conceptual designs against NCC and DDA standards
- Advanced BIM/Revit coordination, IFC interoperability, and computational scripting
- Whole-of-life embodied carbon calculation (One Click LCA, Green Star 2.0)
- Statutory contract administration, procurement risk management, and client communication
