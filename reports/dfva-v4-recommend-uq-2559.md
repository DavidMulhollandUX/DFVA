## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This document argues from evidence; it is not itself evidence. UQ's Bachelor of Computer Science passes both gates: G1, on the disciplinary depth documented through Australian Computer Society accreditation and a staged prerequisite chain — an introductory software engineering course leading into programming in the large, then into an algorithms and data structures course that states the earlier course as assumed knowledge; and G2, on defended trade-off decisions already assessed (selecting and justifying combinations of data structures and algorithms, analysing operating-system performance trade-offs) plus a staged major project defended in a pass/fail identity-verified interview hurdle and open-ended studio projects with conflicting constraints. Neither gate constrains the redesign below; both need only to be preserved, not created.

AI exposure rests on a field-of-education basis (83.3, field grain, estimated) — the destinations are the whole 0201 Computer Science field's, not this program's own graduates — so it is sound to say the field is highly exposed and no more. The program holds broad headroom on both sub-scales: 5 of 15 on Adaptiveness (currently 10/15) and 4 of 9 on Workplace (currently 5/9). W3 (1/3) is the single largest gap — work-situated learning exists only as an occasional, application-gated elective, with the one live-client project that would otherwise qualify undocumented as core — followed by C1 through C5 and W1 through W2, each sitting at level 2: collaborative assessment, evaluative appraisal, AI-literacy content, novel-problem application, designed inquiry, professional-genre communication and end-to-end practitioner tasks are each documented as assessed, but none is yet documented as reaching the level-3 threshold this instrument sets for AI-tool coordination, reliance-justification, governance critique, self-scoped progression, required primary research, external-audience judgement or an authentic-task spine.
