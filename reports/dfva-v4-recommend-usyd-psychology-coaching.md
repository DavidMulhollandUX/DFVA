## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This document argues from evidence; it is not itself evidence. Coaching Psychology passes both gates: G1, on a staged, all-compulsory specialist core — PSYC4721 as the entry unit, PSYC4722 as its corequisite, PSYC4724 carrying both as prerequisites, and every elective in the program gated behind PSYC4721 and PSYC4722 — and G2, on the semester-long coaching practicum's assessed, unscripted engagement with a partner's real-life issues, which carries real uncertainty and accountability rather than recall or a scripted response. Neither gate constrains the redesign below; both need only to be preserved, not created.

AI exposure rests on a field-of-education basis (89.85, field grain, estimated) — this program has no destination record of its own, so the figure is the whole 090701 Psychology field's, not this cohort's own graduates. The program holds substantial headroom on both sub-scales: 10 of 15 on Adaptiveness (currently 5/15) and 6 of 9 on Workplace (currently 3/9). C3 (0/3, digital and AI literacy including governance) and W3 (0/3, work-situated learning) are the largest gaps — no AI or digital content appears anywhere in the core, and the practicum's peer-partner design, together with the entry requirement's three years of employment experience, both fall short of an assessed, real-recipient or documented own-practice component. C2, C4, C5 and W1 each sit two levels below the ceiling, and C1 and W2 one level below, on assessment that currently documents reflection, transfer, inquiry, communication, collaboration and authentic practice as outcomes or a single instance rather than as required, progressive, or governance-level assessment.
