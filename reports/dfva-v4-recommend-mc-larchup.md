## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This program's workplace practice sub-scale is its weakest area: work-situated learning (W3) scores 0, with no placement, practicum or live-client project documented in the captured core content, even though §2 names design and planning consultancies and local councils actively hiring this field's graduates. Adaptive capabilities sit at 9/15, held back mainly by AI literacy (C3), which stays at level 1 because the program's only AI-specific unit is an elective, against a market where §3 finds augmentation dominant over automation for the professional roles this program feeds.

Two gates bound any redesign. G1 (disciplinary foundation) passes on the program's accredited, largely-core structure. G2 (decision-making under uncertainty) does not currently stand — the cited evidence sits under an outcomes heading, not an assessment page — so no intervention below assumes G2 is passed, and closing it needs re-verification against genuine assessment-page text before it needs a curriculum change.
