## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Monash's Master of Public Health passes both gates: G1, on a compulsory, staged foundation core — Part B's epidemiology, biostatistics, research-methods and public-health-fundamentals sequence taken by the whole cohort at commencement, continuing into Part C's mandatory Professional practice development unit in the final year, and the Research specialisation's explicit prerequisite chain into its project units — and G2, on the trade-off decisions already assessed in the Research methods study-protocol hurdle, the Public health fundamentals policy position statement, and the Introductory epidemiology study-design problem-solving. Neither gate constrains the redesign below; both need only to be preserved, not created.

AI exposure rests on a field-of-education basis (76.5, field grain, estimated) — this program has no destination record of its own, so the figure is the whole 0613 Public Health field's, not this cohort's own graduates. The program holds headroom on both sub-scales: 7 of 15 on Adaptiveness (currently 8/15) and 4 of 9 on Workplace (currently 5/9). C3 (digital and AI literacy, including governance), C5 (inquiry and evidence generation) and W3 (work-situated learning) are the largest gaps, each capped at level 1 because level 2 requires core placement and each currently sits only in an elective (Practical data management, Public health placement) or a single specialisation route (the Research specialisation's project units) rather than in the shared compulsory core. C1, C2, C4, W1 and W2 each sit one level below the ceiling on a recurring pattern: the core assesses the capability once — one graded team task, one critical appraisal, one novel-problem protocol, one policy-genre artefact — where the level-3 anchor asks for recurrence, a real external audience, or a required rather than optional route.
