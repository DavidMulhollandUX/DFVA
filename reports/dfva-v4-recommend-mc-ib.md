## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of International Business runs an all-compulsory international-business core — The Multinational, Global Corporate Governance, Cross Cultural Management and Teamwork, Managing in Asia, Global Value Chains — plus a compulsory capstone, so G1 passes; the capstone's competitive strategy simulation and orally defended consulting-style assignments carry G2.

Operating under an estimated destination exposure of 87.44 (field-tier basis, not this program's own graduates), the degree shows substantial headroom on both sub-scales: 6/15 on Adaptiveness and 4/9 on Workplace. The most critical deficit is C5 (0/3): no core subject documents research-methods training, methodology selection or primary data collection. C2 and C3 each score 1/3 — criterion-referenced appraisal and AI/digital literacy both surface only in optional capstone-selective subjects (peer review, 360-degree feedback) or electives (MGMT90280's generic reference, 'Artificial Intelligence in Organisations', MGMT90267), never in the guaranteed core. W1 and W3 also score 1/3: professional communication stays inside the teaching team, and the program's real work-situated learning (Business Practicum, Global Business Practicum, Business and Economics Internship) is one option among several rather than a guaranteed core requirement.
