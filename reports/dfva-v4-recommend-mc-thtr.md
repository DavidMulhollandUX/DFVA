## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is interpretation, not observation. Master of Theatre has no destination record of its own, so its market evidence rests on the Drama and Theatre Studies field-of-education profile: performance destinations with no empirical automation-exposure record, alongside an administrative destination — Office Clerks, General — that carries one. Every intervention below is warranted by that administrative signal, not by a claim about the field's performance occupations.

The captured core sits at level 2 across most of both sub-scales, with digital and AI literacy (C3) the exception at level 0. The interventions favour extending assessment already in place — the group-devising unit, the pitch tasks, the self-scoped capstone — over new units, because the market evidence available for this program is thin (§6 of the market report: MEDIUM confidence on most signal areas, LOW on the destination basis itself) and does not warrant large curriculum redesign.
