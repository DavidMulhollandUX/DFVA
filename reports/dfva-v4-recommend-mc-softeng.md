## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The program's furthest gap is Inquiry & evidence generation (C5, 0/3): no compulsory unit requires methodology selection or primary data collection, and the two units named explicitly around inquiry sit only in the general elective list. Hybrid metacognition & evaluative judgement (C2), Digital & AI literacy (C3) and Work-situated learning (W3) each sit at 1/3, blocked by the same pattern — the relevant content exists, but only as an elective, or without documented judging criteria.

Distributed cognition & relational capability (C1), Life-long learning & transfer (C4) and Professional communication & conduct (W1) sit at 2/3, one step short of the ceiling their own core curriculum already approaches. Authentic task design (W2) is already at the ceiling and needs no action.
