## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The two compulsory editing subjects already reach criterion-referenced appraisal and reproduce a professional editing task end to end, and the thesis capstone already reaches the instrument's top level for life-long learning and transfer. The remaining gaps are structural rather than incremental: no core unit assesses collaborative practice, digital content stays at tool-operation level, and work-situated learning sits only in an elective — the three items closest to the parts of this program's destinations that reports/dfva-market-d01lf.md marks most exposed to automation (Office Clerks, General; §2 Signal 1; §3 Theme 2, Theme 6).

Both gates are recorded FAIL. G1 fails because only 37.5 of 200 credit points are strictly compulsory, with Foundation, Capstone and Electives all optional menus; G2 fails because the cited evidence was learning-outcome text, not an assessed component, and the thesis assessment table grades only the 15,000-word written submission. Any intervention that adds compulsory content should be checked against G1's compulsory-share problem, and any C5 intervention should add an assessed, defended component rather than another outcome statement.
