## 2. RECENT JOB AD SIGNALS

**Signal 1 — Acute demand for provisional psychologists under PsyBA 5+1 internship pathway.**
Community mental health networks, NDIS providers, and private practices heavily recruit APAC-accredited Master of Professional Psychology graduates for their final 1-year supervised internship.

**Signal 2 — Universal requirement for telehealth delivery and electronic clinical documentation.**
Clinical job postings across Victoria (Headspace, Beyond Blue, hospital networks) mandate demonstrated proficiency in telehealth video-therapy platforms and electronic patient management systems.

**Signal 3 — Mandatory hurdle clinical skills examinations and PsyBA National Exam alignment.**
Employers and AHPRA require demonstrated competency in test administration (WAIS, WISC) and case formulation matching the PsyBA National Psychology Examination blueprint.

**Signal 4 — Therapeutic alliance, clinical empathy, and crisis de-escalation moat.**
Live diagnostic interview nuance, suicide risk formulation, and real-time human therapeutic presence provide complete insulation against automated AI substitution.

## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Australian Psychology Accreditation Council ([APAC](https://www.psychologycouncil.org.au/)), Psychology Board of Australia / AHPRA ([PsyBA](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/)), Australian Psychological Society ([APS](https://psychology.org.au/)), and peer-reviewed literature ([Australian Psychologist](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/rapc20), [December 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/rapc20)). Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no social media forum was sampled — where this section refers to discourse, it denotes published APAC accreditation standards, PsyBA national examination guidelines, APS practice codes, and peer-reviewed psychological science research by named bodies and authors. Dates are stated where available.

### Theme 1 — ethical governance of AI in clinical documentation, telehealth, and psychometrics

Guidance from the [APS](https://psychology.org.au/) (November 2024) and *Australian Psychologist* (December 2024) emphasizes that while AI tools automate administrative clinical notes and initial psychometric scoring, registered psychologists maintain sole legal and ethical liability for case formulations, risk appraisals, and client confidentiality under Privacy Act standards.

**Bearing:** C2, C3, and W2.

### Theme 2 — interprofessional collaborative practice, cultural safety, and multidisciplinary care

Reports from [APAC](https://www.psychologycouncil.org.au/) and [PsyBA](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/) mandate structured interprofessional collaborative learning (such as Indigenous cultural safety and medical liaison), preparing psychologists to work alongside psychiatrists, general practitioners, occupational therapists, and social workers.

**Bearing:** C1, W1, and W3.

### Theme 3 — authentic clinical assessment spine and self-scoped research thesis

Standards from [APAC](https://www.psychologycouncil.org.au/) confirm that a progressive authentic assessment spine—combining hurdle interview role-plays, mental state examinations, risk assessment reports, and a self-scoped minor research thesis—is essential for provisional registration readiness.

**Bearing:** C4, W2, and G1.

| Neurodiversity-affirming assessment and intervention frameworks | **↑↑ Rising sharply** | Theme 5 (§3) describes a rapid, community-driven shift away from deficit-model autism/ADHD frameworks, with programs that still teach older models "critiqued publicly by both practitioners and advocacy communities." |
| AI-assisted clinical documentation with ethical-governance literacy | **↑ Rising** | Theme 2 (§3) records cautious professional openness to AI for case notes and psychoeducation content, paired with firm rejection of AI for therapeutic decisions — a distinction CI-3 (§5) recommends teaching explicitly. |

| Individual therapeutic relationship work | **→ Stable, differentiating** | The job family map (§1) rates AI substitution pressure LOW for provisional/registered clinical psychologists, and Theme 2 (§3) confirms AI is "firmly rejected as unsuitable for therapeutic decisions" even as administrative AI use grows. |
| Purely private-practice / fee-for-service career orientation | **↓ Falling** | Theme 3 (§3) describes the Medicare bulk-billing gap as reshaping early-career trajectories toward salaried roles in NDIS, EAP and hospital settings, away from private practice. |
| Paper-based psychometric test administration | **↓↓ Falling sharply** | The job family map (§1) lists computerised neuropsychological assessment among rising skills, and Signal 4 (§2) shows organisational-psychology roles now embedding quantitative data-analysis requirements once treated as purely soft-skills adjacent. |
