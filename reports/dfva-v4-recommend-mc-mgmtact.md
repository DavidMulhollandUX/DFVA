## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Management (Accounting) is a professional entry-to-practice degree accredited by CPA Australia and CAANZ, preparing graduates for professional careers across financial accounting, audit, tax advisory, and commercial management (AIOE 97.99). The curriculum demonstrates rigorous technical and statutory foundations (C1: 2/3, C2: 2/3, C3: 2/3, C5: 2/3, G1 PASS) with group financial analyses in ACCT90002, corporate reporting in ACCT90012, auditing projects in ACCT90014, and information systems control in ACCT90030. However, the accounting profession faces extreme automation pressure on routine bookkeeping, ledger reconciliation, and statutory tax calculations. The program's primary developmental headroom lies in formalizing human-AI workflow allocation charters (C1), introducing assessed reliance and anomaly verification logs for AI audit and tax reconciliation outputs (C2), embedding systemic AI audit governance and APESB ethical compliance (C3), and transitioning optional accounting internships into guaranteed core client engagements (W1, W3).
