## 2. RECENT JOB AD SIGNALS

**Signal 1 — Mandatory PsyBA registration and area of practice endorsement (AoPE).**
State health networks (Alfred Health, Royal Melbourne, Austin Health) and private clinics require Psychology Board of Australia (PsyBA) registration with Clinical Psychology endorsement, maintaining an insurmountable regulatory gate against AI substitution.

**Signal 2 — Ambient AI clinical documentation tools (Upheal, Heidi Health).**
Adoption of ambient AI transcription platforms in clinical psychology accelerates session note drafting and intake summaries, shifting demand toward clinical psychologists who can critically audit AI notes against clinical nuance, patient consent, and privacy law.

**Signal 3 — Severe national shortages in youth and complex comorbidity mental health.**
Commonwealth and state funding expansions for child and youth mental health hubs (headspace, early psychosis units) sustain severe, unfilled clinical psychologist demand across Victoria and nationally.

**Signal 4 — Rigorous clinical trial and translational PhD research demand.**
Dual-degree PhD graduates are uniquely sought after by medical research institutes (Orygen, Florey Institute, Turner Institute) to lead clinical trials on novel psychiatric and psychological interventions.

## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Australian Psychological Society ([APS](https://psychology.org.au/)), Psychology Board of Australia ([PsyBA](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/)), Australian Clinical Psychology Association ([ACPA](https://acpa.org.au/)), and peer-reviewed clinical psychology scholarship ([Australian Psychologist](https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/rapc20/current), [January 2025](https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/rapc20/current)). Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no social media forum was sampled — where this section refers to discourse, it denotes published professional guidelines, regulatory board communiqués, and documented clinical debates by named bodies and authors. Dates are stated where available.

### Theme 1 — ambient clinical AI documentation: diagnostic fidelity and privacy governance

Professional advisories from the [Australian Psychological Society](https://psychology.org.au/) (November 2024) and [ACPA](https://acpa.org.au/) emphasize that while ambient AI transcription tools reduce administrative burden, clinicians maintain non-delegable legal liability for diagnostic accuracy, patient consent, and sensitive therapeutic data storage. Clinical psychologists must exercise rigorous evaluative judgement to audit and correct AI-generated progress notes.

**Bearing:** C2, C3, and W2.

### Theme 2 — complex comorbidity, trauma-informed care, and relational therapeutic alliance

Clinical research in *Australian Psychologist* (January 2025) and [APS](https://psychology.org.au/) highlights that the core relational mechanism of psychotherapy—empathic attunement, real-time rupture repair, and trauma-informed safety—is entirely insulated from algorithmic automation. Demand is surging for advanced clinical training in complex personality disorders and co-occurring substance abuse.

**Bearing:** C1, W1, and G1.

### Theme 3 — ethical supervision and research integrity in combined PhD translational science

Guidelines from the [Psychology Board of Australia](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/) (2024/2025) on postgraduate supervision stress the imperative of independent methodological defence and research transparency in clinical trials. Doctoral candidates must defend causal models and clinical intervention protocols under live academic and clinical scrutiny.

**Bearing:** C5, C4, and G2.
