## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Master of Commerce (Economics) scores 7/15 on the adaptive sub-scale and 1/9 on the workplace-practice sub-scale, with both gates — G1 Disciplinary foundation and G2 Decision-making under uncertainty — passing on verification review. Headroom concentrates in the two items scored 0, Authentic task design (W2) and Work-situated learning (W3), and in the four items scored 1: Distributed cognition & relational capability (C1), Hybrid metacognition & evaluative judgement (C2), Digital & AI literacy, including governance (C3) and Professional communication & conduct (W1). The destination basis is a cognate-tier estimate, not this program's own graduate destinations, so this plan's market evidence is read as directional for the field rather than program-specific.

Two occupation families and two regulatory themes carry most of the evidence. Accountants and Auditors is this report's largest and highest-confidence signal — 6,240 live postings — and Theme 1 (§3) is scoped directly to C2, C3 and G1: professional standards require auditors to verify automated ledger anomaly-detection algorithms and maintain documented audit trail logs for AI-generated financial estimates. Theme 2 (§3) is scoped directly to C4, C5 and W2: mandatory Australian Sustainability Reporting Standards create an acute shortage of accounting graduates skilled in multi-capital greenhouse gas assurance and climate risk governance, and §4 marks that skill Rising sharply. No theme or signal in this report names W1, W3 or C1 in its bearing; those items are planned on the instrument's own anchor terms, informed only by the scale and named employers of Signal 1 and Signal 2.
