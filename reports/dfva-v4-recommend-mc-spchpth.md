## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Speech Pathology is a 200-point entry-level professional qualification accredited by Speech Pathology Australia (SPA), preparing graduates for clinical practice across hospital neurorehabilitation, paediatric language, acute dysphagia, and voice disorders (AI Occupational Exposure (AIOE) 89.56). The program demonstrates a flawless benchmark across workplace practice (**9 / 9** on Sub-scale W: W1: 3/3, W2: 3/3, W3: 3/3, G1 PASS, G2 PASS), anchored by extensive clinical placement blocks (AUDI90032, AUDI90033, AUDI90039) — approximately 400 hours of supervised clinical work — evaluated via the COMPASS® competency assessment, alongside rigorous OSCE hurdles across every semester. On the adaptive capabilities sub-scale (**9 / 15**), the curriculum reaches Level 2 on four of the five items (computer-based statistical practicals in AUDI90002, clinical evaluative judgement in OSCE hurdles, lifespan transfer in complex cases in AUDI90041) and Level 1 on C3, where the capture records no AI content of any kind and the digital content it does record sits in unassessed intended learning outcomes. The largest curriculum headroom is C3, two levels below the maximum: core assessment of what automated speech and language tools can do and where their limits lie. The remaining headroom is establishing human-AI clinical transcription and workflow charters (C1), introducing assessed reliance and override logs for automated acoustic voice analysis and speech recognition tools (C2), and documenting the AUDI90040 research project's methodology defence (C5).
