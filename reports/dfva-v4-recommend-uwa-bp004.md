## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

BP004 sits on an estimated field-basis exposure of 76.63 (JSA HEO field 0001 Natural and Physical Sciences), with Curriculum Adaptiveness at 8/15 and Workplace practice at 6/9. Headroom concentrates on the two adaptive items still at level 1 — C2 Hybrid metacognition & evaluative judgement and C3 Digital & AI literacy, including governance — while the remaining six items sit one step short of the top anchor.

The market evidence points the same way from two directions. Retail and clerical roles are the field's largest recorded destinations and both are named by Jobs and Skills Australia as automation-exposed (market report §5 CI-1), so most BP004 graduates apply their training somewhere outside the discipline that trained them. Among the scientific destinations that remain, geoscience — the largest of them — carries no statutory registration (market report §3 Theme 5), so no external body certifies competence and the assessed record has to carry the whole signal.
