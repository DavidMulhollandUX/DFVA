### Theme 4 — jobs and Skills Australia's Generative AI Capacity Study (August 2025) scores 358 ANZSCO occupations on tas…

Jobs and Skills Australia's Generative AI Capacity Study (August 2025) scores 358 ANZSCO occupations on task-level augmentability and automatability. It finds augmentation outweighs automation across the workforce: about 79% of Australian workers face low or very low automation risk, while routine clerical and administrative roles - including bookkeepers, receptionists, and business-analysis, sales, marketing and PR positions - carry the highest automation exposure. The study flags entry-level and clerical business roles as most exposed, with limited mobility options. (Business Operations Specialists, All Other; lane L2, scholarly and grey-literature studies.)

Sources: [Jobs and Skills Australia, Australia's AI Transition: Jobs, Skills and the Future of Work (Generative AI Capacity Study)](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/studies/generative-artificial-intelligence-capacity-study) (2025-08).

Scope: Australian occupations nationally; model-derived task-level estimates, not employment forecasts. Business-operations residual sits in the higher-exposure clerical/business cluster.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

Registered nurses must meet the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia's (NMBA) mandatory registration standards - continuing professional development, criminal history, English language skills, professional indemnity insurance and recency of practice - under the Health Practitioner Regulation National Law, administered by AHPRA. Use of the protected title 'registered nurse' requires current general registration with the NMBA. (Registered Nurses; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia, Registration standards for nurses (NMBA)](https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/codes-guidelines-statements/faq/registration-nurse-or-midwife.aspx) (2025).

Scope: Australia; AHPRA-regulated registered nurses. Does not cover enrolled nurses, non-registered staff, or overseas-only practice.
