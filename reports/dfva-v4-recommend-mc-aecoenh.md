## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Applied Econometrics (Enhanced) provides a technically rigorous core in data wrangling, econometric theory and applied analysis. The destination basis for this program is estimated (cognate basis, Destination AI Exposure 96.53) rather than drawn from the program's own graduates, so read this diagnostic as a signal, not a precise position. G1 (Disciplinary foundation) and G2 (Decision-making under uncertainty) both fail on the verified evidence: G1 finds one prerequisite link, not a documented chain, between core subjects, and G2 finds two of its three cited assessment tasks are learning outcomes, not graded tasks. Both gates constrain any redesign — see §5.

The program holds substantial headroom on both sub-scales: **7 / 15 on Adaptiveness** and **3 / 9 on Workplace**. C2 (0/3) is the largest single gap — no core assessment anywhere requires students to appraise the quality of their own, a peer's, or a tool's work. C3, W1, W2 and W3 (all 1/3) each stop at the operational or scenario-framed level. C1, C4 and C5 (all 2/3) fall one step short of the ceiling, on tool-coordination, documented capstone progression and a defended methodology review, respectively.
