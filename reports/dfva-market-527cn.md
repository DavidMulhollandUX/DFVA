## 2. RECENT JOB AD SIGNALS

**Signal 1 — Acute hospital demand for specialist neuropsychological differential diagnosis.**
Major public healthcare networks (Royal Melbourne Hospital, Alfred Health, Austin Health) actively recruit clinical neuropsychologists for neurology, neurosurgery, stroke, and memory clinics.

**Signal 2 — Escalating demand for paediatric and neurodevelopmental assessment specialists.**
Specialist paediatric hospitals (RCH Melbourne, Monash Children’s) seek clinical neuropsychologists skilled in complex developmental, genetic, and acquired brain injury diagnostics.

**Signal 3 — Integration of digital cognitive testing platforms and normative scoring AI.**
Clinical neuropsychology practices require practitioners capable of supervising and critiquing automated psychometric administration (e.g. iPad-based CANTAB, Q-interactive) and detecting algorithmic scoring anomalies.

**Signal 4 — Low substitution pressure in live patient psychometric assessment and forensic testimony.**
Direct clinical observation, qualitative behavioural analysis during testing, performance validity assessment, and expert medico-legal testimony remain resilient against automation.

## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Australian Psychology Accreditation Council ([APAC](https://psychologycouncil.org.au/)), Psychology Board of Australia ([PsyBA](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/)), Australian Psychological Society College of Clinical Neuropsychologists ([APS CCN](https://psychology.org.au/about-us/special-interest-groups/college-of-clinical-neuropsychologists)), and peer-reviewed literature ([The Clinical Neuropsychologist](https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/ntcn20/current), [December 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/ntcn20/current)). Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no social media forum was sampled — where this section refers to discourse, it denotes published PsyBA registration guidelines, APAC accreditation standards, APS CCN professional communiqués, and peer-reviewed neuropsychological research by named bodies and authors. Dates are stated where available.

### Theme 1 — digital cognitive assessment tools, automated norming, and diagnostic AI oversight

Guidance from the [APS CCN](https://psychology.org.au/about-us/special-interest-groups/college-of-clinical-neuropsychologists) (November 2024) and *The Clinical Neuropsychologist* (December 2024) emphasizes that while AI-driven computerized testing systems expedite scoring and report generation, clinical neuropsychologists retain non-delegable statutory liability for test interpretation, validity verification, and culturally safe differential diagnosis.

**Bearing:** C2, C3, and W2.

### Theme 2 — interprofessional stroke, neurosurgery, and memory clinic teams

Reports from [PsyBA](https://www.psychologyboard.gov.au/) and [APAC](https://psychologycouncil.org.au/) highlight that clinical neuropsychologists work in high-stakes multidisciplinary hospital teams alongside neurologists, neurosurgeons, speech pathologists, and occupational therapists, requiring collaborative case conferences and communication.

**Bearing:** C1, W1, and W3.

### Theme 3 — structured empirical neuropsychological inquiry and longitudinal research

Standards from [APAC](https://psychologycouncil.org.au/) confirm that a compulsory minor research thesis, empirical cognitive data collection, and formal proposal presentations represent the benchmark for advanced clinical neuropsychology training.

**Bearing:** C4, C5, and G1.

## 4. SKILL SHIFT SUMMARY

### Declining Demand
- Manual calculation of psychometric norm tables without digital administration literacy
- Isolated reporting without interprofessional medical team recommendations
- Sole reliance on fixed test batteries without tailored qualitative diagnostic analysis

### Rising Demand
- Supervised digital and automated cognitive testing platforms (Q-interactive, CANTAB)
- Performance validity testing (PVT) and forensic capacity evaluations
- Cognitive rehabilitation and behavioral intervention planning in NDIS/TAC contexts
- Multidisciplinary memory clinic and paediatric neurodevelopmental diagnostics
