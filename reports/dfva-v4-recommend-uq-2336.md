## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Bachelor of Commerce carries a field-level destination exposure of 88.71 (estimated — no institution-specific graduate record) against a curriculum that assesses almost none of the adaptive capabilities the market signals call for: three items score 0 and none reaches level 2. G1 (disciplinary foundation) passes on the compulsory core and the accredited accounting pathway; G2 (decision-making under uncertainty) fails because no captured assessment requires a defended trade-off decision.

The market report's own regulatory and skill-shift evidence — an accounting professional standard that makes an auditor answerable for verifying an AI tool's output, a mandatory sustainability-reporting regime creating a named graduate shortage, and a field where about a fifth of destinations sit in the roles the market itself calls most automation-exposed — gives three of the eight items (C1, C3, C5) a named, dated reason to move first.
