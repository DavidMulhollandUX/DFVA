## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This program scores 7 of 15 on adaptive capabilities and 3 of 9 on workplace practice, against an estimated destination exposure of 76.03 on a field-of-education basis. The largest single gap is C3 (Digital & AI literacy, including governance), currently at level 0 with no AI or digital content in any core unit — a gap the market evidence makes a registration-relevant obligation, not a discretionary addition, since AHPRA's AI-in-healthcare guidance requires practitioners to understand a tool's bias and governance before relying on it (§3, Theme 6). The next-largest gaps are spread across C5 and the three workplace items (W1–W3), each at level 1. Both gates pass: the program documents a coherent specialist core (G1) and assesses defended clinical decision-making under uncertainty (G2), so neither constrains the prioritised interventions in §4, but both set boundaries the redesign options must respect.
