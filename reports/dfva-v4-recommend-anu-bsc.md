## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Both gates fail: G1 because the program documents no compulsory specialist core — no course is required, there are no formal program prerequisites, and no accreditation is claimed — only a 312-course prescribed pool spanning science and non-science disciplines; G2 because no assessment in the program is required of any student, so nothing is documented that 'requires' a defended trade-off decision, even though COMP3500's stakeholder-facing engineering-decision review would satisfy the anchor if it were core rather than restricted-entry and optional.

Operating on an estimated field-of-education exposure of 76.63, drawn from Jobs and Skills Australia Higher Education Outcomes destinations for the whole Natural and Physical Sciences field rather than this program's own graduates, the program scores the minimum documented level (1/3) on every one of the eight Panel C items: 5/15 on Adaptiveness (C1-C5) and 3/9 on Workplace (W1-W3). Every anchor above level 1 is already partly met somewhere in the 312-course pool — assessed group work, peer review, AI-ethics content, research-methods training, professional-genre tasks and work-situated placements all exist — but none of it is compulsory, so the flexible-pool structure that defines this degree is also the single cause of every gap below.

| G1 Disciplinary foundation | FAIL | The PASS anchor needs a documented coherent specialist core with progressive technical or methodological depth by one of three routes — an all-compulsory specialist core, an accredited sequence, or a staged prerequisite chain. The handbook denies the first (no specific course must be taken), denies the third (no formal program prerequisites, and first-year science courses assume little specific knowledge), and documents no accreditation. What is documented is a 312-course prescribed pool spanning anthropology, economics, finance, management, sociology and computing alongside the sciences, with up to ten courses permitted from another ANU College — the FAIL descriptor's interchangeable content. G1 stays FAIL until the underlying major structure documents one of those three routes; the interventions above address Panel C items, not this gate, and should not be read as resolving it. | [[1]](https://www.teqsa.gov.au/guides-resources/resources/corporate-publications/assuring-quality-learning-gen-ai-integrated-future-role-adaptive-capabilities)[[6]](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjaa021) |
