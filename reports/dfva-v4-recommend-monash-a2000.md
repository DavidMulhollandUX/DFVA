## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This Bachelor of Arts scores 1 out of 3 on every item in this instrument, for an adaptive capabilities subtotal of 5 out of 15 and a workplace practice subtotal of 3 out of 9. The destination basis behind the market evidence below is a field-of-education estimate (Society and Culture), not a measured destination record for this program, so the market alignment below is indicative of the field a Bachelor of Arts sits within rather than a measurement of this specific cohort. Every item stalls on the same structural fact: the capability is claimed in a course-level outcome, or offered through an elective, but no unit is compulsory anywhere across the degree's 144 points, so no anchor above level 1 is reachable without a change to what counts as core. Because the degree has no shared compulsory curriculum, most of the interventions below work through a single named major rather than through a unit every student takes.
