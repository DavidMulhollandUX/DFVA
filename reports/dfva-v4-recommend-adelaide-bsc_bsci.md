## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Bachelor of Science scores 5/15 on adaptive capabilities and 2/9 on workplace practice. Every item but W2 sits at level 1 for the same structural reason: the program states graduate outcomes and runs named majors with methods courses, but the extract documents no assessment task for any course in the program structure, so most items score only because R2 credits an outcome statement that is never assessed. W2 sits at the floor because no assessment description of any kind survives in the extract for any core course.

The market report grounds two distinct levers for this program. First, CI-2 and CI-4 note that geoscience carries no statutory registration and that Australian Consumer Law binds the retail roles many graduates first enter, so the transcript itself must carry the professional signal — motivating W1 and W2. Second, CI-1 and CI-3 note that retail is the field's largest recorded destination, ahead of every scientific occupation, and that the augmented task JSA's Generative AI Capacity Study finds dominant is interpreting a model's output — motivating C3, C4 and C5 specifically. G1 passes on the staged prerequisite chain inside each major; G2 fails because no assessment description survives in the extract to show a defended trade-off decision, and constrains any redesign that would substitute a scripted task for a live one.
