## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Master of Human Rights Law scores 4 of 15 on adaptive capabilities and 2 of 9 on workplace practice, against a destination exposure figure of 94.83 (partial basis, estimated). Both redesign gates fail: the program documents one compulsory subject (International Human Rights Law) rather than a coherent specialist core, and no assessed task in the extract carries real decision-making uncertainty. The market evidence available for this program rests on a field-of-education basis (0909 Law), so the alignment below reads the shared entry-level destinations — audit and accounting, business operations, and office administration — for the skill shifts a Human Rights Law curriculum can act on. The steepest gaps are C2 (evaluative judgement) and W2 (authentic task design), both starting from zero.
