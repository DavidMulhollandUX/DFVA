### Theme 4 — for registered nurses (ANZSCO 2544), JSA's Generative AI Capacity Study shows Jobs and Skills Australia's G…

For registered nurses (ANZSCO 2544), JSA's Generative AI Capacity Study shows Jobs and Skills Australia's Generative AI Capacity Study (Our Gen AI Transition: Exposures, Adaptation, Dynamism, August 2025) scores all 358 Australian occupations at task level using ANZSCO v1.3, rating augmentability and automatability (0-1). It finds augmentation potential outweighs automation risk across most occupations; 79% of Australian workers face low or very low automation risk. Healthcare occupations rank among the lowest-exposure groups. JSA-based exposure analyses place registered nurses and physiotherapists at about 4.1/10 AI exposure and occupational therapists below 4.0/10 - a high-augmentation, low-automation profile consistent with AHPRA's requirement that a registered human remain accountable for care decisions. (Registered Nurses; lane L2, scholarly and grey-literature studies.)

Sources: [Jobs and Skills Australia, Our Gen AI Transition: Exposures, Adaptation, Dynamism - Occupation data on AI exposure](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/studies/generative-artificial-intelligence-capacity-study/occupation-data-on-ai-exposure) (2025-08); [Will AI Take My Job (JSA-based analysis), How Safe Are Healthcare Jobs From AI in Australia?](https://willaitakemyjob.com.au/blog/how-safe-are-healthcare-jobs-from-ai-in-australia) (2025).

Scope: Australian ANZSCO-based JSA study; scores are model-derived task-level estimates, not employment-change forecasts. Per-occupation scores are from a JSA-based derivative analysis, not JSA's primary explorer.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.
