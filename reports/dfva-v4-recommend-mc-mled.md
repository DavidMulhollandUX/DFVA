## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This program scores 7 of 15 on Curriculum Adaptiveness and 3 of 9 on Workplace Practice, against a Destination AI Exposure of 93.99 estimated on the Teacher Education, n.e.c. field basis. Every scored item sits at least one level below its available anchor, and the accompanying market report names three forces bearing on that gap: an AI-governance mandate binding teachers and school leaders (Themes 2, 4, 5 and 6), an assessment-integrity shift toward oral, performance and in-person tasks (Theme 3), and sustained entry-level hiring gated by AITSL accreditation and state registration (Signals 1–4, Theme 1).
