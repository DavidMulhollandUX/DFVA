## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This program pairs an accredited Master of Architecture core with urban planning, but its live market signals come from adjacent occupations — Office Clerks, General; Construction Managers and Quantity Surveyors; and a residual Business Operations Specialists category (§1) — because the destination occupation Architect has no occupation mapping in this report. The plan below reads what these adjacent signals say about assessed AI governance, evaluative judgement and work-situated learning, and treats gaps the profession records do not speak to as gaps this plan cannot claim market warrant for.

Two regulatory changes dominate the available signal: NSW's 2026 work health and safety duties for AI-enabled site tools and state builder licensing's accuracy-accountability rule (§3 Themes 3–4) both fall on named individuals rather than on the tools they use, and both name the construction-management roles a share of this program's graduates enter. The interventions below sequence low-cost documentation and rubric changes ahead of the larger studio and placement redesigns.
