## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Regulatory and statutory instruments, scholarly and grey-literature studies, drawn from the committed profession records for the occupations in §1, each with its publisher, date and link. Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no professional forum was sampled for this report; the profession records declare their own practitioner-discourse sample — practitioner_forums (45 items, National Office Clerks, General Association discourse); reddit (None items, r/agriculture, r/farming); x (None items, agriculture / agtech AI discourse); hackernews (None items, agtech) — which is a declared sample, not a scrape. Themes are the claims as recorded; nothing is paraphrased, and a claim without a source was not carried. Dates are given where the source carries one.

### Theme 1 — office clerks handle personal and commercial information; the Privacy Act 1988 APPs bind their APP-entity e…

Office clerks handle personal and commercial information; the Privacy Act 1988 APPs bind their APP-entity employers and the Fair Work Act 2009 requires seven-year employee-record retention. No occupation-specific licensing regime governs general office clerks. (Office Clerks, General; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [Jacmac Law, Accessing employment records: Privacy Act and Fair Work Act intersection](https://jacmac.com.au/insights/accessing-employment-records) (2025).

Scope: Applies via employer; employee-records exemption; no specific regulator for the occupation.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 2 — 2025 evidence names clerical/secretarial work as the clear automation-exposed group; routine, rules-based o…

2025 evidence names clerical/secretarial work as the clear automation-exposed group; routine, rules-based office tasks are the most readily automated. (Office Clerks, General; lane L2, scholarly and grey-literature studies.)

Sources: [scty.org, AI at Work: The 2025 Evidence](https://scty.org/words/ai-work-2025) (2025-12).

Scope: Synthesis; clerical specifically flagged.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 3 — the peak professional body, Ag Institute Australia (Australian Institute of Agricultural Science and Techno…

The peak professional body, Ag Institute Australia (Australian Institute of Agricultural Science and Technology), administers the Certified Practising Agriculturalist (CPAg) national accreditation scheme for agricultural, horticultural and natural-resource professionals. (Agricultural, Plant, and Horticultural Scientists; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [Ag Institute Australia, Ag Institute Australia — membership and accreditation](https://aginstitute.com.au) (2026).

Scope: Voluntary professional accreditation; not a statutory registration requirement to practise.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 4 — plant breeders' rights in Australia are governed by the Plant Breeder's Rights Act 1994 (Cth), administered…

Plant breeders' rights in Australia are governed by the Plant Breeder's Rights Act 1994 (Cth), administered by IP Australia, granting exclusive commercial rights over new plant varieties — the core IP regime for plant scientists developing cultivars. (Agricultural, Plant, and Horticultural Scientists; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [Legislation.gov.au, Plant Breeder's Rights Act 1994 (Cth)](https://legislation.gov.au/C2004A04783/2024-10-14/2024-10-14/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) (2024-10-14).

Scope: Binds plant variety IP and breeding work; not a claim about field agronomy employment generally.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 5 — biological testing laboratories are accredited by NATA to ISO/IEC 17025 (and ISO 15189 where medical/biolog…

Biological testing laboratories are accredited by NATA to ISO/IEC 17025 (and ISO 15189 where medical/biological testing applies); the TGA regulates in-vitro diagnostics. There is no single statutory registration for biological technicians — competence is demonstrated through the facility's NATA accreditation. (Biological Technicians; lane L1, regulatory and statutory instruments.)

Sources: [NATA, ISO/IEC 17025 Application Document](https://nata.com.au/files/2026/02/ISOIEC-17025-Application-Document-Effective-May26.pdf) (2026-05).

Scope: Binds lab quality systems; individual technicians certified via facility accreditation, not personal licence.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.

### Theme 6 — jSA's Gen AI Capacity Study (Aug 2025) finds augmentation outweighs automation nationally; science-technici…

JSA's Gen AI Capacity Study (Aug 2025) finds augmentation outweighs automation nationally; science-technician roles (ANZSCO 3111 group) sit in the lower-exposure band for physical/labour tasks but share exposure on routine data-handling. Occupation-specific exposure is in JSA's occupation-data release. (Biological Technicians; lane L2, scholarly and grey-literature studies.)

Sources: [Jobs and Skills Australia, Our Gen AI Transition: Implications for Work and Skills](https://www.jobsandskills.gov.au/studies/generative-artificial-intelligence-capacity-study) (2025-08-14).

Scope: Whole-of-market finding; Biological Technician (ANZSCO 3111 group) specific score in JSA occupation-data release.

**Bearing:** not stated in the profession record.
