## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of International Education (International Baccalaureate) is a 100-point postgraduate specialist degree designed for educators and school leaders seeking professional accreditation with the International Baccalaureate Organization (IBO). Both gates pass unconditionally: G1 passes on an accredited specialist curriculum featuring staged prerequisite chains (Introduction to the IB gating curriculum frameworks, Assessment & Reporting gating advanced units, and Researching Education Practice gating the capstone); G2 passes on an independent AQF research capstone requiring defended methodological design decisions and authentic curriculum unit plans evaluated against IBO standards and practices.

Operating in a highly exposed domain (**92.44**, above the portfolio median of 90.9), the program demonstrates robust independent inquiry capability, achieving a maximum score in **C4: 3 / 3** (a staged prerequisite chain leading to a compulsory independent research project in EDUC90871 assessed via a Research Review, Research Poster, and 50% Final Research Report). However, Adaptiveness sits at **9 / 15** and Workplace Practice at **3 / 9** because the fully online structure includes zero assessed work-integrated learning or structured practicum (**W3: 0 / 3**), core digital content treats ICT operationally without assessed AI pedagogical governance (**C3: 1 / 3**), the capstone uses supplied datasets rather than requiring primary classroom inquiry conduct (**C5: 1 / 3**), presentations are delivered to internal online classes without external panels (**W1: 1 / 3**), assessed collaboration is limited to a single 10% paired presentation (**C1: 2 / 3**), research reviews lack documented student reliance logs on tools and sources (**C2: 2 / 3**), and authentic curriculum planning documents do not form the full assessment spine (**W2: 2 / 3**).
