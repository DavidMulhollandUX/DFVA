## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Cyber Security has no destination record of its own; job families rest on a field-of-education basis spanning seven distinct entry occupations (§1), so the plan below treats every graduate as potentially entering any one of them. G1 and G2 both pass — the compulsory specialist core and the simulated-cross-examination and hurdle-gated capstone tasks already establish disciplinary identity and defended decision-making under uncertainty. C4 (3/3) is already at the instrument ceiling through the required self-scoped Research or Capstone pathway.

The primary gap is workplace practice: W3 (0/3) has no work-situated learning of any kind, against a market where 'ai' is rising sharply across every job family sampled (§4) and the largest destination family's ad pool is both shrinking and skewing toward more experienced hires (Signal 1, §2). The remaining adaptiveness items (C1, C2, C3, C5) and the other two workplace items (W1, W2) each sit one level below their ceiling; the actions below target that single next level for each, at a documentation-only or single-task-revision cost before any structural change.
