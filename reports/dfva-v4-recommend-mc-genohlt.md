## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Genomics and Health carries a strong workplace sub-scale: W3 sits at the instrument ceiling (3/3) on the compulsory, year-long Genomics and Health Internship, which places every student under practitioner supervision with structured reflection and documented host accountability. G1 and G2 both pass on a coherent, staged specialist core and on real clinical decision-making under uncertainty. The adaptive sub-scale carries most of the addressable headroom: **7/15 on Adaptiveness** against **7/9 on Workplace**.

Three adaptive items sit one level above the floor (C3, C4, C5 all score 1/3), each capped by a construct the current core does not assess: C3 by tool-operation content with no assessed AI-limitations or governance component, C4 because the internship's cross-context evidence is reserved for W3 under the v4.1 migration note, and C5 because the only universally compulsory research-methods subject requires designing but not conducting a study — the stronger data-collection stream is optional. C1 and C2 each sit one level below their ceiling.
