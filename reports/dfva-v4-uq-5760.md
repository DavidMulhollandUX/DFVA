## 3. GATES — Basis: scored

- **G1 Disciplinary foundation:** PASS — Satisfies the PASS condition through a staged prerequisite chain and a coherent specialist core: the prescribed list is entirely public health coded, running from a foundations unit through epidemiology, biostatistics and research methods, and the second epidemiology unit names a completed prerequisite as a condition of enrolment. The program's own rules also refer to a set of core courses, so the content carries disciplinary identity rather than being generic or interchangeable.
- **G2 Decision-making under uncertainty:** PASS — Satisfies the PASS condition: assessment requires defended trade-off decisions rather than recall or scripted responses. The economic evaluation unit runs resource-allocation simulations of the trade-offs decision-makers face under limited resources and assesses a hurdle evaluation plan whose second part is a viva voce defence; the program-planning unit assesses a plan and logic model that must account for feasibility, risks and enablers; and a hurdle health risk assessment requires judgement under uncertainty about hazards and exposure.

## 6. EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE & LIMITATIONS — Basis: reported

**Panel A metadata.** Basis tier **field** (field grain), n = 0, 11 destination titles, 0 at medium mapping confidence. Index variant AIOE-2021; destination titles mapped to the published Felten index. Basis note: field-of-education occupation list (ATO-linked), not this program's own graduates

**Panel C provenance.** Scored from the 2026 handbook capture (27 pages) and verified on 2026-09-07: adversarial refutation of every level-3 score and every gate PASS (run), verbatim evidence check (run), and the construct-boundary pass.

**Ambiguities and construct-boundary notes** (8):

- Program-wide: the extract lists 26 prescribed courses for a 32-unit program, and the listed courses total well over 32 units, so which courses are compulsory core cannot be established from the extract. Every anchor above level 1 is worded in terms of core units or core assessment, so wherever a level depended on compulsoriness the lower level was taken.
- C2 between 2 and 3: 'Audit Trail of Qualitative Research and Learning' and 'Written personal meta-reflection' are process-focused titles that could carry the level-3 requirement to evidence strategy adjustment over time, but the extract documents no task requirement to that effect. R1 (score documented evidence, not plausible capability) resolved it to 2.
- C4 between 2 and 3: the Project courses are self-scoped and negotiated with a supervisor, which reads as level-3 structured progression toward independent learning, but enrolment requires coordinator permission and a minimum GPA of 5.5. The lower-level rule resolved it to 2.
- C5 between 2 and 3: PUBH7003 has students collect and analyse primary interview data, and PUBH7618 assesses a viva voce, but no single assessment combines required primary-evidence generation with methodology defended under scrutiny, and the Project route is gated. The lower-level rule resolved it to 2.
- W1 between 2 and 3: professional genres do recur across the prescribed units, which is half of the level-3 condition, but the external-audience half rests on the restricted placement and the optional consultancy course, and no assessed criterion of professional conduct or accountability is documented. The lower-level rule resolved it to 2.
- W2 between 2 and 3: practitioner artefacts recur widely enough to read as an assessment spine, but the level-3 genuine-constraint condition rests on the consultancy course's externally supplied problem, which is optional, and on 'real-world' overview prose that R4 forbids scoring. The lower-level rule resolved it to 2.
- W3 between 1 and 2: PUBH7044 is a supervised, assessed industry placement at 15 hours per week and PUBH7045 is an assessed live client consultancy, either of which would meet level 2 if it were core; the documented restriction (final semester only, restricted numbers, expression of interest and CV, coordinator approval) and the word 'opportunity' resolved it to 1.
- One construct, one home: the placement evidence (PUBH7044) is scored in W3 only, and is not counted toward C1 collaboration or C4 transfer; the consultancy course's client deliverable was considered for W1 and W2 but its optionality prevented it lifting either item.

**Standing v4 limitations.**

- Scores describe **documented curriculum intent**, not demonstrated graduate
  capability; the extrapolation warrant is constructive alignment, stated as an
  assumption (Kane [17]).
- Indigenous data governance is not scored as a distinct construct; C3 level 3
  counts it where taught (CARE Principles; Lodge et al. 2025 Principle 4 [[2]](https://www.acses.edu.au/publication/australian-framework-for-artificial-intelligence-in-higher-education/)).
- Perception/manipulation (Frey & Osborne's third bottleneck [[7]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.techfore.2016.08.019)) is unscored.
- A high W3 score describes curriculum design, not student welfare: required
  placements can disadvantage students who cannot afford unpaid work or
  relocation (Accord [[30]](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-universities-accord/resources/final-report); Fawns et al. [[24]](https://doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2024.2404634) on ableism and elitism).
- W2 is scored against each discipline's own criterion situation [[22]](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02504676), so
  cross-disciplinary W2 comparison is weaker than within-discipline comparison.
- W3 excludes simulation by design (simulation scores in W2), so programs whose
  professional norm is high-fidelity simulation may score low on W3 for a
  defensible pedagogic choice. Read the two sub-scores together.
- The authentic-assessment employability evidence rests largely on student
  self-report [[25]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stueduc.2021.101030); W2's warrant is documented design fidelity plus the
  assessment-drives-learning mechanism [26], not demonstrated employment effects.
- Work-integrated learning is NOT claimed to raise employment rates — Jackson &
  Collings [[28]](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-017-0216-z) found it does not. W3's warrant is skill development, employment
  relevance and professional identity formation [[29]](https://doi.org/10.1080/07294360.2016.1139551).
