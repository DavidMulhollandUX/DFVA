## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Medical Technology Innovation (MC-MTI) is built around four compulsory
BioDesign subjects totalling 75 of 100 credit points, including the 37.5-point BMEN90044
capstone, plus a 25-point elective basket. Curriculum adaptiveness sits at 7/15 and
workplace practice at 4/9. Both gates pass: the compulsory sequence carries a coherent
specialist core (G1), and BMEN90044's defended prototype-and-business-plan work under
real market, regulatory and IP uncertainty carries decision-making under uncertainty
(G2).

The two lowest scores are C2 (Hybrid metacognition, 0/3 — no assessment anywhere
appraises the quality of work, a student's, a peer's or a tool's) and W3 (Work-situated
learning, 0/3 — no placement, practicum or community-based project exists in either the
core or the elective list). C3 (Digital & AI literacy) sits at 1/3: AI content is
confined to two elective subjects a student can complete the degree without ever
choosing. C5 and W2 were scored conservatively where the evidence was genuinely
ambiguous, per the scoring-direction rule; W1's Level 2 placement rests on the same kind
of ambiguity (whether its Lay Person Oral Presentation audience is a real external
practitioner or an in-class exercise). None of the three has a market-evidence-supported
lever in this record, so none appears below. Separately, the course-level
graduate-attributes page returned content for an unrelated Master of Education subject,
so C1–C4 rest on subject-level assessment evidence only, not a verified course-level
outcomes statement.

A realistic outcome from P1–P4 alone — the four levers that need no host partnership or
credit-structure change — is an adaptiveness of 11 and a workplace practice of 5. Adding
P5 and P6 raises workplace practice to 7. The plan's ceiling is 11/15 and 7/9, not 15/15
and 9/9: C4, C5 and W1 carry no market-warranted lever in this record, so they stay
where they are scored.

Five categories of change would not move any score, and are recorded so that effort is
not directed toward them.
