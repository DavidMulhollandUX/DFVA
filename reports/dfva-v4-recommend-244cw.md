## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Four of the five items score 2 of 3 and both gates pass. The documented curriculum
records a 75-point compulsory core, three assessed methods subjects, recurring
team-based assessment, criterion-referenced appraisal, and a required capstone. One
item, C3 (Digital and AI literacy, including governance), scores 1 of 3: no core unit
addresses AI capabilities and limitations, no assessment requires a justified reliance
decision on machine-assisted output, and no team task specifies a role for AI tools.

C3 is treated as the first priority on two grounds recorded in the table below: it is
the only item two anchor levels below the maximum, and it is the only gap the market
evidence identifies independently.

| Item | Score | Levels below maximum | Market evidence for the gap | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C3 Digital & AI literacy, incl. governance | 1 / 3 | 2 | Direct — AI verification rising sharply; destinations average 89.46 exposure | 1 |
| C2 Hybrid metacognition & evaluative judgement | 2 / 3 | 1 | Direct — appraisal skill reported as lagging tool adoption | 2 |
| C1 Distributed cognition & relational capability | 2 / 3 | 1 | Indirect — co-design rising, solo drafting falling | 3 |
| C4 Life-long learning & transfer | 2 / 3 | 1 | Indirect — evaluation named as a scarce capability | 4 |
| C5 Inquiry & evidence generation | 2 / 3 | 1 | Direct — causal inference rising sharply | 5 |
| **Adaptiveness** | **9 / 15** | — | — | — |

flagged regardless of its effect on the adaptiveness score.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | C4 | 2 | 3 | +1 | 10 |

| P3 | C2 | 2 | 3 | +1 | 13 |

A realistic twelve-month outcome from P1, P2 and P4 alone is an adaptiveness of 12; the
plan's ceiling is 15.

Three categories of change would not move any score, and are recorded so that effort is
not directed toward them.

| Change | Why it does not move a score | Governing rule | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revision of intended learning outcomes alone | Rule R2 scores a capability that is stated but not assessed at level 1 in every item | Scoring rule R2 | [[8]](https://doi.org/10.1257/pandp.20181019) |
| A new elective addressing AI capability | The C3 anchors specify the compulsory core; the exposure in question attaches to destinations open to every graduate | C3 anchors, levels 2 and 3 | [[1]](https://www.teqsa.gov.au/guides-resources/resources/corporate-publications/assuring-quality-learning-gen-ai-integrated-future-role-adaptive-capabilities) |
| A new AI subject replacing core methods content | Would put gate G1 at risk while adding little beyond what P4 achieves within existing subjects | Gate G1 | [[6]](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjaa021) |

Re-scoring after implementation follows the harness without modification: capture the
new handbook vintage, score, verify adversarially, and publish the change. Because the
instrument is unchanged, v4-to-v4 comparison is valid; comparison with v3.1 values is
not [17][[18]](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08969-3).
