## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Engineering scores 5/15 on Adaptive capabilities and 2/9 on Workplace practice. Seven of the eight items sit at level 1 with two levels of headroom; Work-situated learning (W3) sits at level 0 with three levels of headroom, the largest single gap. Both gates — G1 Disciplinary foundation and G2 Decision-making under uncertainty — pass, so redesign options need only observe the constraints in Section 5, not repair a gate failure. The market report's dominant signal is state-based professional registration, mandatory or rolling out nationally from 6 March 2025, which makes a named individual legally answerable for engineering work regardless of what produced it (market report §3 Themes 2, 3, 5) — reflected below in the C2 and W1 actions.
