## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Two of eight items sit at zero or one out of three, and none is at ceiling. Work-situated learning (W3) scores zero: the extract documents no placement, practicum, internship or community-based project anywhere in the compulsory structure, and this is a pre-professional coursework cohort rather than one already practising, so the instrument's in-practice route does not apply either. Five further items (C1, C2, C3, W1, W2) sit at one of three for a related reason: collaboration, criterion-referenced appraisal, professional-genre communication and practice-based task design are all claimed in program-level outcomes but not assessed in any core subject, and the extract's only technology content is training in a statistical-software package rather than anything AI-specific.

The market report's job families point the same direction. Sales Managers is the largest destination by advertisement volume, and its recurring keywords — CRM and pipeline management, forecasting and target delivery, B2B/B2C relationship management, team leadership and coaching, commercial negotiation — are operational and relational skills the compulsory core does not yet assess (§1; §2 Signal 2; §4 skill shift, all rising). Market Research Analysts and Marketing Specialists sit inside a discipline the market report documents moving toward specialised AI-powered research platforms (§3 Theme 2), a shift the core's single AI-adjacent content item does not prepare graduates to govern. Office Clerks, General is a documented destination that the report itself flags as shrinking: Theme 6 (§3) names clerical, rules-based office work as the clearest automation-exposed group, a caution against treating administration as a stable skill rather than a curriculum gap (§4 skill shift, falling sharply).
