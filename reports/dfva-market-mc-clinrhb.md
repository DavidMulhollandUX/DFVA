## 5. CURRICULUM IMPLICATIONS

| # | Implication | Dimension | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| CI-1 | NHMRC's applicable-laws guidance (Theme 1) binds researchers conducting GMO-related biological research to named statutory instruments, and "research" and "biology" recur across the 310-posting advertisement sample for the family this program's field feeds (Signal 1). An inquiry task that never asks students to name the governing instrument doesn't demonstrate this compliance context. | C3 | Add a core assessment item requiring students to identify and justify which regulatory instrument governs a given research design (cost: new assessment criterion and marking guide). |
| CI-2 | NATA's ISO/IEC 17025 Life Sciences annex (Theme 2) sets an accreditation standard that "many regulators and clients require," and "laboratory" recurs in the same advertisement sample (Signal 1). A capstone inquiry task marked only against internal criteria doesn't show whether it meets that external competence standard. | C5, W2 | Benchmark the capstone inquiry task's rubric against the ISO/IEC 17025 Life Sciences competence elements (cost: rubric redesign, no added credit points). |
| CI-3 | ASORC's self-regulation model requires an accredited qualification plus at least 20 continuing-development hours a year (Theme 3), and rehabilitation counsellors coordinate return-to-work under government programs such as Disability Employment Services (Theme 4). Work-situated learning that isn't mapped to that accreditation pathway leaves the CPD requirement unaddressed by the program. | W1, W3 | Map placement hours and reflective-practice assessment against ASORC's continuing-development categories (cost: placement-coordinator time to document the mapping). |
| CI-4 | 2025 evidence names clerical and secretarial work as the clearest automation-exposed group in this program's job-family map (Theme 6), while Office Clerks, General still carries 122 live postings (Signal 3). Core content that overlaps with routine administrative tasks trains toward the family's most exposed segment rather than away from it. | C4 | Audit core units for assessment overlap with routine administrative or clerical tasks and substitute a novel-context judgement task where found (cost: curriculum-review time only). |
