## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Music (Orchestral Performance) (MC-MUSORP) is a graduate performance degree built around a staged, largely compulsory Orchestral Instrument Performance sequence and an orchestral-audition preparation core. Both gates pass unconditionally: Gate G1 on 100 compulsory points in Year 1 and 62.5 compulsory points in Year 2, staged and explicitly building on prior subjects toward a named capstone; Gate G2 on assessed live performance under real audition pressure and participation on real audition panels making complex artistic decisions.

Set against a field-of-education destination basis estimated at 70.7 — the program carries no destination record of its own — it shows strong authentic task design (**W2: 3 / 3**) built on its solo-concerto and orchestra-audition assessment spine, alongside collaborative ensemble practice (**C1: 2 / 3**) and structured self-appraisal (**C2: 2 / 3**). It carries no assessed digital or AI content anywhere in the core (**C3: 0 / 3**), work-situated learning sits only in an elective practicum (**W3: 1 / 3**), and inquiry training is limited to a single literature-review task (**C5: 1 / 3**). Adaptive capabilities sit at **7 / 15** and workplace practice at **6 / 9**.
