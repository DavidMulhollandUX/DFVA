## 2. RECENT JOB AD SIGNALS

**Signal 1 — Infrastructure Big Build and consulting demand in VIC and NSW.**
Major consultancies (Arup, WSP, Aurecon, Jacobs, AECOM) actively recruit structural engineering specialists for major infrastructure projects (Suburban Rail Loop, Metro West, regional bridge upgrades).

**Signal 2 — 3D BIM and computational scripting becoming baseline requirements.**
Firms mandate proficiency in Autodesk Revit, Tekla, and computational modeling scripts over legacy 2D drafting.

**Signal 3 — Embodied carbon and sustainable materials selection.**
Structural engineering employers increasingly require knowledge of low-carbon concrete, mass timber (CLT), and whole-of-life lifecycle carbon assessments.

**Signal 4 — Premium on structural integrity verification and engineering judgement.**
As automated structural sizing algorithms proliferate, engineering directors prioritize engineers who can perform hand-calculation reality checks and defend design assumptions under code compliance audits.

## 3. CURRENT DISCUSSION SIGNALS — MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

**What these sources are.** Engineers Australia ([EA](https://www.engineersaustralia.org.au/)), Australian Steel Institute ([ASI](https://www.steel.org.au/)), Concrete Institute of Australia ([CIA](https://concreteinstitute.com.au/)), and peer-reviewed structural engineering scholarship ([Journal of Structural Engineering](https://ascelibrary.org/journal/jsendh), [February 2025](https://ascelibrary.org/journal/jsendh)). Direct extraction from X or LinkedIn was **not** performed and no social media forum was sampled — where this section refers to discourse, it denotes published engineering practice standards, Engineers Australia competency frameworks, and documented technological reviews by named bodies and authors. Dates are stated where available.

### Theme 1 — algorithmic structural sizing tools vs physical engineering verification

Reports from [Engineers Australia](https://www.engineersaustralia.org.au/) (2024/2025) and the *Journal of Structural Engineering* (February 2025) emphasize that while automated FEA software and generative structural design tools rapidly generate member sizes, black-box algorithms frequently obscure non-standard boundary conditions and non-linear bucking modes. Structural engineers maintain statutory liability and must exercise rigorous evaluative judgement to audit software outputs with independent hand calculations.

**Bearing:** C2, C3, and W2.

### Theme 2 — multidisciplinary engineering collaboration in digital delivery

Guidelines from [ASI](https://www.steel.org.au/) and [CIA](https://concreteinstitute.com.au/) (November 2024) underline that modern infrastructure design requires complex collaboration across structural engineers, geotechnical consultants, MEP teams, and construction contractors within federated digital models. Human communication and contractual negotiation are completely insulated from automation.

**Bearing:** C1, W1, and W3.

### Theme 3 — primary empirical evidence and defended structural testing

Reports from [Engineers Australia](https://www.engineersaustralia.org.au/) (November 2024) and the [Journal of Structural Engineering](https://ascelibrary.org/journal/jsendh) highlight that high-performance structural systems (mass timber, modular steel) require engineers who can design empirical physical tests, interpret laboratory sensor data, and defend their design methodologies under peer review.

**Bearing:** C5, C4, and G1.

## 4. SKILL SHIFT SUMMARY

### Declining Demand
- 2D AutoCAD drafting without 3D model integration
- Routine manual member sizing without computational optimization
- Prescriptive single-code compliance without performance-based structural analysis

### Rising Demand
- Evaluative verification and audit of automated finite element analysis (FEA) software
- Performance-based earthquake and wind engineering for complex geometry
- Low-carbon material design (mass timber, geopolymer concrete) and embodied carbon auditing
- Interprofessional coordination across digital BIM and construction environments
