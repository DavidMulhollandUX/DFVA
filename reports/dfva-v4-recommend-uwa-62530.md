## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This document argues from evidence; it is not itself evidence. UWA's Master of Data Science passes both gates: G1, on its ACS- and Seoul Accord-benchmarked staged prerequisite chain with a compulsory 30-point specialist group and a 66-point capstone gate, and G2, on the capstone's assessed scope, effort and cost trade-off decisions taken under milestone accountability to a mentor or client. Neither gate constrains the redesign below; both need only to be preserved, not created.

AI exposure rests on a field-of-education basis (81.84, field grain, estimated) — the destinations are the whole Computer Science, n.e.c. field's, not this program's own graduates — so it is sound to say the field is highly exposed and no more. The program holds broad headroom on both sub-scales: 7 of 15 on Adaptiveness (currently 8/15) and 5 of 9 on Workplace (currently 4/9). C2 (metacognition), C3 (AI literacy and governance), W1 (professional communication) and W3 (work-situated learning) sit at level 1 with the widest headroom; C1, C4, C5 and W2 already clear level 2 through the Data Science Capstone Project or the Data and Information Technologies Research Project, and hold headroom only to level 3.
