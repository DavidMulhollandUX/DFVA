## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The methodological spine of this degree is strong and both gates pass on it. Seventy-five
credit points of all-compulsory discipline core run Epidemiology 1 → Epidemiology 2 →
Epidemiology in Practice alongside Biostatistics → Regression Methods → Survival Analysis,
terminating in a mandatory 25–50 point research project taken over two consecutive
semesters at the end of the degree. "Working with uncertainty" is a named generic skill of
the two most advanced core subjects; students construct causal diagrams, assess confounding
and collider bias, quantify bias direction and magnitude, and judge effect-measure
modification against external validity. C4 sits at the ceiling on that structure, and C5 is
one conjunct short of it.

Everything relational, communicative or situated sits at level 1. C1, C2, C3, W1, W2 and W3
are all at 1, and in five of the six cases for the same reason: the capability is claimed in
course outcomes or generic skills, or it exists in the surrounding elective and
professional-skills lists, but it is not assessed in the core. Group projects live in
COMP90089, GEOM90006 and GEOM90008; machine learning lives in COMP90089; ethics in science
lives in SCIE90005, which is marked not available in 2026; communication lives in SCIE90012
and SCIE90013; work placement lives in SCIE90017. The core's own digital content is Stata
and Excel operation, its communication is tutorial oral assessment to the teaching team, and
its written artefacts are short-answer assignments, case-study responses, academic reports
and examinations. The distance to the maximum is therefore two levels on six of the eight
items, but the missing conjunct is usually narrow, named, and already half-built inside a
core subject that exists. The market report makes the direction unambiguous: the fastest
emerging job family it identifies — AI validation epidemiologist and algorithmic fairness
analyst — is this program's own methodology turned onto AI systems, and the program teaches
the method without teaching the object.

Priority below ranks items by the weight of market evidence against the gap. §4 orders the
same interventions by cost and sequence, which is a different ordering.

P12 and P13 are justified by skill development, employment relevance and the
pre-professional identity formed through participation in a community of practice
[[29]](https://doi.org/10.1080/07294360.2016.1139551), not by employment outcomes: work-integrated learning was not found to raise
full-time employment rates [[28]](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-017-0216-z). Both carry a declared equity cost — unpaid or
relocation-dependent placement is the mechanism behind placement poverty
[[30]](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-universities-accord/resources/final-report) — and a placement-capacity risk that a 12.5-point host commitment shares
with every other program competing for the same surveillance units. The instrument's
own-practice route [[35]](https://doi.org/10.1080/03075070903216635) would be cheaper, since it assesses activity conducted in the
student's own workplace, but it is available only to a cohort documented as already
practising, and the scoring recorded that this cohort is not. Taking that route would
require documenting the cohort's concurrent employment first, which the captured evidence
does not do.

The plan reaching both instrument maxima is a consequence of the starting position — six of
eight items are two steps below the maximum — not a claim that all thirteen levers are
achievable. The realistic near-term subset is much smaller and much cheaper: P1 through P3
are low or low–medium effort, all three are assessment-brief edits inside subjects that
already exist, none requires a course-design decision, and together they reach an
adaptiveness of 10 and a workplace practice of 4 within a single teaching year. P4 is the
hinge for everything beyond that — the second steps on C1, C2 and C3 are all conditional on
AI content existing in the core at all — and the workplace sub-scale beyond 5 depends on
decisions about external question pipelines (P11) and host placement capacity (P12, P13)
that sit above subject level.

Four categories of change would not move any score, and are recorded so that effort is not
directed toward them.

Two dependencies bind the sequence and are worth stating plainly. P7 and P8 are the two
highest-value interventions against the market evidence — they are what the "epidemiologist
as AI auditor" job family actually asks for — and neither is reachable until P4 puts AI
content into the compulsory core. And P1 and P2 are the cheapest levers in the plan: the
policy-recommendation work and the systematic review already happen, and what is missing in
each case is a published criteria set and a named audience.

Re-scoring after implementation follows the harness without modification: capture the new
handbook vintage, score, verify adversarially, and publish the change. Because the instrument
is unchanged, v4-to-v4 comparison is valid; comparison with earlier instrument versions is
not.
