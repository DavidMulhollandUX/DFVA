## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Both gates pass. The gaps split into two families. Three items — C3, W3 and, less
sharply, W2 — share the same shape: the program already has the relevant content
(MGMT90267 "Artificial Intelligence in Organisations"; the three placement subjects,
Business and Economics Internship, Global Business Practicum and Business Practicum),
but it sits in the program's single free-elective slot rather than in the compulsory
core the anchors require. C1, C5 and W1 are each one step short on a narrower clause —
tool-allocation documentation, a defended methodology review, and a documented external
judge — layered onto assessment that otherwise already exists.

C2 is the outlier. It scores 1 of 3, and the extract's only documented reflective
assessment (MGMT90229's written assignments on personal entrepreneurial abilities,
ethical decision-making and becoming an entrepreneur) is real and is marked, but the
evidence record finds no documented criterion-referenced appraisal in it. It has a real
gap and no market lever: neither a discussion-signal theme nor the job family map in
reports/dfva-market-mc-entrpsp.md names C2, so no intervention on it appears in this
plan — see the note at the end of §6.

One further note belongs here rather than in a table. This plan recommends no
intervention on C2 (Hybrid metacognition & evaluative judgement), which scores 1 of 3 —
the same distance below maximum as C3 and W3. The instrument's market-warranted rule
requires that every intervention name the skill-shift row or signal that makes it worth
doing, and neither a §3 discussion-signal theme nor the §1 job family map in
reports/dfva-market-mc-entrpsp.md names C2. Recommending a curriculum change here on
the strength of the general evaluative-judgement literature alone [[4]](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-017-0220-3),
without a market signal specific to this program's occupation, would violate that rule.
If a future revision of the market report finds evidence bearing on C2 — for instance,
founder discourse on judging AI-drafted business-model outputs — this plan should be
revisited rather than treated as final on that item.

Re-scoring after implementation follows the harness without modification: capture the
new handbook vintage, score, verify adversarially, and publish the change. Because the
instrument is unchanged, v4-to-v4 comparison is valid; comparison with v3.1 values is
not [17][[18]](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08969-3).
