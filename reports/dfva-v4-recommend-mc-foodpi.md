## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Both gates pass. G1 rests on an all-compulsory 87.5-point seven-subject core spanning
food processing, packaging materials and design, and innovation management. G2 rests on
the Minor Project's hurdle-gated proposal and defended final report, and on the
Internship's 200-hour placement with a host-supervisor assessment report. The core is
otherwise thin on adaptive capability: C3 (Digital & AI literacy) scores 0/3 — no core
unit or program-level outcome names AI or digital content at all — and C4 (Life-long
learning & transfer) scores 1/3 after an evidence-attribution review demoted it from 2:
its strongest evidence lines (the FOOD90031 product-launch task and FOOD90032 design
brief) are cited verbatim under W1 and W2, and the single-attribution rule assigns them
there, not to C4's cross-context-application claim.

Adaptive capabilities sit at 7/15, workplace practice at 6/9. A structural pattern runs
through four of the eight items: the course's "Internship (FOOD90036–38) OR Minor
Project (AGRI90070/79/81)" requirement means C4, C5, W1 and W3 are each capped at the
lower of the two levels their alternative routes would separately support, per the
rubric's scoring-direction rule and the evidence file's own ambiguities note. Closing
that structural gap — making the stronger route's element common to both — recurs as
the mechanism behind several interventions below.
