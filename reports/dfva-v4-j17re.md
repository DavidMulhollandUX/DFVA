## 3. GATES — Basis: scored

- **G1 Disciplinary foundation:** FAIL — Extract documents only generic HDR candidature administration (seminar attendance, residency minimum, individual supervision, periodic progress review), none of it specialist Social Work content. The two named coursework subjects that would carry disciplinary sequencing are referenced but their content is not in this extract, so no accredited sequence, staged prerequisite chain, or compulsory specialist core with progressive depth is documented.

**G1 FAILS.** A disciplinary-foundation failure is reported regardless of the adaptiveness score: the adaptive capabilities are scored on top of a foundation this program is not documented to establish.

## 6. EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE & LIMITATIONS — Basis: reported

**Panel A metadata.** Basis tier **cognate** (cognate program), n = 203, 15 destination titles, 12 at medium mapping confidence. Index variant AIOE-2021; destination titles mapped to the published Felten index. Basis note: Advanced Social Work is the post-qualifying version of the MSW.

**Panel C provenance.** Scored from the 2026 handbook capture (7 pages) and verified on 2026-08-29: adversarial refutation of every level-3 score and every gate PASS (run), verbatim evidence check (run), and the construct-boundary pass.

**Ambiguities and construct-boundary notes** (4):

- C5: evidence is consistent with level 2 (methodology selection assessed) and level 3 (primary evidence REQUIRED, defended under scrutiny) — extract never states primary-evidence/data-collection is required and describes only written external examination plus progress review, not a viva/defence; resolved to the lower level (2).
- W2: verification demoted the initial level-2 score to level 1 — the thesis is externally examined against academic/scholarly standards, but no documented feature (client contact, fieldwork, practice-derived criteria) establishes social-work practice fidelity rather than academic research fidelity; the 'examined externally' evidence was already load-bearing for C4/C5 and its reuse for W2 without a disclosed ambiguity was the basis for the demotion.
- C4 and C5 both draw on the same thesis evidence (self-scoped research project vs methodology/primary-evidence generation) but are scored independently per their own construct definitions, not double-counted.
- The course-structure page references 'two coursework subjects' whose own pages are not included in this extract. Items scored 0 (C1, C2, C3, W1, W3) reflect the absence of qualifying evidence in the supplied overview, outcomes, course-structure and Thesis(FT)/(PT) pages only — not a confirmed absence across the full course, since the coursework-subject content is unseen.

**Standing v4 limitations.**

- Scores describe **documented curriculum intent**, not demonstrated graduate
  capability; the extrapolation warrant is constructive alignment, stated as an
  assumption (Kane [17]).
- Indigenous data governance is not scored as a distinct construct; C3 level 3
  counts it where taught (CARE Principles; Lodge et al. 2025 Principle 4 [[2]](https://www.acses.edu.au/publication/australian-framework-for-artificial-intelligence-in-higher-education/)).
- Perception/manipulation (Frey & Osborne's third bottleneck [[7]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.techfore.2016.08.019)) is unscored.
- A high W3 score describes curriculum design, not student welfare: required
  placements can disadvantage students who cannot afford unpaid work or
  relocation (Accord [[30]](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-universities-accord/resources/final-report); Fawns et al. [[24]](https://doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2024.2404634) on ableism and elitism).
- W2 is scored against each discipline's own criterion situation [[22]](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02504676), so
  cross-disciplinary W2 comparison is weaker than within-discipline comparison.
- W3 excludes simulation by design (simulation scores in W2), so programs whose
  professional norm is high-fidelity simulation may score low on W3 for a
  defensible pedagogic choice. Read the two sub-scores together.
- The authentic-assessment employability evidence rests largely on student
  self-report [[25]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stueduc.2021.101030); W2's warrant is documented design fidelity plus the
  assessment-drives-learning mechanism [26], not demonstrated employment effects.
- Work-integrated learning is NOT claimed to raise employment rates — Jackson &
  Collings [[28]](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-017-0216-z) found it does not. W3's warrant is skill development, employment
  relevance and professional identity formation [[29]](https://doi.org/10.1080/07294360.2016.1139551).
