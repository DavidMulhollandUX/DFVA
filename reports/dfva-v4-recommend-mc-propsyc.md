## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Professional Psychology is a 100-point APAC-accredited postgraduate professional degree structured around the 5+1 provisional psychologist registration pathway governed by the Psychology Board of Australia (PsyBA / AHPRA). Both gates pass unconditionally: G1 passes on an all-compulsory specialist core taken in a mandated sequence with staged progression hurdles and APAC professional accreditation; G2 passes on defended trade-off decisions under genuine clinical uncertainty, evaluated across clinical case formulations, suicide risk assessment rationales referencing empirical literature, and real patient case-work management.

Operating in a low AI exposure domain (**44.92**, well below the portfolio median of 90.9), the program demonstrates exceptional clinical task fidelity and independent research rigor, achieving maximum scores in **C4: 3 / 3** (a compulsory 25cp year-long minor research thesis opened by a hurdle proposal and culminating in a 6,000-word independent inquiry) and **W2: 3 / 3** (a comprehensive authentic task spine across all stages featuring hurdle clinical interview role-plays, mental state examinations, risk assessment reports, and single-case study formulations aligned to the PsyBA National Psychology Examination). However, Adaptiveness sits at **10 / 15** and Workplace Practice at **7 / 9** because digital tools focus on software operation and telehealth essays without assessed clinical AI governance (**C3: 1 / 3**), collaborative practice lacks human-plus-AI role allocation (**C1: 2 / 3**), critical appraisal lacks longitudinal reliance-or-override logs (**C2: 2 / 3**), minor research allows secondary datasets alongside primary empirical investigation (**C5: 2 / 3**), oral clinical demonstrations are delivered internally without external practitioner marker panels (**W1: 2 / 3**), and core placement hours combine simulation and actual client contact without disaggregated host accountability (**W3: 2 / 3**).
