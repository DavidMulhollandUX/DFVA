## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Chemical Engineering is an EA- and IChemE-accredited professional degree preparing chemical process engineers, plant safety specialists, clean energy transition engineers, and extractive metallurgists for top-tier energy, resources, and biopharma corporations (Woodside, BHP, Rio Tinto, Orica, CSL, Viva Energy). Both gates pass cleanly: G1 passes across an extensive 225-point compulsory core covering transport phenomena, reactor design, separation, process control, and plant design; G2 passes on defended multi-variable optimization, HAZOP failure trade-offs, and Environmental Effects Statements.

Operating in a moderate AI exposure domain (**89.78**, below the portfolio median of 90.9), the program exhibits solid Workplace Practice (**5 / 9**) and targeted headroom on Adaptiveness (**7 / 15**). Workplace practice is carried by the 25-point capstone CHEN90022 reproducing complete industrial plant design (W2: 2/3), multiple professional genres including EES and oral feasibility presentations (W1: 2/3), and an available internship selective (W3: 1/3). Adaptiveness is anchored by collaborative design projects with individual component pass hurdles (C1: 2/3) and complex whole-facility plant modeling (C4: 2/3), but C2 (1/3), C3 (1/3), and C5 (1/3) are restricted because process simulation is taught at the software operation level without assessed AI optimization limits, evaluative appraisal lacks criterion-referenced peer review or software override logs, and primary experimental data collection is not required in the coursework core.
