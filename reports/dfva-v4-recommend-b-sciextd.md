## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Bachelor of Science (Extended) is a shared first-year science foundation, and the foundation-year extract does not yet document a coherent specialist core (G1 fails) or an assessed decision task with genuine uncertainty and accountability (G2 fails); both gates flag the program regardless of the following item scores. Destination AI exposure rests on a pooled basis (82.72), not this program's own graduates, so it is sound enough to say the destinations are highly exposed and no more.

The program holds broad headroom on both sub-scales: 7 / 15 on Adaptiveness and 3 / 9 on Workplace. C2 and C3 (1/3 each) are the largest adaptive gaps: the extract's routine-office-task destinations (administration, clerical, data entry) are falling sharply, and its geoscience and teaching destinations sit in an augmentation-favoured band, both of which call for evaluative judgement and discipline-embedded AI literacy the extract does not yet assess. On workplace practice, W1, W2 and W3 (each 1/3) reflect that professional communication, authentic task artefacts and workplace exposure all remain at an introductory or unassessed level across the four named entry occupations.
