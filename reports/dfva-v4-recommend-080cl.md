## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Four of the eight items are already at the maximum anchor level and both gates pass.
The documented curriculum records an all-compulsory, APAC-accredited sequence, an
assessed literature-review-to-research-proposal progression that scopes the PhD, a
required thesis defended at three separate points, professional-genre assessment judged
by registered supervisors, and 125 days of required, supervised, assessed placement.
The workplace sub-scale sits one point below its ceiling.

The gap is concentrated and it is a single gap wearing two names. C3 (digital and AI
literacy, including governance) and C2 (hybrid metacognition and evaluative judgement)
each score 1 of 3. The extract contains no occurrence of AI, algorithms or data
governance in any subject; the only technology content is 4.5 hours of training in
administering and scoring neurocognitive tests, which is the tool-operation level the
anchor names as non-durable. C2 fails for an adjacent reason: the criterion language
about evaluating the quality of psychological data sits in PSYC90030's intended
learning outcomes and generic skills, and no assessment table anywhere in the extract
documents peer review, structured critique, a portfolio with standards or marking
against exemplars. The program teaches students to produce clinical judgements to a
professional standard; it does not yet assess them appraising judgements — their own,
a colleague's, or a machine's — against stated criteria.

Both gates pass and function as regression checks: a redesign that breaks either is
flagged regardless of its effect on the adaptiveness score. G1 binds unusually hard
here, because the accredited sequence is also the registration pathway.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 Disciplinary foundation | PASS | All subjects compulsory; the sequence is APAC-accredited as the required sequence for generalist registration with clinical-specialisation competency; staged year by year from introductory assessment and basic interventions through advanced psychopathology and advanced practice to the PhD | Every lever in §4 places its content inside an existing accredited subject. Displacing accredited content to make room for a standalone AI subject would put both this gate and the registration pathway at risk, and would add nothing P2 and P6 do not achieve within PSYC90030 and PSYC90008. | [[1]](https://www.teqsa.gov.au/guides-resources/resources/corporate-publications/assuring-quality-learning-gen-ai-integrated-future-role-adaptive-capabilities)[[6]](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjaa021) |

## 6. WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE SCORE — Basis: inferred

If implemented and documented in the handbook, the interventions correspond to the
following changes. The two sub-scales are tracked separately and are never added.

| Lever | Item | From | To | Change | Cumulative adaptive | Cumulative workplace |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | C2 | 1 | 2 | +1 | 11 | 8 |
| P2 | C3 | 1 | 2 | +1 | 12 | 8 |
| P3 | C2 | 2 | 3 | +1 | 13 | 8 |
| P4 | W2 | 2 | 3 | +1 | 13 | 9 |
| P5 | C1 | 2 | 3 | +1 | 14 | 9 |
| P6 | C3 | 2 | 3 | +1 | 15 | 9 |

A plan that moved adaptiveness to 15 and left workplace practice at 8 has done
something different from a plan that moved both, and a single total would hide which.

Unusually, the full ceiling on both sub-scales is reachable by curriculum action, because
four of the eight items are already at maximum and the workplace gap is a single task
redesign rather than a placement-capacity decision. A realistic twelve-month outcome from
P1, P2 and P3 — all inside existing subjects, none requiring an external agreement or a
course-design decision — is an adaptiveness of 13 and a workplace practice of 8. P4
depends on a referring-service agreement and P5 on restructuring a subject's assessment,
so both sit beyond that horizon.

Five categories of change would not move any score, and are recorded so that effort is
not directed toward them.

| Change | Why it does not move a score | Governing rule | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revising intended learning outcomes or generic skills to name AI literacy, evaluative judgement or teamwork | This is exactly the failure that produced the C2 score of 1: the criterion language already sits in PSYC90030's outcomes and generic skills and in PSYC90009's skills outcome, and scores level 1, not level 2. Rule R2 scores a capability that is stated but not assessed at level 1 in every item. | Scoring rule R2 | [[8]](https://doi.org/10.1257/pandp.20181019)[33] |
| Describing the existing role plays, simulated client interactions or supplied case material as "authentic", "real-world" or "industry-relevant" | Rule R4 scores documented task features only. A W2 step requires a change to the task, the artefact produced, or the criteria applied — which is why P4 changes the source of the case and the standard it is judged against, not its label. | Scoring rule R4 | [[24]](https://doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2024.2404634)[[22]](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02504676) |
| Adding AI content as an elective, an optional workshop, or an unassessed training module on the pattern of the existing 4.5-hour test-administration training or the online risk module | The C3 anchors at levels 2 and 3 require core units and core *assessment*. An unassessed module is the tool-operation level the level 1 anchor already identifies as non-durable; an elective would not reach the core requirement even if the program had electives, and it does not — all subjects are compulsory. | C3 anchors, levels 2 and 3 | [[1]](https://www.teqsa.gov.au/guides-resources/resources/corporate-publications/assuring-quality-learning-gen-ai-integrated-future-role-adaptive-capabilities)[13] |
| Extending placement days, adding a fourth placement, or citing placement volume as evidence of employability | W3 is already at level 3, so no additional placement moves the score. Any future placement change should be argued on skill development, employment relevance and professional identity formation [[29]](https://doi.org/10.1080/07294360.2016.1139551), and must account for the equity cost of unpaid and relocation-dependent placement [[30]](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-universities-accord/resources/final-report). | W3 anchors at ceiling; W3 honesty rule | [[28]](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-017-0216-z)[[29]](https://doi.org/10.1080/07294360.2016.1139551)[[30]](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-universities-accord/resources/final-report) |
| Adding a standalone AI subject in place of accredited content | Puts gate G1 and the registration pathway at risk while adding nothing P2 and P6 do not achieve inside PSYC90030 and PSYC90008. | Gate G1 | [[6]](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjaa021) |

Re-scoring after implementation follows the harness without modification: capture the
new handbook vintage, score the eight items, verify adversarially, and publish the
change. Because the instrument is unchanged, v4-to-v4 comparison is valid; comparison
with v3.1 values is not [17][[18]](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-08969-3).
