## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Panel C v4 scores this program's adaptive capabilities at 10/15 and its workplace practice at 8/9. The remaining gaps concentrate in AI governance (C3) and in extending existing collaborative and evaluative-judgement assessment (C1, C2) to cover AI-assisted decision support explicitly — pressures the market evidence in the diagnostic table names directly. Workplace practice, authentic task design and life-long learning already sit at the ceiling through the program's Integrated Professional Practice hours and the negotiated Health Innovation & Improvement Project.
