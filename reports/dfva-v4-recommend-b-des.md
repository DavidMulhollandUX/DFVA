## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the scored evidence and market data above; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

All eight items score 1 of 3 and both gates pass. Two defects, recorded in the
verified scoring and applied consistently across the panel, produce that result. The
first is structural: the captured course structure documents no compulsory subject at
all — its requirements are credit-point minima by level plus completion of one to two
prescribed majors — and all sixteen captured subject pages sit beneath an Electives
heading. Five items (C1, C2, C4, W1, W2) carry anchors that require core placement, so
each is capped at 1 whatever the elective evidence shows. The second is documentary:
no assessment criteria, rubrics, exemplars or peer review appear for any task in any
captured subject, which independently defeats the level-2 anchors of C2, W1 and W2.

The underlying activity is stronger than the score. Assessed group work, a 40 per cent
hurdle oral presentation, a 20-minute technical interview for a named professional
role, studio critique points, assessed site-observation and field-study data
collection, and a cross-context planning transfer task are all documented — they are
simply documented as electives, without criteria. Two consequences follow. Publishing
the prescribed majors' compulsory subject lists and the assessment criteria already in
use is the first and cheapest lever, and C5 is the one adaptive item whose level-2
anchor carries no core clause, so it can be moved before the structural question is
settled.

The two columns are tracked separately and are never added: a plan that moved
adaptiveness to 11 and left workplace practice at 3 has done something different from a
plan that moved both, and a single total would hide which.

P1 is recorded at zero because publishing does not by itself change what is assessed. It
may nonetheless change the score, in either direction: if the prescribed majors already
require the group work, the transfer task and the professional-genre presentation, then
C1, C4 and W1 re-score on evidence that exists today and P6, P7 and part of P3 fall away.
If they do not, the structural work stands as written. A re-score decides this; this plan
does not assume it. A realistic twelve-month outcome from P1, P2, P3 and P4 — all low or
medium effort, none requiring a course-design decision — is an adaptiveness of 7 and a
workplace practice of 4. The plan's ceiling is 11 and 6; the level-3 anchors on C1, C2,
C4, C5, W1, W2 and W3 are excluded because each presupposes a documented level-2 state
that does not yet exist.

Four categories of change would not move any score, and are recorded so that effort is
not directed toward them.
