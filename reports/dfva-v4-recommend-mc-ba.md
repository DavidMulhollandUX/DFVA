## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Melbourne Business School Master of Business Administration (MBA) prepares executive leaders, management consultants, and general managers across ASX100 corporates, global consultancies, and high-growth ventures. Both gates pass cleanly: G1 passes on an all-compulsory 150-credit-point specialist core (67% of 225 points) staged across foundational economics, quantitative analytics, and functional disciplines; G2 passes on live client problem-solving and trade-off analysis under market uncertainty in the core Immersive Business Experience and Data Analytics units.

Operating above the portfolio median exposure (**91.73**), the program exhibits considerable headroom on Adaptiveness (**7 / 15**) alongside moderate workplace grounding (**5 / 9**). The adaptiveness gap spans C2 (1/3), C3 (1/3), and C5 (1/3): while core coursework features extensive syndicate assignments (C1: 2/3), it leaves generative AI strategy and AI transformation leadership in the elective list, assesses generic reflection without criterion-referenced peer review or AI reliance logs, and assesses quantitative modeling without primary data collection. In workplace practice, W1 (2/3) and W2 (2/3) reflect real client engagement in the Immersive Business Experience, but W3 (1/3) is constrained because formal workplace internships sit exclusively in the elective pool.
