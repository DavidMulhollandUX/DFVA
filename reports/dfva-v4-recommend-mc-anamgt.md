## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Analytics Management runs an AACSB-accredited core moving from foundation management through analytics foundations to a specialised applied capstone, the Applied Analytics Lab. G1 passes on that staged, all-compulsory specialist sequence; G2 passes on the Applied Analytics Lab's live industry-data project — a report and presentation carrying an 80% hurdle — plus syndicate case work in Operations and Marketing.

Operating on an estimated exposure of 90.73 on a partial destination basis (§1: the program has no destination record of its own, so job families rest on the field-of-education grain), the program shows headroom on both sub-scales: 8/15 on Adaptiveness and 4/9 on Workplace. The clearest deficit is W3 (0/3): no core unit places students in a supervised workplace or professional-community setting, and the Applied Analytics Lab's industry-data project carries no documented practitioner supervision or accountability to a host, so it resolves as W2 rather than W3. C2 and C5 (1/3 each) show unassessed reflection and a graduate-attribute-level research claim without criterion-referenced appraisal or a required primary-evidence project. C1, C3, C4, W1 and W2 each sit one level below maximum, with collaborative practice, AI/analytics literacy, transfer, professional communication and authentic task design all assessed but not yet reaching their level-3 anchors.
