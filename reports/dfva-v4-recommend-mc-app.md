## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Master of Applied Positive Psychology (MC-APP) runs an all-compulsory, discipline-specific core with a documented sequencing rule and a staged prerequisite chain into its Research Capstone. G1 passes on that coherent specialist core; G2 passes on the individually defended Interactive Oral Assessment and both capstones' defended project design with real-world implications.

Operating on an estimated field-of-education exposure of 89.85, drawn from the Jobs and Skills Australia Higher Education Outcomes (JSA HEO) destinations for field 090701 Psychology rather than this program's own graduates, the program shows headroom on both sub-scales: 10/15 on Adaptiveness and 2/9 on Workplace. The clearest deficit is W3 (0/3): the extract documents the complete course structure and no placement, practicum, live-client or community-based work appears anywhere in it. C3, W1 and W2 each sit one level above the floor, with a documented but under-specified line short of the next anchor. C1, C2 and C5 each sit one level below maximum, with existing collaborative, reflective and research-methods assessment not yet reaching the level-3 bar. C4 already scores at the maximum (3/3) — both capstone routes' self-scoped, staged progression is the program's strongest feature and needs no action.
