## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

Master of Counselling scores 6/15 on adaptive capabilities and 8/9 on workplace practice. The clearest gap sits at the floor: AI literacy and governance (C3), which scores 0 while the market has already moved — Theme 4 (§3) records GenAI use increasing practitioner-reported competence in the field's own asynchronous-therapy practice mode, and the Privacy Act's automated decision-making transparency obligation (Theme 5, §3) becomes binding from 10 December 2026 for the charities and non-government organisations many graduates of this field enter. C1 (distributed cognition and relational capability) sits one level above the floor for a related reason: no core assessment yet requires coordinating work between a practitioner and an AI system.

The remaining adaptive items (C2, C4, C5) each sit one or two levels short of the maximum and can move with additions to existing assessed tasks rather than new units. Workplace practice is close to the ceiling: W2 and W3 already meet the maximum anchor via the required placement sequence, and W1 needs one further step — a real external audience with conduct assessed — to reach it. Both gates pass and constrain how the interventions below may be sequenced.
