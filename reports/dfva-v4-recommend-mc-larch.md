## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

This program's widest gaps are Work-situated learning (W3, headroom 3) and, at headroom 2 each, Digital & AI literacy (C3) and Professional communication & conduct (W1). The market report shows Stable demand for the core Landscape Architect skills — design, urban, client, construction, environmental (§4) — against a Falling drafting trend for the adjacent Architectural and Civil Drafters occupation (§4; §3 Theme 4), which most directly warrants the AI-literacy intervention below; the remaining gaps rest on the Stable rows and on the AILA professional-recognition context (§3 Theme 1).
