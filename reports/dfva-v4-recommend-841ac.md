## 1. DIAGNOSTIC SUMMARY — Basis: inferred

*This plan argues from the preceding scored evidence and market data; it is
interpretation, not observation.*

The Bachelor of Oral Health (841AC) is a fixed 3-year full-time undergraduate specialist degree accredited by the Australian Dental Council (ADC) and recognised by the Dental Board of Australia (DBA) for registration as an Oral Health Therapist (dental therapy and dental hygiene). Both gates pass unconditionally: Gate G1 on a staged, all-compulsory ADC-accredited sequence from simulation to full clinical scope, and Gate G2 on live clinical diagnosis, viva voce examinations, and patient treatment adaptation under acute clinical uncertainty.

Operating in a low-exposure, highly manual clinical domain (**40.23**, well below the portfolio median of 90.9), the program exhibits outstanding clinical immersion and adaptive research foundation (**Adaptiveness: 9 / 15**, **Workplace Practice: 7 / 9**). It achieves maximum ceiling scores for independent transfer through self-scoped research progression (**C4: 3 / 3**) and extensive multi-year practitioner-supervised clinical placements at the Royal Dental Hospital and community clinics (**W3: 3 / 3**). Core assessments also feature recurring collaborative presentations (**C1: 2 / 3**), longitudinal formative portfolios (**C2: 2 / 3**), viva voce examinations (**W1: 2 / 3**), and end-to-end OSCE clinical examinations (**W2: 2 / 3**). Remaining improvement headroom focuses on introducing clinical AI radiographic diagnostics in the core (**C3: 1 / 3**), expanding the research proposal into primary empirical data collection (**C5: 1 / 3**), human-plus-AI team allocation protocols (**C1: 2 / 3**), clinical reliance override decision logs (**C2: 2 / 3**), external practitioner evaluation of final vivas (**W1: 2 / 3**), and time-constrained authentic triage simulations (**W2: 2 / 3**).
